#### B5. 副执棒官实验室（Paralictor's Lab）　**严峻 11**

> 走廊最终通入一间实验室。金属桌台立于石地板上**刻有的线条**之上——这些线标记着房间内表面之间的距离。每张桌上摆放着**蒸馏器、坩埚或试剂瓶托盘**；这般布置似乎专为**维系基本安全**而设。

执棒官分身乏术，无法事事兼顾——所及之外便由其**副执棒官**（paralictor）代为掌管。副执棒官**拉西德·伊利托**（Racid Illito）所辖的便是高级监狱。她的办公室位于北侧走廊尽端。其旁配有一间**玻璃壁牢房**，让她在工作时能监视自己最爱的囚犯。当**厄登**来到堡垒时，她将他置于此牢以便观察。他在寇兰廷蔑视地狱骑士的事迹让他的**绝望对她格外美味**，尤其因为他还要为她同行猎物的消失承担责任。

她的职责虽包含无尽的安保规划、采购订单与地狱骑士管理事务，但她同时也为**镣铐骑士团**供应**炼金毒药与物资**。她的办公室兼作处理此类请求的实验室。

**生物**：拉西德正在实验室中合成毒药，两名她麾下的地狱骑士在协助她。她假定**任何非地狱骑士都是来破坏骑士团的**，二话不说便发起攻击。在尝试近战打击之前，她会向她的匕首上**涂抹双足飞龙毒**（wyvern poison）。**万用制剂**是她从远程作战时偏好的炼金炸弹。她和她的地狱骑士都会**死守监狱**。

B5. PARALICTOR'S LAB
MODERATE 11
The hallway finally ends in a laboratory. Metal tables stand on etched lines in the stone floor. The marks measure distances between the surfaces in the room. As each holds a still, crucible, or tray of vials, the arrangement seems to focus on maintaining a semblance of safety.

A lictor can't be everywhere, and it falls to their paralictor to oversee what they can't. Paralictor Racid Illito's charge is the high prison. Her office sits at the end of the northern hallway. A glass-walled prison is installed by it so she can watch over her favored prisoner while working. When Urdun came to the citadel, she placed him in the cell for observation. His flouting of Hellknights in Corentyn makes his despair particularly delicious to her, especially since he was responsible for the disappearance of her colleagues' quarries.

While her duties include endless security planning, purchase orders, and managing Hellknights, she also supplies alchemical poisons and goods to the Order of the Manacle. Her office doubles as the lab where she processes these requests.

Creatures: Racid synthesizes poisons in the lab while two of her Hellknights assist her. She assumes that any non-Hellknight is here to damage the order and attacks. Before attempting melee Strikes, she applies wyvern poison to her dagger. Her versatile vials are her preferred alchemical bomb attack when fighting from range. Both she and her Hellknights fight to the death to defend the prison.
