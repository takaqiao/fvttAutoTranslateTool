### 船长与船员（Captain and Crew）

**庄园本宅清场**之后，**囚犯们如今可安全藏匿于此**——**等监狱越狱掀起的风波平息**。**然而对 PC 们而言**，**没有时间休息**。**泽拉米尔与厄登催促 PC 们**为 A5 区那艘**废弃船凑出一支船员队伍**——**好启航前往泽拉米尔的故乡**。

### 沿海航行（Sailing the Coast）

行程**约 225 英里至地狱之口海湾边缘**，**再 75 英里抵达泽拉米尔的故乡**。**每日行进 32 英里**，**PC 们的船可在 9 天内完成航程**。下文列出**沿途可能发生的事件**——**你可按你认为合适的任意顺序安排**。

#### 事件 1：奥拉克斯的猎犬（The Hounds of Oourax）　**严峻 12**

通过其**狡猾的地狱协约**，**恶魔教士奥拉克斯**（Oourax，第 63 页）**已获得对三只冥河海蛇**（Stygian sea serpents）**的控制权**——她以此**在地狱之口海湾设下扼喉之锁**。她**派两只海蛇巡逻海湾边境**——**当 PC 们靠近时**，**它们便开始尾随 PC 们的船只**。

**危害**：**感受到风暴将至**，**海蛇会等待数日再发起攻击**。**海面变得越来越汹涌**，**最终汹涌到足以演化为一种危害**。

Sailing the Coast
The journey spans approximately 225 miles to reach the edge of the Hellmouth Gulf and then another 75 miles to reach Zeramial's home. Traveling at 32 miles per day, the PCs' ship can complete the journey in 9 days. The following outlines events that befall PCs on the way, which can occur in any order you see fit.

EVENT 1: THE HOUNDS OF OOURAX	MODERATE 12
Through her clever infernal bargaining, Oourax the diabolist (page 63) has gained control of three Stygian sea serpents, which she used to establish a choke-hold on the Hellmouth Gulf. She has two serpents patrolling the borders of the gulf, and as the PCs draw near, they begin to stalk their ship.

Hazards: Sensing a storm is in the air, the serpents wait for a few days before attacking. The waters steadily grow choppier until eventually they become turbulent enough to develop into a hazard.
