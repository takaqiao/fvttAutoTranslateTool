### 撤离（Getaway）

战斗持续 5 轮结束时，或伊格纳罗与他的军团魔被击败时，警报响彻街巷。任一 PC 通过成功的 DC 25 寇兰廷学识或社会检定来**识破**（Recall Knowledge）便知这些警报是在召唤援军。PC 们必须在被逮捕之前逃离现场。

本场景使用**追逐子系统**（chase subsystem，《主持人核心》192）：PC 们试图在多塔里增援赶到之前抵达一处安全屋。本场追逐每轮代表 1 分钟。多塔里增援在追逐第二轮的第一个障碍处出现，按先攻 10 行动，并每轮自动推进一个障碍。

若多塔里与 PC 们处于同一障碍上，PC 们将面临一场两支精英多塔里折磨者分队（NPC 核心 5，第 236 页）的遭遇战。如守卫击败 PC 们，PC 们会被带到德霍夫拉姆堡（Dhoffram Keep）的一处地方监狱；详见第 17 页的"被捕！"边栏。

Getaway
At the end of 5 rounds of combat, or Ignaro and his vordine are defeated, alarms blare throughout the streets. A PC who succeeds at a DC 25 Corentyn Lore or Society check to Recall Knowledge knows that these alarms are signaling reinforcements. The PCs must escape the scene before they're arrested.

This scene uses the chase subsystem (Pathfinder GM Core 192) as the PCs try to reach a safe house before dottari reinforcements reach them. Each round of this chase represents 1 minute. The dottari reinforcements appear at the first obstacle in the second round of the chase, move on initiative count 10 and advance automatically at the rate of one obstacle per round.

If the dottari are ever on the same obstacle as the PCs, the PCs face an encounter with two elite dottari excruciator divisions (NPC Core 5, page 236). If the guards defeat the PCs, they're brought to a local jail in Dhoffram Keep; see the "Arrested!" sidebar on page 17.

WHISPER IN THE ALLEY	OBSTACLE 10

An alley cuts away from the Museum of History, where Linetta Seacarver waits to intercept the PCs. When the PCs enter it, Linetta warns them that it's time to run—the dottari will surely be looking to arrest them. THROUGH HIGH QUARTER STREETS	OBSTACLE 10
Chase Points 3; Overcome DC 25 Deception to blend in with the High Quarter crowds, DC 29 Religion to locate hidden shrines that offer temporary sanctuary, DC 27 Stealth to sneak around the cramped buildings
Crowds and cramped streets provide ample cover as the PCs avoid the dottari. Those who notice the illicit shrines to gods other than Asmodeus can seek temporary sanctuary, though the caretakers will quickly turn the PCs back out to the streets once the danger passes.

PAST THE WALL	OBSTACLE 10
Chase Points 3; Overcome DC 27 Athletics to scale the wall, DC 27 Crafting to demolish a crumbling wall section, DC 29 Deception or Stealth to evade the dottari's notice
An ancient wall divides Corentyn. While it's mostly a relic of history and citizens can move freely between neighborhoods, the typical passages between the west and east halves of the city swarm with dottari. Scrambling over the walls or finding crumbling sections to knock down creates alternative paths.

EASTTOWN STROLL	OBSTACLE 10
Chase Points 2; Overcome DC 29 Diplomacy to charm any observers, DC 27 Society to move with the confidence of the well-off residents
Linetta warns the PCs as they enter Easttown that the residents quickly report strangers. She warns them to slow down and act as though they're on a leisurely stroll.
