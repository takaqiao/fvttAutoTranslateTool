## Page 6 — 战役概览：战役背景

### 战役背景（Campaign Background）

恶魔教派的斯戎家族（House Thrune）掌控切利亚斯（Cheliax）后不到十年，该帝国的部分疆域因抗议而宣告分立，建立起安多安（Andoran）这一新国。此后六十年，切利亚斯一直对邻国的不服从与那种令人作呕的民主政治积怨在心，不断谋划着压垮这"暴发户"政府、把安多安重新收归帝国治下。安多安亦同样急于推翻斯戎家族的暴政。尽管数十年来双方互有可"合理推诿"的小规模冲突与谍报往来，却始终无人敢冒险点燃全面战争。

这一僵局因神雨（Godsrain）而打破——战神古拉姆（Gorum）陨落，其神体的碎片散落于全宇宙，化为"战神碎片"（warshards）。切利亚斯女王阿波罗盖二世（Queen Abrogail II）悬赏任何呈交于她的战神碎片，邻国对此忧心忡忡，不解她如何打算运用这般巨量的神力。当一块战神碎片在依斯嘉（Isger）——切利亚斯的傀儡国——布雷克屯（Breachill）的叛军手中浮出水面时，阿波罗盖派出士兵前去夺取，并镇压这场起义。这群名为"破狱者联盟"（Hellbreakers League）的叛军向安多安求援，而安多安派出了一队雄鹰骑士（Eagle Knights）以维持和平。切利亚斯则以擒杀雄鹰骑士将军雷金纳德·科莫斯（Reginald Cormoth）作为回应。激怒的雄鹰骑士奋起还击，最终取胜，解放了布雷克屯，但却给了切利亚斯宣战所需的全部口实。

这场冲突释放了数十年来淤积的紧张关系，双方都急于一举决出胜负。起初，战事在两个战场上推进：两国陆地边界沿线的阿斯珀德尔长城（Aspodean Wall），以及内海（Inner Sea）上的海军交锋。尽管安多安略占上风，但照此速度，双方都难以迅速且无灾难性损失地获胜。因此，两国都向邻邦与盟友求援，希望把它们拖进战火、扭转局势。

当然，这些邻邦各有各的算盘。拉哈多姆（Rahadoum）打算利用这场令切利亚斯分心之际，夺回卡利（Khari）——切利亚斯在伽伦德（Garund）大陆上最后一处微小的殖民据点。摩尔苏恩（Molthune）则以南方战事为契机，向邻国发动攻势抢占土地，几乎不必担心政治反弹。新近独立的瑞瓦诺（Ravounel）与边境上的切利亚斯军队不断擦枪走火——它绝不愿冒险让切利亚斯那机动便利的大军卷土重来，再次将其征服并吞并。其余多数国家都宣告中立，因种种经济与政治考量而不愿表态站队。然而，这些国家整体上只是隔靴搔痒地反咬切利亚斯几口，并未给安多安实质性的助力。

但这一切即将改变。切利亚斯的港口城市寇兰廷（Corentyn）控制着奥罗登拱桥（Arch of Aroden），因而掌握着内海与阿卡迪亚洋（Arcadian Ocean）之间的航运咽喉。然而，寇兰廷这座国际化都市的市民如今已公开地抨击战争，数个派系把这股怒火化作了行动。"碎冠者"（Crowncrushers）是粗豪的自由战士，急欲摧毁压迫寇兰廷的那位异端魁首——即便代价是抢掠、混乱与几桩纵火案。"铃花会"（Bellflower Network）则主要由半身人运营，专门把弱势人群偷渡出切利亚斯安全转移（偶尔也把英雄人物偷渡入境）；自战争爆发以来，他们更是加倍努力，处处掣肘切利亚斯并疏散平民。

且不仅平民百姓对沉重赋税不满——这些赋税本是用来供养斯戎家族的大军——阿波罗盖二世更要求所有地狱骑士（Hellknights）都为其军队效力，这是对地狱骑士独立性的公然侵犯。一些地狱骑士团向切利亚斯屈膝臣服，另一些则严辞拒绝（哪怕冒着个人乃至整个骑士团被驱逐出境的风险）；而寇兰廷的锁链骑士团（Order of the Chain）则一分为二，亲斯戎派的地狱骑士另立"镣铐骑士团"（Order of the Manacle），并将昔日锁链骑士团的同袍尽数关押。

察觉到寇兰廷的反抗气息，切利亚斯近日颁布戒严令，严格限制经由奥罗登拱桥的通行，如今更对寇兰廷展开警察突击搜捕，意图肃清所有异见者。

### 关于 PC（About the PCs）

冒险开始时，PC 们正是被困在寇兰廷的人之一。他们可能仅是被切利亚斯扣押的某艘商船上的旅客，也可能是当地居民——出于援助寇兰廷、解放家园的动机而起身行动；甚至有可能是《地狱破灭》冒险之路（Hellbreakers Adventure Path）的资深队伍，在依斯嘉新近成功独立之战后离开当地，前来探寻打击切利亚斯的新途径。

Campaign Background

Within a decade of the diabolical House Thrune seizing control of Cheliax, part of that empire seceded in protest to form the nation of Andoran. Cheliax has spent the last 60 years seething at its neighbor's disobedience and sickeningly democratic politics, ever scheming ways to crush the "upstart" government and return Andoran to imperial control. Andoran is equally eager to topple the Thrunes' tyrannical regime. Despite decades of plausibly deniable skirmishes and espionage, neither side has risked all-out war.

This status quo changed with the Godsrain, when the war god Gorum perished and fragments of his divine body fell across the cosmos as warshards. Cheliax's Queen Abrogail II placed a bounty on any warshard delivered to her, worrying neighbors as to how she planned on using so much divine power. When a warshard surfaced in the hands of rebels in Breachill, a town in Cheliax's puppet state of Isger, Abrogail dispatched soldiers to seize it and stamp out the uprising. The rebels, known as the Hellbreakers League, sought aid from Andoran, which sent a contingent of Eagle Knights to keep the peace. Cheliax responded by capturing and executing the Eagle Knights' general, Reginald Cormoth. Outraged, the Eagle Knights retaliated and ultimately prevailed, freeing Breachill but providing Cheliax all the justification it needed to declare war.

The conflict has unleashed decades of simmering tensions, with both sides eager to finally and decisively defeat the other. Initially, the war advanced in two theaters: the nations' land border along the Aspodean Wall as well as with clashing navies on the Inner Sea. Even though Andoran narrowly has the upper hand, at this rate neither side can win swiftly and without devastating losses. Thus, both countries have appealed to neighbors and allies, hoping to draw them into the conflict and tip the balance.

Of course, those neighbors have plans of their own. Rahadoum intends to exploit the distraction to reclaim Khari, Cheliax's last tiny colonial foothold on Garund. Molthune has attacked its neighbors, using the war to the south as an opportunity to seize land with minimal political backlash. The recently liberated country of Ravounel skirmishes with Chelaxian forces on its border, unwilling to risk Cheliax's conveniently mobilized armies should they attempt to subjugate and annex Ravounel again. Most others have declared neutrality, unwilling to commit to either side for sundry economic and political reasons. Yet, collectively, these countries are merely sniping at Cheliax, not assisting Andoran in substantive ways.

That will soon change. The Chelaxian port city of Corentyn controls the Arch of Aroden and thus controls traffic between the Inner Sea and the Arcadian Ocean. However, cosmopolitan Corentyn's populace has become openly critical of the war, with several factions channeling that outrage into action. The Crowncrushers are rowdy freedom fighters eager to destroy the archheathen oppressing Corentyn, even if that means robbery, anarchy, and a bit of arson. Operated primarily by halflings, the Bellflower Network specializes in smuggling vulnerable people to safety out of Cheliax (and occasionally smuggling heroes in), and it's worked twice as hard to stymie Cheliax and evacuate civilians since the war began.

And it isn't just citizens who balk at the heavy taxes levied to fuel Thrune's armies; Abrogail II has demanded that all Hellknights serve in her armies—a violation of their independence. While some Hellknight orders bent the knee to Cheliax and others rebuked the demand (at the risk of both those individuals and their orders being expelled from the country), the Order of the Chain in Corentyn fractured in two with pro-Thrune Hellknights forming the new Order of the Manacle and imprisoning their Chain colleagues.

Sensing Corentyn's defiance, Cheliax recently imposed martial law, severely limited passage through the Arch of Aroden, and has now launched police raids on Corentyn to flush out all dissidents.

About the PCs

The PCs are among those trapped in Corentyn when the adventure begins. They might be mere passengers on a ship that Cheliax seized or longtime residents of the area motivated to aid Corentyn and liberate their home. The PCs might even be veterans of the Hellbreakers Adventure Path, having departed Isger after its recently successful secession battle and seeking out new ways to undermine Cheliax.
