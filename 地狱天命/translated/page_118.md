### E. 天悬之墓（The Skybound Tomb）

**阿瑟沃克斯**知道她所教导的那批切利亚斯学徒在窥探她，但他们突然背叛——盗走她的典籍——这件事却让她始料未及。值得一说的是，那些学徒做这事时其实并非奉**斯戎家族**之命；切利亚斯政府是真心想履行他们与这位巨龙天文学家的交易——他们将获得一位强大盟友以及天文研究，而几乎不必付出代价。

事实上，这些学徒是受到**维拉尔·布雷坦**（Verlar Bretan；威严的男性巫妖 奥能法师 12 级）的魔法支配。**维拉尔**生前曾是数百年前一位强大的法师，定居于**沉言林**，专注于增强自己的魔法潜力。他对天文学痴迷成性，事实上正是**阿瑟沃克斯**目前作为巢穴所用的这座天文台的最初建造者与拥有人。生前，**维拉尔**了解到一种被称为**暗域之主**（Dominion of the Black）的存在，以及它们与盟友所掌握的伟大力量。深知与之联络并将其带至**戈拉里翁**地表归他掌控这件事，远非他一生所能完成，他便举行了化身**巫妖**的仪式，并刻意让自己的转化进行得低调而不显眼，以免引起当时掌控切利亚斯的**奥罗登**信众的注意。

近来，从长眠中苏醒之后，**维拉尔**便着手探索当今世界，并开始在他旧日的天文台里观察**阿瑟沃克斯**。当她收下凡人学徒时，他便利用自己的魔法**支配**他们，让他们盗走她最珍贵的研究并悉数带回他的巢穴；他随后处置了这些信使，把典籍据为己有。自从弄到她的书之后，他设法联络到了来自世界边界之外的几个存在，并对这些他喜欢称为「**星外之魔**」的家伙抱有宏大的图谋。

玩家角色们无需任何检定即可找到墓室入口——**阿瑟沃克斯**早已在沮丧之中一次次飞越其上，并在地图上清楚标注。除非另有说明，所有内部结构高 10 英尺，且无照明。所有门都由加固木材所制，墙壁皆为厚石。

### E. The Skybound Tomb

Athervox knew that the Chelaxian apprentices she was training were spying on her, but their sudden betrayal by stealing her tomes caught her by surprise. To her credit, the apprentices weren't actually acting on orders from House Thrune when they did so; the Chelaxian government truly wished to keep up their half of the bargain with the draconic astronomer, for they stood to gain a powerful ally and astronomical research while giving up little in return.

In truth, the apprentices were under the magical sway of Verlar Bretan (imperious male lich arcanist 12). Verlar was a powerful wizard in life many centuries ago, making his home in the Whisperwood and focusing on increasing his magical potential. He was fascinated by astronomy and was in fact the original builder and owner of the observatory now used as a lair by Athervox. In life, Verlar learned of the creatures known as the Dominion of the Black and the great power that they and their allies wield. Knowing that it would take more than a single lifetime to contact them and bring them to the surface of Golarion under his control, he performed the rites to become a lich, intentionally making his transformation subtle so as to not attract the attention of the faithful of Aroden who were, when he lived, in control of Cheliax.

Having recently awoken from his long slumber, Verlar set about exploring the modern world and began observing Athervox in his old observatory. When she admitted mortal apprentices, he used his magic to dominate them into stealing her prized research and bringing it all to his lair, disposing of the couriers and taking the tomes for his own. Since obtaining her books, he managed to reach out to a few beings from beyond the edge of the world and has great designs in mind for these "demons from beyond the stars," as he likes to call them.

The PCs don't need to attempt any checks to find the tomb's entrance, as Athervox has flown over it many times in frustration and clearly marked it on her map. Unless otherwise noted, all internal structures are 10 feet in height and aren't illuminated. All doors are made of reinforced wood, and all walls are thick stone.
