### 章节结尾（CONCLUDING THE CHAPTER）

就在玩家角色侥幸挺过与梅尔托雷贡的劫难后片刻，**燃狱之门再度激活**。又一束猩红地狱火从燃狱之门的束缚五芒星中喷涌而出，传送门进一步扩张，宽至覆盖整个发掘坑。**又有四只攻城魔鬼**穿越位面之间的迷雾走来，一**支军团魔军团**齐步穿越位面之门列队入场，三叉戟笔直握于身侧。

一位**身披重甲的尼萨利指挥官——一只暴君魔鬼（tyrant devil）**——在这一片密集地狱部队上空飞行。他披着险恶铠甲，挥舞着一柄沉重战锤，此锤似乎能将光线吞噬殆尽。此身影非他人，正是**戈索克莱克（Gorthoklek）**——**阿波罗盖·斯戎二世女王**的仆从、统辖**所有切利亚斯军队**的**元帅**。

塔尔曼铎毫不畏惧，振翅飞向前去，身侧伴有几位骑乘飞行坐骑的勇敢鹰骑士，一同直面这位地狱将军——明显意图**一举永远终结这场冲突**！

接下来发生什么，**完全取决于玩家角色是否赢得了沉言林之战**。

**如果玩家角色输掉了渗透**（亦即输掉此战），你应描述塔尔曼铎与几位空中鹰骑士冲锋向前，与四只攻城魔鬼及戈索克莱克在一阵惨烈却短暂的厮杀中相撞。**鹰骑士们被分尸撕裂**，梅尔托雷贡们重创塔尔曼铎，将他制伏。它们抓住他的羽翼、臂膀与双腿，**将这位天界使彻底固定住**。在玩家角色与安多兰军队的恐惧注视下，戈索克莱克将其暗金属战锤**高高举过头顶**，重重砸落于**塔尔曼铎的脑袋上**，伴着一声令人作呕的脆响打断了他的颈骨——那声响传遍整片战场，**清楚地终结了他的生命**。戈索克莱克发出一阵邪笑，**抓起这位天界使瘫软的尸首**，趁安多兰前线开始崩溃之际，**将其抛入传送门——丢进地狱深处**。片刻之后，**燃狱之门猛然爆裂**——堪比火山喷发的爆炸，不圣之火**瀑布般倾泻而出**，蹂躏已然溃逃的安多兰部队，毫不留情地割倒步兵、鹰骑士、指挥官与将军。剩下的切利亚斯士兵不受这次爆炸所伤，反而以野蛮的得意欢呼着，看着他们的敌人四散奔逃、燃烧成炭。鉴于玩家角色在战场上的位置，他们因此幸免于这场神圣之怒爆炸的伤害。但他们如今**深陷敌后**，**经多个小时鏖战已身心俱疲**，**可呼救的盟友寥寥**。他们必须立刻**逃入林中**——否则迟早被肃清剿灭。

**反之，若玩家角色赢得了渗透**，你应描述塔尔曼铎与其随从勇敢冲锋上前，与梅尔托雷贡们厮杀片刻，**甚至成功击杀其中两只**。然而鹰骑士们随即就被这些泰坦级邪魔从空中击落，**只剩塔尔曼铎独自迎敌**。当只剩两只攻城魔鬼、塔尔曼铎仍在拳打、抓挠、挥击邪魔之时，戈索克莱克只得**亲自出手**——他不得不放下他的暗黑战锤（任由地面上的魔鬼拾起），动用自身可观的力量去压制这位天界使。当戈索克莱克与梅尔托雷贡们将塔尔曼铎扭拽过传送门、拖入地狱之际，这位天界使的目光与玩家角色相锁——他**口型默念出一个天界力量之词**。当他越过通往地狱的门槛之际，**塔尔曼铎扭转了门的能量**，引发一场**带蓝晕的力场碎片爆炸**——刺穿了数百名军团魔士兵和大部分残存的切利亚斯部队（他们正被安多兰军队压回门前）。**此爆炸对安多兰部队丝毫无损**，反而让**披红黑斯戎家族战衣的部队**仓皇逃命——**摧毁其地狱增援**！然而尘埃落定之时，玩家角色却发现**塔尔曼铎踪迹全无**——很明显，戈索克莱克成功将这位天界使**绑架至地狱**用作某种邪恶图谋，**尽管他显然依旧活着**。**玩家角色与盟友赢得了战役**，但**对一国之灵魂代价惨重**。

**无论玩家角色输或赢这场战役**，他们都能安全离开燃狱之门所在地。然而，**塔尔曼铎被擒（或战死）所引发的混乱**，使**玩家角色与他们的地狱骑士盟友**与安多兰主力部队彼此分离，**迫使队伍进入沉言林更深处的一片林地**——但所幸此处安全。

**总之，沉言林之战已落幕。**

Concluding the Chapter
Moments after the PCs survive their ordeal with the merztorlegon, the Inferno Gate flares to life once again. Another scarlet burst of hellfire erupts from the Inferno Gate's binding pentagram as the portal enlarges, widening to encompass the entire excavation pit. Four more siege devils step through the haze between planes as a legion of vordines march in lockstep through the planar gateway, tridents held rigidly at their sides.

A heavily armored nessari commander—a tyrant devil—flies above these massed infernal troops. He is dressed in sinister armor and carries a hefty mace that seems to absorb the light. This figure is none other than Gorthoklek, servant of Queen Abrogail Thrune II and marshal of all Chelaxian armies.

Dauntless, Talmandor flies forward, flanked by a few brave Eagle Knights on flying mounts to confront the diabolic general, clearly intending to bring an end to this conflict once and for all!

What happens next entirely depends on whether the PCs won or lost the Battle of the Whisperwood.

If the PCs lost the infiltration (and thus the battle), you should describe Talmandor and a few aerial Eagle Knights charging forward, being met by the four siege devils and Gorthoklek in a terrible but short-lived clash. The Eagle Knights are torn limb from limb, and the merztorlegons heavily wound Talmandor, disabling him. They grab his wings, arms, and legs, completely immobilizing the agathion. As the PCs and Andoren armies watch in horror, Gorthoklek raises his dark metal mace high in the air and smashes it down on Talmandor's head, breaking his neck with a sickening crack that can be heard all across the battlefield, clearly ending his life. With an evil laugh, Gorthoklek grabs the agathion's limp body as the Andoren front begins to falter, then throws it through the portal—into Hell. A moment later, the Inferno Gate explodes in an eruption that's nothing short of volcanic, with unholy fire cascading out and devastating the already-fleeing Andoren troops, cutting down infantry, Eagle Knights, commanders, and generals without mercy. The remaining Chelaxian soldiers are unharmed by this explosion and cheer with savage pride as their enemies scatter and burn. Given the PCs' position on the battlefield, they're spared being damaged by this explosion of divine wrath. But they are now caught deep behind enemy lines, are likely exhausted from many hours of battle, and have few allies to call upon for succor. They must now flee into the woods or face eventual extermination.

On the other hand, if the PCs won the infiltration, you should describe Talmandor and his retinue bravely charging forward and fighting the merztorlegons for a few moments, even managing to kill two of them. The Eagle Knights are knocked out of the sky by the titanic fiends in short order, though, leaving Talmandor alone. With only a couple of siege devils left and Talmandor still bravely fighting, clawing, and slashing at the fiends, Gorthoklek must lend his own considerable strength to restraining the agathion, dropping his dark mace for the devils on the ground to pick up. As Gorthoklek and the merztorlegons wrestle Talmandor through the portal into Hell, the agathion's eyes lock with the PCs as he mouths a word of heavenly power. As he crosses the threshold into Hell, Talmandor alters the gate's energies, causing an explosion of azure-tinged force shards that impale hundreds of vordine legionnaires and most of the surviving Chelaxian troops, who the Andoren military pushed back toward the gate. This explosion spares the Andoren troops entirely and sends the red-and-black clad forces of House Thrune fleeing for their lives as it devastates their infernal reinforcements! When the dust clears, however, the PCs find no sign of Talmandor, and it's clear that Gorthoklek managed to kidnap the agathion and take him into Hell for some malign purpose, even though he clearly still lives. The PCs and their allies have won the battle, but at great cost to the soul of a nation.

Regardless of whether the PCs won or lost the battle, they can leave the site of the Inferno Gate safely. However, the chaos caused by Talmandor's capture (or death) separates the PCs and their Hellknight allies from the rest of the Andoren army, forcing the party into a deeper, albeit safe, patch of the woods.

One way or another, the Battle of the Whisperwood is over.
