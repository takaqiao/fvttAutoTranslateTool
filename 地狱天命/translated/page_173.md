#### A8. 图书馆（LIBRARY）　**中等 18 级**

> **数排书架自入口向内延伸**——**配以一张小阅读桌与一把孤椅**。**此处的光线略亮于走廊**——**但声响则略受闷抑**。**一只高凳被推入南墙边一张更高的书桌之下**。**桌上摊开着一本巨大的书卷**。

皇宫图书馆藏有斯戎家族数十年间收集的大部分知识——**其中包括许多在切利亚斯各处其他地方已遭禁毁的书籍副本**。**阿波罗盖正是借此处的资讯——结合 A9 区所藏「斯戎家族与地狱之契约」的细节——为最近赢下这场仍在进行的战争而筹划计谋**。

**通往 A9 区的暗门隐藏在本室后部一只书架之后**——**可通过 DC 42 知觉检定 或 DC 38 图书馆学识检定发现**；**若玩家角色想到寻找《地狱律法解读》第六卷（实则没有第 1 至 5 卷），则此检定获得 +4 状况加值**。**当这本假书被拽出时**——**该书架旋开**。

**生物**：**阿波罗盖的研究得到皇宫图书馆员奥克西利斯·韦鲁拉**（**Auxilis Verula**）**的协助**——**他已为斯戎家族效力近一个世纪**。**这位看似羸弱的老法师在他的日常工作中**——**有一对名为「篇章」与「典籍」**（**Page and Chapter**）**的「高等鳞书蜥」**（**advanced scalescribes**）**协助**。**这些娇小的爬虫畸变生物与奥克西利斯结为契约——其性质类似于一位施法者可结契的「**契兽**」——**它们存在的意义即是协助他**——**尤其在保持图书馆有序方面**。

奥克西利斯坐在他的书桌前，**记录着这场战争最近的一些事件——他认为这便是他一生的代表作品**：**一本他效力斯戎家族的生涯回忆录**。**这位图书馆员是阿斯摩蒂斯信仰的虔诚信徒**——**对皇室真心忠诚**。**他不喜被人打扰**——**会赶走任何进入图书馆的人**。**若玩家角色坚持不退**——**奥克西利斯终于自他的书写中抬起头来**——**要这些闯入者解释自己**。**若他们流露出任何想要阻挠阿波罗盖的迹象**——**这位图书馆员便会勃然大怒——立即发动攻击**。**「篇章」与「典籍」随即自书丛中现身——加入战斗**。

**A8. LIBRARY	MODERATE 18**

> Several rows of shelves stretch back from an entryway, with a small reading table and a single chair. The light in here is slightly brighter than that of the hallways, but sounds are slightly muffled. A tall stool has been pushed under an even taller desk along the southern wall. A massive tome sits open on the desk.

The palace library contains most of the knowledge collected by House Thrune over the decades, including copies of many books that have otherwise been banned or destroyed across Cheliax. Abrogail used the information here—combined with details from House Thrune's contract with Hell found in area A9—to formulate her recent plan to win the ongoing war.

The secret door to area A9 is hidden behind one of the shelves in the rear of the room and can be discovered with a successful DC 42 Perception or DC 38 Library Lore check; the PCs have a +4 circumstance bonus to this check if they think to look for Understanding Infernal Law Vol. 6 (there are no volumes 1 through 5). The shelf swings open when this fake book is pulled.

**Creatures**: Abrogail's research was aided by the palace librarian, Auxilis Verula, who's been in the employ of House Thrune for nearly a century. The seemingly frail old mage is assisted in his daily tasks by a pair of advanced scalescribes named Page and Chapter. These tiny reptilian aberrations are bound to Auxilis similar to how a familiar can be bound to a spellcaster and live to aid him, especially in matters of keeping the library organized.

Auxilis sits at his desk, chronicling the latest events of the war in what he believes will be his life's master work: a memoir of his life serving House Thrune. The librarian is a devout believer in the Asmodean faith and truly loyal to the royal family. He doesn't appreciate being interrupted and shoos away anyone who enters the library. If the PCs are insistent, Auxilis finally looks up from his writing and demands that the intruders explain themselves. If they give any indication that they want to thwart Abrogail, the librarian is incensed and immediately attacks. Page and Chapter then appear from within the stacks to join the fight.

However, if the PCs succeed at two consecutive DC 40 Deception checks, they can fool Auxilis into believing they're also loyal to House Thrune. He then helps them find the information described in Development below but doesn't allow them to enter area A9, noting that the contract archive is off-limits. The PCs can stealthily open the secret door to the contract archive with a successful DC 42 Stealth check or distract Auxilis with another successful DC 40 Deception check.
