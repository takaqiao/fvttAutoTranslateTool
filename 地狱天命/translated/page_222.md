House Leroung traces its origins to Taldor's seventh
Army of Exploration, which pacified the regions now
known as Isger in 2133 ar. The house's founder,
Alcikabia Leroung, documented the campaign's
battles, movements, and achievements. She compiled
these reports into a popular history that earned
renown in Oppara, which she leveraged to fundraise
new schools that could raise the children of Taldor's
newest territory to become upstanding citizens. The
Leroung name has been synonymous with educational
excellence in southern Avistan ever since.

Even so, Alcikabia and her descendants remained
commoners (albeit wealthy ones) for generations.
Their fortunes changed when Pelsus Leroung became
the lifelong tutor to the young Prince Talgasten. While
he never took the throne, he did champion educational
reform when Cheliax became a province, including by
ensuring Pelsus was granted a barony and a mandate
to oversee Cheliax's educational resources. Since then,
House Leroung has founded schools across the old
Taldan empire, ranging from country schoolhouses
and trade schools to towering universities in Almas,
Egorian, Korvosa, and beyond.

Despite the various territories the house formally
oversees, House Leroung focuses more on academics
than on politics. Indirectly, this policy has granted the
house far more power than any traditional politicking
could, for Leroung educators have continued
Alcikabia's legacy by mentoring countless highborn
children, most of whom hold House Leroung in high
esteem as adults. These connections ultimately saved
House Leroung during Cheliax's civil war. Despite not
siding with any faction in the first decade, Leroung
seemed like a friend to all and an enemy to none.

Thus, when Leroung ultimately allied with House
Thrune in the war's latter half, Abrogail I didn't hold
their grudging support against them; she was better
served making the scholars allies than examples. Each
knows it's better to offer the other ongoing support for
their projects, exercise soft power, and spark no feuds.

House Leroung still maintains their schools in
Cheliax's sphere of influence. However, nearly half of
their institutions have fallen under other governments'
control: military academies in Molthune, Almas
University in Andoran, the since ruined Torvin
Academy in Galt, and many more. Leroung academics
fight to remain employed by these surviving schools,
both to maintain their house's legacies as well as to
spread Leroung influence abroad.

An old yet modest house, Sarini historically
controlled swathes of agricultural land along the
Andoshen River in central Andoran, ruling over
towns such as Sauerton and Oregent. When civil war
erupted, the Sarinis eagerly supported House Thrune
while conquering lands for themselves, hoping that
afterward these new territories would be ceded to
them. The conflict drained the house's resources and
earned it countless enemies; historians rightfully
credit Sarini aggression and atrocities for turning
Andoran's population against House Thrune, even
before Abrogail I made her deal with Hell. By the
time the war ended, House Sarini ruled over two
counties. Counties full of civilians who loathed them.

When Andoran threw off Chelaxian rule in 4669
ar, it wasn't just in defiance against House Thrune;
it was a memorandum on House Sarini. The latter
briefly skirmished with revolutionaries and called for
support from House Thrune, receiving only imperious
demands that House Sarini quash the rebellion in
its figurative backyard. Cut off, Sarini aristocrats
surrendered to the mobs and were extended the same
offer as other nobles in the People's Revolt: Either be
exiled or cast aside one's noble titles and holdings to
become an equal citizen in Andoran. Appalled, most
of House Sarini fled to Cheliax.
