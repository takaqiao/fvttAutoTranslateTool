#### 事件 2：被掠而走（Carried Away）　**中等 12**

**安弗里塔石柱群**（Pillars of Anferita）之中**藏匿着御风者灯塔**（Lighthouse of the Aeromancer，第 57 页）——**这座灯塔如今被一只暴躁的雪鬼**（snow oni）**所占据**——**他名为"消融者"雷玛斯**（Rematsu the Melted-One）。**温暖的海风让他烦躁不已**——雷玛斯**一年中数次于夜空巡游**，**搜寻可供折磨的猎物**。**PC 们航行途中一个雾蒙蒙的傍晚**，**雷玛斯发现了他们的船**。

**生物**：**当 PC 们的船被浓雾吞没时**，**雷玛斯爬上他的宠物大鹏"灰萎"**（Greywither），**悄无声息地俯冲而下**，**从甲板上掳走一名水手**。**鉴于雾的遮蔽性**，**除非 PC 们对意外有所防备**，**否则在雷玛斯逃入雾中之前**，**他们多半无法发起有效的追击**。在此情况下，**他将回到灯塔**，**用他的新俘虏自娱自乐**。**若 PC 们能在他归塔之前赶上他**，**雷玛斯与灰萎会以"打了就跑"的战术与 PC 们在雾中周旋**。**若 PC 们杀死灰萎**，**或雷玛斯被削减至 90 HP 或更低**，**这只雪鬼便会隐形并试图逃走**。

**进展**：**为鼓励 PC 们去解救被掳走的水手**，可考虑让大鹏**抓走泽拉米尔、厄登或另一位深受喜爱的 NPC**。**雷玛斯袭击大约 10 分钟后**，**雾会短暂散开**——**让 PC 们能瞥见远处的一座灯塔**——**若他们抓紧时间**，**救援或许仍有可能**。

#### 事件 3：海螳号（The Sea Mantis）　**中等 12**

**PC 们所不知的是**——**傲世号那位复仇心切的船长沃蕊·塔尔米纳里**（Vorrea Talminari）**已派出声名远播的切利亚斯赏金捕手战舰"海螳号"**（the Sea Mantis，**地图见第 58 页**）**追踪 PC 们**。**在他们的航程中某时**，**该船趁夜色掩护逼近 PC 们发动攻击**。

> 一艘**乌黑船帆**的船在**晴朗夜空之下**逼近。**船的木料覆有厚重的乌木漆**，**让它呈现出甲壳般的外观**——**在月光下熠熠生辉**。**船首雕成超巨型螳螂的前半身**——**前肢蜷曲于其三角形头部之下**。

**雷纳德·豪克**（Reynard Hawk）——**海螳号的船长**——**借由当初突袭"傲世号"时甲板上沾染的血**追踪到了 PC 们。**虽然这次遭遇发生在晴朗的夜晚**，**海面仍然相当昏暗**。**120 英尺以外的物体处于全黑之中**，**而 120 英尺以内的物体则处于昏光照明**。**遭遇开始时双船相距 120 英尺**。

**生物**：**雷纳德·豪克**是个**狡黠之人**，自称**多年前与一只魔鬼玩牌赢得了海螳号**（**作为一艘帆船**；GM Core 第 217 页）。**真相是**：**切利亚斯巫师以邪魔魔法召出一只超巨型邪魔螳螂**，**将其束缚于他的船上作为活体（且危险）的船首雕像**。**作为交换**——**雷纳德与他的船须为切利亚斯效力**。**雷纳德的副手是尼弗拉斯·扬多**（Nyvorlas Yandor）——**一位以杀法师为长的冷酷女性**；**他的船员则主要由各骑士团中**展现出**海事造诣**的地狱骑士组成。

**海螳号逼近时**，**邪魔螳螂保持不动以避免吸引火力**，**而船员们大多利用船舷栏杆"寻找掩蔽"**（Take Cover；这授予他们标准掩蔽）。**雷纳德对自己驾船能力深感自信**——**他亲自掌舵**，**以动作"驾驶"直奔 PC 们的船**。**一旦相距 30 英尺以内**，**地狱骑士们便使用"登船擒抱"**（Boarding Grapple）**将两船拉拢一处**。

**遭遇战**：**本遭遇使用 Battlecry! 中的遭遇战规则**——**但 PC 们与他们的敌人各只指挥一支分队**。**遭遇即将开始时**，**让玩家们决定由哪位 PC 在战斗中掌控他们的船员**——**应用其遭遇战羁绊**。**尼弗拉斯指挥一支地狱骑士分队**。

EVENT 2: CARRIED AWAY	MODERATE 12
Nestled away within the Pillars of Anferita is the Lighthouse of the Aeromancer (page 57), which is a cantankerous snow oni called Rematsu the Melted-One now occupies. Made miserable by the warm sea air, Rematsu roams the night skies several times per year looking for prey to torment. During one foggy evening on the PCs' journey, Rematsu spies their ship.

Creatures: While the PCs' ship is engulfed in fog, Rematsu climbs atop his pet roc Greywither and swoops down silently to snatch a sailor from the deck. Given the concealing nature of the fog, unless the PCs are prepared for mischief, they likely can't mount an effective pursuit before Rematsu escapes into the fog. In this case, he returns to the lighthouse to amuse himself with his new captive. If they can catch him before he returns, Rematsu and Greywither use hit-and-run tactics to battle the PCs in the fog. If the PCs kill Greywither or if Rematsu is reduced to 90 Hit Points or fewer, the oni turns invisible and attempts to flee.

Development: To encourage the PCs to rescue the snatched sailor, consider having the roc grab Zeramial, Urdun, or another well-liked NPC. The fog briefly clears approximately 10 minutes after Rematsu's attack, allowing the PCs to catch a glimpse of a lighthouse in the distance—rescue might still be possible, if they hurry.

EVENT 3: THE SEA MANTIS	MODERATE 12
Unbeknownst to the PCs, Vorrea Talminari, the vengeful captain of the Impervious, has set the renowned bounty-hunting Chelaxian warship the Sea Mantis (depicted on the map on page 58) on their trail. At some point during their journey, the ship approaches the PCs under cover of night to attack.

A ship with jet-black sails approaches under the clear night sky. The wood of the ship is covered in thick ebony lacquer, giving it a chitinous appearance that catches the moonlight. The ship's prow is carved to resemble the front half of a gargantuan praying mantis with its front claws curled under its triangular head.

Reynard Hawk, the captain of the Sea Mantis, has tracked the PCs using blood from the deck of the Impervious during their earlier assault. Although the encounter occurs on a clear night, the sea is still quite dark. Objects farther than 120 feet away are in total darkness, while objects within 120 feet are dimly lit. The encounter begins with the ships 120 feet apart.

Creatures: Reynard Hawk is a cunning man who claims he won the Sea Mantis (as a sailing ship; GM Core 217) in a game of cards with a devil years ago. In truth, Chelaxian wizards used diabolic magic to conjure a fiendish mantis of gargantuan size and bound it to his ship as a living (and dangerous) figurehead. In exchange, Reynard and his ship are bound to Cheliax's service. Reynard's second-in-command is Nyvorlas Yandor, a ruthless woman who specializes in killing mages, and his crew is composed mainly of Hellknights from a variety of orders who have demonstrated skill at sea.

As the Sea Mantis approaches, the fiendish mantis remains still to avoid attracting fire while most of the crew uses the ship's railings to Take Cover (granting them standard cover). Confident in his sailing abilities, Reynard pilots the ship and uses his actions to Drive directly toward the PCs' ship. Once within 30 feet, the Hellknights use Boarding Grapple to pull board the ships together.

Skirmish Encounter: This encounter uses the skirmish rules from Battlecry!, though the PCs and their foes only command one troop each. As the encounter is about to begin, have the players decide which PC takes control of their ship crew in combat, applying their skirmish bond. Nyvorlas commands a troop of Hellknights.
