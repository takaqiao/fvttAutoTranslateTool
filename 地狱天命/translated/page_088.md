## Page 88 — Ch4 最终突击：三障碍 + 派系机遇 + 章节结语 + 寇兰廷街区表

除了三个可选障碍，若 PC 已成功招募第一章中的全部四个派系，他们还可在此环节获得**四个独特的机遇**。

**调遣兵力（MANEUVER TROOPS）　障碍**
**渗透点**　2（个人或团队）；**化解**　杂技、运动、潜行 或 战争学识

你利用回合带领部队穿行城市街道，布设伏击、推开障碍、调动增援至最需要之处。

**号召市民（RALLY CITIZENS）　障碍**
**渗透点**　2（个人或团队）；**化解**　欺瞒、交涉、威吓 或 表演

过去一个月，沃蕊残酷地压迫了寇兰廷的市民，他们早已蓄势欲反。你以一回合提供火花，说服市民推翻该街区。

**暗中破坏防御（UNDERMINE DEFENSES）　障碍**
**渗透点**　2（个人或团队）；**化解**　奥秘、自然、通神、宗教 或 巧手

除士兵部队外，沃蕊还在寇兰廷布下了大量魔法与机关防御。PC 可花一回合，运用自己对魔法或机械的知识来禁用这些防御，或令其反伤其主。

**自由之歌（SONG OF FREEDOM）　机遇**
**条件**　PC 已说服铃花会加入起义。

随着切利亚斯部队节节败退，铃花会涌上街头，高唱一曲自由之歌，如野火般传遍全城。PC 可花一回合协助传唱此歌，尝试一次 DC 31 表演检定。若成功，本场战斗剩余时间内 PC 尝试的所有其他检定获得 +1 状况加值。该动作可重复执行，每次成功累加 1 点加值，最高至 +4。

**异端魁首陨落（ARCHHEATHEN'S FALL）　机遇**
**条件**　PC 已说服碎冠者加入起义。

PC 可加入碎冠者的一场惊险屋顶突袭，潜入异端魁首的营驻地，终结他的统治。通过 DC 31 杂技或潜行检定，PC 带领碎冠者攻入驻地。这使征服**贵族区**的 DC 减 3（大成功时减 6）。该机遇可反复尝试，直到成功为止。

**砸碎锁链（BREAK THE CHAINS）　机遇**
**条件**　PC 已说服锁链骑士团加入起义。

锁链骑士团利用其对寇兰廷防御的了解，扰乱沃蕊的补给线——命令市民堵塞特定街道，或威吓敌军按兵不动。PC 可花一回合尝试 DC 31 交涉或威吓检定来助传此扰乱。若成功，所有支援街区加值在 1 轮内被抵消。该机遇可反复尝试，直到成功为止。

**盛大演出（THE BIG SHOW）　机遇**
**条件**　PC 已说服细缕者加入起义。

细缕者已将魔法与化学烟火藏匿于全城各处。单个 PC 可花一回合尝试 DC 31 奥秘或自然检定来引爆烟火，让全城陷入混乱。成功时，其他每位 PC 在其 1 轮（大成功时 2 轮）内尝试的技能检定结果上加 1d4。该机遇可反复尝试，直到成功为止。

### 章节结语（Concluding the Chapter）

在沃蕊一个月的残酷压迫之后，寇兰廷的市民最终在城市被收复时将 PC 奉为英雄。然而，这场更大的战争远未结束，PC 不久之后将被需要在别处。

OBSTACLE 
Infiltration Points 2 (individual or group); Overcome 
Deception, Diplomacy, Intimidation, or Performance
Vorrea has harshly repressed the citizens of Corentyn 
for the past month, and they're ready to revolt. You 
spend your turn providing the spark to convince the 
citizens to overthrow the district. 
UNDERMINE DEFENSES
OBSTACLE 
Infiltration Points 2 (individual or group); Overcome 
Arcana, Nature, Occultism, Religion, or Thievery
In addition to troops of soldiers, Vorrea has filled Corentyn 
with magical and mechanical defenses. PCs can spend a 
turn and use their knowledge of magic or machinery to 
disable these defenses or cause them to backfire. 
SONG OF FREEDOM
OPPORTUNITY
Requirements The PCs convinced the Bellflower Network 
to join the rebellion.
As Chelaxian forces begin to fall, the Bellflower Network 
takes to the streets with a song of freedom that spreads 
like wildfire through the city. A PC can use their turn to 
help spread this song by attempting a DC 31 Performance 
check. If successful, all other checks attempted by the 
PCs receive a +1 circumstance bonus for the remainder 
of the battle. This action can be performed multiple times 
and increases the bonus by 1 with each success, up to a 
maximum bonus of +4.
ARCHHEATHEN'S FALL
OPPORTUNITY
Requirements The PCs convinced the Crowncrushers to 
join the rebellion.
A PC can join the Crowncrushers in a daring rooftop caper 
to infiltrate the Archheathen's Garrison to finally end his 
reign. With a successful DC 31 Acrobatics or Stealth check, 
the PC leads the Crowncrushers into the garrison. This 
reduces the DC to conquer the Noble Quarters by 3, or 6 
on a critical success. This opportunity can be repeated until 
it succeeds.
BREAK THE CHAINS 
OPPORTUNITY
Requirements The PCs convinced the Order of the Chain to 
join the rebellion.
The Order of the Chain uses their knowledge of Corentyn's 
defenses to disrupt Vorrea's supply chains by ordering 
citizens to clog certain streets or by intimidating enemy 
troops into inaction. PCs can use their turn to attempt 
a DC 31 Diplomacy or Intimidation check to help spread 
this disruption. If successful, all supporting district 
bonuses are negated for 1 round. This opportunity can 
be repeated until it succeeds.
THE BIG SHOW 
OPPORTUNITY
Requirements The PCs convinced the Thin Wisps to join 
the rebellion.
The Thin Wisps have stashed magical and chemical 
fireworks throughout the city. A single PC can use their turn 
to attempt a DC 31 Arcana or Nature to check to help set 
them off, causing chaos throughout the city. On a success, 
each other PCs adds 1d4 to the result of skill checks they 
attempt for 1 round (or 2 rounds on a critical success). This 
opportunity can be repeated until it succeeds.
Concluding the Chapter
After a month of brutal repression at the hands of 
Vorrea, the citizens of Corentyn hail the PCs as heroes 
when Corentyn is retaken at last. The greater war 
is far from over, however, and soon the PCs will be 
needed elsewhere.
