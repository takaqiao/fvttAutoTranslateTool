#### E3. 实验典藏（EXPERIMENTAL ARCHIVE）　**轻微 15**

此室形状与北侧的姊妹典藏完全相同，但书架上收藏的是关于不同生物类型，以及借魔法控制他者的学问。在此室中央附近，一颗小巧的宝石悬浮于空中，散发着柔和的多彩光芒。

**危害**：这里悬浮的宝石是**维拉尔**曾经研究过、后来感到无聊而搁置的**魔法迷魂与控制实验**的中心节点——任其落了一身比喻意义上的灰尘。它在此处无人照料地飘浮着，其威力已增长到超出这位巫妖的最初设计，如今会自动伸出力量去削弱所有靠近之人。

#### E4. 会议厅（MEETING CHAMBER）　**低 15**

此室的中心装置是一张以单块**山铜**（orichalcum）雕成的奇形桌，桌四周围着数张椅子。室东侧一张椅子比其他几张大得多、华丽得多。在这间室里，**维拉尔**与他从这颗行星疆界之外引来的存在们密议……目前正有人占着。

**生物**：一名来自**暗域之主**的代表（一只**高斯瑞格**，gosreg）正以本相在此，与三只**闪辉之子**（shining children）共论：如何最有效地拓展**维拉尔**的能力以将更多**位外行星存在**带至**戈拉里翁**。当玩家角色们闯进此室时，他们将竭力攻击入侵者。**高斯瑞格**已经习惯了**闪辉之子**那令人目盲的灵光（其也照亮了此室），后者则同样适应了**高斯瑞格**令人不安的灵光。**高斯瑞格**逃不掉，会拼死战斗，但其他三者在被打到 HP 50 以下时会用**次元移位**（translocate）逃走。

#### E3. EXPERIMENTAL ARCHIVE　TRIVIAL 15

This room is shaped identically to its sister archive to the north, although its shelves contain lore about different creature types and methods of magically controlling others. A small gem hovers in the air near the center of this chamber, giving off soft, multihued light.

Hazards: The floating gem here is the locus of a magical captivation and control experiment that Verlar once worked on but grew bored with, leaving it to proverbially gather dust. As it floated here unattended, its power increased beyond the lich's original designs and now automatically reaches out to debilitate anyone who draws near.

#### E4. MEETING CHAMBER　LOW 15

The central fixture of this room is an oddly shaped table carved from a single piece of orichalcum, with several chairs around it. One of the chairs to the east of the room is much larger and grander than the others. In this chamber, Verlar takes counsel with the beings he brings to Golarion from outside of this planet's borders… and it's currently occupied.

Creatures: A representative from the Dominion of the Black (a gosreg) is here in its natural form, along with three shining children, discussing how best to expand upon Verlar's capabilities to bring more extraplanetary beings to Golarion. When the PCs intrude upon this room, they attack the intruders to the best of their ability. The gosreg has gotten used to the blinding aura of the shining children (which also illuminates the room), and they have in turn become inured to the gosreg's unsettled aura. The gosreg can't escape and fights to the death, but the other three use translocate to escape when brought below 50 HP.
