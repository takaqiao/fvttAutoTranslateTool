要探索这座要塞首先需要进入其中。随着拱桥的衰败要做到这一点便需要绕过已坍塌的部分留心其建筑尚存的骨架并避开城内多塔里的耳目。寻求刺激者常会尝试探索其中。那些成功者会讲述已衰朽的营房以及锈死的脚柜。一群刚刚成年的渔夫转型为业余探险者常在同伴怂恿之下试图进入沉没的塔只得发现水面之上的入口已设有新近上膛的陷阱。

#### 3. 贝尔维里奥堡（CASTLE BELVERIO）

贝尔维里奥堡的废墟坐落于一条早已废弃的入城路径之上。当它的塔楼在一连串的入侵之后被重建之时穿过城墙的门便被封堵了。鉴于这座要塞的布局再无可辨之用途它已被除役并在更新版的名录之中常被——「**异端魁首庄园**」——取代成为第九堡。然而夜间在其附近行走的人常汇报神秘的光与声响由此衍生出关于闹鬼的传闻。

#### 4. 伊索诺堡（CASTLE ISSONO）

自——**埃戈里安**、**西冠城**——以及切利亚斯大部分地区驶来的道路终止于伊索诺堡的大门。随着奥罗登拱桥与通往——**卡利**（Khari）的便道的毁灭这座要塞已是一人能经陆路前往并仍身处切利亚斯境内的最远之地。

#### 5. 蒂亚佩特拉堡（CASTLE TIAPETRA）

蒂亚佩特拉堡欢迎自西南而来的旅人。鉴于那一方向上仅有的大型站点是——「**安弗里塔林**」（Anferita Wood）——与——「**格拉戴斯卡堡垒**」——它主要迎接地狱骑士。然而它自诩为通往寇兰廷的门户并在一颗五芒星的每一尖端升起切利亚斯的旗帜。许多带游客前去观赏寇兰廷九堡的徒步参观以此为起点给它再加上了一份专以城中毫不知情的新来者为目标的景点名声。它的一层大多已被改造为马厩。出一笔可观的价格平民便能把他们的马与贵族的坐骑一同寄养。

#### 6. 多弗拉姆要塞（DHOFFRAM KEEP）

阿斯摩蒂斯教会在斯戎家族崛起后不久获独占使用多弗拉姆要塞的权利。任何人除非因官方事务皆不得进入其内。其石墙之后发生着什么无人知晓也从无一丝声响自其内传出。大多数市民避免途经其附近即便这意味着身处商贸区的人必须走更长的路。

#### 7. 奥勃兰之坚（OBRAND'S STAND）

在拱桥与城市相接之处奥勃兰之坚拔地而起。两者彼此提供结构性的支撑。奥勃兰之坚借拱桥之助耸立于这座城市的城墙之上而其保养良好的防御工事则保护拱桥不致倾塌。

驻扎在上层要塞之中的是轮换的弓箭手们他们在为期一季的部署之中保护这座城市抵御空中的攻击。他们的射击可达一英里之外为海域以及周围的陆地提供掩护。

#### 8. 惩戒监狱（THE PENITENTIARY）

惩戒监狱曾关押寇兰廷最为危险的罪犯。如今它已坍塌至只能关押轻罪的程度。即便是这些也很容易逃出但通常被判处于此的典型的盗窃犯与宗教异端分子往往会选择支付罚款而非面对一辈子做逃犯的生活。它保有一座可观规模的宗教法器金库而其安保也同样松懈。

#### 9. 临海堡（SEAWARD FORT）

临海堡锚定着海军坞之上的军事行动而封锁舰队的指挥官的旗舰则以目前在港的舰船的海军上将身份驻于其中。临海堡已被列入除役名册但一处合适的替代地点尚未觅得。

3. CASTLE BELVERIO
The ruins of Castle Belverio sit on a long-abandoned
route into the city. When its towers were rebuilt after a
series of invasions, the gate through the walls was sealed
off. As the fort's configuration serves no discernible
purpose, it has been decommissioned and often replaced
by the Archheathen's Manor as the ninth fort on more
updated lists. However, those walking near it at night
often report mysterious lights and sounds, leading to
rumors of a haunting.

4. CASTLE ISSONO
The road from Egorian, Westcrown, and most of
Cheliax ends at the gates of Castle Issono. With the
destruction of the Arch of Aroden and the footpath to
Khari, this fort is the furthest that a person can travel
overland and remain within Cheliax.

5. CASTLE TIAPETRA
Castle Tiapetra welcomes travelers approaching from
the southwest. Since the only major sites in that direction
are Anferita Wood and Citadel Gheradesca, it mostly
greets Hellknights. However, it bills itself as the gateway
to Corentyn and flies a flag of Cheliax on each tip of a
five-pointed star. Most of the foot tours that take tourists
to see the nine forts of Corentyn start here, adding to
its reputation as an attraction focused on unsuspecting
newcomers to the city. Much of its ground floor has been
converted to stables. For a handsome price, commoners
can board their horses alongside noble mounts.

6. DHOFFRAM KEEP
The church of Asmodeus received sole use of Dhoffram
Keep shortly after the rise of House Thrune. None
are permitted within unless on official business. What
happens behind its stone walls is unknown, and no
sound ever escapes it. Most citizens avoid the path near
it, even it if means those in Mercanto must take a more
extensive route.

7. OBRAND'S STAND
Where the Arch meets the city, Obrand's Stand rises.
The two provide structural support to each other.
Obrand's Stand looms above the city's walls with the
Arch's assistance, and its well-maintained fortifications
protect the Arch from collapse.

Stationed in the upper forts are rotations of archers,
who protect the city from aerial attacks during their
season-long deployment. Their shots can reach up to a
mile out, providing coverage over both the water and
the surrounding land.

8. THE PENITENTIARY
The Penitentiary once held the most dangerous criminals
of Corentyn. Now, it lies collapsed to the point that it
can only hold petty criminals. Even those would find
it fairly easy to escape, but the typical shoplifters and
religious miscreants who would be sentenced there
typically pay their fines rather than face life as a fugitive.
It holds a sizable vault of religious paraphernalia with
similarly lax security.

9. SEAWARD FORT
The Seaward Fort anchors the military operations on
the Navy Yard, where the commander of the blockade's
flagship serves as admiral for the ships currently
in port. The Seaward Fort has been scheduled for
decommissioning, but a suitable location has yet to be
found for its replacement.

10. THE ARCH OF ARODEN
The Arch of Aroden begins its stretch over the Hespereth
Strait in the western edge of Corentyn. It once served as
a footbridge that connected to Khari on the southern
side of the strait. This function stopped when the center
of the bridge collapsed, which also halted any potential
invasions that would approach on foot. The Arch
continues to crumble, with more of the structure falling
into the sea each year. Now, only a scant mile remains of
the nearly 15-mile-long span, though some pillars can
still be seen, lone survivors jutting from the sea.

The Arch serves as a visible reminder that Cheliax
once worshipped Aroden. The Eyes of Aroden, once
carved into the structure, have long been defaced.
However, not all signs to Aroden were destroyed. The
keystone to the Arch was removed from the water's
depth and brought to the capital of Andoran, where it
was used in a monument to their patron Talmandor.
