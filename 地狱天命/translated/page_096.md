### LOVE AND WAR

Pezzack's southern edge is controlled by Chelaxian loyalists who aren't loyal to House Thrune, but rather to the idea of a unified nation of law and order. Few people wander the street except near The Throne Defiant inn. If the PCs ask about Darjan Tauranor, these passersby point them toward the Governor's Manse.

When the PCs arrive, they're admitted into the courtyard. Darjan emerges with four bodyguards to greet them. A PC who succeeds at a DC 30 Perception check to Sense Motive notices the loyalists here hold Darjan in high regard and will fight bravely for him.

Darjan is stern but open with the PCs, noting that since Ravounel has been implicated in the assassination attempt that took place during the peace talks, he isn't happy to see them here. He also acknowledges that during the assassination, he offered Marynn shelter and has kept her as a well-treated hostage since then. He believes that her captivity prevents insurgents from acting rashly, signals the strength of Darjan's own faction, and will encourage Amalia Wraxton to negotiate a truce for Pezzack's sake.

In truth, Darjan is in love with Amalia and her dashing ways. He had hoped that negotiating peace with the strix might also ease factional tensions and allow him to express his affections openly, but the assassination inflamed old grudges, endangered the town, and spoiled Darjan's romantic aspirations.
