#### E1. 伪墓（FALSE TOMB）　**琐碎 15**

双扇门后是一间开阔之室，墙边林立着一排排古代天文学家的雕像——他们的名字早已湮没于时光。室西端立着一座巨大青铜望远镜雕塑，其基座上以古旧**通用语**刻着铭文：「天文学家**维拉尔·布雷坦**——愿其手永不止于求索群星。」雕塑看似覆于墓上。六具身着破烂学者袍服、袍上染有**斯戎家族**色彩的干瘪尸体堆叠在雕塑之上以及它前方的地面上。

通过 DC 29 察觉检定，一名玩家角色可看出这是一座假墓——望远镜下的地面其实是实心岩石。通过 DC 36 察觉检定，一名玩家角色还能注意到：其中两座雕像可在底座上转动，露出暗藏的走廊。然而，如果有人扰动了那些尸体、雕塑或可旋转的雕像，便会有显著反应！

**生物**：那批把从**阿瑟沃克斯**那里偷来的研究带给**维拉尔**的切利亚斯学徒们，遭到这位巫妖谋杀，被随便扔在此处。由于**巫妖**巢穴四周盘绕的死灵能量，他们以**报殃尸**（revenants）的姿态复活了过来。尽管玩家角色们未必就是他们的杀手，但只要房中有任何东西被扰动，他们便会站立起来，试图强行进入巢穴更深处。如果玩家角色们尚未找到那些暗道，**报殃尸**们便会花费动作把这些雕像撬开，然后才会向附近任何人挥击。

#### E2. 空旷典藏（EMPTY ARCHIVE）

这间室的墙边摆着石制图书馆架，上面陈列着各种关于天文学、其他世界与其他维度的书籍。一个专设区域致力于研究**艾欧克斯**（Eox）星，许多卷宗暗示该世界蕴藏着大量**不死生物**——他们也可能反过来正运用天文学来研究**戈拉里翁**。魔法附魔让此室与其内全部书籍保持原貌如新。

#### E1. FALSE TOMB　TRIVIAL 15

The double doors reveal an open area, the walls lined with statues of ancient astronomers whose names are lost to time. At the western end of the room is a large bronzed sculpture of a telescope with an inscription at its base in archaic Common that reads, "Astronomer Verlar Bretan, may his hand never stop reaching for the stars." The sculpture appears to cover a grave. Six desiccated bodies wearing the tattered remains of scholar's robes with House Thrune's colors lay heaped atop the sculpture and the ground in front of it.

With a successful DC 29 Perception check, a PC discerns that this is a false grave, and the ground beneath the telescope is actually solid rock. With a successful DC 36 Perception check, a PC also notices that two of the statues can be rotated on their bases to reveal secret hallways. If anyone disturbs the bodies, sculpture, or movable statues, however, there's a noticeable reaction!

Creatures: The Chelaxian apprentices who brought Verlar the research stolen from Athervox were murdered by the lich and then unceremoniously dumped here. Due to the necromantic energies swirling around the lich's lair, they reanimated as revenants. Though the PCs aren't necessarily their murderers, they'll rise to their feet when anything in the room is disturbed, trying to force their way deeper into the lair. If the PCs haven't yet found the secret passages, the revenants will spend actions forcing these statues open before lashing out at anyone nearby.

#### E2. EMPTY ARCHIVE

Stone library shelves line the walls of this chamber, containing all sorts of books on astronomy, other worlds, and other dimensions. A special section is devoted to the study of the planet Eox, with many volumes suggesting that world contains multitudes of undead creatures who might in turn be using astronomy to study Golarion. Magical enchantments keep this room and all of its books in pristine condition.
