### B4. 工坊（WORKSHOP）　轻微 17

**这间工坊的宽大工作台上摆满了处在不同装配阶段的衣物**。**数百卷布料填满深邃的货架**——**橱柜抽屉中放着各式缝制工具**。**裁缝模特们沉默肃立**——**毛绒身躯插满备用别针**。

**此区是制作定制成衣的主工坊**。**除了工具与材料的卓越品质，这间工坊乍看并无显眼之处**。**话虽如此，房间内还藏着通往邪教地下圣所的密道**——**一扇活板门**，**门面覆有陶瓷砖以融入地板的几何马赛克**——**外面还覆盖着一块宽大地毯**。**玩家角色搜寻时凭 DC 38 察觉检定成功，可找到这道暗门**。**任何携带店钥之人，在距活板门 5 英尺内时**，**能感觉到钥匙微微震动**——**使 DC 降低 5**。

**生物**：**营业时间内此处的四名裁缝皆是非战斗员**——**若战斗爆发便寻找掩护**。**然而，首席裁缝则是该邪教的精英成员之一**——**只要有人威胁店员、试图打开活板门、或问出太多危险问题**，**他便挺身护卫圣所**。

B4. WORKSHOP 　TRIVIAL 17
This workshop's wide tables are covered with garments in varying stages of assembly. Hundreds of bolts of cloth fill the deep shelves, and bureau drawers contain arrays of sewing tools. Dressmaker dummies stand in silent vigil, their plush bodies riddled with spare pins.

This area is the main workshop for creating custom outfits. Except for the tools' and materials' exceptional quality, this workshop is unremarkable at a glance. That said, the room also hides the secret passage leading into the cult's underground sanctuary, a trap door covered in ceramic tiles to blend in with the floor's geometric mosaic, further covered by a wide rug. A PC can locate the secret door with a successful 38 Perception check while Searching. Anyone carrying one of the shop keys can feel the key vibrate slightly when it's within 5 feet of the trap door, decreasing the DC by 5.

Creatures: The four tailors here during business hours are noncombatants who seek shelter if combat occurs. The master tailor, though, is one of the cult's elite members and fights to defend the sanctuary if anyone threatens the staff, tries to open the trap door, or asks too many dangerous questions.
