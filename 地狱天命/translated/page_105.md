### E13. 武库（ARMORY）

闪光岩堡的密武库一直深藏不露，连今日住在这里的枭鬼们都不知其存在，库内仍堆满了源自其原始矮人住民被迫弃守此堡之时的武装。

**战利品**：库中堆有五十锭已硬化的**刚玉锭**，只待会其打造技艺的矮人将其炼成武器与盾。以其目前形态，每锭负载 1，价值 100 gp。库内另有五柄**刚玉**（标准品质暮木）**+1 强击回力长矛**、一套**高盔堡要塞甲**（《探索者迷踪志：高盔堡》112）以及一具**观星仪**（stargazer；《珍宝宝库》35）。

### E14. 王座厅（THRONE ROOM）	中等 或 严峻 14

这间巨厅布置得像围绕中央火坑的营地，但厅的南端是一座立于斜角石台上的石质王座。东墙上半截被打通，形成一处临时阳台，连通至 E15 二层楼。这间屋子是闪光岩枭鬼们平日聚集听**玛拉拉**——她是他们的**罗科亚**（rokoa），即精神领袖——讲故事的所在。但近几个月来，自从玛拉拉变了样、像军阀般统领部族以来，部族成员愈发宁愿待在自己的小栖巢里，而非共聚此处。**梅兰迪亚**——这只附身玛拉拉的诡谎魔——已把此室恢复其原本作为王座厅的职能，只接见玛拉拉最狂热的追随者们；他们栖身于 E15 区域。

如果前门守卫把玩家角色们领到了这里，或玩家角色们在 E8 或 E12 没经战斗便潜入了此处，那么梅兰迪亚（以玛拉拉之身）正沉睡在王座上。在此情形下，玩家角色们可尝试一次 DC 34 察觉检定**察觉动机**，或一次 DC 32 神秘或宗教检定。成功时，他们能注意到地狱附身的典型迹象，并有机会驱逐附居于这位枭鬼首领体内的恶魔。但若闪光岩堡的住户已醒或处于高警戒，则梅兰迪亚清醒地端坐于王座上，她的爪牙栖息在通往 E15 的阳台上。这种情况下，他们会先对玩家角色们做一番"莫管闲事"的简短斥责，再借玛拉拉之身与其盟友们出手攻击。

**仪式**：若玩家角色们识破附身且梅兰迪亚正在沉睡，他们便有机会解救玛拉拉并将其体内的恶魔驱逐。如果玩家角色们将一柄**银**或**曙光银**武器置于这位枭鬼首领面前的地上作为法器，所有仪式施法者的检定都会获得 +2 物品加值，但放置武器的人必须以 DC 33 隐匿检定成功，否则梅兰迪亚会醒来，使仪式准备前功尽弃。

**快速地狱祛魔仪式（QUICK DIABOLIC EXORCISM）	仪式 7**
施法 10 分钟；**次要施法者** 1 至 3 人
**主要检定** 地狱学识（专家）、神秘（专家）或宗教（专家）；**次要检定** 奥秘、地狱学识、自然、神秘、宗教或社交
**距离** 10 英尺；**目标** 1 个生物
你召唤自身关于下层位面的知识，将一只地狱魔从凡躯中驱逐出去。该仪式只对当前正在附身凡躯的魔鬼有效，且仅在该凡躯被束缚、昏迷或自愿的情况下生效。
**大成功** 同"成功"，并额外使该恶魔**震慑 3**。
**成功** 你成功将附身凡躯的恶魔驱逐出去，不伤及凡躯或恶魔。
**失败** 你未能驱逐恶魔。若目标原本昏迷或对仪式毫无察觉，此时它会醒来。
**大失败** 同"失败"，但所有仪式施法者因这鲁莽之举遭受地狱反噬，皆**震慑 1**。

**生物**：若玩家角色们未能完成驱逐梅兰迪亚的仪式，恶魔会借玛拉拉之身与她的三名枭鬼追随者一同迎战玩家角色们（在场的门卫站旁观，不知如何是好）。玛拉拉死亡之前，梅兰迪亚便会主动**取消**附身效果，从枭鬼首领体内现形，迎战玩家角色们。一旦获释——无论是经由仪式还是其他途径——这位枭鬼首领会以支援法术协助玩家角色们。怒不可遏的梅兰迪亚则战至阵亡。如果拉沃努尔的奎伦特魔仍存活，且梅兰迪亚已被仪式从玛拉拉身上驱逐，那么它会以**次元移位**进入此室加入战斗——孤注一掷。

### E13. ARMORY

Glittercrag Keep's secret armory was kept obscured enough that even the strix living here today are unaware of its presence, and it remains full of armaments that date back to when its original dwarven occupants were forced to abandon the fortress.

**Treasure**: Fifty ingots of hardened corundum are stacked in this room, ready to be turned into weapons and shields if any dwarves still alive know the secret of their crafting. As they exist now, each ingot weighs 1 Bulk and is worth 100 gp. There are also five corundum (standard-grade duskwood) +1 striking returning spears, one suit of Highhelm stronghold plate (Pathfinder Lost Omens Highhelm 112), and one stargazer (Treasure Vault 35).

### E14. THRONE ROOM	MODERATE OR SEVERE 14

This large chamber is set up like a campsite surrounding a fire pit, but at its southern end is a stone throne sitting on an angular dais. The upper section of the eastern wall has been broken through, creating a makeshift balcony that leads to area E15's second floor. This room is where the Glittercrag strix normally gather to tell stories led by Marala, their rokoa, or spiritual leader. But over the last few months, as Marala changed and led the tribe more like a warlord, the tribe's members spend more time in their smaller roosts rather than in this communal haven. Melandiar, the belier devil possessing Marala, has returned this room to its original function as a throne room, attended to only by Marala's most fervent followers, who roost in area E15.

If the front gate guards led the PCs here, or if the PCs snuck in here without combat in areas E8 or E12, Melandiar (as Marala) is asleep upon the throne. In this case, the PCs can attempt a DC 34 Perception check to Sense Motive or a DC 32 Occultism or Religion check. On a success, they notice the telltale signs of infernal possession and have a chance to cast out the devil who's inhabiting the body of the strix leader. If, on the other hand, Glittercrag Keep's inhabitants are awake or on high alert, Melandiar is awake on her throne with her underlings perched on the balcony to area E15. In this instance, they give the PCs a simple rebuke about meddling in affairs that don't concern them, then use Marala's body and their allies to attack.

**Ritual**: If the PCs identify the possession and Melandiar is asleep, they have an opportunity to free Marala and exorcise the devil inside her. If the PCs use a silver or dawnsilver weapon as a focus by laying it on the ground in front of the strix leader, all ritual casters gain a +2 item bonus to their checks, but the person placing the weapon on the ground must succeed at a DC 33 Stealth check or else Melandiar wakes up, ruining the ritual preparations.

**QUICK DIABOLIC EXORCISM	RITUAL 7**
Cast 10 Minutes; Secondary Casters 1 to 3
Primary Check Hell Lore (expert), Occultism (expert), or Religion (expert); Secondary Checks Arcana, Hell Lore, Nature, Occultism, Religion, or Society
Range 10 feet; Target 1 creature
You call upon your knowledge of the lower planes to cast an infernal fiend out of a mortal's body. This ritual only works on devils who are currently possessing mortals, and only if that mortal body is bound, unconscious, or willing.
Critical Success As a success, but the devil is stunned 3.
Success You exorcise the devil inhabiting the mortal body without harming either one.
Failure You fail to exorcise the devil. If the target was unconscious or unaware of this ritual being enacted, it now wakes up.
Critical Failure As failure, but all ritual casters suffer infernal backlash from their foolish attempt and are stunned 1.

**Creatures**: If the PCs don't complete the ritual to exorcise Melandiar, the devil uses Marala to fight the PCs alongside her three strix followers (any door guards present stand back and watch, unsure about what to do). Before Marala dies, Melandiar Dismisses the possession and emerges from the strix leader's body to fight the PCs. Once freed, whether by ritual or otherwise, the strix leader aids the PCs with support spells. Angered beyond reason, Melandiar fights to the death. If the ochiastis devil from Ravounel is still alive and Melandiar is ritually exorcised from Marala's body, they translocate into the room and join the fight, making their last stand.
