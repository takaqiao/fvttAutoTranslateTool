…obtain one piece of local information. Alternatively, the PCs can attempt a DC 29 Diplomacy check to Request or a DC 29 Intimidation check to Coerce to learn one of the following.

- Brucks is only loyal to profit, although the loss of the blockade makes it harder for them to strong-arm the merchants into following their leadership.
- Brucks is annoyed at a pop-up tavern called the Bleached Whale that opened a few months ago near the ruins of the old Glass on the Hill theater. It's owned and operated by merchant twins Barynn and Marynn, who have trade deals with both orc and strix tribes to import exotic liquors, which they both bottle and serve on tap.
- When the assassins attacked the peace negotiations, Brucks took the opportunity to capture Barynn in the confusion. He's currently tied up in Brucks' office below-decks, where Brucks is trying to force him to give up his contacts with the strix who brew a liquor called shyinda.

### C. BELOW-DECKS	LOW OR MODERATE 14

Upon learning that Brucks is in their below-decks office along with one of the merchant twins, the PCs might head there. A pair of orcs guard the hatch, denying them entry. They let the PCs in if the heroes pay a "booking fee" of 500 gp each, backing down to half that price if rebuffed. That said, a PC who succeeds at a DC 30 Deception, Diplomacy, or Intimidation check can secure the party's entry for free.

Inside, Brucks is dressed in peacock-colored finery. Their interrogator stands near an open hatch, having recently dumped whale-flesh chum to attract a pair of blue sharks that circle in the water below. Barynn is tied to chair just next to the hatch, and the pair have been threatening to drop him if he doesn't talk.

The PCs can defuse this situation without violence. Brucks is an opportunist by nature, so if the PCs make a reasonable case for why releasing Barynn would be more profitable, they'll listen. Allow each PC to make their case and attempt a skill check that best fits the argument (DC 32). For example, they might use Intimidation for open threats of retaliation or Society to convey how beloved the local brewer is (and how upset Pezzack would be if they learned Brucks was responsible).
