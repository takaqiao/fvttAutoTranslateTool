### E10. 矮人工坊（DWARVEN WORKSHOP）	低 14

枭鬼们从未完全征服这堡垒的这一部分。这间屋子尤其曾是工坊，矮人铁匠在此精进创造**活体雕像**（living statue）的技艺，以护卫位于险峻的米纳多山脉中的矮人要塞。因为迷信（与对神龛中亡灵的恐惧）退避三舍，枭鬼只是尽量把这块区域封闭起来，而非直接清剿。由于其中的住客既不需要食物，也无需饮水或空气，他们从未试图离开此地、亦未骚扰过枭鬼。

**生物**：这间昔日的工坊里满是他们的最终成品，但岁月已侵蚀其指挥结构。它们对所有非矮人见之即攻，把入侵者追入邻近房间后才折返岗位。万一所有玩家角色都呈矮人外貌（或成功伪装为矮人），此屋中的生物会被动地站立、不予攻击——直到其中某只生物失去至少 30 点生命值为止。一旦交战，它们便战至毁灭。

### E10. DWARVEN WORKSHOP	LOW 14

The strix never fully conquered this part of the fortress. This room in particular was a workshop where dwarven smiths perfected creating living statues that could defend dwarven fortifications in the dangerous Menador Mountains. Driven away by superstition (and fear of the undead in the shrine), the strix closed this area off as best they could rather than confronting it directly. Since the occupants have no need of food, drink, or air, they've never tried to leave or bother the strix in turn.

**Creatures**: This former workshop is filled with the final products of their creators, but the passage of time has eroded their command structures. They attack any non-dwarves on sight, chasing the intruders into nearby rooms before returning to their stations. On the off chance that all of the PCs appear dwarven (or successfully disguise themselves as dwarves), the creatures in this room simply stand passively and don't attack until one of the creatures has lost at least 30 Hit Points. Once engaged in combat, they fight until destroyed.
