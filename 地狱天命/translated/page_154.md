## Page 154 — 第八章：裁缝与叛徒

——身着最新流行款式的人偶面向过客陈列。**该店出售的成衣不多**——**而是将富有的客户引至私密区域**——**为其量身、款待**，**展示一连串令人心动的面料与设计**。**直到几小时或几天后**，**客户与随员才会回来试穿完成的成衣**、**确认合身**、**听热情的接待大力赞美他们光鲜的新形象**。

**玩家角色可以多种方式调查「金活」**。**他们可能用莉安妮·楚奇的成衣凭证拿下个人量体**——**借此进入限制区、让裁缝放松警惕、并寻找线索**。**他们也可能偏好潜行**——**用技能与魔法**潜入限制区**而无需与店员打交道**。**他们也可以直接开打**——**前述方式无论哪种**最终也可能演变为这一选项（**只不过玩家角色处于有利位置**）；之后他们可慢慢探索店面。

**下面汇总玩家角色可能遭遇的线索与互动**。**请根据玩家角色的策略灵活引用或调整**。

---

**店员（Shop Staff）**：**营业时间内**，**店中通常有 7 名员工**——**一名接待（host）**、**四名裁缝**、**一名店助**、**一名首席裁缝（master tailor）**。**裁缝通常在 B4 区，接待与店助通常在 B1 与 B2 区**。**首席裁缝与接待都是「丝缕信徒（disciples of the thread）」（第 154 页）**——**他们了解部分邪教秘密**（**包括如何进入地下室**）——**但他们并不知晓阿尔卡巴诺斯魔鬼的存在**。**其余员工是低阶非战斗员**，**只是邪教中的初阶入教者**——**几乎不知任何能助玩家角色的事**。

**奥罗登艺术（Arodenite Art）**：**「金活」的装潢极为奢华**——**许多艺术品中都暗含对奥罗登的微妙引用**（**例如含眼、兔子、数字 12，以及青绿、金色等元素的图样**）以及对阿尔卡巴诺斯的引用。**调查装饰的玩家角色凭借 DC 31 艺术学识、DC 31 宗教、或 DC 36 社交学检定成功**，**可识别出大量奥罗登灵感**——**显示店主显然痴迷于这位陨落神祇**。**极佳成功**：**玩家角色注意到一些点缀**——**有时甚至直接嵌入受奥罗登启发的艺术中**——**暗示店主也与地狱有关联**——**例如巧妙融合到插画里的魔鬼语字符**。

**以客户身份渗透（Infiltrating as Clients）**：**一位有分量的客户及其随员便可进入店内大部分区域**。**玩家角色可以用莉安妮·楚奇的凭证**——**或单纯撒钱让人当真看待**（**预算至少 250 gp 便足以**）。**接待会带客户在展示厅巡走以展示不同设计**——**店助则沏茶或倒酒，再摆出几本满载备选成衣与刺绣设计的书供翻阅**。**一旦客户选定服装与定制项目**，**便会被引至试衣间量体**。

**裁缝与首席裁缝由此接手**。**DC 39 欺骗检定成功**，**玩家角色可提出请求让首席裁缝退入工坊 1d6+4 分钟去找珍稀面料或其他奇物**。**普通裁缝则远比首席容易摆布**（**察觉与意志 DC 各为 22**）——**这或许可为几位玩家角色争取时间潜入办公室（B3 区）或探索其他区域**。

**使用莉安妮的密码（Using Lianni's Code）**：**如果玩家角色按莉安妮的信件订购成衣**，**接待或首席裁缝便愉快地确认玩家角色品味卓越**，**随后心灵联络阿尔卡巴诺斯，告知有新人到来**。**那只魔鬼则联络凯森提娅（第 154 页）**——**她将在 30 分钟内赶到**——**亲自接管该玩家角色的量体环节**，**并借着闲谈尽可能套出关于他们的情报**。**这同时也是玩家角色微妙地反过来试探她的好机会**。**这一过程可简单到只是几次「建立印象」技能检定**（**她的态度为「不关心」**）——**也可复杂到一次短影响遭遇（《GM 核心》第 187 页）**——**如果不止一位玩家角色想入教或想进入圣所，该遭遇会略微变难**。**如果她印象不错**，**便会更直接地问相关玩家角色**：**他们是否怀念奥罗登庇佑戈拉里翁与其子民的安宁时代**……**以及他们是否对其复活感兴趣**。**如果凯森提娅满意**，**便邀请选中的玩家角色到 B4 区**——**遣走那里的裁缝**——**通过密道进入 B8 区**。

**如不满意**：**如果凯森提娅不满意**，**她便像没事似的继续量体**——**但从那时起，玩家角色在西冠城期间她都会密切监视**。**她邀请他们 2 天后回来试穿成衣**——**但玩家角色可凭 DC 41 察觉检定成功感知她的疑心与可能的害意**。**如果玩家角色当真回来**，**接待会欢迎他们**，**并叫来两名裁缝协助玩家角色穿衣**——**这其中包括玩家角色脱下身上所穿的任何护甲**。**而且**，**该成衣有几处缝线被巧妙拼接以阻碍移动**——**让玩家角色陷入笨拙 1**——**直到他们逃脱**：**DC 36 体能成功撑破缝线，或 DC 36 巧手剪断关键缝线**。

**一旦玩家角色卸甲且被成衣束缚住**，**凯森提娅与首席裁缝便发起攻击**——**同时激活 B1 区的杀人时装人偶**。**在店中作战时**，**凯森提娅战至 HP 80 或更低，便用封禁术（Sequester）撤退恢复**（**使用 B8 区的药剂**）——**并在 B9 区准备最后的防御**。

The shop sells relatively few premade outfits, instead escorting wealthy clients to private areas to be measured, pampered, and presented with a parade of exciting fabrics and designs. The PCs can investigate Living Gold in a variety of ways. They might use Lianni Ciucci's outfit voucher to secure a personal fitting, using that to access restricted areas, set the tailors at ease, and look for clues. They might favor stealth, employing skills and magic to sneak into restricted areas even without engaging with shop staff. They can also start a fight, which the aforementioned strategies might lead to anyway (albeit with the PCs at an advantage), after which they explore the shop at their leisure.

The following summarizes clues and interactions the PCs are likely to experience. Reference or adapt them as needed based on the PCs' strategy.

Shop Staff: During business hours, the shop typically has seven employees—a host, four tailors, a shop assistant, and a master tailor. The tailors are typically in area B4, whereas the host and assistant typically operate in areas B1 and B2. The master tailor and host are all disciples of the thread (page 154), and they know some of the cult's secrets (including how to access the basement) but aren't aware of the devil Alcabanoth. The others are low-level noncombatants who are mere initiates in the cult and know little that can assist the PCs.

Arodenite Art: Living Gold is lavishly decorated, and much of the art includes subtle references to Aroden (such as imagery involving an eye, rabbits, the number 12, and colors like teal and gold) and Alcabanoth. With a successful DC 31 Art Lore, DC 31 Religion, or DC 36 Society check, a PC Investigating the decorations can identify the plentiful Arodenite inspirations, showing the owner is clearly fascinated by the dead god. With a critical success, the PC notes little flourishes—sometimes even embedded directly into the Aroden-inspired art—that suggest the owner also has infernal ties, such as Diabolic letters cleverly incorporated into the illustrations.

Infiltrating as Clients: A worthy client and their entourage can access much of the shop. The PCs might use Lianni Ciucci's voucher or just throw around enough money to be taken seriously; a budget of at least 250 gp suffices. The host parades the client around the showroom to demonstrate different designs as the assistant brews tea or pours wine before setting out several books full of potential outfits and embroidery designs to peruse. Once the client chooses their outfit and customizations, they're ushered to one of the fitting rooms for measurements.

A tailor and master tailor take over from there. With a DC 39 Deception check, a PC can make requests that cause the master tailor to retreat to the workshop for 1d6+4 minutes to find rare fabrics or other oddities. The lower-ranking tailor is far easier to manipulate (Perception and Will DCs of 22 each), potentially buying several PCs time to sneak into the office (area B3) or explore other areas.

Using Lianni's Code: If the PCs request an outfit based on Lianni's missive, the host or master tailor pleasantly confirms the PC's excellent taste and then mentally contacts Alcabanoth that a new recruit has arrived. The devil contacts Kaisentia (page 154), who arrives within 30 minutes and personally takes over the PC's fitting session while learning whatever she can about them through small talk. This is an opportunity for the PCs to subtly probe her for information. It can be as simple as a couple skill checks to Make an Impression (her attitude is indifferent), or it can be as complex as a short influence encounter (GM Core 187), made slightly more difficult if more than one PC is aiming to join the cult or access its sanctuary. If she's suitably impressed, she asks the PCs in question more directly whether they miss the peaceful times when Aroden shepherded Golarion and its people… and if they're interested in his revival. If Kaisentia is satisfied, she invites the chosen PCs to area B4, dismisses the tailors there, and accesses the secret passage to area B8.

If Unimpressed: If Kaisentia isn't impressed, she resumes the fitting session as though nothing were out of the ordinary, though she carefully monitors the PCs from then on while they're in Westcrown. She invites them to return in 2 days to try on their finished outfit, though a PC can sense her suspicion and likely harmful intentions with a successful DC 41 Perception check. If the PCs do return later, the host welcomes them and calls in two tailors to help the PC don the outfit, which involves doffing any armor the PC is wearing. Further, the outfit has several sections cleverly stitched together to inhibit movement, making the PC clumsy 1 until they Escape with either a successful DC 36 Athletics check to burst the seams or a DC 36 Thievery check to snip the correct seams.

Once the PC is unarmored and inhibited by their outfit, Kaisentia and the master tailor attack, activating the murderous mannequins in area B1. While in the shop, Kaisentia fights until reduced to 80 Hit Points or fewer, after which she uses Sequester to recover (using the potions in area B8) and prepare a final defense in area B9.
