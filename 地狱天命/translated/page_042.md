#### A14. 堡垒小径（Citadel Trail）

虽然被称为"小径"，这条地带其实更像一条**贫瘠的岩石架**。地狱骑士既用它来奖励囚犯，也用它来惩罚囚犯。它给囚犯提供了一次罕见的接触户外的机会——对那些预计死也见不到太阳的人而言，是一份强有力的诱饵。然而，囚犯有时也会被绑住、暴露在悬崖之上，作为对地狱骑士毫不犹豫终结生命的提醒。外墙上装有锚点，足以承载铁链。

**巡逻**：守卫常找借口来此小径转转。由于他们已习惯于看见囚犯在此露面，本区"安置囚犯"机遇（第 34 页）的欺瞒与交涉检定 DC **降低 2**。

#### A15. 囚犯的厩舍（The Prisoner's Stables）　**低 10**

> 这间无窗附属建筑的后缘伸入开阔的空中，仿佛随时会向下方海面斜冲而去。一道拱形屋顶把这座建筑与堡垒主体相连，为该结构那扇巨大的滑动门提供遮蔽。

地狱骑士把此建筑称为厩舍，但实际上它更常用来**关押囚犯**。利刃大师被免职之前，他把中央的栏位留给自己从 8 英里外的小屋骑来的坐骑。其他居住者的舒适度在这一安排中并不被重视。

**囚犯**：牛头人**焦掌**（Scorchpalm）——为碎冠者效力的炼金术士——占据着中央栏位。她属**大型**，因此尺寸庞大令她在堡垒中潜行时尤难隐藏。藏匿她时，"安置囚犯"机遇（第 34 页）需要 **20 分钟**。

**生物**：两只**葛斯雷格**（gosregs）被关在外侧栏位之内——它们伪装成被擒的安多安人类士兵。**黑暗主宰**（Dominion of the Black）派他们渗透安多安雄鹰骑士组织。一次倒霉的运气让他们被擒。这两只伪装成名为**英格丽德**（Ingrid）和**戴林**（Dalin）的一对士兵（欺瞒 +24）并恳求获释。若 PC 们识破他们的伎俩或拒绝释放他们，葛斯雷格便会发动攻击。若获救，葛斯雷格会维持伪装，去寻找新的组织继续渗透。

#### A16. 执棒官走廊（Lictor's Walk）　**严峻 10**

> 楼梯环绕格拉戴斯卡堡垒尖塔外侧盘旋而上。仅有一道铁制栏杆抵御着高空狂风与对下方结构的高坠。
>
> 取代石墙的，是厚到能折射视线的玻璃窗——构成这间孤独房间的边缘。室内，玻璃的形状放大了周围的景色。向南，寇兰廷的港口映入眼帘，硝烟弥漫、战乱混乱。

执棒官走廊是格拉戴斯卡堡垒地狱骑士团领袖的传统办公场所，他们可在此俯视周围的潮坪与寇兰廷港口。尖塔高出地面层 100 英尺，距海底总高 500 英尺。东南角的一架望远镜提供进一步的放大——除了那由精工玻璃墙提供的放大效果之外。

**生物**：执棒官**乌罗·阿登**正在观察寇兰廷港口的战斗，并思忖局势的展开。当阿波罗盖·斯戎二世命令地狱骑士把忠诚置于切利亚斯之上时，他同意了。但日复一日，他私下里愈发怀疑当初的决定。

法治本意是为了灌输秩序与纪律。乌罗相信，文明的舒适会侵蚀这点，威胁正是那些允许这些舒适存在的制度本身。最好把缰绳紧握，而且要握在正确的手中——阿波罗盖承诺正确的手就是她自己。

由于她的计划尚未完成，乌罗对自己的选择是否正确并不确定。他既可能被说服赶走斯戎特工、重整锁链骑士团，也同样可能更深地与王冠紧密绑定。他考虑放弃斯戎家族的意愿，取决于 PC 们抵达他时**累计的警觉点数**。

若 PC 们持有 **10 或以下警觉点**，他会以反问句欢迎 PC 们入内，同时凝视着数英里外被放大的寇兰廷港口画面："**秩序的价值何在？**"他停顿，明显在等待回答。若某 PC 给出一个不与他作为地狱骑士的根本信条冲突的回答，他会斟酌他们的话；若玩家不知如何应答以取悦他，可由 PC 通过成功的 DC 20 地狱学识、地狱骑士学识或信仰检定编出合适的回应。否则，他发起攻击。

A14. CITADEL TRAIL
Despite being referred to as a trail, this strip of land is more a barren shelf. The Hellknights use it for both rewarding and punishing prisoners. It offers a rare glimpse of the outdoors, which serves to be a powerful bribe for those expecting to die without seeing the sun again. However, prisoners are occasionally bound and left exposed on its cliff as a reminder that the Hellknights will end lives without hesitation. Anchoring points are installed into the outer walls that are sturdy enough to hold chains.

Patrol: Guards make frequent excuses to visit this trail. As they're used to seeing prisoners out here, the DCs for Deception and Diplomacy checks for the Secure Prisoner opportunity (page 34) decrease by 2 in this area.

A15. THE PRISONER'S STABLES	LOW 10
The back edge of this windowless outbuilding juts into open air as if threatening to careen into the sea below. An arching ceiling connects the building and fortress, sheltering the structure's large, sliding door.

The Hellknights refer to this building as the stables, but it's more often used to hold prisoners. Before the Master of Blades was removed from his post, he kept the central stall for the steed he rode from his cabin 8 miles northeast. The comfort of the other occupants wasn't important when creating this arrangement.

Prisoners: The minotaur Scorchpalm, an alchemist who works for the Crowncrushers, occupies the central stall. She is Large, so due to her size, it's particularly difficult to hide her while sneaking through the citadel. It takes 20 minutes for the Secure Prisoner opportunity (page 34) when hiding her.

Creatures: Two gosregs are detained in the outer stalls, where they maintain disguises as captured human Andoren soldiers. The Dominion of the Black sent them into Andoran to infiltrate the Eagle Knights. Poor luck resulted in their capture. The two pretend to be a pair of soldiers named Ingrid and Dalin (Deception +24) and beg to be released. If the PCs see through their ruse or refuse to release them, the gosregs attack. If rescued, the gosregs maintain their disguises and find a new organization to infiltrate.

A16. LICTOR'S WALK	SEVERE 10
Stairs wind around the exterior of Citadel Gheradesca's spire. Only an iron railing protects from the high-altitude winds and the drop to the structure below.

Instead of stone walls, windows with glass thick enough to refract the view form this solitary room's edges. Inside, the shape of the glass magnifies the surrounding landscape. To the south, Corentyn's harbor comes into view, smoky and chaotic with battle.

The Lictor's Walk is the traditional office of the leader of the Hellknight Order in Citadel Gheradesca, where they can watch over the surrounding tidal flats and the Corentyn harbor. The spire rises 100 feet over the ground floor, and a total of 500 feet over the seabed. A telescope in the southeast corner allows for more magnification, in addition to that provided by the crafted glass walls.

Creatures: Lictor Uro Adom watches the battle in Corentyn harbor and considers the unfolding situation. When Abrogail Thrune II commanded the Hellknights to pledge their loyalty to Cheliax above all, he agreed. With every passing day, he privately doubts that decision.

The rule of law is meant to instill order and discipline. Uro believes the soft comforts of civilization erode that, threatening the very institutions that allow for those comforts. Best to keep the leash short and in the right hands. Abrogail promised that the right hands were hers.

With her plans still incomplete, Uro is unsure if he made the right decision. He can be persuaded to turn out Thrune's agents and reform the Order of the Chain, but he can just as easily entrench himself with the crown. His willingness to consider abandoning House Thrune depends on the PCs' Awareness Points when they reach him.

If the PCs have 10 or fewer Awareness Points, he welcomes the PCs to his office with a rhetorical question while peering at the magnified image of Corentyn's harbor miles away: "What is the value of order?" He pauses, clearly waiting for an answer. If a PC provides an answer that doesn't conflict with his fundamental beliefs as a Hellknight, he considers their words; if the players don't know how to answer in a way that appeases him, a PC can concoct a suitable response with a successful DC 20 Hell Lore, Hellknight Lore, or Religion check. Otherwise, he attacks.
