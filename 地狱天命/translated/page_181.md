### 海莉雅·贝伦内的遗产（The Legacy of Helia Belunne）

贝伦内家族在切利亚斯的政坛上从未显赫——这很可能与他们的信仰有关。他们坚定地忠于艾欧曼狄——尊崇她作为亚罗登的承嗣者；他们认为切利亚斯——这个曾经的亚罗登信仰之国——本应在**末代亚兹兰提**陨落之后随她而去。他们拒绝崇拜阿斯摩蒂斯以谋取政治恩荫——但他们的反对声音又不足以让他们成为王室的敌人。

年轻的**海莉雅·贝伦内**——在十多年前——被恩赐为艾欧曼狄的勇者——其后不久——她继承了子男爵夫人的爵位。尽管她未曾参与失败的「**辉煌光复**」起事——但她的信仰使她持续受到斯戎家族的监视。事实上——尽管海莉雅希望那场由艾欧曼狄骑士所领导的辉煌光复能够成功——但她并未直接援助他们。海莉雅有她自己的计划。

海莉雅开始了一项漫长而艰难的工程——在她家族宅邸地下隐藏的地窖中——制作一支**神圣构装体**大军。艾欧曼狄的魔法蒙福每一具被赋予活体的石像——让它们准备就绪——一旦斯戎家族显出足以被推翻的虚弱迹象——便从内部攻击埃戈里安。如此大胆的进攻——需要一套万无一失的方案——以推翻斯戎家族——并涤净所有阿斯摩蒂斯信仰的痕迹。海莉雅未能取得足够的盟友——而当**戈鲁姆**（Gorum）陨落之时——她离开此城——前去寻找他神性的残痕——以期为她的构装体大军重新祝圣。然而——她至今未归——其下落不明。

数周无薪之后——贝伦内家的仆从纷纷离去——这座宅邸便无人居住。自与安多兰开战以来——贝伦内家的命运便不再是优先事项——故而斯戎家族尚未没收她的财产。因此——贝伦内宅邸已空置数月——海莉雅的造物伫立其中——无人发现、无人启用——至少——在玩家角色到来之前是如此。

The Legacy of Helia Belunne

The Belunne family never achieved prominence in
Chelaxian politics, likely due to their faith. They were
staunchly loyal to Iomedae in her capacity as Aroden's
inheritor, believing that Cheliax, once a nation of
Arodenites, should've followed her after the Last
Azlanti's death. They refused to worship Asmodeus
to achieve political favor, but weren't vocal enough in
their opposition to become enemies of the crown.

A young Helia Belunne was blessed as a champion
of Iomedae over a decade ago, shortly before inheriting
her title of demibaroness. Though not involved
in the failed insurrection known as the Glorious
Reclamation, her faith earned her ongoing surveillance
by House Thrune. In truth, while Helia hoped the
Glorious Reclamation would succeed—led as it was
by Iomedaean knights—she didn't directly aid them.
Helia had her own plans.

Helia began the long and difficult process of crafting
an army of divine constructs in a basement hidden
beneath her family manor. Iomedae's magic blessed
each of the animated stone statues, preparing them to
attack Egorian from within in case Thrune ever seemed
weak enough to overthrow. Such an audacious attack
would require a foolproof plan to oust the Thrunes
and drive out all traces of Asmodean worship. Helia
couldn't secure enough allies, and when Gorum died,
she left the city to find traces of his divinity that she
could reconsecrate for her construct army. However,
she has yet to return, and her fate is unknown.

After weeks without pay, Belunne servants left,
and the building went unoccupied. The Belunnes'
fate hasn't been a priority since the onset of war with
Andoran, so House Thrune hasn't seized her property.
Consequently, Belunne Manor has been vacant for
months, and Helia's creations stand undiscovered and
unused—at least until the PCs come along.
