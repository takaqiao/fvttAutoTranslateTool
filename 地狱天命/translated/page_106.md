### 闪光岩抉择（GLITTERCRAG RESOLUTION）

**梅兰迪亚**身份暴露并被击败后，玩家角色们与枭鬼们可以总结现状。如果玛拉拉熬过了这场劫难，部族首领便会感谢英雄们识破自己被附身。她记得透过自己的双眼，看着恶魔召唤刺客袭击拉沃努尔与佩札克的领袖们。**梅兰迪亚**原本希望挑起一场战争，借机最终掌控**魔鬼栖**所有枭鬼部族，将他们统一于自己治下，自立一国。

如果玛拉拉死了，留下来的最年长枭鬼**德嘉塔**（Dgata）会满怀哀伤，但仍感激玩家角色们查明了首领其实是被恶魔附身。无论现在由哪位枭鬼领导闪光岩部族，他们对玩家角色们的开放程度都取决于玩家角色们杀了多少枭鬼。如果玩家角色们杀了 6 名或更少，部族愿意接受玩家角色们的邀请，与佩札克联手对抗切利亚斯与斯戎家族。他们承诺会在接下来几天内把口信传达给**魔鬼栖**的其他部族。如果玛拉拉熬过来，她会透露：恶魔把她的珍爱武器扔到了 E7 区域的垃圾坑里（并让她的一名族人取回作为送给玩家角色们的礼物），她也保有梅兰迪亚关于通往矮人武库（E13 区域）暗门的全部知识。她会很乐意把武库里的全部物资赠予他们。

如果玩家角色们杀了 7 名或更多枭鬼，部族需要再三确认才愿信任他们并与佩札克协作。一名玩家角色可通过一次交涉检定**留下良好印象**来争取这份信任，DC 等于 25 加上他们所杀的枭鬼数目。玩家角色们最多可尝试 3 次此检定，部族此后便不愿再听。玩家角色们随后有时间按自己意愿往返佩札克与拉沃努尔，并安排所有新盟友会面的高峰会。

**奖励**：如果玩家角色们说服枭鬼与佩札克合作，他们获得 60 XP。

### 章节结语（Concluding the Chapter）

在挫败威胁拉沃努尔与佩札克的阴谋之后，玩家角色们的副官们安排了一场在**安多兰**召开的战争高峰会，盟友们从**卡利**、**寇兰廷**、**拉沃努尔**与**佩札克**前来汇合，新结识的水栖精灵与枭鬼诸部族也加入其中。为表彰功绩，玩家角色们在宴会上被尊为英雄（这次刺杀未遂的次数为零），他们的盟友们正式建立了对抗切利亚斯的「**西方同盟**」！

正式宣战后不久，一位地狱骑士信使到访——**忒尔齐纳**（Telzinah；心碎的男性卓玛尔战斗法师 6 级），来自**锁链骑士团**（Order of the Chain）。在那场令他骑士团一分为二的灾难性声明之后，忒尔齐纳加入了**破狱者**（Hellbreakers），如今他来带给玩家角色们一个噩耗——西线方面英雄们的努力固然成效显著，但切利亚斯决定无视他们，相信山脉天险足以拖慢他们的进度。尽管失去了寇兰廷，斯戎家族仍把这一切视为暂时的不便。切利亚斯的战争机器在东方加大了对**安多兰**国的进攻力度，而安多兰现在急需玩家角色们的援助！

### GLITTERCRAG RESOLUTION

Once Melandiar has been exposed and defeated, the PCs and strix can take stock of their situation. If Marala survived her ordeal, the tribe's leader thanks the heroes for figuring out that she was possessed. She remembers watching through her own eyes as the devil summoned the assassins who attacked the leaders of both Ravounel and Pezzack. Melandiar had hoped to create a war that she could capitalize on by eventually taking control of all the Devil's Perch strix tribes and uniting them under her rule, claiming a kingdom of her own.

If Marala dies, the senior-most strix left alive, Dgata, is mournful but grateful that the PCs discovered their leader was being possessed by a devil. Regardless of which strix now leads the Glittercrag tribe, their openness to the PCs depends on how many of the strix the PCs killed. If the PCs killed six or fewer, the tribe is receptive to the PCs' invitation to collaborate with Pezzack against Cheliax and House Thrune. They promise to pass word along to the other tribes in Devil's Perch over the next few days. If Marala survived, she reveals that the devil threw her prized weapon into the refuse pit in area E7 (and has one of her people retrieve it as a gift to the PCs), and she also retains Melandiar's knowledge of the secret doors that lead into the dwarven armory (area E13). She happily gifts them its contents.

If the PCs killed seven or more strix, the tribe requires reassurance before trusting them and working with Pezzack. A PC can secure this trust by succeeding at a Diplomacy check to Make an Impression with a DC equal to 25 plus the number of strix they killed. The PCs can attempt this check three times before the tribe refuses to listen further. The PCs then have time to travel back to Pezzack and Ravounel as they wish and arrange a summit for all of their new allies to meet.

**Reward**: The PCs receive 60 XP if they convince the strix to work with Pezzack.

### Concluding the Chapter

After unraveling the plots threatening Ravounel and Pezzack, the PCs' adjutants arrange for a war summit held in Andoran, with allies joining them from Khari, Corentyn, Ravounel, and Pezzack, alongside new friends from the aquatic elves and strix tribes. For their service, the PCs are feted as heroes at a banquet (this time with precisely zero assassination attempts), and their allies formally establish the Western Alliance against Cheliax!

Not long after the formal declaration of war, a Hellknight signifier arrives—Telzinah (heartbroken male dromaar battle mage 6) from the Order of the Chain. Telzinah joined up with the Hellbreakers following the disastrous declarations that splintered his order in half, and now he comes to bring the PCs dire news—while the heroes' efforts here in the west have clearly been successful, Cheliax has decided to ignore them, trusting in the mountainous natural defenses to slow their progress. Despite losing Corentyn, House Thrune sees this all as a temporary inconvenience. The Chelaxian war machine has ramped up its efforts in the east against the nation of Andoran, and Andoran now needs the PCs' help!
