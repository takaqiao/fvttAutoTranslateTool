### C. 深层监狱（The Deep Prison）

深层监狱**嵌入岛屿的基岩之中**，关押着堡垒中**最危险的居民**——至少**按乌罗的标准如此**。它位于**毁损的西翼另一侧**，沿一条螺旋通道向下进入，那里**潮汐与岁月正缓缓侵蚀着监狱的结构**。**弃誓者**（Foresworn）——一位曾被**锁链骑士团**囚禁、又被**镣铐骑士团**释放的**死墓骑士**（graveknight）——担任这些囚犯的**看守**。

由于此区域**位于魔鬼与不死出没的西翼之后**，囚犯与 PC 们**可以在此地休息**而不必担心被地狱骑士发现。PC 们能透过**塌陷的墙壁**看到**潮水随潮汐变化**，并在渗透**时限耗尽**时**经此墙逃往潮汐滩**。若 PC 们在**渗透结束前**就离开此区域，**他们仍须为深层监狱中的囚犯通过"安置囚犯"机遇检定**（第 34 页）——因为他们必须**与此处的囚犯协调逃脱计划**。本区域地图在**第 42 页**。

C. The Deep Prison
Built into the island's bedrock, the deep prison holds the citadel's most dangerous inhabitants—at least, the most dangerous by Uro's standards. It's on the far side of the ruined west wing, down a spiraling passage and into a place where tide and time slowly chip away at the prison's structure. The Foresworn, a graveknight once held by the Order of the Chain and released by the Order of the Manacle, serves as the keeper for these prisoners.

As this area is past the devil- and undead-infested west wing, prisoners and the PCs can rest here without risking discovery by the Hellknights. The PCs can watch the water level change with the tides through the collapsed wall, and they can escape through it to the tidal flats when the infiltration runs out of time. If the PCs leave this area prior to the end of the infiltration, they must still succeed at the Secure Prisoner opportunity (page 34) since they must coordinate an escape plan with the prisoners here. The map for this area is on page 42.
