男性人类间谍

**察觉** +21
**语言** 通用语、精怪语、半身人语、奥斯里昂语
**技能** 运动 +18，钟爱之城学识 +25，工艺 +18，唬骗 +22（在其钟爱之城内 +26），表演 +18，潜行 +24（在其钟爱之城内 +28），贼活 +20

**力 +4，敏 +1，体 +2，智 +2，感 +3，魅 +6**

**物品** 廉价啤酒瓶装（5），中级黑暗视觉酏剂，+1 强击重击拳套，中级治疗药剂（2），中级雾形态酏剂，弹弓（20 颗弹丸），5 颗冰冻弹弓弹丸（《玩家核心 2》301）

**深度卧底专家**（Deep Cover Specialist） 在涉及间谍技艺以及卧底行动的事项上厄登·砾掌是一个——14 级挑战。

**钟爱之城**（Favored City） 厄登可以通过花费 1 周在其中探索并建立人际关系把一个至少 5,000 人的定居点确立为其钟爱之城。他可以使用其——「**钟爱之城学识**」——以——「**回忆知识**」——关于这座城市；他在其钟爱之城之内对其——「**唬骗**」与——「**潜行**」检定获得一项——+4 状况加值；他在其钟爱之城之内对其同伴施加例如——「**城邦牧者**」（city shepherd）——的能力。厄登一次只能拥有一座钟爱之城（本冒险开始之时为寇兰廷）——每当他确立一座新的钟爱之城便替换其先前的定居点。

**AC** 27；**强韧** +21，**反射** +16，**意志** +20；抗毒 +2

**HP** 165；**抗性** 毒 10

**城邦牧者**（City Shepherd） 在其钟爱之城之内所有能观察厄登的同伴可以把他们的等级 + 2——作为他们尝试——「**唬骗**」——或——「**潜行**」检定之时的熟练加值一如——「**跟随专家**」。

**搏战之饮**（Fighting Drink）　[自由动作]；**触发** 厄登掷先攻；**效果** 他喝下一份酒并作为一项自由动作开始其——「**烂醉伪装**」。他以——「**唬骗**」掷先攻。尚未行动的生物对他是——「**措手不及**」。

**烂醉伪装**（Soused Ruse）　[反应]；**触发** 厄登喝下一份酒；**效果** 他看似喝醉（与屈服于酒精的毒第 1 阶相同）——但并不遭受该状态他对——「**唬骗**」检定以——「**佯攻**」——对抗以为他醉酒的生物获得一项——+2 状况加值。一只生物可以通过以下方式识别他的伪装或者——「**察觉动机**」（Sensing Motive）——或者在——30 英尺之内观察他之时在一项对抗其——「**唬骗 DC**」——的——「**医学**」检定上成功。

**速度** 35 英尺

**近战** [1 个动作] 拳 +21（敏捷、灵巧、魔法、徒手），**伤害** 2d6+10 钝击

**远程** [1 个动作] 弹弓 +18（射程递增 50 英尺），**伤害** 2d6+8 钝击

**砸瓶**（Break Bottle）　[1 个动作]（操控）；**频率** 每回合一次；**效果** 厄登把一只瓶子砸碎并以它搏斗。在接下来的 1 分钟之内当他发起一次拳一击之时他可选择令该一击失去——「**徒手**」特征获得——「**多用 P**」特征并在重击之时造成 1d6 持续流血伤害。

**伪装饮者**（Ruse Drinker）　[1 个动作]（操控） 厄登拔出并启用一件他能饮用的消耗物品例如药剂、酏剂或含酒精的饮料。

**偷袭**（Sneak Attack） 厄登的一击对——「**措手不及**」——的生物造成 2d6 额外精准伤害。

**清醒大步**（Sobering Stride）　[1 个动作]（移动）；**所需** 厄登的——「**烂醉伪装**」——为开启状态；**效果** 厄登假装清醒过来一边更快地移动。他——「**步行**」——两次并结束其——「**烂醉伪装**」。

**烂醉一击**（Soused Blow）　[1 个动作]（修饰）；**所需** 厄登的——「**烂醉伪装**」——为开启状态；**效果** 厄登假装在准备一击之时绊倒。他——「**佯攻**」——然后发起一次近战一击。

Deep Cover Specialist For matters relating to spycraft
and undercover operations, Urdun Gravelhands is a
14th-level challenge.

Favored City Urdun can establish a settlement of at least
5,000 people as his favored city by spending 1 week
exploring and making contacts there. He can use his
Favored City Lore skill to Recall Knowledge about the
city; he gains a +4 circumstance bonus to his Deception
and Stealth checks while in his favored city; and he
applies abilities like city shepherd to his allies while in
his favored city. Urdun can have only one favored city
at a time (Corentyn, at the beginning of this adventure),
replacing his prior settlement whenever he establishes
a new favored city.

AC 27; Fort +21, Reflex +16, Will +20; +2 vs. poison
HP 165; Resistances poison 10

City Shepherd While in his favored city, all allies who
can observe Urdun can add their level + 2 as their
proficiency bonus when attempting Deception or
Stealth checks, as if Following the Expert.

Fighting Drink [free-action] Trigger Urdun rolls Initiative; Effect
He consumes a dose of alcohol and begins his Soused
Ruse as a free action. He rolls Deception for Initiative.
Creatures that haven't acted yet are off-guard to him.

Soused Ruse [reaction] Trigger Urdun consumes a dose of
alcohol; Effect He appears to become drunk (the
same as succumbing to stage 1 of alcohol's poison)
without suffering from the condition, and he gains a
+2 circumstance bonus to Deception checks to Feint
against creatures that assume he's intoxicated. A
creature can identify his ruse either by Sensing Motive
or by succeeding at a Medicine check against his
Deception DC while observing him within 30 feet.

Speed 35 feet

Melee [one-action] fist +21 (agile, finesse, magical, unarmed),
Damage 2d6+10 bludgeoning

Ranged [one-action] sling +18 (range increment 50 feet), Damage
2d6+8 bludgeoning

Break Bottle [one-action] (manipulate) Frequency once per round;
Effect Urdun shatters a bottle and uses it to brawl. For
the next 1 minute when he makes a fist Strike, he can
choose for the Strike to lose the unarmed trait, gain the
versatile P trait, and deal 1d6 persistent bleed damage
on critical hits.

Ruse Drinker [one-action] (manipulate) Urdun Draws and Activates
a consumable item he can drink, such as a potion, elixir,
or alcoholic drink.

Sneak Attack Urdun's Strikes deal an additional 2d6
precision damage against off-guard creatures.

Sobering Stride [one-action] (move) Requirements Urdun's Soused
Ruse is active; Effect Urdun pretends to sober up as
he moves more quickly. He Strides twice and ends his
Soused Ruse.

Soused Blow [one-action] (flourish) Requirements Urdun's Soused
Ruse is active; Effect Urdun pretends to stumble while
preparing a punch. He Feints then makes a melee Strike.
