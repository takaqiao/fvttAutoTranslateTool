### 将军帐（GENERAL'S QUARTERS）

抵达安多兰军营时，玩家角色们被维持周界岗哨的卫兵盘问，但**忒尔齐纳**说出正确的应答口令，让他和玩家角色们得以入营。一进入营区，**忒尔齐纳**便引他们前往位于这一众支起帐篷正中央的**指挥帐**。

帐内，参与本次军事行动的各部主官——从上尉直至将军——皆已齐聚。多名**执政官**（Consul）也在场，他们是**安多兰人民议会**派给**至尊议长**（Supreme Elect）的军政顾问，代表文官政府监督军务。**忒尔齐纳**简要地向这些列席军官介绍英雄们，述及他们在寇兰廷的功绩，以及最近在西线的战果（若这些玩家角色曾参与**破狱者**冒险路线，则也包括在依斯嘉的战绩）。这位信使明确表态：作为**破狱者**联盟在本议会上的代表，他所在的组织对玩家角色们怀有十足信任，希望他们在即将到来的冲突中担当远比跑腿活更重要的角色。

集会现场的反应即便不算冷淡，也称不上热络。一名玩家角色若通过 DC 34 察觉检定**察觉动机**，便能注意到：尉级与少校级的多数中级军官明显乐见玩家角色们的到来，欢迎这股额外的助力；但更高层却心存疑虑，把他们视作冲着钱财与荣耀而来、靠不住的雇佣兵，并非真心投身大义。

### General's Quarters

Upon their arrival at the Andoren military camp, the PCs are challenged by guards maintaining a picket line around the area, but Telzinah gives the proper greetings, allowing him and the PCs access. Once inside, Telzinah directs them to the command tent, located in the center of the many pavilions that have been erected here.

Inside the tent, the commanding officers of each unit are taking part in the military operation, from captains all the way up to generals. Several Consuls—military advisors for the Andoren People's Council to its Supreme Elect—are also present, providing oversight from the civilian government. Telzinah briefly introduces the heroes to the assembled officers, recounting their successes in Corentyn and more recently in the west (as well as Isger, if these PCs took part in the Hellbreakers Adventure Path). The signifer makes it clear that, as he represents the Hellbreakers league at this council, his organization trusts the PCs implicitly and wants them to play more than a menial role in the upcoming conflict.

Reactions throughout the gathering are cool if not outright cold. A PC who succeeds at a DC 34 Perception check to Sense Motive notices that most of the junior officers of captain and major ranks are clearly happy to see the PCs and welcome the extra help, but the leadership is doubtful, seeing them as untrustworthy mercenaries in it for coin and glory, not the cause.

To put these negative thoughts at ease, the PCs can engage with the most popular representatives of those who have doubts about the characters' abilities and intentions.
