## Page 28 — Ch1 C 奥罗登拱桥地下复合体 C1-C8

斯戎家族的恶魔教士将这片区域（地图见第 20 页）凿入石拱之中。他们并非专业工程师，不过他们刻意避开了中央支撑柱。目前，只有**图里尼奥·梅扎福**（Tourinio Metzapho，令人不快的男性精灵恶魔教士，10 级）被派驻看守地牢最低层的囚犯。

### C1. 假入口（False Entrance）

进入拱桥的隧道与桥面平行延伸。已开凿出约 250 英尺隧道，末端还堆放着额外设备。然而 PC 通过成功的 DC 25 生存检定会注意到，这些设备已数周未动。PC 可通过成功的 DC 27 察觉检定发现通往下层的暗门陷阱。

### C2. 折返坡道（Switchback）

> 一排黄色瓷砖勾勒出隧道的最高点。坡道急速下降，凿入石内的折返坡用以减缓行进速度。

那些黄色瓷砖曾标记着一道**警报术**（alarm）法术区域（匿踪 DC 29；5 环级），但下方的恶魔教士发现访客们说密语过慢。警报术已转至地图上标记的区域。一旦触发，它会通知图里尼奥。

### C3. 竖直通道（Vertical Passage）

在拱桥地牢各层之间穿行需通过 10 英尺宽的开口下降 15 英尺。一台移动平台便于上升。升降机制是一组装入远端墙壁的滑轮系统。以**互动**动作启动滑轮可使平台上升或下降 5 英尺。开口近侧的梯子供中型生物**攀爬**下方（DC 5）。

上层开口环绕着一道 2 英尺宽的窄沿，角色可通过成功的 DC 25 体技检定横越。

### C4. 中央走廊（Central Hallway）

> 内部走廊墙面打磨得完全光滑，是建造此层时使用的**塑石术**留下的痕迹。走廊中无环境光，使本区域（以及 C5 至 C7）一片漆黑。

### C5. 储藏室（Storage Room）

> 这间储物间里有一个便壶和两套铺盖卷。

它们用于恶魔教士不得不在此过夜的时候，不过自六个月前**萨格拉贡（sarglagon）契约**敲定以来，凡间工作人员就大幅减少。PC 检视这些用品并通过成功的 DC 17 生存或 DC 19 察觉检定，可推断它们已数月未使用。重击成功时，PC 可看出这些用品曾被密集使用——以致需要定期修补——但自上次摆放至现位置以来已过了一段时间。

### C6. 准备室（Preparation Room）

虽然有厨房的炉灶、洗涤池与台面，本室主要充作**仪式准备室**。墙上一道滑门掩着通往下层的垃圾滑槽。小型生物可**挤入**（Squeeze）其中，但角色必须通过成功的 DC 20 运动检定**攀爬**其 15 英尺长度。

### C7. 契约室（Contract Room）　**严峻 10**

> 红色颜料在石面上绘出由两个圆环围合的十字。靠边一把椅子旁的地上散落着各式卷轴。

这些卷轴是地狱与切利亚斯代表之间的契约草案。PC 若懂魔鬼语并通过 DC 24 法务学识检定查阅这些契约，便会发现切利亚斯近来扩大了使用魔鬼监视异见者的范围（包括泽菲罗）。契约引用了近期的绑架行动，向斯戎家族指出这些服务可"**作为对付阿扎克缇（azarketis）的筹码**"。

**生物**：图里尼奥·梅扎福在此复合体担任首席恶魔教士。他管理与服务此地牢的魔鬼们订立的契约，正处于一场为期数日的谈判之中。图里尼奥与他正在谈判的**契约魔**（phistophilus）都把入侵者视为共同的敌人。

若图里尼奥察觉到 PC 们（例如通过被触发的警报术），他会通知魔鬼，并移动至中央走廊迎击 PC 们。

### C8. 竖直通道（Vertical Passage）

> 这层与下一层之间的通道光秃秃的。它向下落 15 英尺通往下方的监狱。

<details><summary>English Source</summary>

Thrune diabolists have carved this area (see the map on page 20) into the stone arch. They aren't expert engineers, though they did purposefully avoid the central supporting pillar. Currently, only Tourinio Metzapho (unpleasant male elf diabolist 10) is assigned to watch over the prisoners in the dungeon's lowest floor.

C1. FALSE ENTRANCE
The tunnel into the arch runs parallel to its surface. About 250 feet of tunnels have been excavated already, with additional equipment stowed at the end of the tunnel. However, a PC who succeeds at a DC 25 Survival check notices that the equipment hasn't moved in weeks. A PC can find the secret trap door that leads to the lower levels with a successful DC 27 Perception check.

C2. SWITCHBACK
Yellow tiles outline the highest point in the tunnel. The slope dips quickly, with switchbacks carved through the stone to help slow movement.

The yellow tiles once marked an alarm spell's area (Stealth DC 29; 5th rank), though the diabolists below discovered that visitors were slow to say the passphrase. The alarm spell has moved down the hallway to the marked area on the map. If triggered, it alerts Tourinio.

C3. VERTICAL PASSAGE
Travel between the levels of the arch dungeon requires moving through 10-foot-wide openings that drop 15 feet between levels. A moving platform facilitates the climb back up. The mechanism to raise and lower it is a pulley built into the far wall. Activating the pulley with an Interact action raises or lowers the platform 5 feet. A ladder on the near side of the opening provides a way for a Medium creature to Climb down (DC 5).

A 2-foot-wide ledge surrounds the upper opening, which a character can traverse with a successful DC 25 Acrobatics check.

C4. CENTRAL HALLWAY
The interior hallway walls are perfectly smooth, a remnant of the shape stone that was used in the creation of this level. No ambient light reaches these halls, leaving this area (and areas C5 to C7) dark.

C5. STORAGE ROOM
A chamber pot and two sets of bedrolls are stored in this closet. They're used when the diabolists are required to spend their nights in the complex, though there have been far fewer mortal staff since the sarglagon contracts were finalized six months ago. A PC examining the supplies and who succeeds at a DC 17 Survival or DC 19 Perception check deduces that they haven't been used in months. On a critical success, the PC can tell these supplies were once heavily used to the point of needing regular repair, though some time has passed since they last moved from their current position.

C6. PREPARATION ROOM
While it has a kitchen's stove, basin, and countertop, this room mostly functions as a ritual preparation room. A sliding door in the wall conceals a trash chute that leads to the layer below. A Small creature can Squeeze into it, though a character must succeed at a DC 20 Athletics check to Climb its 15-foot length.

C7. CONTRACT ROOM	MODERATE 10
A cross surrounded by two circles is drawn in red on the stone. Scrolls litter the floor near a chair to the side.

The scrolls here are drafts of contracts between Hell and Chelaxian representatives. If a PC who understands Diabolic reviews these contracts and succeeds at a DC 24 Legal Lore check, they find that Cheliax had recently expanded its use of devils for monitoring dissidents, including Zefiro. The contracts reference recent abductions, pointing out to House Thrune that these services could be used as "leverage against the azarketis."

Creatures: Tourinio Metzapho serves as the lead diabolist for this complex. He manages the contracts with the devils who serve this dungeon and is in the middle of such a negotiation that has lasted for several days. Both Tourinio and the phistophilus he's negotiating with see the intruders as a shared enemy.

If Tourinio becomes aware of the PCs (such as through a triggered alarm spell), he informs the devil and moves to meet the PCs in the central hallway.
