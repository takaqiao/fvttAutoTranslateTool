### 安全屋与冒险计划（Safe House, Risky Plans）

琳奈塔的安全屋并不像通俗小说中描绘的暮光之爪安全屋那般光鲜。显眼的门锁、缺少窗户（此区仓库典型布局）令多塔里不会对这座建筑产生兴趣。其平凡外观与有规律的货物轮换提供的掩护，胜过任何夹墙暗道或暗语口令。琳奈塔在仓库中央举行机密会议——以板条箱围合成一间房，提供额外的隔音效果。板条箱之间的缝隙提供了三条不同的出口和守望线。

PC 们抵达时，中央房间因人体散热而显得闷热。出席者包括：作为铃花会代表的**无声剪影**；碎冠者派的双胞胎——**凯旋**（Triumph，冲动的男性人类伪暮光之爪，8 级）和**艾里尔**（Eryr，性情乖戾的女性人类新晋叛逆者，8 级）；细缕者的**佩瑟**（Pesze，机敏的男性人类演员，8 级）；以及地狱骑士**卡蒂利娅·穆托尔**。各派代表互相警惕地观望，直到琳奈塔开口。

> "欢迎来到寇兰廷的革命，"琳奈塔向聚集的人群优雅一挥手。她的手臂大开大合，而代表们则尽板条箱所允许地彼此远远站开。"——或者说，至少是革命的开始。博物馆那一出风波意味着我们都将上观察名单。但我们已经点燃了革命的火花！要让它发展壮大，我们就得齐心协力，而且得**够聪明**。"
>
> 角落里一对戴着红色头巾的少年低声呻吟。皱眉很快蔓延开来——首个皱眉来自一位用蓝色布制面罩遮蔽身份的半身人。地狱骑士后退一步，差一点彻底离开聚会。

Safe House, Risky Plans
Linetta's safe house doesn't match the glamour of Twilight Talon safe houses as depicted in popular novels. Visible locks and a lack of windows (typical of a warehouse in this district) keep the dottari from becoming interested in the structure. Its mundane appearance and regular rotation of goods provides a better cover than any series of false walls and code phrases ever could. Linetta holds secure meetings in the center of the warehouse, using crates to enclose a room and provide additional soundproofing. Gaps in the crates provide three different exits and sight lines to guard stations.

When the PCs arrive, the central room is hot from the crowd of bodies. In attendance are Silent Silhouette as a representative of the Bellflower Network, the twins Triumph (impulsive male human false Twilight Talon 8) and Eryr (surly female human upstart 8) of the Crowncrushers, Pesze (clever male human actor 8) of the Thin Wisps, and the Hellknight Cathilia Mutol. The representatives of each faction watch the others warily before Linetta speaks.

"Welcome to Corentyn's revolution," Linetta says with a flourish to the cluster of people gathered. Her arm swings wide as the representatives stand as far apart as the crates allow. "Or at least, the start of it. That display at the museum means we'll be on a watch list. But we've lit the spark of revolution! To help it grow, we'll need to work together, and we'll need to work smart."

A groan erupts from the corner with a pair of teenagers in red bandannas. Scowls quickly follow, with the first coming from a halfling whose blue cloth mask disguises their identity. The Hellknight takes a step backward, nearly leaving the gathering entirely.
