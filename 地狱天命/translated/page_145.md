## Page 145 — 第八章：裁缝与叛徒

**安娜贝丝·格林辛海军上将（Admiral Anabeth Grinceen，直率女性人类海军上将 10 级）**——身为辛吉外部威胁的守护者，她统辖**戈索克莱克堡（Fort Gorthoklek）**驻防的海军部队。**在她的眼皮底下要建立一座传送法阵几乎不可能**。但对玩家角色而言走运的是：**她对皇室的忠心在「地狱火危机」期间已然动摇**。

伊维奇大公市长长期以来令格林辛上将厌烦。**他多年来动摇着辛吉的稳定，且对她公然不敬，但她没有他犯罪的确凿证据便不能动手**。**若他下台，她便有望接管这座城市**——剩下的只是收集证据而已。**当玩家角色来到辛吉时，她发现了一个毁掉塔雷斯的机会**。

**玩家角色在辛吉公开露面一小时后**（这段时间足够安娜贝丝的眼线在他们"收集信息"的过程中获悉其到来），**她将他们请至自己的办公室**。邀请由一名海军信使送达，**信封上盖着安娜贝丝的私人印章**，字面写着"**欢迎如此知名的访客莅临辛吉**"。如果被威胁，**信使会把信扔下逃走**；否则，她会强忍着客气把消息交出，随即快步离开。

### 会见海军上将（MEETING WITH THE ADMIRAL）

**戈索克莱克堡**俯瞰港口与海岸，**安娜贝丝·格林辛上将办公室的窗户正面临海**。从访客座椅望去，**她办公桌上的视角恰好框出她权力的象征徽记**。**安娜贝丝便沐浴在这种气场中迎接来客**。她递上酒——一款**切利亚斯红酒**，**葡萄园距此不到一里格**——同时**专注地打量他们**。等众人坐定，她**抛出几个问题试探水深**，譬如**「是什么风把你们吹到辛吉来了？」**与**「你们了解辛吉的政治情形吗？」**。一旦她确信玩家角色不是斯戎家族的精明特工，便会说："**让我们打开天窗说亮话。你们告诉我你们想要什么，我也告诉你们我想要什么。我心里已有一个价码，我想会相当划算**。"

如果玩家角色表明**想在城内建立一座传送法阵**（或类似的、要她不在场以遂其愿的请求），**她会表示这是一个很大的要求，但她心里也有一项任务可换**——**她要伊维奇大公市长贪赃枉法的证据，借此把他赶下台**。**更进一步，她要他彻底蒙羞**。**让他失去贵族身份就是上等**——而在她最美好的梦想中，**他将成为全国上下的笑柄**。她**需要外人来办这件事**，**以便保住自己的名声**。**作为回报，她愿意考虑让一座传送法阵在城内保持激活**——**只要他们先完成她这一头的交易**。

如果玩家角色不确定从何处开始调查，她会**给出一条线索：塔雷斯本人**。**塔雷斯的私人办公室很可能存有不光彩的私密信息，而他下属办公室里有财务记录**。

### 羞辱点（MORTIFICATION POINTS）

虽然把塔雷斯赶下台所需的**证据门槛相当低**，**安娜贝丝更希望他被彻底羞辱**。**玩家角色将在辛吉期间累积「羞辱点」（MP）**。**应记录这些机会中所得的 MP**，因为它们将决定**玩家角色离开辛吉后塔雷斯名声的去向**（见第 149 页「**辛吉余波**」）。

**奖励**：每获得 1 MP，**奖励玩家角色 20 XP**。

# A. 伊维奇大宅（IVICI MANSION）

大公市长的宅邸**坐落在城东**的一处庄园中。**它的大门与一片清空的土地将其与辛吉的城区隔开**。**从外面看，庄园显得十分安静**。**对仆人而言，那是个混乱与恐惧之地**。**塔雷斯生活在极度偏执之中——他在自家宅邸布置陷阱，却不告诉仆人**。**他要求他们各司其位，所以任何伤害都被他归咎为暗中作梗**。**他与仆人打交道时，对他们破口大骂，还任由伊维诺以暴力相威胁**。**这群心灰意冷、饱受创伤的仆人，已经不再热衷于阻挡闯入者**——**反而会把任何"客人"引向图书馆翼**。

Admiral Anabeth Grinceen (direct female human admiral 10) protects Hinji from external threats, as she oversees the naval forces out of Fort Gorthoklek. Establishing a teleportation circle under her nose would be near impossible. Fortunately for the PCs, her loyalty to the crown has wavered during the Hellfire Crisis.

Long has Lord-Mayor Ivici irritated Admiral Grinceen. He undermined the stability of Hinji for years and treated her with open disrespect, but she can't act without concrete proof of his actions. If he's removed from power, she stands to take over the city. It's just a matter of collecting evidence. As the PCs come to Hinji, she spots an opportunity to ruin Tareth.

An hour after the PCs have appeared in public in Hinji (which provides time for Anabeth's watchers to learn about the PCs' arrival as they Gather Information), she summons them to her office. The invitation arrives via a naval courier bearing a letter sealed with Anabeth's personal sigil that offers a "Welcome to Hinji for such notable visitors." If met with threats, the courier drops the letter and runs. Otherwise, she hands the message over with forced politeness before quickly leaving.

MEETING WITH THE ADMIRAL
Fort Gorthoklek watches over the port and coast with Admiral Anabeth Grinceen's office window overlooking the water. From the visitor's chairs, the view frames her desk with the symbol of her power. Anabeth basks in this image as she greets her visitors. She offers them wine—a fine Chelaxian red from a vineyard not even a league away—while she studies them intently. Once everyone is settled, she asks the PCs a few questions to test the waters, such as, "What brings you to Hinji?" and "Are you aware of the political circumstances in Hinji?" Once she confirms that the PCs aren't sophisticated Thrune agents, she says, "Let's speak plainly. Tell me what you want, and I'll tell you what I want. I have a price in mind already, which I think would be quite affordable."

If the PCs indicate that they want to create a teleportation circle in the city (or a similar request that would have her be absent enough for that to happen), she indicates that's a lofty request, but she has a task in mind. She wants evidence of Lord-Mayor Ivici's malfeasance so he can be ousted. More than that, she wants him humiliated. Driving him from nobility would be nice, and in her greatest dreams, he would become the laughingstock of the country. She needs outsiders to do that work, so she can keep her reputation intact. She's willing to entertain keeping a teleportation circle active in the city in exchange, if they complete their end of the deal first.

If the PCs aren't sure where to start their investigation, she offers a lead: Tareth. Tareth's personal office is likely to hold unsavory personal information, while his staff's office contains financial records.

MORTIFICATION POINTS
While the standard of evidence for removing Tareth from power is fairly low, Anabeth would prefer that he be utterly humiliated. The PCs collect Mortification Points (MP) while in Hinji. Track the MP earned during these opportunities, as they determine the status of Tareth's reputation after they leave Hinji (see Hinji Aftermath on page 149).
Reward: For each MP earned, award the PCs 20 XP.

A. Ivici Mansion
The lord-mayor's mansion occupies an estate on the eastern side of the city. Its gates and a span of cleared land separate it from the city blocks of Hinji. From the outside, the estate seems quiet. For the staff, it's a place of chaos and fear. Tareth lives with intense paranoia, so he traps his mansion without informing them. He expects them to keep their places, so any injuries are due to subterfuge. When he does interact with the staff, he berates them and allows Ivirno to threaten violence. The jaded and traumatized staff no longer care to turn away intruders. Instead, they lead any "guests" to the library wing.

Tareth brags that he's protected against any threat, but his resources are more limited than his boasts. He has Ivirno cast a 6th-rank detect scrying each morning, but that protection only alerts Ivirno to scrying effects within its 30-foot emanation.
