65). Over the course of the battle, a number of events are possible as outlined below.

Event 1 (Arrival): When the PCs first arrive, they see Oourax emerge from the red-tinged doorway in the northeast quadrant of the map, completing her 13th journey through the doorways. She now only has one doorway remaining before she'll be able to open the ziggurat portal. During battle, she does her best to move to the southwest corner of the map to the final doorway.

Event 2 (The Serpent): On the third round of combat (or at another dramatic moment you feel is appropriate) on initiative count 20, a stygian serpent bursts through the gate at the top of the ziggurat to aid Oourax, crashing down to attack the PCs.

Event 3 (Death of Oourax): When Oourax is defeated, her two fiendish patrons each send spiritual emissaries to collect her soul. This creates the soul-ripper haunt.

Event 4 (The Final Doorway): If Oourax manages to reach the final doorway in the southwest quadrant, she uses an action to unlock the doorway and then Strides through. She emerges from the doorway at the end of her turn 1 round later, and the portal atop the ziggurat fully opens. A torrent of water from the River Styx floods outward from the ziggurat in all directions, advancing by 20 feet per round until it eventually engulfs the entire city and spills into the Hellmouth Gulf. If this dire event occurs, Oourax uses the waters of the river to brainwash the many denizens of the gulf and force them into her service, creating a vast army.

Hazards: The two red-tinged doorways as well as the soul-ripper haunt triggered by Oourax's death (see Event 3, above) are hazards for this area.
