### B. 御风者灯塔（Lighthouse of the Aeromancer）

这座**摇摇欲坠的灯塔**曾是**一位隐居巫师"御风者"加勒鲁斯**（Galerous the Aeromancer）**的堡垒**。**通过其对奥秘与原野魔法的研习**，**加勒鲁斯常将自己化作风本身**——**周游天下以追寻冷僻知识**。**在他前往天下大陆的一次远行中**，加勒鲁斯**于一座山巅捕获了一只名为雷玛斯的雪鬼**——**将其灵魂封入一只豆罐**。他**强迫这只雪鬼为其效命**——**每当自己远行时**，**便从豆罐中召出雷玛斯看守其塔**。

**九十年前**，**加勒鲁斯离开后再未归来**。**自此**，**雷玛斯便以暴君之姿统治这座小岛**。

**船员**：**接下来的几次遭遇假设船员上岸协助探索岛屿**，**但不进入灯塔本身**（**灯塔内空间过于局促**——**一支分队无法施展**）。**若船员在 B1 区与 PC 们并肩作战**，**则将该遭遇加入两只雾犬**。

**当加勒鲁斯仍住于此时**，**他的凡人仆人住在这些建筑里**。**他失踪后**，**这些仆人不是逃走便是被雷玛斯吃掉了**。

B. Lighthouse of the Aeromancer
This crumbling lighthouse was once the fortress of a reclusive wizard called Galerous the Aeromancer. Through his studies of occult and primal magic, Galerous often transformed himself into the very wind to travel in search of obscure knowledge. On one such excursion to Tian Xia, Galerous captured a snow oni called Rematsu atop a mountain peak, binding his spirit into a jar of beans. He forced the oni into his service, conjuring them from the bean jar to mind his tower whenever he was away on his journeys.

Ninety years ago, Galerous left and never returned. Ever since, Rematsu has ruled the tiny island as a tempestuous tyrant.

Ship Crew: The encounters that follow assume the ship crew disembarks to help explore the island but doesn't enter the lighthouse (which is too cramped for a troop to operate). If the ship crew fights alongside the PCs in area B1, add two fog hounds to that encounter.

B1. DOCKS	LOW 12 OR MODERATE 12
A rotted wooden pier juts into the ocean on this northern side of the island. On shore, thick strands of fog partially obscure a handful of crumbling white stone buildings and a small well. A gravel path winds between the buildings and up the sloped hill of the island toward the lighthouse. All seems quiet, and the small settlement appears abandoned.

When Galerous still lived here, his mortal servants occupied these buildings. After his disappearance, all the servants fled or were eaten by Rematsu.

Creatures: Four fog hounds, unique servants of Galerous, now dwell in the ruins. They hide among the buildings and fog, waiting for the PCs to walk into the village center before attacking from all sides.
