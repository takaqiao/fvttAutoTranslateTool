### A. 寇兰廷历史博物馆（Corentyn Museum of History）

PC 们抵达时，博物馆突袭才刚刚开始。多塔里小队的头领**伊格纳罗·沃莱**（Ignaro Vollai，令人厌恶的男性人类恶魔教士，11 级）正站在博物馆大门前，他的手下则在准备攻门木桩。门后，博物馆馆长**泽菲罗·巴林杰**（Zefiro Balinger，狡黠的男性人类历史学家，7 级）正把一只铁制废物桶塞进一道更大的路障，意图拖延守卫的脚步。本区域地图见第 12 页。

> 一根攻城木桩猛撞着铁制大门，每一记沉重的撞击都为外面的公开演说作着节拍。演说者——身披黑红长袍、绣着多塔里徽记的男子——高声宣告："我宣布，历史博物馆持有煽动叛乱之物。我有责任查扣并销毁一切违禁物品，并制服所有阻碍我之人。"
>
> "你的搜查令呢？"馆长一边向博物馆退去、一边离开门栅缝隙问道。他给一张倾倒的长凳最后一推，将其撑入路障。大门在那股推力下嘎啦作响。
>
> "你会发现我不需要任何搜查令，泽菲罗。"伴随着一声响指，多塔里头领凭空消失。门另一侧惊慌的喊叫立刻暴露了他的新位置——他已出现在博物馆的台阶之上，挡在馆长与安全之间。

多塔里是寇兰廷的卫队，但他们并非凌驾于法律程序之上。任一 PC 若通过 DC 25 的**法务学识**（Legal Lore）或**社会**（Society）检定，或通过 DC 29 的**察觉**（Perception）检定来**察觉动机**（Sense Motive），便能确认这般突袭须凭搜查令；在泽菲罗要求出示而未能出示之际，已是侵犯其权利。重击成功时，该 PC 可推断出多塔里很可能并无搜查令，且此次行动并未获得伊格纳罗上级的许可。

A. Corentyn Museum of History
As the PCs arrive, the museum raid has barely started. The dottari leader, Ignaro Vollai (insufferable male human diabolist 11), stands at the museum gate while his underlings prepare a battering ram. Behind the gates, the museum's curator, Zefiro Balinger (sneaky male human historian 7), lodges an iron wastebasket into a larger barricade meant to slow down the guards. The map for this area is on page 12.

A ram batters against iron gates, violent thuds punctuating a public speech. The speaker—a man in black-and-red robes emblazoned with the dottari's sigil—bellows, "I declare the Museum of History to be in possession of seditious material. It is my duty to seize and destroy all offending objects, and to subdue all who obstruct me."

"Where's your warrant?" asks the curator as he retreats from the gaps in the bars toward the museum. He gives one of the tipped benches a final shove against the barricade. The gates rattle with the push.

"You'll find that I need no warrant, Zefiro." With a snap, the dottari leader disappears. A panicked shout from the other side of the gates betrays his new location—on the steps of the museum, between the curator and safety.

The dottari are the guards of Corentyn, but they aren't above legal processes. A PC who succeeds at a DC 25 Legal Lore or Society check or a DC 29 Perception check to Sense Motive confirms that such a raid requires a warrant. Failure to produce a warrant at Zefiro's request is a violation of his rights. On a critical success, the PC deduces that the dottari likely don't have a warrant and are here without the approval of Ignaro's superiors.

A1. AT THE GATES OF HISTORY	MODERATE 10
Outside of the museum is a street—normally busy, but silent this morning. Nearby stalls typically cater to visitors by offering trinkets and light fare, though they're currently closed. The museum's grand gates stand shut and chained. Beyond it, benches and wastebaskets have been piled in a barricade.

A PC who's trained in Corentyn Lore or an expert in Society knows that these shops are typically busy at this time of day, so the closure means the raid was planned and coordinated in advance. The walls of the museum's complex are 10 feet tall and crafted from stone (DC 20 to Climb, Hardness 14, HP 56, BT 28).

Creatures: The dottari excruciator divisions ram against the museum's gates, obeying their orders to support their leader in the fight. The first step of their plan is to prevent external forces from interrupting the raid. Their next objectives are to open the gate, provide backup to Ignaro, and loot the museum.
