### 风暴酝酿（Brewing Trouble）

冒险开始之际，一支多塔里（dottari）小队正在突袭寇兰廷历史博物馆（Corentyn Museum of History），此事吸引了不同反抗派系的注意——他们各自盘算着如何利用这一事件。

PC 们与其他派系出现在此并非巧合。琳奈塔·海凿（Linetta Seacarver，干练的女性人类间谍，11 级）从一名多塔里告密者那里得到了突袭风声后，便以训练有素的信鹰联络每一派，承诺博物馆将有"一场有趣的早晨"。她希望各派现身、承认彼此使命相通、停止内斗，并与琳奈塔共同投入她自己派系即将在寇兰廷展开的计划：**破钥行动**（Operation Broken Key）。

实情是，她下了一场严重的赌注。博物馆及其馆长泽菲罗（Zefiro）是寇兰廷市民共同珍爱的存在，各派系都希望加以保护；但她的讯息几乎未提及如何防守现场与人员。每一派都自有其与前来抓捕泽菲罗的多塔里对抗的方式。

**泽菲罗的角色（Zefiro's Role）**：数十年来，泽菲罗一直默默抵制切利亚斯篡改历史的行为。每当审查员索取前斯戎时代的文献，泽菲罗便会复制一份给他们，以确保原件被秘密妥善保存，以防切利亚斯日后试图清除旧档、伪造新史。这一做法偶尔会招来争端，泽菲罗也勉强守住了他的人身自由与职位。然而战争爆发后，切利亚斯对政治异见者的搜捕日益加紧。泽菲罗正是多塔里下一步要瞄准的目标。

**琳奈塔的忠诚（Linetta's Loyalties）**：琳奈塔为暮爪（Twilight Talons）效力——这是安多安雄鹰骑士组织内一支鲜为人知的——（接下页）

Brewing Trouble

The adventure begins as a dottari raid is breaking into the Corentyn Museum of History, which draws the attention of various rebel groups with different ideas of how to exploit the event.

The PCs' and other factions' presence isn't a coincidence. Having received a tip about the raid from a dottari informant, Linetta Seacarver (talented female human spy 11) contacted each group by trained carrier hawk promising an "interesting morning" at the museum. She hopes that the factions will appear, acknowledge their shared mission, stop squabbling, and work with Linetta in her own faction's upcoming plan for Corentyn: Operation Broken Key.

The reality is that she's taken a serious gamble. The museum and its curator, Zefiro, are beloved fixtures of Corentyn whom each faction hopes to protect, but her message provides little instruction to how to defend the site and staff. Each faction has chosen its own methods to fight back against the dottari sent to extract Zefiro.

Zefiro's Role: For decades, Zefiro has quietly resisted Chelaxian historical revisionism. Whenever censors requested pre-Thrune documents, Zefiro would create a copy to give them, ensuring the original remained safe and hidden in case Cheliax tried to wipe out old documents and fabricate new history. This practice has occasionally resulted in squabbles with Zefiro barely retaining his freedom and position. However, the outbreak of war has escalated Cheliax's hunt for political enemies. Zefiro is the latest target in dottari sights.
