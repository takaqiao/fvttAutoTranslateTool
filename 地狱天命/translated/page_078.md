#### 审讯（Interrogation）

**PC 们可能俘获并审问齐利亚尼的居民**。**为此**——**非战斗者通常意志 DC 为 25**——**但他们只知道基本的齐利亚尼掌故与神殿内所发生之事的模糊概念**（**例如俘虏被押往那里**、**神殿出入受限**、**索克斯伦掌权等**）。**典型的邪教徒相信并可传达以下内容**：

- **对邪教徒而言**——**齐利亚尼是一座为侍奉沉眠者之眼而建的圣城**——**该神器有时直接将思绪投射进他们脑海**。**他们能讲述该神器是如何被发现的**。**邪教徒崇拜该神器更多是出于恐惧而非敬畏**——**期望完成它的黑暗使命**——**从而获得自由休息**。**他们不愿协助 PC 们摧毁该神器**——**怕遭报复**。
- **每年**——**邪教徒们前往地表绑架一整艘船的囚犯**——**带至折磨之殿**。**囚犯全部被活养一整年**——**期间经受无可言说之刑**——**以采收他们的灵能苦痛**——**这便是沉眠者之眼的食粮**。**据该神器所言**——**邪教徒须收集 111 艘这样的船——它才会将他们从奴役中释放**。**他们现在只差几艘船便达成目标**。
- **一位名为索克斯伦的强大心能者领导着该邪教**。**他的宗教职责由"眼之言者"**（Speaker for the Eye）**协助**——**这是一位高位祭司**——**从受害者中选出**——**服役一年后被祭献**。**索克斯伦与他最精锐的战士驻守在神殿内**。
- **索克斯伦近期与切利亚斯的罗特罗维奥达成了一笔交易**。**作为切利亚斯提供数船祭品的回报**——**索克斯伦同意出兵协助打破对寇兰廷的围攻**。**索克斯伦打算很快亲自领导这场战斗**。
- **沉眠者之眼与所有海下生物有连接**——**邪教徒可使用其魔法将水栖生物征服为其奴**。
- **极少邪教徒被允许进入神殿的内环**——**他们对其布局只有模糊印象**。**他们知道神殿由三道"圣环"组成**——**用以收集并放大灵能能量**。

#### D1. 外齐利亚尼（Outer Killiani）　**严峻 13**

> **这根巨大的桩基从海底破淤而出**——**如一位巨神的手指插入泥淖之中**。**诡异的辉光球体漂浮在柱基附近的水中**——**照亮着附着在桩基与海底之上的珊瑚与石质建筑**。**类人生物在浑浊的水中穿梭**。

**这处定居点容纳着数百名阿扎克缇人以及他们的乌拉卡尼仆从**。**多数居民生活简朴**——**他们的时间用于捕鱼、种植海藻**——**或参加宗教仪式安抚沉眠者之眼**。**生物发光的海带照亮多数房屋的内部**。**然而**——**外部光源主要来自数十只水栖鬼火**（aquatic will-o-wisps）——**它们被神殿散发的恐惧所吸引而至此**。

**生物**：**虽然外表邪恶**——**齐利亚尼的典型市民只是被洗脑的平民**——**对 PC 们威胁不大**。**同样**——**鬼火对高等级 PC 威胁也不大**——**但它们可能试图将 PC 们引离村庄、深入大洋**——**指望 PC 们使用的任何水中呼吸魔法最终失效**。**这些鬼火与齐利亚尼市民共生**——**很少打扰他们**。**齐利亚尼的普通市民在战斗爆发时会逃跑**——**或躲入家中**——**或游入附近的海带田**。

**只有该城最强大的战士才被允许进入神殿或前往地表搜寻受害者**——**因此当 PC 们抵达时**——**城中战斗者很少**。**然而**——**此处并非完全无人守卫**。**一队两名邪教徒与两名骑乘巨齿鲨坐骑的乌拉卡尼侦察兵在城上水域巡逻**——**监视市民**。**他们立刻冲来攻击任何入侵者**。

INTERROGATION
The PCs might capture and question inhabitants of Killiani. For this purpose, noncombatants typically have a Will DC of 25, though only know basic Killiani lore and vague notions of what happens in the temple (such as captives being taken there, temple access being limited, and Xoxren being in charge). The typical cultist believes and can convey the following.

• To the cultists, Killiani is a holy city built in service to the Eye of the Slumberer, which sometimes projects thoughts directly into their minds. They can convey how the artifact was discovered. The cultists worship the artifact more due to fear than reverence, hoping to complete its dark mission and be free to rest. They're unwilling to aid the PCs in destroying the artifact, fearing retribution.

• Each year, the cultists venture to the surface to kidnap a ship full of prisoners and take them to the Temple of Torment. The prisoners are all kept alive for a full year while undergoing unspeakable tortures to harvest their psychic anguish, which feeds the Eye of the Slumberer. According to the artifact, the cultists must collect 111 such ships before it'll release them from their servitude. They're now only a few ships away from their goal.

• A powerful psychic named Xoxren leads the cult. He's aided in his religious duties by the Speaker for the Eye, a high priest chosen from among the victims serves for one year before being sacrificed. Xoxren and his best warriors reside in the temple.

• Xoxren recently struck a deal with Rotrovio of Cheliax. In exchange for Cheliax providing several shiploads of sacrifices, Xoxren agreed to lend his forces to break the siege of Corentyn. Xoxren plans to lead that battle soon.

• The Eye of the Slumberer has a connection to all undersea creatures, and cultists can use its magic to subjugate aquatic creatures into their service.

• Few cultists are allowed into the temple's inner rings, and they have only a vague idea of its layout. They know the temple is composed of three "holy rings" that collect and amplify psychic energy.

D1. OUTER KILLIANI	MODERATE 13
This massive piling juts from the ocean floor like the finger of a great god stuck into the muck. Eerie globes of lambent light drift through the water around the pillar's base, illuminating buildings made of coral and stone that cling to the piling and sea floor. Humanoids flit through the murky water.

This settlement houses hundreds of azarketis and their ulat-kini servitors. Most inhabitants live simply, spending their time fishing, growing seaweed, or engaging in religious ceremonies to appease the Eye of the Slumberer. Bioluminescent kelp illuminates the interiors of most houses. Exterior light, however, comes mostly from several dozen aquatic will-o-wisps attracted here by the fear radiating from the temple.

Creatures: Although outwardly wicked, Killiani's typical citizens are brainwashed civilians who pose little threat to the PCs. Likewise, the will-o-wisps pose little threat to high-level PCs, though they might try to lead the PCs away from the village and deeper into the ocean, hoping that any water breathing magic the PCs are using eventually expires. The wisps have a symbiotic relationship with the citizens of Killiani and rarely trouble them. The regular citizens of Killiani flee if combat breaks out, either hiding in their homes or swimming into their kelp fields nearby.

Only the most powerful warriors of the city are allowed to enter the temple or venture to the surface in search of victims, so there are few combatants in the city when the PCs arrive. It isn't completely unguarded, however. A group of two cultists and two ulat-kini scouts riding megalodon mounts patrol the waters above the town, keeping an eye on the citizens. They immediately rush to attack any intruders.
