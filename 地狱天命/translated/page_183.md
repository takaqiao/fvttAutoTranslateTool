> **贝伦内宅邸外（OUTSIDE BELUNNE MANOR）　　中等 19**
>
> 玩家角色靠近贝伦内宅邸时——他们听到一个男子的高声呼喝与喋喋演说。若他们想小心翼翼地接近——他们每人必须以**潜行**检定胜过格拉吉乌斯的**察觉**DC。无论是哪种情况——他们看到以下景象：
>
> 「这座一度威仪堂堂的宅邸——其门面如今已不复昔日光景。墙体亟需新漆一层——门前的小草坪——显然已数月未经修剪。一道铁制大门——歪斜地挂在合页上——一块铜牌上镌刻着姓氏「**贝伦内**」（Belunne）。」

**生物**：宅邸外街上——立着一组三只**叛教魔**（deimaviggas）——它们正在聆听屋前抬高门廊处那位高声演说之人。它们出于好奇被吸引至此——因它们识出了那位呼喝者持续独白中的意图——他正是前**大公市长**格拉吉乌斯·阿拉扎里奥。他仍穿着他平日里的市长盛装——拄着他的职杖——但他的面容与身体——与数日前相比——已然不同。一位以 DC 30 **社交**检定成功、或曾见过格拉吉乌斯本人的玩家角色——能认出他那红色的皮肤、卷曲的犄角、与黄色的双眼——并非常态。**阿斯佩西娅·鲁加托恩**已「**赐予**」他一具更加邪魔的形态与使命——将他部分地变易为一只邪魔。

身处新的形态之中——格拉吉乌斯已弃了他的治政职务——任凭阿斯佩西娅在全城肆意妄为。他被这座宅邸所吸引——隐隐感知到其内的神圣大军——是必须处理的威胁。因此——他立于前廊——向城中所有魔鬼布道——令其襄助他的「圣战」。若他见到玩家角色——他便确信他们是闯入者——并向他们发动攻击。叛教魔随之响应。战斗期间——格拉吉乌斯的独白持续不断——他赞美此城的变易——盛赞阿斯佩西娅授予他新得的力量——一切皆以阿斯摩蒂斯之名。他狂热地战至殒命——但每一只叛教魔——在其生命值减至 30 以下时——便会离开战场。

OUTSIDE BELUNNE MANOR	MODERATE 19

As the PCs approach Belunne Manor, they hear a
man shouting and ranting. If they want to approach
cautiously, they each must succeed at a Stealth check
against Grachius's Perception DC. In either case, they
see the following.

The facade of this once stately manor has seen better
days. The walls could use a new coat of paint, and the
small lawn clearly hasn't been mowed in several months.
An iron gate to the grounds hangs off its hinges, the name
"Belunne" etched onto a brass plaque.

Creatures: A trio of deimaviggas stand in the street
outside the manor, listening to the individual orating
from the house's raised front stoop. They were drawn
here out of curiosity, recognizing the intent of the
continual monologue of the shouter, former Lord
Mayor Grachius Alazario. He's still dressed in his
usual mayoral finery and carrying his staff of office,
though his face and body look different from just a
few days ago. A PC who succeeds at a DC 30 Society
check or has seen Grachius before recognizes that
his red skin, curling horns, and yellow eyes aren't
normal. Aspexia Rugatonn has "gifted" him with a
more diabolical form and purpose, transforming him
partially into a fiend.

In his new state, Grachius has abandoned his
governmental duties, leaving Aspexia to run riot
across the city. He's been drawn to this manor, dimly
sensing the divine army within as a threat that must
be handled. And so, he stands on the front stoop,
preaching for all devils in the city to aid his quest. If
he sees the PCs, he's certain they're interlopers and
attacks them. The deimaviggas follow suit. During
the fight, Grachius's monologue continues; he praises
the transformation of the city and lauds Aspexia for
granting him his newfound power, all in the name of
Asmodeus. He zealously fights to the death, but each
deimavigga leaves the scene when reduced to fewer
than 30 Hit Points.
