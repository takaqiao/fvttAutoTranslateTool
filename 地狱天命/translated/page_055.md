## Page 55 — Ch3 异端讲座（影响遭遇 + 三阶段 + 亵典数据上半）

这是一场**影响遭遇**（influence encounter，GM Core 第 187 页），**共 5 个回合**，**分为下文三个段落**。**无论谈判得多么出色**，**整夜聆听堕落讲座对最坚毅的灵魂亦是耗损**。**在每个影响回合结束时**，**每位 PC 都会暴露于"亵典之课"诅咒**。

> **亵典之课（LESSONS OF APOCRIPHANY）**　**诅咒 12**
> 诅咒　精神　邪秽
> 虽然你能拨开魔鬼的部分论断与"证明"，**其论辩的巨大重量**仍**压在你的精神之上**，**侵蚀其防御**，**让最乐观的听众也变得苦涩**。**虽属诅咒**，**讲座的效果却像一种疾病那样按阶段进展与消退**——**让 PC 既可能随时间摆脱该教导**，**也可能陷入对令人不安的细节的思虑漩涡**。
> **豁免** DC 33 意志；**潜伏期** 1 分钟；**阶段 1** 只要你仍参与亵典的讲座，便处于**恐惧 1**（1 天）；**阶段 2** 如阶段 1，外加**对魔鬼的技能检定与豁免承受 -1 环境减值**（1 天）；**阶段 3** 如阶段 2，但恐惧改为 **恐惧 2**（1 天）；**阶段 4** 如阶段 3，并感到**最终违反一条你所遵循的非邪恶诫令或禁忌的冲动**——**在你这样做之前**，**你无法自然恢复出此阶段**。

讲座从**黄昏**开始，**贯穿整夜**。整个晚上，**亵典极尽所能用其偏邪的知识腐化 PC 们**，而**他们则必须设法让她保持好心情**。**只要 PC 不依赖肤浅而扼杀思考的应答**，**她并不介意神圣的反驳与辩论**。

**夜初（2 回合）**：黄昏降临之际，**亵典将众人聚于讲台前**，**感谢他们出席**，并致一段简短演讲。她**已在讲台前的地上准备了三个奥秘符文召唤圆环**，**用每一个召出地狱中不同的"客座讲师"**。这些魔鬼是：**杰格罗（Jeglor）**（**契约魔 phistophilus devil**）、**昂格拉杜姆（Ongradoom）**（**军团魔 vordine devil**）和**萨拉斯（Thalrath）**（**仆魔 gylou devil**）——它们**以其真身的暗影幻象**出现。每只都发表一段冗长讲演，**讲述腐化与说服凡人的最佳方法**。讲座期间**亵典坐在 PC 们身旁**，**鼓励他们提问并征询他们的看法**。这是 PC 们**使用"刺探"（Discover）行动来了解她的怪癖**，以及**使用"影响"（Influence）行动以其知识打动她的绝佳时机**。

**讲座效果**：讲座结束后，每位 PC 必须**通过 DC 30 意志豁免**，**否则将在一个月内饱受梦魇折磨**（**视作具备诅咒特征的梦魇术**；每晚一次 DC 28 意志豁免）。

**子夜飨宴（1 回合）**：午夜时，讲座中止以**举办飨宴**。**祭司们在低桌上用餐**，**亵典则邀请 PC 们与她及其科阿提魔同党在主桌共餐**。**亵典口味精雅**，**所设之宴出人意料地美味**——尽管**几道菜中含有还在蠕动的活食海鲜**。在此段中她要求 PC 们**反思迄今为止的扭曲讲座**，并**鼓励他们分享自己人生中的腐化或黑暗的实例**。**对于背景坎坷的 PC 而言**，这或许是分享过往的**绝佳角色扮演机会**。**以此方式分享的 PC 会让亵典印象深刻**，**其影响亵典的检定获得 +2 加值**。

**飨宴效果**：宴中场亵典点名加演余兴，**她的科阿提魔追随者中有一位起身吟唱了一连串令人不安的叙事歌谣**。**PC 们必须通过 DC 30 意志豁免**，**否则将变得恐惧 3**（**此后每过一个影响回合而非战斗回合便减 1 阶**）。**若 PC 们愿亲自表演一段节目并通过 DC 35 表演检定**，**便可免去此豁免**。

**深夜（2 回合）**：晚间剩余时间里，**讲台被亵典挑选的一系列祭司与历史学家占据**——**他们详述斯戎家族历来所犯的种种罪行与不公**。**她希望追随者们能借此情报更有效地破坏斯戎家族的统治**。**夜里某时**，她**会请 PC 们上讲台**，**邀请他们讲述自己与斯戎家族交集的经历**。

**讲座效果**：**PC 们所不知的是**——**今晚不只是一系列讲座**——**它同时是亵典早早就精心筹备的仪式**。**最后一段讲座结束时**，**一道"灵魂之锚"诅咒**（spirit anchor，GM Core 第 87 页）落在庄园本宅之上——**降咒于其中的每个生物**，**除非他们通过 DC 31 强韧豁免**。**失败者的灵魂会被永久锚定在戈拉里昂世界**——**只要斯戎家族仍统治切利亚斯**——**让他们即便死后**，**也会以鬼魂之身复起，继续动摇斯戎家族的统治**。

This is an influence encounter (GM Core 187) with 5 rounds, divided into the three sections below. Regardless of how well they negotiate, spending an entire evening listening to the depraved lectures is draining for even the most stalwart souls. At the end of each influence round, each PC is exposed to the Lessons of Apocriphany curse.

LESSONS OF APOCRIPHANY	CURSE 12
CURSE MENTAL UNHOLY
Though you can deflect some of the devil's assertions and proofs, the sheer weight of her arguments weigh on your spirit, erode its defenses, and sour even the most optimistic listener. Though a curse, the lectures' effects progress and wane in stages more like a disease, allowing a PC to either shake off the teachings over time or mentally spiral as they ponder the disturbing details.
Saving Throw DC 33 Will; Onset 1 minute; Stage 1 frightened 1 so long as you participate in Apocriphany's lessons (1 day); Stage 2 as stage 1, plus you take a –1 circumstance penalty to skill checks and saving throws against devils (1 day); Stage 3 as stage 2, but you are frightened 2 (1 day); Stage 4 as stage 3, and feel a compulsion to eventually violate some non-evil edict or anathema you have, and you cannot recover from this stage naturally until you do so.

The lectures begin at dusk and run through the entire night. Over the course of the evening, Apocriphany does her best to corrupt the PCs with her deviant knowledge, while they in turn must work to keep her in a good mood. She doesn't mind holy counterpoints and debate so long as a PC doesn't rely on shallow, thought-stopping remarks.

Early Evening (2 rounds): As dusk falls, Apocriphany gathers everyone before the podium, thanks them for attending, and gives a brief speech. She's prepared three conjuration circles of occult runes on the ground before the podium and uses each to conjure a different "guest lecturer" from Hell. The devils are Jeglor (a phistophilus devil), Ongradoom (a vordine devil), and Thalrath (a gylou devil), and they appear as shadowy illusions of their true forms. Each gives a lengthy speech on the best ways to corrupt and persuade mortals. Apocriphany sits next to the PCs during these lectures, encouraging them to ask questions and asking for their thoughts. This is an excellent time for PCs to use the Discover action to learn her quirks as well as Influence to impress her with their knowledge.

Lecture Effect: After the lectures, each PC must succeed at a DC 30 Will save or be plagued by nightmares for one month (as the spell nightmare with the curse trait; DC 28 Will save once per night).

Midnight Feast (1 Round): At midnight, the lectures pause for a feast. While the priests eat at lower tables, Apocriphany invites the PCs to dine with her and her coterie of coarti devils at the head table. Apocriphany has a refined palate and serves a surprisingly delicious spread, although several dishes do contain wriggling seafood that's eaten while still alive. During this section, she asks the PCs to reflect on the perverse lectures so far and encourages them to share examples of corruption or darkness from their own lives. For any PCs with a troubled past, it might be an excellent roleplaying opportunity to share their history. PCs who share in this fashion impress Apocriphany and receive a +2 bonus to their checks to Influence Apocriphany.

Feast Effect: Mid-feast, Apocriphany calls for entertainment and one of her coarti followers rises to sing a series of disturbing ballads. The PCs must succeed at a DC 30 Will save or become frightened 3 (this decreases by one step after each influence round, rather than combat round). The PCs can avoid the save if they offer to give a performance instead and succeed at a DC 35 Performance check.

Dead of Night (2 Rounds): For the remainder of the evening, the podium is occupied by a variety of priests and historians picked by Apocriphany to detail the many crimes and injustices committed by House Thrune throughout history. She hopes that her followers can use this information to better undermine Thrune rule. At some point during the night, she calls the PCs to the podium and invites them to speak of their own experiences with Thrune.

Lecture Effect: Unbeknownst to the PCs, this evening has been more than just a series of lectures—it also functions as a carefully prepared ritual that Apocriphany crafted long beforehand. When the final lecture concludes, a spirit anchor curse (GM Core 87) settles on the manor house, cursing each creature within unless they succeed at a DC 31 Fortitude save. Those who fail have their souls permanently anchored to Golarion for as long has House Thrune rules Cheliax, so that even in death, they'll rise as ghosts to undermine Thrune rule.
