## Page 136 — 第七章：战争即地狱

**第三阶段结束时，玩家角色穿越废墟、贴近燃狱之门。运行遭遇 E3。**

---

**地狱护印（INFERNAL WARDS）　障碍 16**
**渗透点** 3（团体）；**克服** DC 35 奥秘、自然、神秘或宗教以魔法解除邪魔能量；DC 37 巧手以划除关键地狱符文、让护印安全可穿越；或 DC 39 意志豁免，直接咬牙挺过这些护印对胆敢越界又未与之契合者投下的地狱幻象。

玩家角色与他们的部队所穿越的整片区域，都覆盖着染红的邪魔文字，护着此地不受未与斯戎家族或切利亚斯军方同盟者的侵入。只有意志坚强（或能解除护印之力）者方可通过。

---

**敌友难辨（FRIEND OR FOE）　障碍 16**
**渗透点** 2（团体）；**克服** DC 35 纹章学识以迅速向迎面而来的营队挥出正确旗号、避免灾难；DC 37 交涉以大声喊出从安多兰军官帐中习得的当日正确口令、让推进而来的友军意识到攻错目标；或 DC 39 社交以使用手势及其他更微妙的信号，向那支营队示意同袍身份。

切利亚斯战线边缘有一支安多兰小型营队突破而来，但在战雾中误将玩家角色与他们的不规则部队当作了**更多敌军**！玩家必须迅速表明自己是盟友，否则便有遭遇友军误伤的风险——徒然削减对抗斯戎家族的整体战力！

---

### 第四阶段：浩劫呼号（PHASE 4: CRY HAVOC）

最后一击的时刻已到！安多兰兵力开始将切利亚斯步兵逼向河岸，玩家角色身处南岸。然而，切利亚斯仍有部署在附近山顶的速射炮兵单位，以及效忠斯戎家族的**铁甲地狱骑士**。玩家角色或可与鹰骑士联手用钳形机动困住这些地狱骑士，或可拔除炮兵阵地。

**第四阶段结束时，运行遭遇 E4。**

---

**拆解炮兵（DISMANTLE ARTILLERY）　障碍 16**
**渗透点** 1（个人）；**克服** DC 35 工程学识以剪断让这些战争器械在再次开火前噤声的特定电线与绳索；DC 37 巧手以卡住炮弹击发机关，让炮即便已装填也无法击发；或 DC 39 工艺以使装填口失效，让炮组只能空射。

机警的玩家可以登山拔除切利亚斯炮兵，以各类方式让这批致命器械沉默——为他们这一方战场上的众多生灵守住性命。

---

**钳形机动（PINCER MANEUVER）　障碍 16**
**渗透点** 1（个人）；**克服** DC 35 战争学识以将特种部队渡河冲入敌人脆弱侧翼；DC 37 杂技以从敌阵中优雅穿行，仿佛对方不存在一般；或 DC 39 运动，以特种部队与从四面八方涌来的鹰骑士一同砸穿敌方铁甲。

斯戎家族忠诚的地狱骑士在河岸做出一场虽勇但误的最终抗争。终结他们的最后一击，落在玩家角色的手中。

### 警觉点阈值（AWARENESS POINT TIERS）

本场战场渗透的警觉点阈值如下：

**5 警觉点**：玩家角色首次到达此阈值时，**警戒线挑战**（Picket Challenge）复杂情况发生。

**10 警觉点**：切利亚斯指挥官察觉到他们后方有更多兵力调动。**所有障碍的 DC +1**。如果首次到达此阈值时玩家角色尚未完成第一阶段，则**为遭遇 E1 增加一只血棘藤**。

**15 警觉点**：玩家角色首次到达此阈值时，**空中防御**（Aerial Defenses）复杂情况发生。如果首次到达此阈值时玩家角色已经杀死或赶走龙（遭遇 E2），你应**用火焰轰炸的魔鬼替换那两只龙**。

**20 警觉点**：切利亚斯进入高级警戒状态。**所有障碍的 DC 累计 +2**。此外，如果首次到达此阈值时玩家角色尚未完成第二阶段，则**切利亚斯精英预备部队（遭遇 E2）出动**。

**25 警觉点**：斯戎家族的仪式者成功将燃狱之门开启至最大孔径，安多兰军方在地狱军团涌出之时溃退。**渗透失败**，**遭遇 E4 开始**。该失败的可怕后果详见「**章末**」。

---

### 复杂情况（COMPLICATIONS）

沉言林之战有数个复杂情况，可在玩家角色达到特定警觉点阈值时触发。每一项，由**一名玩家角色尝试一次检定**以克服。其他玩家角色可通过尝试与试图克服此复杂情况的玩家相同类型的 DC 35 检定进行**协助**。

At the end of Phase 3, the PCs bring their troops through the rubble and close to the Inferno Gate. Run encounter E3.

INFERNAL WARDS　OBSTACLE 16
Infiltration Points 3 (group); Overcome DC 35 Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion to magically disable the diabolic energies; DC 37 Thievery to scratch out key infernal runes and render the wards safe to traverse; or DC 39 Will save to simply endure the Hellish visions these wards visit upon the unattuned who dare cross their threshold

The entire area the PCs and their troops travel through is covered in red-tinged diabolic script, warding the area from those not aligned with House Thrune or the Chelaxian military. Only those strong of will (or able to disable the wards' power) can cross.

FRIEND OR FOE　OBSTACLE 16
Infiltration Points 2 (group); Overcome DC 35 Heraldry Lore to quickly fly the correct signals to the incoming battalion and avoid disaster, DC 37 Diplomacy to shout the day's correct code phrase learned from the Andoren officer's tent to convince the oncoming troops that they have the wrong target, or DC 39 Society to use hand signs and other more subtle signals to indicate shared allegiance with the battalion

A small battalion of Andoren troops breaks through the edge of the Chelaxian line but, in the haze of battle, mistakes the PCs and their irregular units for more enemy troops! The PCs must quickly identify themselves as allies, or else they risk friendly fire that will only diminish the overall effort against House Thrune!

PHASE 4: CRY HAVOC

It's time for the final push! The Andoren forces start to pin the Chelaxian infantry against the river, and the PCs are on the southern bank. However, Cheliax still has fast-firing artillery units located on nearby hilltops and ironclad Hellknights who are loyal to House Thrune. The PCs can either trap these Hellknights in a pincer maneuver with the Eagle Knights, or they can take out the artillery batteries.

At the end of Phase 4, run encounter E4.

DISMANTLE ARTILLERY　OBSTACLE 16
Infiltration Points 1 (individual); Overcome DC 35 Engineering Lore to cut very specific wires and ropes that render these war engines silent before they can be fired again, DC 37 Thievery to jam the firing mechanisms of the artillery pieces so they can't be used even if loaded, or DC 39 Crafting to disable the loading ports so the crews can only shoot blanks

Crafty PCs can scale the hill and take out the Chelaxian artillery in a manner of different ways, saving many lives on their side of the battlefield as they silence these deadly machines.

PINCER MANEUVER　OBSTACLE 16
Infiltration Points 1 (individual); Overcome DC 35 Warfare Lore to charge the special forces units across the river into the enemy's vulnerable flank, DC 37 Acrobatics to gracefully maneuver through the field of foes as if they weren't even there, or DC 39 Athletics to simply smash through the enemy's ironclad armor with their special forces units and the Eagle Knights crowding in from all sides

House Thrune's loyal Hellknights make a valiant, if misguided, final stand at the bank of the river. It falls to the PCs to bring down the final blow and end them.

AWARENESS POINT TIERS

Awareness Point tiers for this battlefield infiltration are as follows.

5 Awareness Points: The first time the PCs reach this tier, the Picket Challenge complication occurs.

10 Awareness Points: Chelaxian commanders become aware of increased movement to their rear. Increase the DCs for obstacles by 1. If the PCs haven't finished Phase 1 when this tier is first reached, add one wemmuth to encounter E1.

15 Awareness Points: The first time the PCs reach this tier, the Aerial Defenses complication occurs. If the PCs have already killed or driven off the dragons (encounter E2) when this tier is first reached, you should replace the dragons with fire-bombing devils.

20 Awareness Points: The Chelaxians go on high alert. Increase the DCs for obstacles by a total of 2. In addition, the Chelaxian elite reserve troops mobilize (encounter E2) if the PCs haven't already finished Phase 2 when this tier is first reached.

25 Awareness Points: House Thrune's ritualists have successfully opened the Inferno Gate to its maximum aperture, and the Andoren military falls back before the infernal legions pour through it. The infiltration is a failure, and encounter E4 begins. See Concluding the Chapter for the dire consequences of this failure.

COMPLICATIONS

The Battle of the Whisperwood has several complications that can arise if the PCs reach certain Awareness Point tiers. For each one, a single PC attempts a check to overcome it. Other PCs can Aid this check by attempting a DC 35 check of the same type as the one attempted by the PC trying to overcome the complication.
