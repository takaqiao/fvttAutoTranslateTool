### A. 格拉戴斯卡堡垒地面层（Citadel Gheradesca Ground Floor）

地面层与执棒官走廊（Lictor's Walk）见第 36 页地图。两者之间的若干层楼包含地狱骑士的居所、办公室和支持该团工作的其他房间，其大部分在本次渗透范围之外。

地狱骑士发生分裂后，巡逻较以往更轻。在地面层（也称**低级监狱**——因为这是关押被转交囚犯的牢房所在地），**斯戎特工**（Thrune agents）把守入口。执棒官乌罗·阿登请求援助，以便镣铐骑士团能把注意力转向位于岛屿深处的**高级监狱**。

除非另有说明，堡垒以墙上烛台中的定期火炬照明，其结构以石材建成（包括门）。门未上锁，除非另有说明；标准门**硬度 10、HP 40、BT 20**，**强行开启的 DC 为 23**。

**守卫巡逻（Guard Patrols）**：每当 PC 通过区域 A3、A9、A11、A14 时，他们便遭遇**巡逻**障碍。PC 克服巡逻障碍后，10 分钟内不会再次面对此障碍。

> **巡逻（PATROL）**　**障碍 10**
> 渗透点 2（团队）；克服：DC 28 欺瞒或生存、DC 30 察觉或社会，或 DC 26 匿踪
>
> 巡逻的厚重靴子撞在石板上闷响阵阵，预告着他们的接近，让你做好准备。

#### A1. 格拉戴斯卡前门（Gheradesca's Front Door）

> 小径在通过堡垒外缘下方时收窄，随后在角塔与主楼之间盘旋而过。外墙箭孔中透出灯光，偶尔有一道路过的影子使灯光闪烁。

通过这道 4 英寸宽的箭孔窥视的生物（需要 DC 28 运动检定**攀爬**；箭孔起始处距地面 6 英尺）可以看到堡垒内部。透过箭孔窥视超过 10 分钟会触发"**我是不是听见什么了？**"复杂情况（第 34 页）。

#### A2. 外塔（Outer Tower）

外四塔中，**唯独这一座真正独立于堡垒**。其底部从岛屿的石面伸出，沿路径转弯而成型，随后作为方形结构向上耸立。上层每升一级便愈发收窄，最终形成一个细尖。

> 越过外塔狭窄的门，篷布覆盖着内部物品。

外塔的主要用途是在外部小径上**制造瓶颈**，但其上层用于供来自其他骑士团的访客地狱骑士住宿。目前，地面层存放着用来攻击漫长攀登上路人的物资，包括**沥青桶**与**油桶**。

杂物间制造出若干隐匿点。所有摆放着板条箱与篷布的方格为其中的生物提供**遮蔽**（concealment）。若 PC 们把囚犯藏于此处作为"安置囚犯"机遇（第 34 页）的一部分，他们对该机遇的克服检定获得 +2 状况加值。但本室一次只能藏匿 **1 个大型生物或 4 个中型生物**。

#### A3. 主入口（Main Entry）

> 一条宽阔走廊在带角的墙壁之间蜿蜒。一幅阿波罗盖·斯戎二世女王的肖像在壁龛中俯视着行经者。

**巡逻**：本段走廊属于常规巡逻障碍路线（见上）。**隐蔽点有限**，将该障碍的匿踪 DC 提高 2。

A. Citadel Gheradesca Ground Floor
The ground floor of the citadel and the Lictor's Walk are presented on the map on page 36. Additional levels between the two contain the Hellknights' housing, offices, and other rooms that support the order's work, most of which are outside the scope of this infiltration.

With the schism in the Hellknights, the patrols are lighter than they once were. On the ground floor (also called the low prison, as it's the location of the holding cells for transferred prisoners), Thrune agents guard the entrances. Lictor Uro Adom requested aid so that the Order of the Manacle could turn its attention to the high prison located deep within the island.

Unless otherwise stated, the citadel is lit with periodic torches in wall sconces, and the structure is made of stone (including the doors). Doors are unlocked unless otherwise stated, and standard doors have Hardness 10, HP 40, and BT 20. The DC to Force Open the standard doors is 23.

Guard Patrols: Whenever a PC moves through areas A3, A9, A11, and A14, they encounter the Patrol obstacle. When the PCs overcome the Patrol obstacle, they don't face it again until 10 minutes pass.

PATROL	OBSTACLE 10
Infiltration Points 2 (group); Overcome DC 28 Deception or Survival, DC 30 Perception or Society, or DC 26 Stealth
The heavy boots of the patrol thud against the stone tiles, warning you to prepare for their approach.

A1. GHERADESCA'S FRONT DOOR
The path narrows as it passes underneath the lip of the fortress, then twists between a corner tower and the main building. Lights shine through arrow slits on the exterior wall, occasionally flickering from a passing shadow.

A creature that peers through the 4-inch-wide arrow slits (DC 28 Athletics to Climb; the arrow slits start 6 feet above the ground) can see into the citadel. Looking through an arrow slit for more than 10 minutes causes the creature to trigger the Did I Hear Something? complication (page 34).

A2. OUTER TOWER
Of the four outer towers, only this one is truly independent from the citadel. Its base juts out of the island's stone, shaping itself around the curve of the path before it rises as a boxy structure. The upper floors narrow with each ascending level, eventually forming a fine point.

Past the outer tower's narrow door, tarps cover the contents within.

This outer tower's main purpose is to create a choke point on the exterior path, though the upper floors are used for housing visiting Hellknights from other orders. Currently, the ground floor holds supplies for attacking wayfarers on the Long Climb, including barrels of tar and oil.

The clutter creates several hiding spots. All squares with crates and tarps provide concealment to creatures in them. If the PCs hide prisoners in here as part of the Secure Prisoner opportunity (page 34), they gain a +2 circumstance bonus to checks to overcome the opportunity. However, this room can only hide one Large creature or four Medium creatures.

A3. MAIN ENTRY
A wide hallway twists between angular walls. A portrait of Queen Abrogail Thrune II watches occupants from an alcove.

Patrol: These hallways are part of the regular Patrol obstacle's route (see above). Limited places to hide increase the Stealth DC of that obstacle by 2.
