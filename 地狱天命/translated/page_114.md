#### C. 邪魔围猎（FIENDISH GAME）　**中等 15**

在**斯戎家族**多年来尝试过的众多狱裔实验之中，包括对**狱魔注入式恐龙蛋**（infernally-infused dinosaur eggs）的造物——这些恐龙蛋最初是从**美第尔加蒂岛**（Mediogalti Island）解放出来，然后在切利亚斯的实验室里被注入**魔鬼之血**。当这些实验体不可避免地逃出囚笼之后，它们与它们的后裔便在**沉言林**里安了家。

这些**狱裔恐龙**之中最强者是**斯戎暴龙**（thruneosaurus rex）——一头**暴龙**（tyrannosaurus rex）与**魔鬼之血**亵秽结合的造物，现已横行肆虐。仅是一头这样的生灵就降临到**梅森·格林**（Mason Grin）这座小小的水晶采矿村落上，把全村屠尽，未让一名幸存者逃出报信。只有一名来自**塞纳拉**（Senara）的、骑术敏捷且运气走好的政府公差正在送信途中，远远瞥见了这野兽，得以逃脱、把消息带回了当地行政官那里。

**斯戎家族**派出了一支**驯兽师雇佣兵团**（beastmaster mercenaries），要把这生灵捕获并赶进一具镶银的巨大笼内，以便日后推上战场放出来重击安多兰侧翼；但**斯戎暴龙**远非他们所能驾驭——它把整支远征队悉数吞噬。它依旧极其凶险，如今正在它产卵的村落残骸上跺地巡视。如果玩家角色们带上任何**地狱骑士**部队同行，**地狱骑士**们会从尸骸与装备认出这是一支精锐怪物猎手——是他们骑士团之外最顶尖的一批人。

**生物**：一头**斯戎暴龙**可在**梅森·格林**正中央巡视。它的脚步声极为震耳、震得大地颤动，玩家角色们与其部队甚至无须任何检定便能在抵达地图之前确定它的方位。这头狱裔恐龙为捍卫自己的领地，会在战斗开打时先释放它的火焰光束，接着尝试用**践踏**碾压挡路者，然后混用**狱火爆吐**及**整吞**对手的能力来重创敌人。即便不能言语，它也能听懂**通用语**与**狱界语**，并会根据玩家角色们在它附近高喊出的战术作出调整。尽管在这里已下了一窝蛋，它若被打到 HP 低于 60，便会撤离村庄——但在场的**派克长枪骑士团**成员将催促玩家角色们追杀此兽，以免它另寻别村去恐怖屠戮。

#### C. FIENDISH GAME　MODERATE 15

Among the many diabolic experiments House Thrune has attempted over the years is the creation of infernally-infused dinosaur eggs, originally liberated from Mediogalti Island and then injected with devil ichor in Chelaxian laboratories. When these test subjects inevitably escaped confinement, they and their descendants made their home in the Whisperwood.

The greatest of these infernal dinosaurs is the thruneosaurus rex, an unholy union of a tyrannosaurus rex and devil's blood that now runs amok. A single one of these creatures descended on the small quartz mining village of Mason Grin and completely depopulated it without allowing a single survivor to escape and tell the tale. Only a lucky (and swift-riding) civil servant from Senara delivering the mail happened to spot the beast from afar and escaped to bring word of it to the local magistrate.

House Thrune sent a team of beastmaster mercenaries to capture the creature and herd it into an enormous silver-lined cage so they could wheel it onto the battlefield and unleash it upon the Andoren flank, but the thruneosaurus rex was too much for them and ate the entire expedition. Still extremely dangerous, it stomps around the remains of the village where it's laid eggs of its own. If the PCs brings any Hellknight troops with them, the Hellknights recognize the corpses and equipment as belonging to an elite group of monster hunters—the best of the best outside of their order.

Creatures: A single thruneosaurus rex can be found patrolling the center of Mason Grin. Its footfalls are so loud and earthshaking that the PCs and their troop don't need to attempt any checks to determine its location prior to arriving on the map. Defending its territory, the diabolic dinosaur unleashes its fiery beam at the outset of combat, followed by attempting to Trample anything in its path, before using a combination of its Hellfire Blasts and ability to Swallow Whole opponents to decimate its enemies. Even though it can't speak, it can understand the Common and Diabolic languages and will adapt its tactics if the PCs shout their strategies out loud near it. Despite having laid a clutch of eggs here, it'll retreat from the village if reduced to fewer than 60 HP, though the Order of the Pike members present will urge the PCs to hunt the creature down and kill it so it doesn't find a different village to terrorize.
