## Page 35 — Ch2 复杂情况 + 营救囚犯 + 格拉戴斯卡堡垒 + 漫长攀登

> **我是不是听见什么了？（DID I HEAR SOMETHING?）**　**复杂情况 10**
> **触发** PC 们首次到达 5 警觉点。
> **克服** DC 26 匿踪、DC 28 欺瞒，或 DC 30 交涉或表演
>
> 有人听见你附近的动静，迫使你在被发现前躲藏或制造分心。
> **成功** 你躲过或转移了守卫。
> **失败** 你被发现，队伍累计 1 AP。
> **大失败** 同失败，队伍累计 2 AP。

> **藏匿尸体（HIDE BODIES）**　**复杂情况 10**
> **触发** PC 们在格拉戴斯卡堡垒中完成一场遭遇且留下死亡或不省人事的生物。
> **克服** DC 26 运动、DC 28 自然、或 DC 30 欺瞒或医疗（让尸体看上去像在熟睡）
>
> 一具尸体所造成的不便无法忽视；任一地狱骑士都会调查。**未藏匿或伪装的尸体会在 1d10 × 10 分钟内被发现**，使队伍累计 1 AP。

> **彻底搜查（THOROUGH SEARCH）**　**复杂情况 10**
> **触发** PC 们的警觉点总数首次到达 15。
> **克服** DC 28 匿踪或生存，或 DC 30 体技或运动
>
> 地狱骑士召集对格拉戴斯卡堡垒的彻底搜查。他们检查每一条走廊与房间寻找入侵者，迫使你在躲避他们的同时不断移动。
> **大成功** 你比搜查领先一步，成功隐藏踪迹。
> **成功** 安保仍处高位；PC 们下一次本会获得 AP 时，额外多累计 1 AP。
> **失败** 你被发现，队伍累计 1 AP。
> **大失败** 同失败，队伍累计 2 AP。

### 营救囚犯（Rescuing Prisoners）

把囚犯从格拉戴斯卡堡垒救出是 PC 们的首要目标。然而，仅把他们从牢房带出来还不够。PC 们必须找一处安全地方让他们暂留，**直到潮水退去后才能最终撤离**。"**安置囚犯**"（Secure Prisoner）机遇允许 PC 们找到合适的隐匿点。若 PC 们在不尝试"安置囚犯"机遇的情况下把一名囚犯从牢房里放出来，队伍**自动累计 1 警觉点**。

能力或合适的法术（如**阈空之门**，liminal doorway）可由你（GM）酌情判定为自动成功。

在高级监狱中，囚犯唯一合适的去处是**已毁的西翼**。由于那里游荡着不死与魔鬼造成的危险，**在该层营救的囚犯，安置囚犯的 DC 提高 2**。

> **安置囚犯（SECURE PRISONER）**　**机遇 10**
> **要求** PC 们定位到一名囚犯。
>
> 把囚犯护送至安全地点等候潮水退去，需要找到一处避开格拉戴斯卡堡垒常规巡逻的位置，需 10 分钟小心地转移囚犯。尝试 DC 28 欺瞒、交涉、匿踪或生存检定。
> **成功** 你把囚犯护送到安全藏身处，等候**越狱！**阶段。
> **失败** 守卫发现了你藏匿囚犯的位置；队伍累计 1 AP。1 小时后，若囚犯在地面层被发现，他们会被押至关押牢房（区域 A12）；若在高级监狱被发现，则押至东翼（区域 B2）。
> **大失败** 同失败，但队伍累计 2 AP。

### 格拉戴斯卡堡垒（Citadel Gheradesca）

格拉戴斯卡堡垒矗立于寇兰廷以北的一座岛屿之上。岩床上的水位线显示潮汐升降在 10 至 15 英尺之间。**低潮时**，露出的潮坪允许人在两岸之间徒步通行。

虽然位于岛屿顶端的堡垒是要塞最显眼的部分，**真正的监狱却几乎位于海平面层**。仅有几码厚的石壁横亘在自由与永世囚禁之间。

一道 60 英尺宽的圆顶环绕岛屿，意在抑制其内的一切魔法，并防止跨域传送（counteract rank 7，counteract modifier +25；若成功，相关法术或魔法效果在圆顶之内被抑制）。一旦上岸（或对于飞行生物而言，在堡垒尖顶以下飞越岸线），魔法立即恢复。此圆顶亦延伸至海床之下，从**深层监狱**的下方开始。

### 漫长攀登（The Long Climb）

从装卸码头开始的小径——名为**漫长攀登**——在岛屿那臭名昭著的崖壁上凿出戏剧性的折返坡。当渗透真正开始时，它们便构成了第一道障碍。那些被迫攀此一行的囚犯，其对外部世界的最后记忆便被这艰苦的坡道与岩石攀爬所玷污。地狱骑士训练自己强逼囚犯走完此程，而他们自己却乐于这般挑战。

PC 们必须按以下顺序克服这些障碍以接入格拉戴斯卡堡垒。在本区尝试某一障碍耗时 10 分钟，因为这些段落代表通往堡垒入口的漫长山路。

**涨潮（Tide Coming In）**：渗透开始后 1 小时，因潮水上涨，这些障碍的 DC 提高 2。

RESCUING PRISONERS
Breaking the prisoners out of Citadel Gheradesca is the primary goal of the PCs. However, removing them from their cells isn't enough. The PCs must find a safe place for them to stay while the tide is still high before they can finally flee. The Secure Prisoner opportunity allows PCs to find an appropriate hiding spot. If the PCs release a prisoner from a cell without attempting the Secure Prisoner opportunity, the group automatically accrues 1 Awareness Point. Abilities or appropriate spells (such as liminal doorway) can result in an automatic success, at your discretion.

In the high prison, the only appropriate place for the prisoners is in the destroyed west wing. Due to the dangers from the roving undead and devils there, the DCs are 2 higher for prisoners rescued on that level.

SECURE PRISONER	OPPORTUNITY 10
Requirements The PCs locate a prisoner.
Ushering a prisoner to a secure location while waiting for the tides to recede requires finding a location away from the regular patrols of Citadel Gheradesca, which takes 10 minutes of carefully moving the prisoner. Attempt a DC 28 Deception, Diplomacy, Stealth, or Survival check.
Success You usher your prisoner to a safe holding location until the Jailbreak! phase.
Failure Guards discover your prisoner's hiding spot; the party accrues 1 AP. After 1 hour, the guards transport the prisoner to the holding cells (area A12) if they're discovered on the ground floor or to the east wing (area B2) if they're discovered in the high prison.
Critical Failure As failure, but the party accrues 2 AP.

Citadel Gheradesca
Citadel Gheradesca rises from an island north of Corentyn. Water lines on the bedrock show that the tides rise between 10 and 15 feet. At low tide, exposed tidal flats allow for an easy walk between shores. While the citadel at the top of the island is the most visible part of the fortress, the true prison lays nearly at sea level. Only a few yards of stone stand between freedom and endless imprisonment.

A dome 60 feet wide surrounds the island that attempts to suppress all magic within as well as preventing teleportation through it (counteract rank 7, counteract modifier +25; if successful, the spell or magical effect is suppressed while it's in the dome). Once on the shore (or over the shore, in the case of a flying creature below the tip of the citadel's spire), the magic returns immediately. This dome also continues under the sea floor, starting below the deep prison.

THE LONG CLIMB
The path from the loading dock, called the Long Climb, cuts dramatic switchbacks up the island's infamous cliffs. As the infiltration truly begins, they present the first obstacle. The prisoners forced to undertake this trek have their last memories of the outside world marred by its grueling slopes and rock scrambles. The Hellknights train to force their charges through the climb while they themselves relish the challenge.

The PCs must overcome these obstacles in the following order to access Citadel Gheradesca. Attempting an obstacle in this area takes 10 minutes, as these sections represent long stretches of the trail to the citadel's entrance.

Tide Coming In: One hour after the start of the infiltration, the DCs for these obstacles increase by 2 due to the rising tide.
