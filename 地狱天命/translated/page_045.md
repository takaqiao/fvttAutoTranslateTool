#### B2B. 1101 号牢房（Cell 1101）

**生物**：本牢房中关押的是**特尼尔·科彭**（Tenir Coppen），一名长期囚犯——他曾反抗奈多。尽管如此，他依然是**宗-库山**（Zon-Kuthon）的虔诚追随者。过去一年中他所承受的折磨般经历，反而**强化了他的信仰**。

不过，特尼尔在向 PC 们坦露身份这件事上**反应迟缓**。若 PC 们直呼其名，另一位名为**卡沃斯**（Kavos）的奈多囚犯（使用第 37 页的**斯戎特工**数据，但卡沃斯无装备）会立刻意识到这是个绝佳的逃脱机会，并谎称自己就是特尼尔。真正的特尼尔在偷听到这件事时只是耸肩——他认定：被基于身份混淆而困在这里，不过是宗-库山延展其折磨的又一种方式。

在与卡沃斯互动之后，PC 可尝试一次 DC 33 的察觉检定**察觉动机**（Sense Motive），意识到这位"获救盟友"有些不对劲。若 PC 们就身份、归属或信仰盘问卡沃斯，并通过一次 DC 33 的欺骗、威吓或宗教检定，他们便能识破他是冒充者。一旦察觉自己将被重新关押，卡沃斯会试图逃跑；若未在战斗 1 轮之内被制住或失能，他制造的喧哗会让 PC 们获得 1 个**警觉点**（Awareness Point）。若 PC 们在盘问检定上获得**大成功**，则他被他们彻底智压，干脆不再尝试逃跑。

若 PC 们相信他就是特尼尔，卡沃斯会随他们同行，甚至偶尔在战斗中提供协助。然而他是个**毫无原则的机会主义者**：若情境许可，他可能会在战斗中倒戈，**指望从地狱骑士那里换取特殊待遇**。

#### B2C. 1091 号牢房（Cell 1091）

第 9 行末端的这间空牢房中并无囚犯。其栏杆是**伪造**的，牢房墙面是一道**暗门**（DC 30 察觉检定方可发现）。地狱骑士们常借此通道在东翼与**副执棒官办公室**之间往来。

**宝藏**：秘密通道内藏有一只**IV 型空间袋**（type IV [spacious pouch]），袋中装有 100 pp。袋中夹一张纸条，上写："**贿赂这一手挺有想法**"（"Nice try with the bribe."）

### B3. 专家牢房（Specialist Cells）

本翼的牢房专为**先天能力会让他们逃脱**的囚犯所设。施法者被关在**抗御任何魔法**的牢房中，而内衬**精金**的牢房则用于关押格外强壮的囚犯。

走廊中**建有一道暗道**通往东区（DC 28 察觉检定方可注意）。**若 PC 们发现了 B2 区两处暗道（B2c 和 B3 走廊），他们在东翼"安置囚犯"机遇的检定上获得 +2 环境加值**（详见第 34 页）。

#### B3A. 4002 号牢房（Cell 4002）

**生物**：4002 号是**雅泽奇**（Jazechi）的牢房，因其能**钻过最狭窄的缝隙**而被关于此。雅泽奇被首次带入格拉戴斯卡堡垒时，曾被关在西翼。直到他被发现正悄悄向楼梯潜行，看守们才意识到——他在自己的牢房里**放了一个分身**。

雅泽奇可以轻易制造此类**分身**；在"安置囚犯"机遇上获得成功后，PC 们另外获得 **1 个优势点**（Edge Point），可用于再次进行"安置囚犯"尝试。

B2B. CELL 1101
Creatures: In this cell is Tenir Coppen, a long-term prisoner who rebelled against Nidal. Despite this, he remains a devout follower of Zon-Kuthon. The torturous experience he's endured in the passing year has only strengthened his faith.

However, Tenir is slow to identify himself to the PCs. If the PCs call his name, another Nidalese prisoner named Kavos (use the Thrune Agent statistics on page 37, though Kavos has no equipment) realizes this is a perfect opportunity to escape and claims to be Tenir. The real Tenir shrugs at overhearing this, assuming that being trapped here based on mistaken identity is just Zon-Kuthon's way of extending his torment.

After interacting with Kavos, a PC can attempt a DC 33 Perception check to Sense Motive and realize something about their rescued ally isn't right. If the PCs quiz Kavos about his identity, affiliation, or faith with a successful DC 33 Deception, Intimidation, or Religion check, they realize he's an impostor. Seeing he's at risk of being imprisoned again, Kavos tries to run away, and if not restrained or incapacitated within 1 round of combat, he makes enough noise that the PCs earn an Awareness Point. If the PCs critically succeed at their check to quiz Kavos, he's so outwitted by them that he doesn't try to escape.

In the event they believe he's Tenir, Kavos accompanies the PCs and might even assist in combat periodically. However, he's an unscrupulous opportunist, and if the circumstances are right, he might turn on them during a fight, hoping to earn special treatment from the Hellknights.

B2C. CELL 1091
The empty cell at the end of row 9 holds no prisoner. It has false bars, and the cell wall is a secret door (DC 30 Perception check to spot it). Hellknights commonly use this passage to move between the east wing and the paralictor's office.

Treasure: Hidden within the secret passage is a spacious pouch (type IV) that contains 100 pp. A scrap of paper inside it reads, "Nice try with the bribe."

B3. SPECIALIST CELLS
This wing's cells are designed for prisoners whose innate abilities would allow them to escape. Spellcasters are kept in cells that are warded against any magic, while adamantine-lined cells hold particularly strong prisoners.

A secret passage built into the hallway leads to the east block (DC 28 Perception check to notice). If the PCs discover both secret passages out of area B2, they gain a +2 circumstance bonus to checks for the Secure Prisoner opportunity (page 34) in the east wing.

B3A. CELL 4002
Creatures: Cell 4002 is Jazechi's cell, where he's kept due to his ability to slip through the narrowest spaces. When he was first brought into Citadel Gheradesca, he was kept in the west wing. It wasn't until he was found slinking toward the stairs that they realized he'd placed a double in his cell.

Jazechi can easily create these doubles; succeeding at the Secure Prisoner opportunity (page 34) grants the PCs 1 Edge Point for an additional Secure Prisoner attempt.

B4. HELLKNIGHT OFFICES
The side rooms that jut from this hallway are multipurpose spaces to be used as needed. Currently, they hold prisoners relocated from the west wing.
