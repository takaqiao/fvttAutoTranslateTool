## Page 24 — Ch1 任务三：无人无辜（续）/ 寻找塞贝尼奥、乌贝诺、埃默汀

——锁链骑士团（of the Chain）在此种时局下提供指引，尤其是那句"**无人无辜**"（none are innocent）。这句话传递的意思是：每个人都可能堕入无法无天——而乌罗·阿登因其堕落该当处死。

卡蒂利娅请求 PC 们把其他地狱骑士召集到仓库，她将主持一场会议，决定锁链骑士团是否与其他派系联合。

---

### 异端魁首的口袋（The Archheathen's Pocket）

塞贝尼奥·杜·托林躲藏在高贵区异端魁首的卫戍所内。要塞城墙抵御了最显而易见的攻击方式。卫戍所与公共行政办公室之间相连的地下通道是其最薄弱之处，尤其是通向贵族区的那些通道入口。

PC 们可以亲自接近塞贝尼奥，也可派信使联系。若 PC 们想亲自前往，他们必须通过 DC 27 的社会或生存检定，或 DC 29 的察觉检定，在贵族区找到一处入口。若"宵禁与出行限制"法令仍在生效，进入贵族区受限，PC 们需要成功的**抗拒**或**闪避**方可入内。或者，若 PC 们拥有可绕过那道 10 英尺厚、30 英尺高石墙的能力，他们可经由那条路径进入卫戍所。

进入之后，PC 可通过成功的 DC 25 地狱骑士学识或 DC 27 交涉检定，为请求塞贝尼奥出席聚会提出陈情。透露**是卡蒂利娅派他们来的**会因她的关系而授予该检定 +2 状况加值。塞贝尼奥目睹的任何公开违法或混乱行径，会对该检定造成 -2 状况减值。检定成功时，他会出席聚会。失败时，他会厌恶地把他们赶出卫戍所，并向卡蒂利娅发去一封措辞严厉的信件，指责她游走于异端边缘。

若 PC 们改为发送信息，他们可以使用合适的法术或能力（例如**梦讯术**，dream message），或试图说服一位工人替他们捎信。要找到一位友善的工人，PC 需通过 DC 27 的察觉检定**察觉动机**，再通过 DC 29 的交涉检定**请求**对方送信。或者，PC 可贿赂一位漠不关心的工人替他送信。典型贿赂金额为 100 gp，但 PC 若在贿赂中通过 DC 29 的交涉检定，可将之降至 50 gp（重击成功时降至 25 gp）。若讯息送达塞贝尼奥，他会出席聚会。

### 寻找乌贝诺·埃诺斯（Finding Ubenor Enos）

自地狱骑士团分裂以来，卡蒂利娅就未再得到乌贝诺·埃诺斯的消息。她提供一段简短描述：一名身材矮壮、肩膀宽阔的人类男性，留着短红发，穿着以**晨曦银**（dawnsilver）打造的地狱骑士铠装。

这描述已经过时——乌贝诺现在已蓄发至肩。然而他仍穿着那套独特铠装，因此 PC 通过成功的 DC 29 交涉检定**搜集情报**（Gather Information），或 DC 27 的制作或社会检定，可得知有一名穿着晨曦银地狱骑士铠装的红发男子正在**船坞区**（Ship Yard）当工人。**抗拒**多塔里的 PC 在该检定上获得 +2 状况加值，因为他们的胆识让愿意发声的市民对他们刮目相看。或者，PC 通过成功施放**窥探术**（scrying）或类似法术，可揭示他在某个码头上工作（乌贝诺的影响力 stat block 在第 26 页，含他的察觉与意志豁免修正值，如有需要可查阅）。

在船坞区找到乌贝诺并不困难，因为他即便在修船时也固执地穿着铠装。他的回应少有超过单字或手势的。PC 通过成功的 DC 27 医疗检定，或 DC 29 的察觉检定**察觉动机**，可判断出他正经历悲痛与抑郁——不仅是因为他阴郁的情绪，还有他作为受训追踪者与骑士本应有的反应速度变得迟缓。通过成功的 DC 25 制作检定，PC 可注意到他的晨曦银铠装因疏于保养而渐渐失去光泽。

乌贝诺对任何会议都心存怀疑，粗声打发"没什么用"的念头，随即回到工作中去。若 PC 们再三催问，他会透露自己认为锁链骑士团**已彻底解散**。要说服他，PC 必须通过成功的 DC 25 地狱骑士学识或 DC 29 交涉检定提供有力论据，论证该骑士团仍在运作。或者，PC 可通过成功的 DC 27 威吓或医疗检定暂时唤起他从抑郁中振作。PC 也可单纯花钱占用他的时间（100 gp）以补偿他因此错过的工作。

### 埃默汀的怒火（Emertine's Fury）

锁链骑士团分裂之时，埃默汀·欧恩转而独立执法。她不像卡蒂利娅那样作为义警行动，而是**陪同多塔里**一起巡查。她坐骑的鞍甲上仍带有黑红相间的**九芒星**（Ennead Star）与该团的"**缠链之手**"标志，鞍上挂着的镣铐每走一步便叮当作响。

埃默汀更可能主动找到 PC 们，而非反过来；她已听闻他们的行迹。若 PC 们已与塞贝尼奥或乌贝诺中任一人有过接触，她会在 PC 们下次穿过寇兰廷街道时主动寻上门。虽然他们一开始可能以为她要逮捕他们，但她真正关注的是**卡蒂利娅的所作所为**。埃默汀几乎压不住心头怒火，指责卡蒂利娅与混乱勾结、堕为无法无天的义警，毁掉了锁链骑士团仅存的一点骄傲。埃默汀警告：若 PC 们继续……（接下页）

THE ARCHHEATHEN'S POCKET
Sebenio du Tollin is sequestered in the archheathen's garrison in the High Quarter. The fortress walls protect it against the most obvious methods of attack. The underground tunnels that lead between the garrison and civil administrative offices are the weakest point, especially the tunnel entrances in the Noble Quarter.

The PCs can reach Sebenio themselves or via a messenger. If the PCs wish to reach him, they must find an entrance in the Noble Quarter with a successful DC 27 Society or Survival check or a DC 29 Perception check. If the Curfew and Limitation on Travel decree is in effect, travel to the Noble Quarter is restricted, and PCs need to successfully Defy or Evade to enter the neighborhood. Alternatively, if the PCs have abilities that can bypass the 10-foot-wide, 30-foot-tall stone walls, they can enter the garrison via that route.

Once inside, a PC can plead their case for Sebenio to attend the meeting with a successful DC 25 Hellknight Lore or DC 27 Diplomacy check. Revealing that Cathilia sent them grants a +2 circumstance bonus to this check due to her association. Open acts of lawlessness or chaos that Sebenio witnesses inflict a –2 circumstance penalty to this check. On a successful check, he attends the meeting. On a failure, he turns them out of the garrison in disgust and sends Cathilia a strongly worded letter about skirting with heresy.

If the PCs instead wish to send a message, they can either use an appropriate spell or ability (such as dream message) or attempt to convince a worker to bring a message for them. Finding a friendly worker requires a PC to succeed at a DC 27 Perception check to Sense Motive, then succeed at a DC 29 Diplomacy check to Request they deliver the message. Alternatively, a PC can bribe an indifferent worker to deliver such a message. The typical cost for this bribe is 100 gp, though a PC who succeeds at a DC 29 Diplomacy check as part of the bribe reduces it to 50 gp (or 25 gp on a critical success). If the message reaches Sebenio, he attends the meeting.

FINDING UBENOR ENOS
Cathilia hasn't heard from Ubenor Enos since the Hellknight orders split. She provides a brief description: a short and broad-shouldered man, human, short red hair, and Hellknight armor made from dawnsilver.

This description is out of date, as Ubenor allowed his hair to grow to shoulder length. However, he still wears his distinctive armor such that a PC who succeeds at a DC 29 Diplomacy check to Gather Information or a DC 27 Crafting or Society check learns of a red-haired man wearing dawnsilver Hellknight armor working as a laborer in the Ship Yard. PCs that are Defying the dottari gain a +2 circumstance bonus on this check, as their brashness impresses the citizens that are willing to speak up. Alternatively, a PC who successfully casts scrying or a similar spell reveals that he works on a pier (Ubenor's influence stat block on page 26 includes his Perception and Will save modifiers, if needed).

Finding Ubenor at the Ship Yard is simple as he stubbornly wears his armor even while repairing ships. His responses rarely involve more than single words or gestures. With a successful DC 27 Medicine check or a DC 29 Perception check to Sense Motive, a PC determines that he is experiencing grief and depression—not just by his dour mood but also his slow response to stimuli compared to what might be expected of a trained tracker or knight. With a successful DC 25 Crafting check, a PC notes that his dawnsilver armor is slowly tarnishing due to a lack of care.

Ubenor is skeptical of any meeting, gruffly brushing off the idea as being "no use," before returning to his work. If pressed, he shares that he thinks that the Order of the Chain is fully disbanded. To convince him, a PC must provide a strong argument that the Order is still functional with a successful DC 25 Hellknight Lore or DC 29 Diplomacy check. Alternatively, a PC can temporarily rouse him from his depression with a successful DC 27 Intimidation or Medicine check. A PC can also just pay for his time (100 gp) to cover his missing out on work.

EMERTINE'S FURY
When the Order of the Chain splintered, Emertine Oughn turned to imposing the law independently. Rather than work as a vigilante like Cathilia, she accompanies the dottari. Her horse's barding still bears the black-and-red Ennead Star and the order's chain-wrapped hand, and the manacles hanging from her saddle jangle with every step.

Emertine is more likely to find the PCs than they are to find her; she's heard of their actions. If the PCs have interacted with either Sebenio or Ubenor, she seeks the PCs out the next time they move through Corentyn's streets. While they might first assume that she aims to arrest them, her interest is in Cathilia's actions. Emertine barely holds back her seething fury at Cathilia, accusing her of ruining what little pride the Order of the Chain still had by consorting with chaos and becoming a lawless vigilante.
