### 投入混乱（Into Anarchy）

若玩家角色们答应调查佩札克及其与刺杀吉莉娅·拜尼卢斯之间的关联，金塔戈的宫廷法师将赠予他们一套**螺旋钟**（spiral chimes；《探索者：元素之怒》76）、价值 1,000 gp 的仪式材料，以及一份描绘极为详尽的地图与油画素描本——所绘是距佩札克约 25 英里外一片富藏炼金材料的森林。这两件信物的结合，足以让英雄们运用新学的仪式跨越这段距离。英雄们或会想到改走海路前往佩札克，但应当告诫他们：此行可能多耗数日。

无论玩家角色们如何前往，他们都只能通过两条通道接近佩札克——从森林下行的山口，或经由海路。寻常状况下，这两条路径都会被重兵防御或海军封锁堵死。然而，玩家角色们发现**肥港**（Fat Harbor）没有任何悬挂切利亚斯旗帜的船只停泊，**山门**（Mountaingate）三道闸门与塔楼也都空荡无人。

**调查点（Investigation Points）**：英雄们尚不知晓，他们的调查正与时间赛跑。暗杀事件背后真正的元凶尚未察觉玩家角色们已入场，但时移势易这种状况可能改变。玩家角色们在佩札克完成调查所花的时间，加上奥奇阿斯提斯是否仍存活，将决定这位幕后凶手在玩家角色们抵达其巢穴时已做了多少准备。这由**调查点**来表示，自现在起一直追踪至玩家角色们抵达**闪光岩堡**（Glittercrag Keep）为止。这些点数将影响堡垒中**枭鬼**守军的警觉与备战程度（第 98 页）。如果玩家角色们在拉沃努尔击杀了奥奇阿斯提斯，他们以 2 调查点起算。若玩家角色们在佩札克调查期间任何时候过夜休整，则会扣除 1 调查点。

### Into Anarchy

If the PCs agree to investigate Pezzack and its connection to the assassination attempt on Jilia Bainilus, Kintargo's court wizard gifts them a set of spiral chimes (Pathfinder Rage of Elements 76), ritual materials worth 1,000 gp, as well as an extremely detailed map and oil-pastel sketchbook of a reagent-rich forest about 25 miles away from Pezzack. These two elements combined are enough for the heroes to use their new rituals to traverse the distance. The heroes might think to take a ship to Pezzack instead of using teleportation magic, but they should be warned the journey could take several days longer.

No matter how the PCs travel, they can approach Pezzack only via two access points—down a mountain pass from the forest or by sea. Normally, both of these routes would be blocked, either by heavy fortifications or by naval blockade. However, the PCs find no ships bearing Cheliax's flag docked at Fat Harbor, and the triple-portcullises and towers of Mountaingate stand empty and unmanned.

**Investigation Points**: The heroes are unaware their inquiry is on a time crunch. The true culprit behind the assassination attempt isn't currently aware that the PCs are on the case, but as time goes on, this can change. The amount of time it takes the PCs to complete their investigation in Pezzack, as well as the possible survival of the ochiastis, will determine how prepared this villain is when the PCs arrive at her lair. This is represented by Investigation Points, which you should track from now until the PCs reach Glittercrag Keep. These points affect the wariness and readiness of the strix defenders at the keep (page 98). If the PCs killed the ochiastis in Ravounel, they begin with 2 Investigation Points. If the PCs rest overnight at any point during their investigation in Pezzack, they lose 1 Investigation Point.

### A ROLL OF THE DICE	LOW 14

When the PCs arrive in the town, they interrupt a group of gamblers when they round their first corner (or when they make landfall on the docks). This disheveled quartet is made up of dirty, drunk, and disorderly types who would rather fight first and ask questions later! This encounter can use any city map, such as Pathfinder Flip-Mat Classics: Red Light District, but you can also reuse the maps on page 72.

**Creatures**: Four high rollers attack the PCs, though arguing among themselves as to why. Some think it's because the PCs are "spies for Ravounel," and others are doing it just because the PCs interrupted their drunken gambling. The high rollers retreat when reduced to fewer than 50 HP. When only one combatant is left, they surrender or make an unsuccessful attempt at fleeing, promising to tell the PCs anything they want to know in exchange for mercy.
