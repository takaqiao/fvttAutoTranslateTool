### 地狱契约

玛蕾希娅被击败之后——玩家角色便能透过此典藏中所存的契约——接触到海量的历史资讯。然而——阅读一份契约——并不像听起来那么简单。首先——玩家角色必须确定哪一份或哪几份契约——载有他们所需的、关于该仪式的资讯。要做到此点——需要一次成功的 DC 38 **图书馆学识**检定——或 DC 40 **奥秘**、**宗教**或**社交**检定。

他们应当首先查阅斯戎家族最初与阿斯摩蒂斯所签、藉以获取力量的那份原始契约。这是典藏中最古老的契约——也变得最为乖戾难驯。要从其格栏中诱出此契约——需要一次成功的 DC 40 **交涉**或**求生**检定——之后再一次成功的 DC 38 **体操**或**运动**检定——抓住契约并将其压制足够长时间以阅读它。每一次约束契约失败——玩家角色都会从纸张割伤中承受 5d6 挥砍伤害。这份契约自然以**魔鬼语**写就——但若玩家角色花一小时通读——便能理解其大意：只要切利亚斯之民众接受阿斯摩蒂斯信仰为他们唯一的宗教——斯戎家族便能援引地狱之力——以维系对该国的政治掌控——基本等同于诅咒了民众的灵魂。这些力量的确切性质——以及斯戎家族被允许如何使用它们——并不明确——但其中数条附则注明：若一位统治者欲讨价还价以换取更大的权力——他们必须另立一份单独的契约。

这一认知应引导玩家角色——去寻找阿波罗盖最近的一份契约——这需要再一次成功的 DC 36 **图书馆学识**检定——或 DC 38 **奥秘**、**宗教**或**社交**检定。该契约非常新——尚未活化——所以抓取它易如反掌——但它已被一道**邪魔符印**所守护——需要一次成功的 DC 36 **知觉**检定方能发现——以及一次成功的 DC 40 **奥秘**、**宗教**或**盗术**检定方能解除。若一名玩家角色未解除符印便去抓取契约——他承受 8d6 火焰伤害与 8d6 心智伤害（DC 36 基础**意志**豁免）——他的心智将名副其实地被点燃。

阅读此契约——证实了阿波罗盖的意图：她要以塔尔曼铎为代价的一部分——无论是被压制的活体天界使、抑或是其陨落后被困住的本源——以此换取将内海地区跌入地狱、并由她独自掌控为新晋大魔王的力量。尽管阿斯摩蒂斯手握神之伟力——施行此种变易——绝非瞬息可成。为履行契约——阿波罗盖必须前往**内瑟斯**的阿斯摩蒂斯领域——参与一场为期数周的仪式——其中包含缓慢而痛苦地汲取塔尔曼铎之本源。事毕——埃戈里安将首先被变易——其效应将自该处向外辐射开来——绵延数周。

不过——此契约确实留出了一丝希望——仪式可被打断。强攻内瑟斯的阿斯摩蒂斯领域以打击阿波罗盖二世——即使是高等级英雄也几无可能办到。话虽如此——她的仪式仰仗**尼萨利**戈索克莱克作为副施法者。他已退守至地狱第一层**阿弗纳斯**的「**铁铸要塞**」之中。击败他可能扰乱仪式——为地狱契约引入毁灭性的矛盾——并为被囚的塔尔曼铎提供一线脱离地狱掌控的良机。

不幸的是——在玩家角色尚未及策划前往阿弗纳斯的行动之前——阿波罗盖仪式的第一阶段已然完成——将皇宫吞入一处**地狱之口**——并昭示着切利亚斯之变易已经开端——而首当其冲的——便是埃戈里安！

The Infernal Contracts

With Mareshiah defeated, the PCs have access to a
wealth of historical information through the contracts
stored in the archive. However, reading a contract isn't
as easy as it sounds. First, the PCs must determine
which contract or contracts contain the information
they require about the ritual. Doing so requires a
successful DC 38 Library Lore or DC 40 Arcana,
Religion, or Society check.

They should first consult the original contract House
Thrune signed with Asmodeus to gain power. This
contract is the oldest in the archive and has grown
the orneriest. Coaxing it from its cubbyhole requires a
successful DC 40 Diplomacy or Survival check, followed
by a successful DC 38 Acrobatics or Athletics check
to grab the contract and hold it in place long enough
to read it. On each failure to restrain the contract, a
PC takes 5d6 slashing damage from paper cuts. This
contract is, of course, written in Diabolic, but if the PCs
take an hour to read through it, they understand the
general gist: House Thrune can call upon the powers
of Hell to retain political control over Cheliax, as long
as the country's people accept the Asmodean faith as
their sole religion, essentially damning their souls. The
exact nature of these powers and how House Thrune is
allowed to use them is unclear, but several codicils note
that should a ruler wish to bargain for more power, they
must establish a separate contract.

This knowledge should lead the PCs to look for
Abrogail's most recent contract, requiring another
successful DC 36 Library Lore or DC 38 Arcana,
Religion, or Society check. That contract is very young
and hasn't animated yet, so while it's a trivial matter
to grab it, it's been warded with a fiendish symbol that
requires a successful DC 36 Perception check to spot
and a successful DC 40 Arcana, Religion, or Thievery
to disarm. If a PC grabs the contract without disarming
the symbol, they take 8d6 fire damage and 8d6 mental
damage (DC 36 basic Will save) as their mind is literally
set aflame.

Reading this contract confirms that Abrogail intends to
use Talmandor—either the subdued celestial if he's alive
or his trapped essence if he died—as partial payment for
the power to plunge the Inner Sea region into Hell with
her in sole control as a new archdevil. Despite the divine
might Asmodeus wields, enacting this transformation
isn't instantaneous. To fulfill the contract, Abrogail had
to travel to Asmodeus's realm in Nessus to participate
in a weeks-long ritual that involves slowly and painfully
extracting Talmandor's essence. Afterward, Egorian will
be transformed first, with the effect radiating outward
from there over the following weeks.

The contract does provide a glimmer of hope, though:
the ritual can be disrupted. Assaulting Asmodeus's
domain in Nessus to strike at Abrogail II would be
virtually impossible, even for high-level heroes. That
said, her ritual relies on the nessari Gorthoklek as a
secondary caster. He's withdrawn to his Iron Fortress
in Avernus, Hell's first layer. His defeat could disrupt
the ritual, introduce devastating contradictions into the
infernal contract, and provide a captured Talmandor
the opportunity to escape Hell's clutches.

Unfortunately, before the PCs can start to make
plans for traveling to Avernus, the first phase of
Abrogail's ritual is completed, plunging the Imperial
Palace into a hellmouth and heralding the beginning of
the transformation of Cheliax, starting with Egorian!
