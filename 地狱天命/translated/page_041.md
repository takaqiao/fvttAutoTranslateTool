## Page 41 — Ch2 A10 宝藏 + A11 转运走廊 + A12 关押牢房（艾欧妮亚）+ A13 临海面

**宝藏**：能打开本层各门的一串钥匙保存在两张上锁办公桌之一中（DC 30 撬锁）。另一张桌内存放着本层的紧急医疗用品：一只卷轴管含 1 卷**清心术**（clear mind，6 环级）、2 卷**强身术**（sound body，6 环级）、2 卷**稳足术**（sure footing，6 环级）。一只帆布袋内有 4 瓶**高级治愈药水**；打开袋子还会露出一枚刺绣的**锁链骑士团徽章**。一根**大型治疗法杖**别扭地塞进了桌子那只窄而立起的抽屉里。

#### A11. 转运走廊（Transport Hallway）

> 这条形状别扭的走廊在拐角与转弯处都保持空畅，以便把大型囚犯转送至合适大小的关押牢房。一条狭窄的通道通向建筑后部的双人牢房。

**巡逻**：本走廊极少使用，因此地狱骑士对其巡逻不甚规律。若 PC 们在此区累计的警觉点会引入新的复杂情况或状态（第 33 页），则尝试 DC 5 平凡检定。失败时，新效果将延迟到 PC 们下一次累计警觉点时再触发。

#### A12. 关押牢房（Holding Cells）

> 大型牢房为关押其中的俘虏提供基本所需。铺盖卷与便壶整齐地沿着空闲牢房的墙摆放。

本牢区设计用于囚犯**临时关押**，直至他们最终被分派至常驻牢房。

**囚犯**：在**手术刀夫人**逃脱与西翼毁灭之后，这些关押牢房中现有 20 名从高级监狱转送来的囚犯。**艾欧妮亚·萨里尼**与另外三名女子共关在一间满员牢房之中。多年来在高级监狱的迷乱与折磨已让她**对自己心智之外的世界毫无兴趣**。她不断哼着自己曾听过的金塔格歌剧中的歌谣。

PC 可通过**跟唱**（表演 DC 26）、**引用著名歌词**（社会 DC 28），或**治疗她饱受折磨的心智**（医疗 DC 30）吸引她的注意。成功时，她变得对 PC 们警觉并愿意与他们交谈；而使用**音乐或戏剧表演中的引用**的 PC，在与艾欧妮亚的所有互动检定（包括"安置囚犯"机遇的检定，第 34 页）上获得 +4 状况加值。

#### A13. 临海面（Face of the Sea）

> 石壁向空中敞开，把海鸟的鸣叫与咸味带入死寂的监狱。岛屿陡然下落，留下数百英尺的悬崖直坠下方海面。苔藓在边缘生长。屋顶滴落的凝结水珠为它的生长提供了淡水。

若有生物伏在边缘向岛屿基岩望去，他们会看见**深层监狱**（deep prison）的通风管道。PC 通过成功的 DC 30 制作或自然检定可识别出那些通风管道**是人工凿入岛屿的**，并非天然形成。下落至通风管的高度为 **300 英尺**，其开口直径为 **4 英尺**。

Treasure: The ring of keys that opens the doors on this floor is kept in one of the two locked desks (DC 30 to Pick the Lock). The other desk contains the emergency healing supplies for this floor. A scroll tube includes one scroll of clear mind (6th rank), two scrolls of sound body (6th rank), and two scrolls of sure footing (6th rank). A canvas bag holds four greater healing potions; opening the bag also reveals an embroidered sigil of the Order of the Chain. A major staff of healing has been lodged awkwardly in the desk's narrow upright drawer.

A11. TRANSPORT HALLWAY
The corners and turns of this awkwardly shaped hallway remain clear so that Large-sized prisoners can be moved into appropriately sized holding cells. A narrow access hallway leads to double occupancy cells in the back of the building.

Patrol: This hallway is rarely used and thus irregularly patrolled by the Hellknights. If the PCs earn Awareness Points here that would introduce a new complication or condition (page 33), attempt a DC 5 flat check. On a failure, the new effect is delayed until the next time the PCs earn an Awareness Point.

A12. HOLDING CELLS
Large cells provide basic needs for captives imprisoned within. Bedrolls and chamber pots are lined neatly against the wall of the unoccupied cells.

This cell block is designed for the temporary holding of prisoners before their final assignment to a permanent cell.

Prisoners: The holding cells contain 20 prisoners transferred from the high dungeon after Lady Scalpel's escape and destruction of the west wing. Aeonia Sarini is held in a full cell with three other women. The years spent in the disorienting and torturous conditions of the high prison have left her uninterested in the world outside her mind. She constantly hums songs from the Kintargan operas she once attended.

A PC can get her attention by singing along (Performance DC 26), quoting famous lyrics (Society DC 28), or treating her tormented mind (Medicine DC 30). On a success, she becomes alert to the PCs and willing to converse with them, though a PC who uses quotes from music or theater performances receives a +4 circumstance bonus to any checks when interacting with Aeonia, including checks for the Secure Prisoner opportunity (page 34).

A13. FACE OF THE SEA
Stone walls open to the air, bringing the squawking of sea birds and the salt tang into the stale prison. The island drops away, leaving a several-hundred-foot drop into the ocean below. Moss grows on the edge. Condensation drips from the ceiling and provides fresh water for it to grow.

If a creature leans over the edge and looks to the island's bedrock, they see the ventilation tubes for the deep prison. A PC who succeeds at a DC 30 Crafting or Nature check identifies that those ventilation tubes were carved into the island and aren't naturally occurring. The drop to the ventilation tubes is 300 feet, and their openings are 4 feet in diameter.
