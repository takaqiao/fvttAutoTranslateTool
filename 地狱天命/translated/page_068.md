#### E. 地狱矛号（The Hellspear）　**低 12**

**80 年前纳罗纳被洗劫之时**——**一次魔法攻击击沉了地狱矛号**——**自此飘流至此处**。

#### 遗产号（The Legacy）　**轻微 12**

**遗产号**原是**纳罗纳小型舰队的旗舰**。**该城所不知的是**——**斯戎家族的忠诚者已渗透其船员**。**攻击之夜**——**他们倒戈反向其船友**——**屠杀所有船员**——**并让港口在毫无抵抗的情况下对斯戎家族的船只敞开**。

**危害**：**这场背叛在船上留下了精神印记**——**形成一个作祟**。**当 PC 们登船时**——**他们会看到自己的盟友化作斯戎家族杀人间谍的幻象**。

#### F. 被遗忘之泪之井（The Well of Forgotten Tears）　**严峻 12**

> **该城的街道在此打开**——**形成一处宽阔的空地**——**环绕一座三层金字塔楼**。**辉光流水自金字塔楼的台阶上以涓涓瀑布之姿流下**——**汇入四条河道**——**将广场切作四份**。**四座颓圮的石桥**——**饰有变异类人生物的不安图像**——**跨越于河道之上**——**允许人从一象限通往另一象限**。
>
> **西南象限**——**一道泛红金属门户**（**门尚封闭**）**立于一处升起台座之上**。**东北象限**——**一道一模一样的门户敞开两门**——**门内是一道旋转的黄色能量传送门**。

**这座金字塔楼曾是任何比纳罗纳更早的残忍文明的中心建筑**。**金字塔楼之顶**——**有一块 30 英尺宽的水平圆盘**——**盖住一道通向冥河的巨大传送门**。**远古之时**——**该城居民定期解封此传送门**——**让河道充满冥河之水**——**这样**——**居民们便可畅饮**——**以遗忘自己的罪愆**。

**地形**：**只有穿越过纳罗纳全部 14 道门户的生物**——**才能完全解封该金字塔楼传送门**——**释放冥河的全部威能**。**尽管如此**——**奥拉克斯过去数周亲自穿越门户的同时**——**已设法将该传送门撬开了近 10 英尺**——**让本区域的河道充满冥河之水**（第 62 页）。

**空地周围的建筑大小不一**——**但一般高 20 至 30 英尺**。**多数情况下**——**这些建筑的上层与屋顶都已腐烂**——**因此只有底层尚可使用**。

**目标与事件**：**为赢得这场战斗**——**PC 们必须完成两个目标**。**首先**——**他们必须杀死奥拉克斯**。**其次**——**他们必须占据两道泛红门户的位置**——**直到每道门户都被永久解除**（**需要一次 DC 35 奥术、神秘或巧手检定**；**详见第……**页的危害）

E. THE HELLSPEAR	LOW 12
The blackened hull of a rotting ship lies wedged between two crumbling buildings on this street. A fire-scorched hole pierces the hull, and rusted siege equipment rests on the deck.

A magical attack sank the Hellspear during Narona's sacking 80 years ago, and it has since drifted here.

Creatures: Although most of the crew perished when the ship sank, an animated trebuchet survived the attack. The mindless construct ignores targets wearing Chelaxian military uniforms but attacks all other creatures who draw near.

THE LEGACY	TRIVIAL 12
The Legacy was originally the flagship of Narona's small fleet. Unbeknownst to the city, Thrune loyalists infiltrated its crew. On the night of the attack, they turned on their crewmates, slaughtering them all and leaving the harbor open for Thrune ships to arrive unopposed.

Hazards: The scars of this betrayal have left psychic impressions on the ship, creating a haunt. When the PCs board the ship, they receive visions of their allies transforming into Thrune spies out for blood.

The streets of the city open into a wide clearing surrounding a three-tiered ziggurat. Glowing water streams in trickling waterfalls down the steps of the ziggurat into four river channels that cut the town square into quarters. Four crumbling stone bridges, engraved with disturbing images of mutated humanoids, arch over the channels and allow passage from one quadrant to the next.

A doorway of red-tinged metal, its doors still sealed shut, stands on a raised dais in the southwest quadrant of the clearing. In the northeast quadrant is an identical doorway with its doors thrown open, revealing a swirling portal of yellow energy.

This ziggurat was a central fixture of whatever cruel civilization preceded Narona. Atop the ziggurat is a 30-foot-wide horizontal disc that covers a massive portal to the River Styx. In ancient times, the city's denizens would periodically unseal the portal, filling the channels with water from the Styx so that its citizens could drink deeply and forget their sins.
