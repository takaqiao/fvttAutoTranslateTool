若 PC 们有 **11–15 警觉点**，他在 PC 们出现时**会犹豫**。他的手悬在长剑之上，警惕地观察着他们。PC 通过成功的 DC 30 交涉或威吓检定可说服他对话——他便会向 PC 们提出与上述相同的问题。

If the PCs have 11–15 Awareness Points, he hesitates at the appearance of the PCs. While his hand hovers over his longsword, he watches them warily. With a successful DC 30 Diplomacy or Intimidation check, a PC can convince him to talk, and he offers them the same question as above.

If the PCs have 16 or more Awareness Points, their chaos precedes them. Uro attacks immediately.

If Uro attacks, his Hell-bound guard is summoned to the fight. This creature is an elite warden devil who fights until Uro is destroyed. When pushed into attacking, he follows his Hellknight training: attack the weakest person he can and fight to the bitter end.

Convincing Uro: Convincing the lictor to abandon the Order of the Manacle involves the PCs attempting several checks to earn Victory Points (GM Core 184). The PCs begin with 3 VP and successfully convince the lictor if their total reaches 6 VP. If their Victory Point total reaches 0 or the PCs argue that all Hellknights are fundamentally flawed or unworthy of being saved, Uro dismisses their arguments and attacks the PCs. Likely skills and their DCs include Hellknight Lore (DC 23), Diplomacy (DC 28), and Intimidation (DC 28).
