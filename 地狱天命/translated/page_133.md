### D. 殒命地狱（HELL OF A WAY TO GO）　低 16

当斯戎家族希望开启**燃狱之门**时，激活手段有诸多。最快速的解封手段，是**人祭**。鉴于自地狱火危机（Hellfire Crisis）伊始以来，各地狱骑士团中拒绝向斯戎家族屈膝的成员被俘人数众多，主持此操作的切利亚斯将军们认定**人选并不缺**。

为剥夺切利亚斯人这些祭品，也为解救那些首要忠于法纪而非斯戎家族的地狱骑士，玩家角色必须救出囚徒——潜入他们被关押的草地。一支象征性的切利亚斯步兵守卫该地，另一些则巡视沉重的囚笼。在忒尔齐纳引导下，玩家角色的部队会引开切利亚斯步兵主力，让玩家角色得以直接对付囚禁守卫。

每只囚笼以一把简单的锁锁固。或者，可以用两次连续 DC 30 运动检定**强行开启**砸开。每开两只囚笼，被救出的囚徒便群起反扑捕获他们的人，给予玩家角色对攻击骰与意志豁免 +1 状态加值的累积加值（最多 +3）。

D. HELL OF A WAY TO GO　LOW 16

There are a myriad of ways to activate the Inferno Gate when House Thrune wishes it to open. The quickest way to unlock some of its seals is by human sacrifice. Given the number of Hellknights from various orders who refused to bend the knee to House Thrune who have been captured since the start of the Hellfire Crisis, the Chelaxian generals in charge of this operation decided that they were spoiled for choice.

To deny Cheliax these sacrificial victims, and also to free Hellknights whose first duty is to law and order rather than House Thrune, the PCs must rescue the prisoners by infiltrating the grassy field where they're being kept. A token force of Chelaxian infantry around the area guard these prisoners, and a few others patrol the heavy cages where they're being kept. Under Telzinah's guidance, the PCs' troops draw away the Chelaxian infantry force, allowing the PCs to face the prison guards directly.

Each cage is secured with a simple lock. Alternatively, a cage can be broken open with two consecutive DC 30 Athletics checks to Force Open. For every two cages the PCs open, the freed prisoners gang up on their former captors, giving the PCs a cumulative +1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls and Will saves (maximum +3).
