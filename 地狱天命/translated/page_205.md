## Page 205 — 战役之外 · 寇兰廷独立 + 其他收获 + 阿波罗盖的遗产

### 独立的寇兰廷（AN INDEPENDENT CORENTYN）

至死桀骜不驯——**寇兰廷**自切利亚斯分离独立——但它礼貌地谢绝了来自**拉沃努尔**与其他国家的归并提议。相反——它已成为一个独立的商贸城邦——为自己开拓命运。它已经开始建造海军（凭借**泰莎·费尔温**（Tessa Fairwind）捐赠的几艘船舰为起点）——以与若干**阿扎克缇**社群协同守护其水域。话虽如此——阿波罗盖最终可能会试图夺回她失去的城市。不仅如此——附近**阿果尔苏**插手的迹象——开始浮现——尤其是若**沉眠者之眼**仍残留在附近的话。

### 其他收获（OTHER GAINS）

战争中亦获得了以下成果。

**依斯嘉**（Isger）：在「**破狱者联盟**」（Hellbreakers League）的引领下——切利亚斯的傀儡国——已获得自由。鉴于「**啁啾林**」（Chitterwood）哥布林作出了不可替代的贡献（他们长期以来被视为敌人）——依斯嘉将其部分领土让予了哥布林——以建立由哥布林领导的「**啁啾家园**」（Chitterhome）城邦。

**卡利**（Khari）：历经数百年苦涩的占领——卡利——重归**拉哈多姆**之手——使其得以部分控制「**赫斯佩雷斯海峡**」。迄今为止——拉哈多姆与寇兰廷一同巡逻并掌控着**奥罗登拱桥**——甚至萌生了将拱桥重建——以作为他们合作象征的半成型计划。话虽如此——双方各自的商人、政客与意识形态者——已在台面之下针锋相对——令人难辨这究竟是一段美好友谊的开端——抑或是一场充满宗教与经济火药味的对峙。

**拉沃努尔**（Ravounel）：其领土翻番有余——拉沃努尔几乎吞并了切利亚斯整段西海岸——以及**地狱口湾**沿岸的全部领地。其政府进一步资助了它的**枭鬼**邻邦——援助它们在「**心旋山脉**」沿线收复故土的事业。然而——切利亚斯顽固地守住了一条狭窄的海岸——位于拉沃努尔与寇兰廷将要交汇处——这是一段荒凉、未经开发的海滩——而即便如今——切利亚斯也正加速将之开发为港口——以便在「**阿卡迪亚洋**」上保有一席之地。

## 阿波罗盖的遗产（Abrogail's Legacy）

玩家角色或许打断了阿波罗盖二世的仪式——限制了她的地狱领域的边界——但他们未能阻止阿斯摩蒂斯将这位「**地狱大女皇**」（infernal majestrix）变易为一位**大魔王**（archdevil）。即便与她身为凡人时已然可观的施法造诣相比——她如今所掌握的——亦是非凡的魔法与肉身伟力。更进一步——斯戎家族中的不少成员——已开始借势其血脉——或崇拜他们这位神格化的堂亲——或创立诸如「**斯戎飞升派**」（Thrune Ascendant）之类的秘传邪教——以唤醒他们所共有的血脉、并求得封圣登神。

然而——此刻就让阿波罗盖庆贺——还为时尚早。她的力量来得有多迅捷——阿斯摩蒂斯就能多么迅捷地将其收回——若她虚掷此恩——也许会通过她所签下契约中——某一条只有他俩才知晓的附带条款——便能如此。因此——阿波罗盖无法在她的地狱桂冠上歇息——她必须在接下来的十年里——寻得新方法以扩张她的领域——并确保其不朽。

这——为那些希望一劳永逸终结切利亚斯地狱威胁的英雄们——开启了大有希望的良机。其一——据推测——阿波罗盖被杀之后——也许并不会如邪魔半神中常见的那样——自动在她的领域内复活——这意味着——一场决定性的对峙与致命一击——或许就能让她永远地完结。其二——切利亚斯与阿波罗盖在未来数年所蒙受的每一次蒙羞——在阿斯摩蒂斯眼中——便是对她又一记减分——即便是低等级的英雄——也能扰乱她的政权——并助力让她最终倒在「**黑暗王子**」（Prince of Darkness）的手中。

**崇拜阿波罗盖**：阿波罗盖能将法术赐予那些愿意不择手段满足自身野心之人——这其中也包括那些将来可能挑战玩家角色的反派。下文呈现阿波罗盖作为神圣赞助者的基础属性条目——更多信息载于《**探路者 失落奥秘：切利亚斯——地狱遗产**》。

AN INDEPENDENT CORENTYN
Defiant to the end, Corentyn seceded from Cheliax,
though it politely declined annexation offers from
Ravounel and other nations. Instead, it's become an
independent mercantile city-state charting its own
destiny. It's already begun (with a few ships donated
by Tessa Fairwind) building a navy to defend its waters
in collaboration with several azarketi communities.
That said, Abrogail might eventually try to reclaim
her lost city. What's more, signs of nearby alghollthu
meddling begin to emerge—especially if the Eye of
the Slumberer still remains nearby.

Isger: Guided by the Hellbreakers
League, Cheliax's puppet state has
secured its freedom. Recognizing the
invaluable contributions of the Chitterwood
goblins (long considered foes), Isger ceded
part of its territory to them to create the
goblin-led state of Chitterhome.

Khari: After centuries of bitter
occupation, Khari is again in
Rahadoum's hands, granting it partial
control over the Hespereth Strait. Thus far, Rahadoum
and Corentyn have patrolled and controlled the Arch of
Aroden together—even with half-formed plans to rebuild
the arch as a symbol of their cooperation. That said,
their respective merchants, politicians, and ideologues
are already butting heads behind the scenes, making it
unclear if this is the start of a wonderful friendship or a
religiously and economically charged rivalry.

Ravounel: More than doubling its territory,
Ravounel has annexed almost the entirety of Cheliax's
western coast and Hellmouth Gulf territories. The
government has further funded its strix neighbors,
aiding their reclamation of ancestral lands throughout
the Mindspin Mountains. However, Cheliax has
stubbornly held onto a narrow strip of coast where
Ravounel and Corentyn would meet—a desolate and
undeveloped beach that even now Cheliax is racing to
develop into a port so it can maintain a presence on

Abrogail's Legacy

The PCs might have interrupted Abrogail II's ritual,
limiting the borders of her infernal domain, but
they couldn't stop Asmodeus from transforming
the infernal majestrix into an archdevil. Compared
to even her considerable spellcasting prowess as a
mortal, she now commands extraordinary magic and
physical might. Further, many in House Thrune have
begun leveraging their lineage, be that in worshipping
their divine cousin or founding mystery cults like
Thrune Ascendant to awaken their shared bloodline
and apotheosize.

Yet it's too soon for Abrogail to celebrate. As quickly
as she gained her powers, Asmodeus could rescind
them if she wastes this boon—perhaps by invoking a
contingency only the two of them know about in the
contract she signed. Thus, Abrogail can't rest on her
infernal laurels and must seek new ways to expand her
domain and guarantee her immortality over
the next decade.

This opens promising opportunities
for heroes hoping to end Cheliax's
infernal threat once and for all.
First, it's hypothesized that Abrogail
might not automatically resurrect in
her domain when slain (as is common
among fiendish demigods), meaning a
decisive confrontation and killing blow
might end her forever. Second, every
embarrassment Cheliax and Abrogail suffer
in the coming years is another mark
against her in Asmodeus' eyes; even
lower-level heroes might undermine
her regime and contribute to her downfall at the hands
of the Prince of Darkness.
