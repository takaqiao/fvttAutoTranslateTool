## Page 90 — Ch5 A. 来袭！（接舷遭遇战开篇）

### A. 来袭！	严峻 14

北上的航程已是当下条件所能给予的最轻松状态——战火笼罩的切利亚斯海岸沿途常驻于视野中，提醒玩家角色们打过的与即将打的每一场仗。航行途中，玩家角色们有机会与水手们及同行的厄尔芬佣兵结下情谊。当舰队驶过安弗里塔石柱群附近，天气骤然恶化，白日雾愈重，夜间则风暴大作。领航员将船队拉近海岸，借附近的一座灯塔指引航向。

驶过灯塔几个小时后，岸上猛然传来一声轰响，紧接是一阵骇人的爆裂声——舰队中最靠海岸的那艘船被一发爆裂弹丸击中，整船向右舷剧烈倾斜！片刻后又是一声炮响，落点近得令船身震颤、船壳渗水。船长高声宣告必须搁浅抢滩，否则就要沉了。

接下来的几分钟里，舰船依次冲滩，岸上的炮兵又开了几轮。这段时间内，玩家角色们可以执行有限种类的探索动作，包括「避开侦测」或如下选项。

- **守护（Protect）**：寻常情况下，挥盾去拦截或用近战武器把弹丸打飞回海里听起来荒唐至极，但玩家角色们是高等级冒险者。此动作功能等同「守御」探索活动（若玩家角色携带盾牌），同时该玩家角色还可尝试对一发飞来的炮击进行打击（AC 34）。成功，可让弹丸在距目标稍远处提前引爆或偏离，给玩家角色们及随行兵团对该轮炮击效果的反射豁免加 +2 状况加值。大成功，则完全拦截弹丸使其落空，不造成任何伤害。大失败，守御玩家角色对应豁免时，其反射检定结果按比掷骰结果差一级的成功度结算。
- **驾船（Sail）**：玩家角色协助操船以加快靠岸并走更难预判的航路。若至少一名玩家角色成功通过 DC 30 操舟学识、DC 32 求生或 DC 34 运动检定，船的灵动让其更难被命中，给所有船上人员对炮击的反射豁免加 +1 状况加值，直到战斗开始。大成功时，炮兵只来得及对该船开两炮，而非三炮。
- **躲藏（Shelter）**：此动作功能等同「避开侦测」，但玩家角色还会在战斗回合开始前对后续炮击获得普通掩蔽。

**来袭炮火**：接近过程中，岸上炮兵又开了三发。大约每分钟有一发落在玩家角色附近，另有数发瞄准其他临近船只，导致下一发何时落地难以预判。这种间歇性炮击给玩家角色们留出回复些许生命值的时间，但可能耗尽其他资源。每当一发命中时，玩家角色及其每个所属兵团（见下）受到 5d8 伤害（DC 33 基础反射豁免）。第一发与第三发为穿刺伤害，第二发为火焰伤害。

玩家角色与同盟船只搁浅之后，他们发现自己并不孤单！在距吉斯特卡帝国遗址拉奇坎不远处，切利亚斯研究者们发掘到了一处新埋藏的遗迹，其中藏有古代灵导械（automaton），他们正为即将到来的战争而修复改装；同时也对场上的炮兵做了现代化改造。

这是一场小规模冲突遭遇战，使用《战吼！》第 158 页起的规则。遭遇目标是击败切利亚斯巨像指挥官并使两门旋风炮（cyclonic cannon）沉默。可通过杀光炮组成员让大炮沉默，或在与大炮地图位置相邻时，每门通过 3 次 DC 32 工艺、工程学识、宗教或巧手检定，临时瘫痪其击发机构。又或者，只要四个吉斯特卡灵导械兵团全部被摧毁，幸存的切利亚斯巨像与工程师便会逃离战场。

**玩家角色兵团**：玩家角色与其盟友们从损毁船只上溃涌登岸时，匆匆编成四个兵团——两个**弓手军团**（《战吼！》174）与两个**维京守卫**（《战吼！》193）。由于登岸与交战的混乱，玩家角色们来不及缔结小规模冲突纽带（skirmish bond），开战时也未必紧邻自己选定的兵团，详见 90 页地图。

**生物**：切利亚斯岸防炮兵由两门旋风炮（第 225 页）组成，每门由五名军团魔工程师作为炮组；为简化处理，每炮组只投一次先攻，而非按个体逐一掷骰。两门大炮中，一门遭遇开始时已装填完毕，另一门尚未装填。支援大炮的是四个吉斯特卡灵导械兵团，由两名切利亚斯巨像（juggernaut）工程师率领。吉斯特卡灵导械使用「**钟表步兵**（clockwork infantry）」的数据，但没有「**上发条**（Wind-Up）」能力。取而代之的是：当一名巨像被击败时，最接近该指挥官的两个吉斯特卡灵导械兵团便会停摆并撤离遭遇。巨像只要走入某个吉斯特卡灵导械兵团所在格便能担任其指挥官，无需任何检定。

此遭遇发生在夜间，沙滩为粘性沙地，全部视为困难地形。

**A. Incoming!	Severe 14**

Sailing north is as relaxing as it possibly can be, with the coast of war-stricken Cheliax a constant reminder of battles fought and battles yet to come. During the voyage, the PCs have time to befriend the crews and the Ulfen mercenaries joining them. As the flotilla passes near the Pillars of Anferita, the weather takes a turn for the worse, growing foggier by day and stormy at night. The lead navigator turns the ships closer to shore, using the nearby lighthouse to guide them.

A few hours after sailing past the lighthouse, a sudden boom erupts from the shore, followed by a horrendous crack as the vessel closest to the coast is wracked by the impact of an explosive projectile, causing it to list heavily to starboard! A second artillery blast follows a moment later, exploding close enough that the ship's hull shudders and begins leaking. The captain shouts that he needs to beach the ship to prevent it from sinking.

As the ships make landfall over the next few minutes, the artillery on shore takes several more shots. During this time, the PCs can perform a limited range of exploration actions, including Avoid Notice or the following.

- **Protect**: While normally it would seem preposterous to shoot a massive projectile out of the air, intercept it with one's shield, or even bat it out to sea with a melee weapon, the PCs are high-level adventurers. This functions as the Defend exploration activity (if the PC has a shield), but the PC can also attempt to Strike one of the incoming artillery attacks (AC 34). If they succeed, they detonate or redirect the incoming projectile a short distance from the intended target, granting the PCs and their troops a +2 circumstance bonus to their Reflex saving throws against that shot's effects. If they critically succeed, they intercept the shot and cause it to miss entirely, dealing no damage. If they critically fail, the Defending PC uses the outcome one degree of success worse than the result of their roll for their saving throw.
- **Sail**: The PC helps operate the ship to reach the shore quickly and take a less predictable path. If at least one PC succeeds at a DC 30 Sailing Lore check, a DC 32 Survival check, or a DC 34 Athletics check, the ship's swiftness makes it harder to hit, granting everyone aboard a +1 circumstance bonus to all Reflex saves against the artillery until combat begins. If a PC critically succeeds, the artillery only has enough time to fire two shots at the ship, not three.
- **Shelter**: This functions as Avoiding Notice, but the PC also gains standard cover against further artillery attacks until the beginning of their first turn in combat.

**Incoming Fire**: During their approach, the artillery fires three more shots. Roughly one shot strikes near the PCs per minute, and several shots fire at other nearby ships, making it difficult to predict exactly when the next shell might hit. While the intermittent attacks allow the PCs some time to recover Hit Points before combat, it might drain their other resources. Each time a shot lands, the PCs and each of their troops (see below) take 5d8 damage (DC 33 basic Reflex save). The first and third shots deal piercing damage, and the second shot deals fire damage.

Once the PCs and their allied vessels run aground, they find they aren't alone! Not far from the famous Jistkan ruins of Rachikan, Chelaxian researchers found a new buried site containing ancient automatons that they've been refurbishing for the upcoming war, as well as artillery they've been modernizing.

This is a skirmish encounter, using the rules outlined starting on page 158 of Battlecry! The objective of the encounter is to defeat the Chelaxian juggernaut commanders and silence the two cyclonic cannons. The cannons can be taken out by killing their crews or by succeeding at three DC 32 Crafting, Engineering Lore, Religion, or Thievery checks per cannon to temporarily disable their firing mechanisms while adjacent to their space on the map. Alternatively, if all four Jistkan automaton troops are destroyed, the surviving Chelaxian juggernauts and engineers flee the area.

**PC Troops**: As the PCs and their allies spill ashore from their damaged ships, they hastily assemble into four troops—two archer regiments (Battlecry! 174) and two viking guards (Battlecry! 193). Due to the chaos surrounding their landing and engagement, the PCs don't have time to form skirmish bonds, nor do they necessarily start near their chosen troops, as shown on the map on page 90.

**Creatures**: The Chelaxian shore batteries consist of two cyclonic cannons (page 225), each of which has a crew of five vordine engineers; for simplicity, roll once for initiative per crew, not per individual vordine. One of the two cannons begins the encounter loaded, but the other isn't. Supporting the cannons are four troops of Jistkan automatons led by two Chelaxian juggernaut engineers. The Jistkan automatons use the statistics for clockwork infantry but don't have the Wind-Up ability. Instead, when a juggernaut is defeated, the two Jistkan automaton troops nearest to that commander shut down and are removed from the encounter. The juggernauts can act as the commander of any Jistkan automaton troop unit simply by moving into its space, without needing to attempt a check.

This encounter takes place at night, and the beach consists of sticky sand, making it all difficult terrain.
