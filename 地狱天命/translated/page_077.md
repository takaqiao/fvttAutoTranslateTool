### 卡利获释（Khari Freed）

**罗特罗维奥被击败后**——**欣喜的市民走上街头**——**连续数日庆祝**。**瑞玛——卡利当地的纯净军团指挥官——亲自感谢 PC 们的援助**——**赠予他们一颗"命源"武器属性符文与一颗"震波"武器属性符文**。**此外**——**她还授予 PC 们进入卡利军械库的权限**——**让他们为前往齐利亚尼的水下行程作准备**。**此时**——**给玩家们时间熟悉水下战斗的规则**（《玩家核心》第 437 页）——**他们正计划着冒险的下一段路程**。

**瑞玛赠予每位 PC 两瓶水中呼吸药水**——**这应足以支持他们至少一次短程深入神殿**。**此外**——**她还可为每位 PC 提供一颗"水下"武器属性符文**（Pathfinder Rage of Elements 第 178 页）**装配在其武器上**。**或者**——**若 PC 愿意把自己的武器作为抵押留下**——**她可从军械库中替换一件大致等价的穿刺武器**——**例如以一柄 +1 强击炽焰长剑换得一柄 +1 强击冰霜镐**。**GM 酌情**——**瑞玛或许没有非凡或稀有装备的等价替换品**。

**下到神殿之前**——**瑞玛敦促 PC 们休息并采购补给**——**期间他们以贵宾身份参加城中庆典**。

### D. 折磨之殿（The Temple of Torment）

**一个多世纪以来**——**居住于水下村庄齐利亚尼**（Killiani）的阿扎克缇人**已掏空了支撑奥罗登拱桥的一根巨大桩基**——**创造出一座阴险的圣域**——**用以存放与研究他们所发现的一件奇异神器**：**沉眠者之眼**（the Eye of the Slumberer，第 225 页）。**他们对该球体愈是崇敬**——**它便愈以心灵感应向他们透露内容**——**同时既赐予他们灵能能力**——**也将这些阿扎克缇人扭折为它的意志**。**事实上**——**虽然该球体是一件强大魔法物品**——**它却由一只强大的阿尔古苏**（alghollthu）——**名为奥拉夫鲁**（Oolavru）——**所操控**——**用以腐化整个社区**。**每年**——**球体催促这些阿扎克缇人从地表绑架类人生物**——**带至海下加以折磨与祭献**。**多年执行这项阴森工作之后**——**齐利亚尼的阿扎克缇人变得残忍扭曲**——**渐渐迷恋上折磨他人**。**关于这处场所的更多信息**——**详见《Pathfinder Campaign Setting: Cheliax, The Infernal Empire》第 43 页**。

**就索克斯伦·齐利亚尼**（Xoxren Killiani，**狂热 男性 阿扎克缇 心能者 14 级**）**所知**——**他统治着这个邪教**——**并领导他们将该神器奉为神明**。**即便如此**——**他仍渴望某一日**——**球体的嗜血得到满足**——**停止其可怕的索求**。**就连索克斯伦也不知道奥拉夫鲁的存在**——**后者已伪装成阿扎克缇人**——**渗透了齐利亚尼**。

**奥拉夫鲁自视为该神器的守护者**——**相信**：**只要球体吸收足够多的灵能能量**——**它便会解锁更加伟大的力量**——**甚至唤醒某种能进一步推动阿尔古苏计划的强大存在**。**因此**——**奥拉夫鲁以灵能引导这些阿扎克缇人前去沉眠者之眼被埋的地方**——**几代以来将他们用作毫不知情的棋子**。

**神殿特征**：**该神殿由三层同心石环构成**——**它们围绕彼此旋转**——**由该球体的魔法所驱动**。**最外环以逆时针方向旋转**——**中环以顺时针方向旋转**——**最内环以逆时针方向旋转**。**三环共同围绕神殿中央的一间球形之屋旋转**——**该屋中安置着沉眠者之眼**。

**当两道门户短暂对齐时**——**生物可在环间移动**。**每道环的边缘以 2 回合 5 英尺的速率旋转**；**由于两环以相反方向旋转**——**相对而言**——**两环看起来每回合相对旋转 5 英尺**。**因此**——**一道 10 英尺宽的门只在 3 回合内可通行**：**第一段露出时 1 回合、整道门露出时 1 回合、第二段露出时 1 回合**。**因此 PC 们在区域间移动的机会有限**——**甚至可能就在遭遇战之中！**

**站在正在闭合的门户里的生物**可**通过 DC 20 反射豁免移到任一侧**。**若失败**——**它承受 6d10+30 钝击伤害**（DC 35 基础强韧豁免）——**因墙壁挤压它**。**若该生物幸存**——**其身体暂时阻止这些环移动**——**但每回合仍承受此伤害**——**直到其挪开**。

**与其在探索模式中跟踪每个环的精确位置**——**你可假定每个环约需 5 分钟完成一周旋转**。**这样**——**PC 们可短暂等待"刚关闭的一道门重新打开"**。

**神殿的墙壁与门户由石头构成**——**天花板高 20 英尺**——**除非另有说明**。

**游荡巡逻**：**索克斯伦已派他大部分战士前往寇兰廷港**——**因此神殿守备稀疏**。**除了房间居民之外**——**你可选择加入在地下城中巡逻的游荡邪教徒小组**（**除 D8 区例外**——**他们害怕此处**）。**若 PC 们试图在地下城内休息或需要被甩入一记弧线球**——**可考虑让下文这一小组突袭其位置**。

D. The Temple of Torment
For over a century, azarketis inhabiting the underwater village of Killiani have hollowed out one of the massive pilings that supports the Arch of Aroden, creating a sinister sanctuary to store and study a strange artifact they'd discovered: the Eye of the Slumberer (page 225). The more they revered the orb, the more it telepathically revealed to them, simultaneously bestowing psychic powers and bending the azarketis to its will. In fact, while the orb is a powerful magical item, it's controlled by a powerful alghollthu named Oolavru who uses it to corrupt the community. Each year, the orb urges the azarketis to capture humanoids from the surface and bring them beneath the waves to torment and sacrifice. After years of carrying out this grim work, the azarketis of Killiani became cruel and twisted, coming to relish the torment of others. Additional information about this site appears in Pathfinder Campaign Setting: Cheliax, The Infernal Empire on page 43.

As far as he knows, Xoxren Killiani (fanatical male azarketi psychic 14) rules the cult, leading it to worship the artifact as a god. Even so, he longs for the day that the orb's blood lust is appeased and ceases its horrible demands. Even Xoxren doesn't know of Oolavru, who has infiltrated Killiani while disguised as an azarketi.

Oolavru considers itself the artifact's guardian, believing that if the orb absorbs enough psychic energy, it will unlock even greater powers or even awaken some powerful being that might further alghollthu plans. Thus, the Oolavru psychically guided these azarketis to where the Eye of the Slumberer was buried, using them as unwitting pawns for generations.

Temple Features: The temple consists of three concentric stone rings that rotate around each other, powered by the orb's magic. The outermost spins in a counterclockwise motion, the middle ring spins clockwise, and the innermost ring spins counterclockwise. All three rings rotate around a spherical room at the temple's center, which houses the Eye of the Slumberer.

Creatures can move between rings when two doorways briefly align. The rim of each ring rotates about 5 feet over the course of 2 rounds; because the rings spin in opposite directions, the rings seem to spin 5 feet per round relative to each other. Thus, a 10-foot-wide door is only accessible for 3 rounds: one round when the first half is uncovered, one while the whole door is uncovered, and one while the second half of the door is uncovered. The PCs thus have limited opportunities to move between areas—possibly even in the middle of an encounter!

A creature standing in a closing doorway can move to either side with a successful DC 20 Reflex save. If they fail, they take 6d10+30 bludgeoning damage (DC 35 basic Fortitude save) as the walls crush them. If the creature survives, its body temporarily stop those rings from moving, though they take this damage each round until they move out of the way.

Rather than tracking each ring's exact position in exploration mode, you can assume that each ring takes about 5 minutes to complete a revolution. Thus, PCs can briefly wait for a recently closed door to reopen.

The temple's walls and doors are made of stone, and ceilings are 20 feet high unless otherwise noted.

Wandering Patrols: Xoxren has dispatched most of his warriors to Corentyn's harbor, so the temple is sparsely defended. Beyond the rooms' denizens, you can optionally include wandering cultist groups who patrol the dungeon (except for area D8, which they fear). If the PCs attempt to rest inside the dungeon or otherwise need a curve ball thrown at them, consider having the group below assault their position.
