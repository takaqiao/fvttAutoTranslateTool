### 破坏傲世号（Breaking the Impervious）

起义军想要摧毁傲世号——既为消除军事资产，也为展示反抗者的实力。要毁掉这艘船，PC 们必须为船只各部位累计足够的**毁坏点**（Destruction Points, DP）。PC 们可尝试**破坏任务**的 2 动作版本或 10 分钟探索活动版本。PC 们**不能**在前哨遭遇中同时尝试破坏任务并指挥部队。

当船的某个区段获得**破损**（broken）状态时，该区域内的生物因毁坏获得**掩护**。一旦三个区段全部摧毁，傲世号开始坍塌，3 轮后彻底毁灭。当船开始坍塌时，琳奈塔会令起义军后撤。

> **傲世号破坏（IMPERVIOUS DESTRUCTION）** [二动作]
> 你试图破坏相邻的傲世号区段。尝试该区段毁坏障碍中列出的技能检定。若你以 10 分钟探索活动版本进行该检定并指挥一支部队，则获得 +2 状况加值。
> **大成功** 你获得 2 DP。
> **成功** 你获得 1 DP。
> **失败** 无效果。

**宝藏**：傲世号被魔法处理过的木材对那些不在意其来源的买家而言极有价值。PC 们可从其撞角中收集相当于 **5,000 gp 的暮木（duskwood）木料**。

BREAKING THE IMPERVIOUS
The rebellion wants to break the Impervious, both to destroy the military asset and to demonstrate the rebels' strength. To destroy the vessel, the PCs must earn enough Destruction Points (DP) for each location of the ship. The PCs can attempt both 2-action or 10-minute exploration activity versions of the destruction tasks. PCs cannot simultaneously attempt destruction tasks and lead troops during a skirmish encounter.

When a segment of the ship gains the broken condition, creatures in that area gain cover from the destruction. Once all three segments are destroyed, the Impervious starts collapsing and is fully destroyed after 3 rounds. Linetta calls the rebellion's forces to retreat once the ship begins to collapse.

IMPERVIOUS DESTRUCTION [two-actions]
You try to destroy an adjacent section of the Impervious. Attempt a skill check listed in that section's destruction obstacle. If you attempt this check as a 10-minute exploration activity and command a troop, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus.
Critical Success You gain 2 DP.
Success You gain 1 DP.
Failure No effect.
Critical Failure You lose 1 DP.
