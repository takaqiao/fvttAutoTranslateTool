## Page 26 — Ch1 任务三：锁链骑士团会议

——与卡蒂利娅及其计划勾结，她便会摧毁他们。若 PC 们提及卡蒂利娅的会议，埃默汀立刻承诺出席——好阻止一个异端毁掉骑士团仅存的一切。

### 锁链骑士团会议（Meeting of the Chain）

锁链骑士团的会议在暮光之爪仓库中举行，但出席者中只有琳奈塔一人不属于骑士团。本场会议使用**影响力子系统**（GM Core 187）。要让骑士团投票支持起义，必须有**至少 3 名成员**（包括卡蒂利娅）投赞成票。**PC 们必须先召集至少另外 2 名地狱骑士**，会议才能开始。

#### 会议议程（Meeting Schedule）

地狱骑士主持的会议井然有序——包括提案、辩论、然后投票的时间。这给 PC 们留下了有限的窗口期来影响出席者，同时还要应对埃默汀与卡蒂利娅之间一触即发的爆裂冲突！

下列为会议议程与各 NPC 出席状况以供影响：

- **会前阶段（第 1 影响轮）**：卡蒂利娅在会议开始之前已到场。埃默汀·欧恩亦在正式开始前抵达。
- **提案介绍（第 2 影响轮）**：所有出席的锁链骑士团成员在会议正式开始时悉数到场。**唯一被介绍的议题**是卡蒂利娅的提案：加入暮光之爪并支持**破钥行动**。卡蒂利娅说明该行动涉及从镣铐骑士团手中夺回格拉戴斯卡堡垒。
- **提案讨论（第 3 影响轮）**：讨论期间，埃默汀显然怒形于色。对她的所有**影响**（Influence）检定承受 -2 状况减值；而对她的**探知**（Discovery）检定获得 +2 状况加值。
- **休会与自审（第 4 影响轮）**：随着讨论进入尾声，埃默汀公开指控卡蒂利娅犯下异端之罪。会议陷入互相喊叫的混乱。塞贝尼奥介入，呼吁休会，暗示大家利用这段间隙冷静思考。
  ——不过埃默汀并未休息，而是要求与卡蒂利娅举行一场**自审**（Reckoning）仪式。自审通常是地狱骑士之间私下的悔过行为。锁链骑士团的自审涉及把锁链缠绕在地狱骑士的手臂上，再把短棒插入链环间扭转，直至不再有松弛之处。如果 PC 们已在卡蒂利娅身上累积了足够的影响力点而让她同意，她将进行自审——此期间她无法被进一步影响。若卡蒂利娅不同意，埃默汀便离开会议，本轮无法对她进行影响或探知检定。
- **提案投票（第 5 影响轮）**：若 PC 们对埃默汀至少累计 3 影响力点，她会在最终投票前归来。否则，她在本轮无法被影响，并恰好在投票时回到现场对该提案投反对票。

Meeting of the Chain
The Order of the Chain meeting takes place in the Twilight Talons' warehouse, though none but Linetta are present. This meeting uses the influence subsystem (GM Core 187). For the order to vote for the rebellion, three of the members (including Cathilia) need to vote in favor. The PCs must have gathered at least two other Hellknights for the meeting to begin.

MEETING SCHEDULE
Hellknights run orderly meetings, including time to make, debate, and then vote on proposals. This provides limited time for the PCs to influence the attendees—and manage the imminent, explosive conflict between Emertine and Cathilia!

The following notes the meeting schedule as well as which NPCs are present to be Influenced.

Pre-Meeting (1st Influence Round): Cathilia is present before the meeting begins. Emertine Oughn also arrives before the meeting's formal start.

Introduction of Proposals (2nd Influence Round): All attending members of the Order of the Chain arrive as the meeting comes to order. The only topic introduced is Cathilia's proposal to join the Twilight Talons and support Operation Broken Key. Cathilia shares that the operation involves retaking Citadel Gheradesca from the Order of the Manacle.

Discussion of Proposals (3rd Influence Round): Emertine is visibly angry during the discussion. All checks to Influence her take a –2 circumstance penalty, whereas Discovery checks for her receive a +2 circumstance bonus.

Recess and a Reckoning (4th Influence Round): As discussion ends, Emertine openly accuses Cathilia of heresy. The meeting devolves into a shouting match. Sebenio intervenes by calling a recess, implying everyone should use the break to clear their heads.

Rather than take a break, Emertine calls for Cathilia to perform a Reckoning with her. Reckonings are typically private acts of atonement among the Hellknights. The Order of the Chain Reckoning involves wrapping chains around the Hellknight's arm, jamming the rod through the links, and twisting until no slack remains. If the PCs have earned enough Influence Points with Cathilia for her to agree, she performs the Reckoning, during which she's unavailable to Influence. If Cathilia doesn't agree, Emertine leaves the meeting and isn't available for Influence or Discovery checks this round.

Voting on Proposals (5th Influence Round): If the PCs earned at least 3 Influence Points with Emertine, she returns before deciding on her final vote. Otherwise, she isn't available to Influence in this round and arrives just in time to vote against the proposal.
