#### B5. 潜行训练塔（Stealth Training Tower）　**严峻 10**

> 这些开阔的高塔俯瞰着高贵区与贵族区的街道。脚下行人微小如玩偶。地面远缘正在崩裂——偶尔会有石块脱落，一路翻滚坠入下方街景。

碎冠者旗帜可见于平台西缘，自一块临近崩裂的石块中伸出。PC 通过成功的 DC 29 制作检定可判断：作为**互动**（Interact）动作取下旗帜会让石块跌入街道、引起下方过路人的注意。**以 3 动作的活动**取下旗帜可使其稳定；或为该石块恢复至少 10 HP 同样有效。若石块跌入街道，PC 们在接下来的 24 小时内对**抗拒**或**闪避**多塔里的尝试上承受 -2 状况减值。

**生物（Creatures）**：旗帜旁伫立着寇兰廷前任异端魁首们的石像。碎冠者从德霍夫拉姆堡边的**泽尔基公园**（Zelkie Park）盗走了这些雕像，年代可追溯至斯戎家族崛起前、德霍夫拉姆堡仍为领主邸宅的时期。它们如今被摆放为旗帜的"石质守望者"。虽然 PC 们可凭战斗克服此障碍，但石卫垒同样易受华丽的虚招与潜行所影响。从区域 B2 引来的拱道上有一个装小石子的桶可用。这些石卫垒作为无心智的守卫，会向声音方向移动。

#### B6. 异端魁首藏身处（Archheathen's Hideout）　**严峻 10**

> 残余的墙面上贴满同一人的草绘。多枚匕首穿透他的面孔将这些纸张钉住。

这些草绘的水准参差不齐。PC 通过成功的 DC 25 艺术学识或 DC 29 察觉检定可注意每张草绘的署名都不同。通过成功的 DC 27 社会检定，PC 可辨认出对象为**异端魁首查塔尼翁**（Archheathen Charthagnion），尽管多数画像不甚相似；重击成功时，他们能识别出这些草图是仅远观过查塔尼翁的人所绘的肖像。**凯旋与艾里尔的署名亦在其中**。完成其中一张草绘并以匕首刺穿之，象征着正式加入碎冠者。

**生物**：本平台的旗帜被绑在一根 15 英尺高木杆顶端的一具"查塔尼翁雕偶"上。它由一名碎冠者打造，配以一套**故意低劣**的指令以讥讽异端魁首。它进攻时丝毫不顾自身安危。雕偶被击败后，旗帜很容易摘下；战斗中要取下则需**偷窃**（Stealing）。

B5. STEALTH TRAINING TOWER	SEVERE 10
The wide-open towers overlook the streets of the High Quarter and the Noble Quarter. People walking below appear doll-like. The far edges of the floor are crumbling, occasionally dropping stones that tumble through the long drop to the scene below.

The Crowncrushers flag is visible on the platform's western edge as it juts from a stone that's close to crumbling. With a successful DC 29 Crafting check, a PC discerns that removing the flag as an Interact action would cause the stone to fall into the street and catch the attention of those passing by below. Removing the flag as a 3-action activity stabilizes it, as does restoring at least 10 HP to the stone. If the stone drops into the street, the PCs take a –2 circumstance penalty to their attempts to Defy or Evade the dottari for the next 24 hours.

Creatures: Stone sculptures of former archheathens of Corentyn wait next to the flag. The Crowncrushers stole the statues from Zelkie Park by Dhoffram Keep, dated from when Dhoffram Keep was the lord's residence prior to the rise of House Thrune. They're positioned as stone watchers over the flag. While the PCs can overcome this obstacle through combat, the watchers are susceptible to flashy misdirection and stealth. A bucket with small rocks is accessible on the arch leading from area B2. The bulwarks move toward noise as mindless guardians.

B6. ARCHHEATHEN'S HIDEOUT	MODERATE 10
Sketches of the same man cover the remaining walls. Daggers driven through his face hold up the papers.

The quality of the sketches varies widely. A PC who succeeds at a DC 25 Art Lore or DC 29 Perception check identifies that each has a different signature. A PC who succeeds at a DC 27 Society check identifies the subject as Archheathen Charthagnion, though they're generally poor likenesses; with a critical success, they identify the sketches as portraits completed by those who have only seen Charthagnion from afar. Triumph's and Eryr's signatures are visible among the portraits. Completing one of these sketches and stabbing it marks official induction into the Crowncrushers.

Creatures: The flag on this platform is attached to a constructed effigy of Charthagnion on a 15-foot pole. It was constructed by a Crowncrusher and given an insultingly basic set of instructions to mock the Archheathen. It attacks without concern for its own safety. The flag is easily detached after the effigy's defeat, while detaching the flag during combat requires Stealing.

HELLKNIGHT ARMOR
Hellknight armor is among the most recognizable in Avistan. Each order includes its own flourishes, from spikes to horns to Hellish motifs all intended to project fearsome authority. Hellknights rarely abide anyone other than a member of their order wearing (much less owning) a suit of this armor; Ubenor Enos makes an exception for the PCs with his gift.

Hellknight plate appears on page 9 of Pathfinder Treasure Vault. Its statistics are identical to those of full plate except that Hellknight plate costs 35 gp (rather than 30 gp). Lighter versions analogous to breastplates also exist. Characters with various Hellknight archetypes often need to wear Hellknight armor to use their special abilities.
