### A4. 图书馆（LIBRARY）　琐碎 17

**开放式书架与充足的座椅，让这间陈设得当的图书馆透出一股欢迎宾客驻足阅读的气氛**。**然而，书上覆着的一层灰尘则透露：或许已经有相当一段时间，没人响应过那份邀请了**。

**生存 DC 36 检定成功**，玩家角色可发现**过去一周确曾有人入内**（多半是家政人员）；**极佳成功**还可让玩家角色估计出：**这些书已有大约 20 年未曾移动过**。

**搜寻 DC 36 察觉检定成功**的玩家角色，会发现**东北角那只剥制熊标本的下颚最近被人动过**。**那对下颚一开**，便揭出**藏在熊腹里的一处储物隔间**。**伸手探入其中，便能找到一本《布拉尔回环》（The Dou-Bral Cycle）的孤本**——**一份号称详述宗-库山（Zon-Kuthon）神祇蜕变全过程的奈多文献**。**该书所有其他副本，向来被认为早已被销毁**。

**危害**：**如果玩家角色翻开这本《布拉尔回环》**，**他们会发现这本书已被挖空**，**内嵌一道至深恐惧之纹（sigil of deepest fear）**。

A4. LIBRARY 　TRIVIAL 17
Open shelves and ample seating give this well-appointed library a welcoming air that invites guests to relax while they read. The layer of dust coating the books show that, perhaps, no one has taken up that invitation in quite some time.

With a successful DC 36 Survival check, a PC discovers that someone was in the room in the past week (likely a housekeeper), and with a critical success, the PC estimates that the books haven't been moved in the past 20 years.

A PC who succeeds at a DC 36 Perception check to Search finds that the jaws of the taxidermy bear in the northeastern corner have been moved recently. The jaws open to reveal a storage compartment in its stomach. Reaching into it reveals a copy of The Dou-Bral Cycle, a Nidalese text that purports to detail the transformation of the god Zon-Kuthon. All other copies of it are long thought destroyed.

Hazards: If the PCs open the copy of The Dou-Bral Cycle, they find that the copy has been hollowed out with a sigil of deepest fear inscribed inside it.
