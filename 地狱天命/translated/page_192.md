## Page 192 — 第十章：午夜焚烧 · B8 宝藏巢穴决战 + 通往地狱的门户

神殿宝库——藏有教会贪婪掠夺的历史——其中堆放的——是从被征服的文明、与宗教战争的失败者那里——劫掠而来的神器。如今——所有这些宝藏皆已被**坎达鲁**据为己有——这位远古邪魔之龙——是被阿波罗盖的邪魔仪式所吸引而来。她将这处地下房室视作自己的新巢穴——一旦切利亚斯落入地狱之手——她便打算将她其余的财库——也搬到这里来。

那几支噼啪燃烧的火炬——在这回荡的穹顶下——只能提供朦胧的昏光。

**生物**：一位自称「**暴君之影**」（Tyrant's Shadow）的**尼萨利**（nessari）——曾是此宝库唯一的守护者——但坎达鲁出现之后——他便与她共谋。他那漆黑的双翼——与他那红色皮肤形成强烈对比——尽管他身高是普通人类的两倍——但在那超大型的坎达鲁面前——他的身量便相形见绌。

玩家角色进入时——坎达鲁懒洋洋地伏卧在她新得的宝藏堆上——一缕缕火苗自鼻孔间卷起。她立刻认出玩家角色是敌人——并宣告他们为「**大邪魔之敌**」（enemies of the Archfiend）。她命令暴君之影「以应有的迅捷」将他们解决——并不听玩家角色的任何恳求或哀求。暴君之影乐于冲入战斗——他在过去数十年里几乎没见过太多动作。坎达鲁不会采取任何敌对动作——直至一位玩家角色对暴君之影造成暴击——或对她本人采取攻击动作。届时——她振翅升至天花板——并以她的「**地狱火吐息**」沐浴英雄们。这邪魔与巨龙——皆战至殒命——但若坎达鲁先被击倒——暴君之影会因接下来的状况而停手（见下文**发展**）。

**发展**：当玩家角色对坎达鲁打出致命一击之时——她踉跄着扑向她新得的宝藏——将她那燃烧着的、邪秽的血——溅洒于这些珍宝之上。这堆物什开始燃烧、熔化——连那些看似不燃之物亦如此——而坎达鲁的躯体——仿佛在为这火焰添薪一般——焕发着木炭般的灼光。一位以 DC 38 **奥秘**或**宗教**检定成功的玩家角色——能识出这条巨龙的血——与此处存放的神器之力、再加上阿波罗盖正在进行的仪式所散出的环境魔法——三者相合——正在制造一道**位面裂隙**。大成功时——他们知道这道裂隙将直通地狱——具体而言——是其最上层的**阿弗纳斯**。

若该裂隙开启时——暴君之影尚活着——他会因眼前所发生之事而敬畏地双膝跪地。若给他机会——他便会冲入裂隙——终于回到地狱——他的契约如今便告作废。

### 通往地狱的门户（A Portal to Hell）

随着坎达鲁的尸首作出最后一阵临终的痉挛——一阵巨大的撕裂之声——回响在宝库之中。那燃烧的宝藏堆上方的虚空本身——被撕开了一道口。透过这道裂口——玩家角色可以看到一片广阔的景象——荒芜的战场——被一处处火山岩的露头切割开来。天空是红色的——远处的火山——喷吐着烈火与浓烟。一座威严的铁铸要塞——立于不远处。这片地狱景观——便是**阿弗纳斯**——靠近**暴君魔鬼**与斯戎家族顾问**戈索克莱克**的领地。

要确保阿波罗盖的仪式——不至将整个内海地区——转化为一个新的地狱层域——玩家角色必须穿过这道门户——在戈索克莱克的权力所在——直面这位魔鬼。一位以 DC 36 **奥秘**或**宗教**检定成功的玩家角色——便知道：在该门户关闭之前——他们只有不到 15 分钟的时间——否则——他们便得另寻去地狱的法子——而那时——便可能为时已晚——无法扰乱仪式。该是来一场信仰之跃的时候了！

The temple treasury holds a history of the church's
grasping greed, with artifacts stolen from conquered
civilizations and the losers of religious wars. All of
this treasure has been now claimed by Kandarlu, an
ancient diabolic dragon drawn to the city by Abrogail's
fiendish ritual. She considers the subterranean chamber
her new lair, and once Cheliax has been delivered to
Hell, she plans on bringing the rest of her hoard here.

The sputtering lit torches provide only dim light in
the echoing vault.

Creatures: A nessari calling themself Tyrant's
Shadow was once the sole guardian of the treasury,
but with Kandarlu's appearance, they've thrown in
their lot with her. Their pitch-black wings appear in
stark contrast to their red skin, and though they're
twice the height of an average human, their size pales
in comparison to the gargantuan Kandarlu.

When the PCs enter, Kandarlu lazes on her
newfound hoard, tiny licks of flame curling from her
nostrils. She immediately recognizes the PCs as foes
and declares them "enemies of the Archfiend." She
orders Tyrant's Shadow to dispatch them with "all
due haste" and doesn't listen to any entreaties or pleas
from the PCs. Tyrant's Shadow gladly charges into
combat, not having seen much action over the past
few decades. Kandarlu doesn't take any hostile actions
until a PC scores a critical hit on Tyrant's Shadow or
takes an offensive action against her. She then flaps her
wings to rise up to the ceiling and bathe the heroes in
her Hellfire Breath. Both the fiend and the dragon fight
until killed, though if Kandarlu is struck down first,
Tyrant's Shadow ceases fighting in light of what
occurs (see Development below).

Development: When the PCs strike a fatal blow
to Kandarlu, she staggers toward her newfound
hoard and spills some of her burning, unholy blood
across the treasures. The pile begins to burn and
melt—even objects that don't seem flammable—as
Kandarlu's body seems to fuel the fire as it glows
like charcoal. A PC who succeeds at a DC 38
Arcana or Religion check recognizes that the
dragon's blood, combined with the power of
the artifacts stored here and the ambient magic of
Abrogail's ongoing ritual, is causing a planar rift. On a
critical success, they know it'll lead straight to Hell—
specifically Avernus, the topmost layer.

If Tyrant's Shadow still lives when this rift opens,
they fall to their knees in awe of what's occurring. If
given the opportunity, they rush into the rift to finally
return to Hell, their contract now null and void.

A Portal to Hell

As Kandarlu's corpse gives its final death spasm, a
great tearing noise echoes through the treasury. The
very space above the burning hoard rips open. Beyond
the tear, the PCs can see a vast landscape of barren
battlefields rent by outcroppings of volcanic rock. The
sky is red, and distant volcanoes belch fire and fumes.
An imposing fortress of iron stands in the middle
distance. This hellscape is Avernus, near the domain of
the tyrant devil and Thrune advisor Gorthoklek.

To ensure that Abrogail's ritual doesn't convert
the entirety of the Inner Sea region into a new layer
of Hell, the PCs must step through the portal and
confront Gorthoklek within his seat of power. A PC
who succeeds at a DC 36 Arcana or Religion check
knows they have fewer than 15 minutes before the
portal closes, or they'll have to find their own way to
Hell, at which point it might be too late to disrupt the
ritual. It's time to take a leap of faith!
