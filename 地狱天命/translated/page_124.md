### 细致打量（Attention to Detail）

玩家角色们被引荐给一支精挑细选的兵团——其中既有安多兰陆军游侠，也有鹰骑士团成员，还有破狱者拳师与地狱骑士骑兵。他们将在接下来数小时里熟悉自己的士兵，按各自或队伍的小规模冲突风格定制部队的最佳作战形态。

在机制上，**每位玩家角色统率一支特种部队单位**（special forces unit）。每支部队拥有相同的基础数据，但可以从下方列表中选出三项可变能力，依玩家个人或团队战术风格定制。由于这些部队由玩家亲自组建，并且队中尽是安多兰最优秀的精兵，他们还获得了与玩家角色建立**小规模冲突纽带**（skirmish bond）的福利——视同他们已花费休闲期一同训练。务必让玩家了解第 232–233 页的**拓展小规模冲突纽带**规则。

Attention to Detail

The PCs are introduced to a group of handpicked troops who represent the finest soldiers available. Some are Andoren army rangers, others are Eagle Knights, and still others are Hellbreaker brawlers or Hellknight cavalry. The PCs get to spend the next few hours getting to know their soldiers, customizing them into the best fighting force that suits their skirmish combat style.

Mechanically, each PC assumes command of one special forces unit. Each troop has the same base statistics but also has three variable abilities that the PCs can select from the list below to customize the troop to their personal or group skirmish style. Because the PCs assemble these troops, and each of the troops has only Andoran's best and brightest, they also gain the benefit of establishing a skirmish bond with these troops as though they'd spent downtime training with them. Be sure to make the PCs aware of the expanded skirmish bonds on pages 232–233.
