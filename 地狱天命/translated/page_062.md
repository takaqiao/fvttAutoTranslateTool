### 纳罗纳废墟（The Ruins of Narona）

**一世纪以前**，**纳罗纳是一座果敢独立、奉行民主的城市**。**阿波罗盖一世登基时**，**纳罗纳拒绝接受她的统治**——**于是她判定该城被毁**——**以警示反抗的代价**。**一支切利亚斯舰队点燃了城镇**——**士兵们包围全城**——**屠杀所有试图逃离之人**。**该城自此化作一片灰烬废墟**——**仅余冒险者与"焚烧骑士团"**（Order of the Pyre）**的地狱骑士前来搜寻"埋于其倒塌城墙之下的玄秘文物"**。**纳罗纳甚至并非此地唯一的废墟**：**这座城市坐落于一个远为古老的文明之废墟上**——**该文明的居民修建了一张地下隧道网**——**至今已被海水淹没**——**还有 14 道由偏红合金构成的神秘门户**。

**去年**，**副执棒官卡琳娜·寇尔**（Carlina Khol）**发现了一种将所有 14 道门挖出并升至地表的方法**——**甚至成功开启了其中一道**。**此发现被证明是可怕的**：**那些胆敢穿过门户的地狱骑士被改变了**——**他们的身体被撕碎、被传送至遥远的世界、再以非自然的方式拼合回来**。**因此而气馁**——**并由于近期对安多兰开战**——**焚烧骑士团撤回堡垒**——**留待日后再恢复研究**。

**奥拉克斯的到来**：**数年前**，**一位有抱负的独眼巨人恶魔教士奥拉克斯**（Oourax）**发现了一种欺骗两位暴君魔鬼的巧妙方法**。**通过一项复杂的双重仪式**，**她在彼此不知情的情况下同时将灵魂卖给了两位过于自信的邪魔**——**实际上以她单一的灵魂换取了两份未来的恩赐**。**切利亚斯的恶魔教士对将一名独眼巨人迎入其中本就心存疑虑**——**奥拉克斯的这一手让他们紧张起来**——**于是给她冠以"二卖者"**（Twice-Sold）的头衔——**并对她保持距离**。**毕竟**——**尽管有人钦佩她的狡黠**——**多数人都惧怕**："**若那两位强大魔鬼有朝一日想出该怎么惩罚奥拉克斯并裁定她的灵魂所属**——**那愤怒会有多么可怕**"。

**当奥拉克斯得知焚烧骑士团在纳罗纳的近期发现**，**她意识到这是她重获地位的机会**。**她使用了她的第一份地狱恩赐**——**获得抵御那些泛红门户的保护**——**让她得以穿越它们**——**并带回可怕的新知识**。**当她意识到"穿越全部 14 道门户便可开启一道通向冥河的传送门"**——**她便使用了她的第二份恩赐**——**获得对那条河流本身的力量**——**让她对河水的恶劣效应免疫**——**并允许她洗脑任何接触其水的生物**。

**自那以来的数月之中**，**她已在纳罗纳集结了足够兵力以控制该城**——**并已成功穿越了 12 道门**。**一旦她走完全部 14 道**，**她便计划用冥河之水淹没整个地狱之口海湾**——**让她拥有一支由被洗脑的水栖精灵、塞达克西人、以及其他海洋生物组成的大军**——**用以击溃寇兰廷的抵抗**——**并从斯戎家族手中赢得难以估量的奖励**。

**探索该城**：**只要稍加侦察**，**PC 们就应能确定**：**奥拉克斯的部队大多集结在 F 区附近**——**即名为"被遗忘之泪之井"的金字塔楼**（Well of Forgotten Tears，第 67 页）。**没有泽拉米尔的军队**，**她的部队过于强大而无法正面对抗**——**所以 PC 们有数日时间探索城中其他地点**。**关键地点列在下文几页**——**但城中亦有许多奇异的遗物与生物驻留于其闹鬼的废墟之中**。**下文"纳罗纳遭遇"表列出了 PC 可能面对的其他危险**——**尤其在他们寻宝磨蹭之时**。

**冒险假定 PC 们在不携带船员分队的情况下探索该城**——**因为更大的部队会冒险招来塞达克西人的战团**。

> **【边栏】雷玛斯的豆罐（REMATSU'S BEAN JAR）**
> **B5 区床头柜上的红布之下**——**藏着一只附魔的豆罐**——**其中封锁着雷玛斯的灵魂本质**——**并将其束缚于侍奉加勒鲁斯。雷玛斯渴望毁掉此罐**——**但又怕它怕得不敢靠近**——**只好把它盖住、假装它不存在**。**若 PC 取下红布**——**他们可通过一次 DC 30 奥术、神秘学或社会检定"回忆知识"**——**意识到此罐在"困住雷玛斯于此"一事上扮演了某种角色**——**因为雪鬼对豆类有一种本能的恐惧**。**大成功时**，**PC 确切知道此豆罐的功用**——**以及毁掉它的后果**。
> **击碎或泼洒该罐**（**硬度 5，HP 20，BT 10**）会让**雷玛斯惊恐万分**——**让他陷入恐惧 2**。**话虽如此**——**这也会将他从奴役中释放出来**——**这一顿悟只在恐惧状态结束后他才意识到**。**通过识别此罐**——**PC 可用这一情报与雷玛斯谈判**——**以"还他自由"换取彼此冲突的和平解决**。

The Ruins of Narona
A century ago, Narona was a fiercely independent and democratic city. When Abrogail I assumed power, Narona rejected her rule, so she condemned the city to destruction to make an example of the price of defiance. A fleet of Chelaxian ships set fire to the town while soldiers surrounded the city and slaughtered all who attempted to flee. The city has remained an ashy ruin, occupied only by adventurers and Hellknights from the Order of the Pyre seeking eldritch artifacts buried beneath its fallen walls. Narona isn't even the only ruin here. The city stands atop the ruins of a much older civilization whose inhabitants built a network of subterranean tunnels that have since flooded, along with 14 mysterious doorways composed of reddish alloy.

Last year, Paralictor Carlina Khol discovered a way to unearth and raise all 14 doorways to surface level, even managing to open one of the doorways. This discovery proved terrible, and those Hellknights brave enough to venture through the portal were transformed—their bodies pulled apart, teleported to distant worlds, and then rejoined together in unnatural ways. Discouraged by this, and after the recent outbreak of war with Andoran, the Order of the Pyre withdrew to their citadel to resume their studies later.

The Arrival of Oourax: Several years ago, an aspiring cyclops diabolist named Oourax discovered a clever way to deceive two tyrant devils. Using a complex dual ritual, she simultaneously sold her soul to each of the overconfident fiends without the other's knowledge—effectively gaining two future boons in exchange for her single soul. Already wary of having welcomed a cyclops into their midst, Cheliax's diabolists became nervous at Oourax's stunt, giving her the title "Twice-Sold" and keeping their distance. After all, much as some admired her cunning, most feared the two powerful devils' ire should they ever figure out how to punish Oourax and determine her soul's ownership.

When Oourax learned of the Order of the Pyre's recent discoveries in Narona, she recognized her chance to regain status. She used her first hellish boon to gain protection from the red-tinged doorways, allowing her to traverse them and return with terrible new knowledge. After realizing that traversing all 14 doorways would allow her to open a portal to the River Styx, she used her second boon to gain power over the river itself, granting her immunity to its ill effects and allowing her to brainwash creatures that touch its waters.

In the months since, she's gathered enough forces in Narona to control the city and managed to traverse 12 of the doorways. Once she's journeyed through all 14, she plans to flood the entire Hellmouth Gulf with waters from the Styx, giving her an army of brainwashed aquatic elves, sedacthies, and other sea creatures, which she'll use to crush resistance in Corentyn and win untold rewards from House Thrune.

Exploring the City: With a little scouting, the PCs should be able to determine that most of Oourax's troops congregate near area F, the ziggurat called the Well of Forgotten Tears (page 67). Her forces are too strong to confront directly without Zeramial's armies, so the PCs have several days to explore other locations in the city. Key locations are outlined on the following pages, but there are many strange relics and creatures residing within the city's haunted ruins. The Narona Encounters table suggests other dangers the PCs could face—especially if they dawdle in search of treasure.

The adventure assumes the PCs explore the city without the aid of their ship crew troop, as a larger force risks attracting sedacthy warbands.

REMATSU'S BEAN JAR
Hidden beneath the red cloth on the nightstand in area B5 is an enchanted jar of beans that contains Rematsu's spiritual essence and binds him in service to Galerous. Rematsu longs to destroy the jar but is too terrified to go near it, opting instead to cover it and pretend it doesn't exist. If a PC removes the cloth, they can Recall Knowledge with a successful DC 30 Arcana, Occultism, or Society check to realize this jar plays some role in keeping Rematsu here, as snow oni have an instinctive fear of beans. With a critical success, the PC knows exactly what the bean jar does and the consequences of its destruction.

Shattering or spilling the jar (Hardness 5, HP 20, BT 10) horrifies Rematsu and makes him frightened 2. That said, it also releases him from his servitude—an epiphany he has only after the frightened condition ends. By identifying the jar, a PC might use the information to bargain with Rematsu, offering him his freedom in exchange for a peaceful resolution to their conflict.
