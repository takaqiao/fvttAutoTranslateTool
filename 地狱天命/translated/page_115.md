#### D. 复杂观测（COMPLICATED OBSERVATIONS）　**严峻 15**

数十年前，古龙**犄角龙阿瑟沃克斯**（Athervox；好学的雌性犄角龙 天文学家 18 级）与**斯戎家族**做了一笔交易：作为修复她在**沉言林**靠近**塞纳拉**的天文台所需的稀有**天空金属**的代价、以及他们承诺永不试图将她逐出巢穴，她同意收下**埃戈里安**派来的几位天文学学徒，跟她学习。能有些聪明同伴并不令她过于不快，于是她应承了——尽管她明知这些人毫无疑问会一边求学一边窥探她。然而她未料到的是：这六名学徒在第一期学业不过过去了半个十年，便擅自带走了她最敏感的几部研究典籍——这些典籍是她珍藏中无可替代的部分。

即便**斯戎家族**履行了协议的其他全部条款，**阿瑟沃克斯**也从未遗忘、更未原谅这场冒犯。她猜测——**维拉尔**（Verlar，一名以龙族标准而言**近来**才觉醒的巫妖）也许曾操纵或控制过他们。无论如何，他们竟弱到为这种小事所动摇，本身便是另一桩侮辱。毕竟，她可是亲自在传授他们；她的学生不该如此孱弱。

最近几日，她比过去几十年更为活跃，会离开巢穴外出走动——尽管不会远行。**派克长枪骑士团**留意到她的活动迹象——以野生动物逃窜为标志——如今催促玩家角色们去处理这位极度古老而危险的威胁，因为她是一张未知牌，且过去曾与切利亚斯合作过。

**阿瑟沃克斯**自身极度古老，能活到现在，靠的便是聪明、谨慎、时时刻刻提防于心。她的巢穴是一座林间天文台，被灌木丛与丛生杂木环绕，向来难以通行。她有意维护出一条贯穿此林的小路，相对易于步行，正是为了能严密监视这条通道、在访客接近时自保。

#### D. COMPLICATED OBSERVATIONS　SEVERE 15

Decades ago, the ancient horned dragon Athervox (studious female horned dragon astronomer 18) struck a deal with House Thrune. In exchange for rare skymetals she needed to repair the telescope in her observatory near Senara in the Whisperwood and their promise to never try and oust her from her lair, she agreed to take on a handful of astronomy apprentices sent from Egorian to learn from her. Not overly displeased by the thought of intelligent company, she agreed, despite knowing that they would undoubtedly spy on her while also taking instruction. What she didn't expect, however, was that these six apprentices would abscond with some of her most sensitive research tomes—impossible-to-replace parts of her hoard—a mere half-decade into their first term of tutelage.

Even though House Thrune kept up all other aspects of their bargain, Athervox has never forgotten, nor forgiven, this slight. She suspects that Verlar, a lich awakened recently, at least in a dragon's reckoning, might have manipulated or controlled them. Regardless, the fact that they were weak enough for such trivialities is an affront as well. After all, she'd been instructing them, and her students shouldn't be so weak.

In the last few days, she's been much more active than in past decades, leaving her lair, though not venturing far. The Order of the Pike noted activity from the dragon in the form of fleeing wildlife and now urge the PCs to deal with this extremely ancient and dangerous threat, as she is a wild card and has worked with Cheliax in the past.

Athervox, for her part, is extremely ancient and has survived this long by being smart, cagey, and defensively-minded at all times. Her lair, a woodland observatory, is surrounded by scrub brush and overgrown trees that are difficult to navigate at the best of times. She's intentionally maintained a single path through these woods that's somewhat easier to travel on-foot, specifically so she can watch this passage closely and guard herself when visitors approach.

When the PCs undertake this mission, no check is required for them to realize that a natural pass through the woods leads to their destination, but with a successful DC 39 Nature or Survival check, a PC realizes that this way was cut intentionally (and neighboring areas encouraged into overgrowth) to funnel anyone in the region down this pass. That said, all routes not using this path are considered greater difficult terrain and would reduce the PCs' overland speed by two-thirds (making a 2-hour journey into a 6-hour journey and leaving all PCs and their troop fatigued when they reach area D). If the PCs take the easier path, they're automatically detected, as described below.

Athervox is in her lair most of the time these days, making observations through her extremely powerful telescope, consulting her hoard of astronomical tomes, or writing new books about her views on the universe and reality itself. To keep herself safe, she maintains an alarm spell with a mental trigger for any non-animal a few miles away from her lair along the aforementioned forest path, using her Telescopic Scrying ability. If she is alerted to anyone approaching, she immediately uses her Telescopic Scrying ability again to locate the PCs (and their troop, if traveling with them) and gauge their arrival time. If the PCs take the more difficult path through the greater difficult terrain, she might still hear their approach as they snap twigs and cut branches out of their way. GMs should roll a Perception check for Athervox against the lowest Stealth DC among the PCs and/or their troop and determine how much lead time she has to prepare.

When the PCs arrive, Athervox is sitting comfortably on her bed of leaves and boughs but has already used her warding focus ability and cast the following spells on herself in case these visitors are looking for a fight: energy aegis, mountain resilience, and truesight.
