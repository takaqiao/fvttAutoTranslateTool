### B. 铁铸要塞（The Iron Fortress）

不久——铁铸要塞便耸立于玩家角色面前——一座由幽铁与钢铁所铸的高耸城堡。入口是一对硕大的双扇门——它们似乎因热度——而冒出阵阵蒸汽。这道入口看似无法通过——但玩家角色队伍中残存的任何天使——皆怒吼着发出战吼——飞向那对大门——开始强行将其推开。那铁——似要灼烧他们的血肉——但他们咬牙撑过痛楚——向把他们派来此地的诸位神圣众神——尖声祷告求其赐力。又过了几个钻心刺骨的瞬间——大门便被推开足够宽——足以让玩家角色通行。其中一位天使——多半便是最先向他们说话的那一位——咬着牙说：「**速去——我等无法将此门撑得更久。前去——成就你等的命运**！」

玩家角色（与戴狄安——若他与他们同行）一进入之后——大门便在他们身后砰然关闭。玩家角色随即听到——他们的天使盟友——与又一支魔鬼大军——交手的——遥远而沉闷的战声。

B. The Iron Fortress

Soon, the Iron Fortress looms in front of the PCs, a
towering citadel of dark iron and steel. The entrance is a
massive pair of double doors that seem to give off wisps
of steam from the heat. The way in seems impassable,
but with a bellowing battle cry, any remaining angels
in the PCs' company fly toward the doors and begin
forcing them open. The iron seems to sear their flesh,
but they persevere through the pain, screaming out
prayers for strength to the various holy gods who sent
them here. After a few excruciating moments, the doors
open wide enough for the PCs to pass through. One of
the angels—likely the first one who spoke to them—
says through gritted teeth, "Hurry, we cannot hold this
gate much longer. Go and fulfill your destiny!"

Immediately after the PCs enter (with Daidian if he's
with them), the door slams shut behind them. The PCs
then hear the muffled sounds of battle as their angel
allies engage with yet another army of devils.
