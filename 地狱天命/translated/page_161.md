## Page 161 — 第九章：荆棘之中

战事结束之后她有何盘算，则只字不诺。**一名玩家角色可以一次 DC 40 的交涉或威吓检定让卡尔泰萨同意留在基地大营**——**直至安多兰发动攻势，且玩家角色进入埃戈里安城内为止——但她要求在此期间获得舒适的住宿**。

本部冒险之路假定：**伊伊斯夫人**（**Lady Kaltessa Iyis**）即使在「地狱火危机」结束之后，仍将持续运作她的阴谋——尽管她的目光更多聚焦在依斯嘉，而非切利亚斯——她在那里为自己谋划着一片领地。

### 新计划（THE NEW PLAN）

随着「阿波罗盖·斯戎打算将格拉利昂的一部分变为地狱」这一真相揭晓，玩家角色的下一步已经明朗：**他们必须前往首都埃戈里安**，**进一步探明她的仪式……并希望找到终止它的方法**。安多兰众将一致认为此项任务事关重大，**并相信他们原定的「对切利亚斯诸城三面合击」方案**，**应足以制造足够强力的干扰，使玩家角色得以渗透首都与皇宫**。然而，众将建议：**英雄们须在攻势发动前抵达埃戈里安城外就位**。一位将军交予玩家角色一卷**短讯术卷轴**（**scroll of sending**），以便他们准备开始时通知安多兰大军。**之后玩家角色应自行为下阶段作好准备**。

另一位将军确保玩家角色知晓他们在埃戈里安可联络的几位线人——这位将军鼓励英雄们与每一位都谈一谈，**因为他们可能掌握着重要情报或资源**，**有望让玩家角色的任务稍微好办些**。**此外，当玩家角色得知这些线人的姓名时**，**他们或许会有兴趣重逢这些来自昔日冒险的友善 NPC**。

- **厄登·砾掌**（**Urdun Gravelhands**）：自从玩家角色将他从**格拉戴斯卡堡垒**释放之后，这位**铁匠转行的间谍**便延续了他的谍报生涯。**玩家角色可在「议和圆环」（Parley Circle）与他会面**，看他在那段时日里是否查到了什么有趣的东西。
- **琳奈塔·海凿**（**Linetta Seacarver**）：**厄登的弟子**，**仍循其师足迹而行**。她被委派负责物资筹措，**可在「长市集」（Long Market）边缘的一座仓库内找到**。
- **玛伊蕾·费兰**（**Maire Phelan**）：**依斯嘉破狱者联盟**（**Hellbreakers League**）的一名主要副官，**玛伊蕾来到埃戈里安**，希望能争取转化城中尚未完全堕入「地狱信奉」（diabolism）的某些年轻**召唤师**。**她在一处名为「魔鬼之舞」（Devil's Dance）的街区租了一间小公寓**。
- **盖里乌斯·亨德塞恩**（**Garius Henderthane**）：**亨德塞恩家族**一位心灰意冷的后嗣，**愿以协助破坏阿波罗盖的计划为代价，换取一条安全离开切利亚斯的通路**。**他会在「地狱猎犬丘」（Hellhound Hill）的一间酒馆等待有人前来与之接洽**。

#### 发出信号（Sending the Signal）

玩家角色可以自行选择任何方式抵达埃戈里安的外缘郊区。**若玩家角色尚未达到 18 级**，**此段旅程或可作为安插额外遭遇、攒取经验值升级的良机**。**然而**，**玩家角色应尽量隐匿行踪——他们毕竟身处敌后**。**以他们当前的等级而言**，**应足以从容抵达埃戈里安而无须遭遇太多波折**。

**当他们抵达某处可监视该城的地点时**，**玩家角色应使用短讯术卷轴向安多兰众将发出讯号开战**。**数百英里之外**，**士兵们正穿过玩家角色在西冠城、辛吉与瑞麦西纳建立的传送法阵**。**除非他们事先布置了观测手段**，**玩家角色无法完全确知那几处的战况进展**；**但在接下来的数小时内**，**埃戈里安的驻军与近郊军营都会明显地动员、调离首都**——**向玩家角色表明计划正在奏效**。**最终**，**前方的道路看似畅通**，**玩家角色得以靠近**。

#### 事件：落单残兵（EVENT: STRAGGLERS）　**中等 18 级**

然而就在城墙外，**玩家角色遭遇了一支被留下来守护埃戈里安的小队**。这支队伍驻在一座军营之外，**军营周遭立有木栅栏**，**距离通往城内的道路约 15 英尺**。**虽然此遭遇没有附带地图**，**但《探路者翻图·攻城》（Pathfinder Flip-Mat: Siege）很适合用于本次遭遇**。

**生物**：**由于驻军大部分都已开拔**，**此处只剩一名精英地狱骑士裁判官与两名身披重甲的叛教魔站岗**。**那两只魔鬼尤其觉得这种平庸的差事有失身份**——**它们近来便通过与地狱骑士争辩哲学自寻乐子**。**埃戈里安暂时对来去交通封闭**，**这位地狱骑士要求玩家角色在被赶走之前接受盘检**（**尤其因为他们多半看起来不像简单平民**）。**玩家角色或许有机会潜行或言辞绕过此三人**——**但叛教魔敏锐的感官**（**尤其是它们辨谎的能力**）**使此举颇为困难**。**它们对可疑陌生人毫不冒险**：**只需稍加挑衅便会出手**，**且死战不退**。

**THE NEW PLAN**

With the revelation that Abrogail Thrune plans to transform part of Golarion into Hell, the PCs' next steps are clear: they must head to the capital of Egorian to learn more about her ritual… and hopefully how to put an end to it. The Andoren generals agree that this mission is of the utmost importance and believe their initial plan for a three-pronged assault on Chelaxian cities should prove a sufficient enough distraction to allow the PCs to infiltrate the city and the Imperial Palace. However, the generals suggest that the heroes get into position just outside Egorian before the attack is launched. One general gives the PCs a scroll of sending to inform the Andoren army when they're ready to begin. The PCs should then prepare themselves for this next phase.

Another general ensures the PCs know about the contacts whom they can meet with in Egorian. This general encourages the heroes to speak with each of them, as they might have important information or resources that will hopefully make the PCs' mission a little easier. In addition, when the PCs learn the contacts' names, they might be interested in reconnecting with these friendly NPCs from past adventures.

**Urdun Gravelhands**: The blacksmith-turned-spy has continued his espionage career since the PCs released him from Citadel Gheradesca. The PCs can meet him in Parley Circle to see if he's uncovered anything interesting during his time there.

**Linetta Seacarver**: Urdun's mentee still follows in his footsteps. She has been tasked with securing supplies and can be found in a warehouse just at the edge of Long Market.

**Maire Phelan**: One of the major lieutenants of the Hellbreakers League in Isger, Maire has come to Egorian in the hopes of converting some of the city's younger conjurers who have yet to fall completely into diabolism. She's rented a small flat in the neighborhood called Devil's Dance.

**Garius Henderthane**: A jaded scion of House Henderthane is willing to help undermine Abrogail's plans in exchange for safe passage out of Cheliax. He'll be waiting for someone to make contact with him in a tavern in Hellhound Hill.

**Sending the Signal**

The PCs can reach the outskirts surrounding Egorian however they wish. If the PCs have yet to reach level 18, the journey might be a good opportunity to include additional encounters to earn enough XP to gain a level. However, the PCs should try to remain mostly out of sight, as they're behind enemy lines. At this level, the PCs should have ample resources to reach Egorian without much incident.

When they reach a place where they can monitor the city, the PCs should use the scroll of sending to alert the Andoren generals to begin their attacks. Hundreds of miles away, soldiers travel through the teleportation circles the PCs established in Westcrown, Hinji, and Remesiana. Unless they set up the means ahead of time, the PCs can't be entirely sure how those battles are going, but over the next several hours, Egorian's garrison and nearby military camps visibly mobilize and deploy away from the capital, signaling to the PCs that the plan is working. Eventually, the coast appears clear for the PCs to approach.

**EVENT: STRAGGLERS	MODERATE 18**

Just outside of the city walls, however, the PCs run into a small group that was left behind to protect Egorian. This group stands outside a military camp surrounded by a wooden palisade built about 15 feet off the road leading into the city. Though this encounter is unmapped, Pathfinder Flip-Mat: Siege is an excellent fit for this encounter.

Creatures: With much of the garrison gone, an elite Hellknight inquisitor and a pair of apostate devils clad in heavy armor stand guard here. The devils in particular consider the mundane task demeaning, but they've entertained themselves by discussing philosophy with the Hellknight. Egorian is temporarily closed to traffic into or out of the city, and the Hellknight demands the PCs submit to inspection before being turned away (especially because they likely don't resemble simple civilians). It's possible the PCs might sneak or talk their way past this trio, but the deimaviggas' keen senses—especially their ability to sense lies—makes it difficult. They don't take risks with suspicious strangers; they attack with little provocation and fight to the death.
