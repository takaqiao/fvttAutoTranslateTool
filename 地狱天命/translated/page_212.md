## Page 212 — 寇兰廷城邦志 · 概览 + 社会

穿过**赫斯佩雷斯海峡**——往来商船的无尽洪流——驱动着**寇兰廷**的经济引擎——而其海军方面的守卫——则将军事根植入了它的文化之中。重型设防的建筑——在整座城市内——比比皆是。即便是它的称号——「**九堡之城**」（City of Nine Forts）——也低估了它军事占据的程度——寇兰廷的城墙之内——所建堡垒——远不止九座。即便它绝大部分财富——都直接流向**埃戈里安**——而在**地狱火危机**之前——攻击之事——在近几十年间——几乎无人听闻——但数千年来——自海陆两面而来的入侵——将这一身份——深深地——铭刻入了人民之中——而切利亚斯海军——驻扎的军官——也享有一种近乎贵族的地位。

寇兰廷——坐落于一条伸入海中的——长矛形陆地之上。仅在它南面 15 英里之处——是它的姊妹城市**卡利**——它伸入大洋。著名的**奥罗登拱桥**——曾横跨这数英里——但如今——只在两岸——分别——残存着倾圮的部分。步行小径——延伸至海岸线——行人——可在那里——抵达拱桥的基座——而渔船——则每日扬帆出航。**阿扎克缇**与**人鱼**——在海岸附近——并不常被看见——但鉴于该城邻近大海——这般见闻——仍引出一些传言——称这些族裔——卷入了走私贸易。实际上——寇兰廷的走私者——无一与这些族群——有过直接合作。

该城——建造于陡峭的悬崖周围——许多建筑——以悬崖为天然的城墙、或额外的支撑结构。小巷——常常会转为之字形上下坡。它们——可能为街与街之间——提供一条捷径——又或者——尽头是一道石墙。其中一些断头巷——已被重新利用——作为富贵之家的庭院——尽管——许多——仍是这座废墟林立的城市中的——又一处废墟。

寇兰廷的建筑——废墟——比未损者——还要多。要保持城里建筑——在堪用的状态——须同盐风、重力——与邻近建筑——不可避免的崩塌——做斗争。修缮工作——鲜少发生——因为新晋阔气的海军军官——与世袭已久的贵族——都宁愿——委托建造新建筑——也不愿在旧建筑的维护上——花费。鉴于寇兰廷几乎所有的建筑——皆为石造——这种做法——留下了一些危险不稳的结构——它们——可能看起来——很安全——直到一桩突如其来的倒塌——发生。然而——这种被遗弃的产业——为下层阶级——提供了——居身之所——而他们——本来——就被寇兰廷的市场——挤出门外。可悲的是——在一座倒塌的庄园或联排别墅的——瓦砾之中——发现擅自占居者的尸首——再常见不过。**大异教官夏塔尼翁**（Charthagnion）——将「**绞架塌方**」（Gallows Collapse）——即**绞架之墙**部分塌陷、并致使住在附近的 11 名半身人丧命的那一事故——称为——居住在被判违建建筑中的——一项「自然的后果」。

The unending flow of trade ships through the
Hespereth Strait fuels the economic engine of
Corentyn, while its naval protections embed the
military into its culture. Heavily fortified buildings are
commonplace throughout the city. Even its title, the
City of Nine Forts, understates the degree of military
occupation, as there are far more than nine forts built
within Corentyn's walls. Even if much of its wealth
flows directly to Egorian, and attacks prior to the
Hellfire Crisis had been virtually unheard of in the
recent decades, millennia of invasions from both land
and sea ingrained this identity into the population,
and stationed officers of the Chelaxian navy possess a
status akin to nobility.

Corentyn sits on a spear of land that juts into the
sea. Only 15 miles to the south, its sister city Khari
reaches into the ocean. The famed Arch of Aroden once
spanned the miles between them, though only ruined
sections remain by each shoreline. Walking paths reach
down to the shoreline, where pedestrians can access
the base of the Arch, and fishing boats launch on daily
trips. Azarketis and merfolk are rarely seen near the
shore, but given how close the city is to the sea, these
sightings still lead to rumors that these ancestries are
involved in the smuggling trade. In reality, none of
the smugglers in Corentyn have worked directly with
these peoples.

The city is built around steep cliffs, with many
buildings using them as a natural wall or additional
support. Alleys often turn into switchbacks. They
might offer a shortcut between streets or end in a stone
wall. Some of these dead ends have been repurposed
as courtyards for the well-off, though many are yet
another ruin in a city filled with them.

More buildings in Corentyn are ruins than not.
Keeping the city's buildings in working order requires
fighting the salt breeze, gravity, and inevitable collapse
of a nearby structure. Repair work occurs rarely, as
both the newly moneyed naval officers and the well-established nobility would rather commission a new
building than invest in the upkeep of an old one. As
nearly all the buildings in Corentyn are stone, this
practice leaves dangerously unstable structures that
might appear safe until a sudden collapse occurs.
However, such abandoned property offers a place for
the underclass to live when they're otherwise priced
out of Corentyn's market. It's sadly all too common to
find the bodies of squatters in the rubble of a collapsed
manse or townhome. Archheathen Charthagnion
referred to the Gallows Collapse—where part of
the Gallows Walls fell and killed 11 halflings living
nearby—as a natural consequence for living in
condemned buildings.
