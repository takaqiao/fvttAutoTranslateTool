Depending on their method of travel, the PCs have a few days of downtime to themselves while the generals ensure their troops are ready for the big push. **An Unexpected Ally**

On the morning after this downtime, a single rider approaches the camp, waving a white flag of peace. Several Andoren scouts confront her outside the camp perimeter and then quickly escort her into the generals' command tent. The PCs are summoned to that same tent shortly after. Read or paraphrase the following as the PCs enter the tent.

> A beautiful Chelaxian woman stands among the grizzled Andoren generals, looking very much out of place. She wears a dark riding outfit with gold and silver accents that somehow appears both practical and extravagant. A single golden coin hangs from a golden chain around her neck, the exposed side etched with the leering face of a devil. She brushes a stray lock of black hair from her face with a smooth hand, showing her impeccably manicured nails. "Now that we're all here," she says in clipped tones, "Perhaps we could get started?
