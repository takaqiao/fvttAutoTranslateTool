### 潜入堡垒（Infiltrating the Citadel）

针对格拉戴斯卡堡垒的预定攻击使用**渗透子系统**（GM Core 196）。作为一场更长时段的渗透，PC 们会在**渗透区域**（PCs 必须在克服障碍时小心累积警觉点）与**有地图的遭遇**（使用更典型的遭遇与探索模式，时有累计警觉点的障碍与机遇出现）之间穿行。

除典型的障碍、复杂情况与机遇之外，若 PC 们引起骚动或发出显著声响，必须面对"**我是不是听见什么了？**"复杂情况（第 34 页）。一些示例：
- 一场超过 3 轮才解决的战斗
- 创造持续 1 分钟以上噪音的法术，例如施法时间长或产生噪音效果的
- 试图**强行开启**门或物品

格拉戴斯卡堡垒周围的潮坪只在**潮水最低点期间**才能通行，大约持续 1 小时。PC 们必须随潮汐安排进出，因为持续中的海战会让所有不参战的船只滞留岸边（参战的船则正忙于战斗）。这一进入窗口给了 PC 们 **11 小时**在堡垒中完成渗透。

岛屿与其岸边 60 英尺水域之上罩着一道**隐形圆顶**，抑制跨越该界线的传送效果（counteract rank 7，counteract modifier +25）。格拉戴斯卡堡垒位于寇兰廷以北 **20 英里**，琳奈塔提供一卷额外的**传送术卷轴**把 PC 们送至堡垒视线范围内。若 PC 们无法激活该卷轴，她自愿代为激活。

### 警觉轨迹（Awareness Track）

随着 PC 们累积警觉点（AP），格拉戴斯卡堡垒的居住者会逐渐察觉到他们的入侵。各 AP 总数有如下效果：

- **5 AP**：PC 们首次到达此层级时，触发"**我是不是听见什么了？**"复杂情况（第 34 页）。
- **10 AP**：格拉戴斯卡堡垒进入封锁。所有未上锁的门被换上**优良锁**，已上锁的门**撬锁**（Pick a Lock）或**强行开启**的 DC 提高 2。
- **15 AP**：PC 们首次到达此层级时，触发"**彻底搜查**"复杂情况（第 34 页）。
- **20 AP**：所有地狱骑士被召集巡查堡垒。所有障碍的 DC 提高 2。
- **35 AP**：地狱骑士拉响警报，意欲以暴力清除任何入侵者。任何敌对地狱骑士都会离开其岗位前来逮捕 PC 们，触发一波遭遇战。根据距 PC 距离决定哪些生物先抵达；后续战斗者每 2d6 轮抵达一波。

Infiltrating the Citadel
The planned attack on Citadel Gheradesca uses the infiltration subsystem (GM Core 196). As a longer infiltration, the PCs will move between infiltration zones (where they need to be careful about accruing Awareness Points as they overcome obstacles) and mapped encounters (which use more typical encounter and exploration mode styles of play, with occasional obstacles and opportunities that accumulate Awareness Points).

In addition to the typical obstacles, complications, and opportunities, if the PCs create a disturbance or make significant noise, they must face the Did I Hear Something? complication (page 34). Some examples include:
• A combat that takes more than 3 rounds to resolve
• Spells that create noise for more than 1 minute, such as through long spellcasting times or by creating noisy effects
• Attempts to Force Open doors or objects

The tidal flats surrounding Citadel Gheradesca are only accessible during the lowest point in the tide, which lasts for approximately 1 hour. The PCs must time their entrance and exit with these tides, as the ongoing naval battle keeps all non-participating ships at shore (and participating ships are too busy fighting). This gives the PCs 11 hours for their infiltration once they enter the citadel.

An invisible dome covers the island and the waters within 60 feet of the shore, suppressing teleportation effects that cross the boundary (counteract rank 7, counteract modifier +25). Citadel Gheradesca is 20 miles north of Corentyn, and Linetta offers an additional teleport scroll to bring the PCs within sight of the citadel. If the PCs can't activate the scroll, she volunteers to do it for them.

AWARENESS TRACK
As the PCs accumulate Awareness Points, the inhabitants of Citadel Gheradesca become aware of their intrusion. Their Awareness Points totals have the following effects.

5 Awareness Points: The first time the PCs reach this tier, the Did I Hear Something? complication occurs (page 34).
10 Awareness Points: Citadel Gheradesca enters a lockdown. All unlocked doors are locked with a good lock, and locked doors increase their DCs to Pick a Lock or Force Open by 2.
15 Awareness Points: The first time the PCs reach this tier, the Thorough Search complication occurs (page 34).
20 Awareness Points: All Hellknights are called to patrol the citadel. The DCs of all obstacles increase by 2.
35 Awareness Points: The Hellknights raise the alarm and seek to violently remove any intruders. Any hostile Hellknights leave their post to arrest the PCs, which triggers a wave of encounters. Based on distance to the PCs, determine which creatures arrive first. Additional combatants should arrive every 2d6 rounds.

COMPLICATIONS
As the PCs' actions leave behind traces, the Order of the Manacle begins to realize that something is amiss. Complications arise, typically when the PCs hit AP thresholds. However, these complications occur outside of the triggered AP threshold as an appropriate response to the PCs' choices. See page 198 of GM Core for the full details regarding complications.
