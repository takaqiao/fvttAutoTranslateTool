### 一处安全屋（A SAFE HOUSE）

厄登与琳奈塔领着玩家角色——沿埃戈里安街道一路向东行进——避开偶尔经过的魔鬼巡逻——途经一座座受损的民居与店铺。琳奈塔解释说——大多数市民都在躲藏——尚不敢离城而逃。所幸——埃戈里安并非全城都在变易。所不幸的是——此城的领导者们似乎并不在意将平民转移至安全之地。这一行人最终抵达「**五赐**」（Five Favors）街区——这里是埃戈里安最高档商铺、客栈与服务于富贵客户的恶习巢穴的所在地。

厄登领他们走入一处名为「**幕影金窟**」（the Dusk Vault）的店铺——它乍看上去是一家无人光顾的珠宝店。柜台后一位白发店员朝众人点头——按下一枚隐藏的按钮——打开一道暗门——露出一道向下的阶梯。在他们下行之时——厄登告知玩家角色：盖里乌斯·亨德塞恩拥有此店及下方的房间——他们可以将此处用作休整之地。琳奈塔在阶梯底端那扇看似坚固的门上——以特定节拍敲击。门那头——盖里乌斯卸去一连串机关——将众人迎入。

地窖是一处小小的房间群——看上去曾被一伙盗贼或走私客所用。据盖里乌斯所言——他年轻时——曾是「**行猎之徒**」（the Prowlers）这支团伙的成员——他们犯些小罪——在此类空间中藏身。这群罪犯接纳了盖里乌斯——并不在意他出身的处境——因此他对这些房间怀有亲切的回忆。数年前——他借了一笔贷款——买下了上方那座曾经空置的建筑——将地窖改造为一处藏身所——「**正是为这种紧急情况所设**」。盖里乌斯不确定行猎之徒后来怎样了。「我猜多塔里最终把他们清剿了。」

玩家角色可以在需要时——使用这些狭窄的房间休整。空间足够他们修理装备——也许还能制作一些小件——比如消耗品——但容不下许多其他停机活动。若玩家角色想要买卖装备——埃戈里安仍在营业——但附近任何被英雄们光顾的店铺——都不具备从外城订制货品的能力。请酌情决定哪些可供购买。

第二天——**玛伊蕾·费兰**抵达这处藏身所——向玩家角色告知此城的情况。

「**我不会说得轻巧**——」她安顿片刻后开口。「**外头很糟。阿斯佩西娅·鲁加托恩**——阿斯摩蒂斯教会的高阶女祭司——决定利用这一切变易——宣布埃戈里安如今由教会全面、彻底地掌控。她以数十只召唤来听其差遣的魔鬼来执行她的规矩。我们说的是一整支整支的邪魔大军。**大公市长**格拉吉乌斯·阿拉扎里奥似乎对此十分满意。事实上——他根本无人见到。」

A SAFE HOUSE

Urdun and Linetta lead the PCs eastward through
the streets of Egorian, dodging the occasional devil
patrol and passing by damaged homes and businesses.
Linetta explains that most of the citizens are hiding,
not yet daring to flee the city. Luckily for them, not
all of Egorian is transforming. Unluckily, it seems the
city's leaders don't care to move civilians to safety. The
group eventually reaches the neighborhood of Five
Favors, home to Egorian's finer shops, inns, and dens
of vice that cater to a wealthy clientele.

Urdun leads them into an establishment called the
Dusk Vault, which at first appears to be a jeweler's
shop with no clientele. A white-haired clerk behind the
counter nods at the group as they enter and presses
a hidden button, opening a secret door to reveal a
descending staircase. As they descend, Urdun informs
the PCs that Garius Henderthane owns the shop and
the rooms below, which they can use as a place to rest.
Linetta raps a pattern of knocks at the solid-looking
door at the stairs' base. From the other side, Garius
disengages a series of locks and ushers everyone inside.

The cellar is a small collection of rooms that looks
like they might have once been used by a group of
thieves or smugglers. According to Garius, when he
was younger, he was a member of a gang called the
Prowlers who committed petty crimes and hid out in
spaces like this one. The criminals accepted Garius
despite the circumstances of his birth, and so he has
fond memories of these rooms. A few years ago, he
took out a loan to purchase the once-empty building
above and converted the basement into a hideout "for
just such an emergency." Garius isn't sure what has
since happened to the Prowlers. "I suspect the dottari
eradicated them eventually."

The PCs can use the cramped chambers to rest
when they need to. There's enough room for them to
make repairs on their equipment and perhaps craft
small things like consumables, but not for many other
downtime activities. If the PCs want to buy and sell
equipment, Egorian is still open for business, but any
nearby establishments the heroes patronize aren't
equipped to order custom items from outside of the
city. Use your discretion when determining what might
be available to purchase.

The next day, Maire Phelan arrives at the hideout to
inform the PCs about the conditions of the city.

"I won't sugarcoat it," she says after taking a moment
to settle in. "It's bad out there. Aspexia Rugatonn, high
priestess of the Church of Asmodeus, has decided to take
advantage of all the changes to declare that Egorian is now
under full and total control of the church. She's enforcing
her rules with dozens of devils conjured up to do her
bidding. We're talking full armies of fiends. Lord Mayor
Grachius Alazario seems perfectly fine with it. In fact, he's
nowhere to be seen."
