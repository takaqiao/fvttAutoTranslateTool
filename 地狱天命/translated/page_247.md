## Page 247 — 阿斯佩西娅·鲁加托恩 · 背景

### 阿斯佩西娅·鲁加托恩 阿斯摩蒂斯教会大高阶女祭司（Aspexia Rugatonn, Grand High Priestess of the Church of Asmodeus）

阿斯佩西娅生于一个次要贵族家族的特权之中并一生把这特权当作一根铁棍挥舞。还在孩提之时她的奶妈常在为阿斯佩西娅的多次发脾气所导致的砸碎的陶器以及损坏的玩具背锅之后离开或者消失。她的家教则在她的父母听到关于他们教她什么的谎言之后被严厉训斥有时还被重重惩罚。她的朋友（少之又少）——则被迫玩她的游戏而规则若她开始输掉或者感到厌倦便很快会被她以对自己有利的方式更改。她的宠物——……嗯在一桩不幸的事件之中——「**米滕兄弟**」（Brother Mittens）——莫名其妙地失去了其所有的毛之后她便不再被允许再养任何宠物。在所有这些事件之中她的父母都支持并相信她无论她的解释是多么荒诞。

唯一真正能抓住阿斯佩西娅兴趣的是关于阿斯摩蒂斯的神话以及他在戈拉里翁的信仰的历史尤其是他在斯戎家族在切利亚斯崛起掌权之中所发挥的关键作用。她会在讲道以及宗教讲座之中全神贯注地倾听。在这些之中她学到了位高权重者应当如何把权力施加于弱者之上的教诲以及慈悲绝不值得给予。阿斯佩西娅以她年轻的心智尚无法表达的方式渴望那种权力。她的父母意识到这种着迷并继续她在阿斯摩蒂斯教会的教育理所当然地假定她成年之后会披上一名牧师的衣钵。如果他们都足够幸运她便会晋升神职人员的阶层以增进鲁加托恩之名的声望。

在接下来的二十年间阿斯佩西娅超越了她父母的期望。作为一名预备修士她精通了神职生活的基本准则从不失误地指出另一名侍祭何时迟到集会或者说错祷词。作为一名正式的祭司她乐于被分派到埃戈里安较贫困的一个区域因为这给了她一个凌驾其会众之上的优越地位。她常会在那些街道上大步行走对她所认为的不遵循阿斯摩蒂斯准则的人投以严厉的目光并竖起眉毛。最终她以自己的阴谋诡计挤进了——「**午夜神殿**」——的一个职位监管新侍祭的教育并成为年迈的大高阶祭司——**塞普特里亚诺·沃卢斯**（Septeriano Volus）——的副手。

在此期间阿斯佩西娅与另一个次要贵族家族的长子——**尤斯塔特·斯卡尔波**（Eustate Scarpo）——成婚。作为埃戈里安的法庭的一名有成就的大律师尤斯塔特在阿斯佩西娅的眼中是一个可接受的伴侣。两人的家族结合会比任何一家单独所能拥有的更具声望而他也足够英俊。这场婚礼是本社交季的大事但若一再追问出席者没有一人会说这是一场爱的结合。阿斯佩西娅很快生下了一个儿子——**卢多维罗**（Ludoviro）——他自此便沿其母的脚步成为一名祭司。

然后一个无云的夏夜塞普特里亚诺自大教堂最高的一座塔之上坠落身躯落在下方——「**斯戎广场**」——的石头之上。无人知晓这位老祭司出了什么事或者他为什么竟会在那么晚上塔顶但次日清晨官员便任命阿斯佩西娅为教会下一任大高阶女祭司。她立即肃清了埃戈里安上层神职人员的名册把曾忠于塞普特里亚诺的祭司发配至这个国家遥远的角落并以她能更容易地使之屈从于其意志的人取而代之。

在她新的职位之中阿斯佩西娅也取得了对——「**地狱审判机构**」（Infernal Inquisition）——的掌控这是教会之中的一个组织对可疑反对切利亚斯的——「**地狱契约**」（Infernal Compact，阿斯摩蒂斯与斯戎家族之间的契约）——的活动具有管辖权。在她丈夫的协助之下公民被审判机构逮捕并定罪的速度已几近增至三倍。

阿斯佩西娅至今已担任阿斯摩蒂斯教会的女祭司二十多年但她对掌控的渴求尚未得到满足。

#### 战役角色（CAMPAIGN ROLE）

当埃戈里安在第 10 章开始之时开始转化为地狱之时阿斯佩西娅以阿波罗盖二世不在场之时的城邦总管身份统治这座城市。阿斯摩蒂斯派出一支小规模的魔鬼军队以协助她的暴政但是不足以维持对民众的彻底铁腕掌控。PC——仍可相对容易地穿行这座城市但日常市民则被迫蜷缩于其家中否则便会因为对教会的哪怕最微小的犯罪而被魔鬼巡逻队逮捕或惩罚。

Aspexia Rugatonn
Aspexia was born to privilege in a minor noble family
and has wielded it like an iron cudgel her entire life.
As a child, her nannies often departed or disappeared
after taking the blame for the smashed crockery and
broken toys resulting from Aspexia's many tantrums.
Her tutors were sternly lectured and sometimes
severely punished after her parents heard her lies
about what they were teaching her. Her friends
(what few she had) were forced to play her games,
with rules that she would quickly change in her
favor should she start losing or growing bored. Her
pets… well, she wasn't allowed any more pets after
an unfortunate incident in which Brother Mittens
somehow lost all his fur. In all these instances, her
parents supported and believed her, no matter how
outrageous her explanations.

The only things that really held Aspexia's interest
were myths of Asmodeus and the history of his faith
on Golarion, especially his instrumental role in House
Thrune's rise to power in Cheliax. She would sit in
rapt attention during sermons and religious lectures.
In them, she learned the lessons of how the mighty
should wield power over the weak and that mercy
is never deserved. Aspexia yearned for that power
in ways her young mind couldn't yet express. Her
parents recognized this fascination and continued
her education with the Church of Asmodeus,
rightfully assuming she would don the mantle of
cleric in adulthood. If they were all lucky, she would
rise through the ranks of the clergy to increase the
prestige of the Rugatonn name.

Over the next two decades, Aspexia exceeded her
parents' expectations. As a novitiate, she mastered
the basic precepts of clerical life, never failing to
note when another acolyte was late to a gathering or
misspoke a prayer. As a full priest, she was happy to
be assigned to one of the poorer districts of Egorian,
as it afforded her a position of superiority over her
congregation. She would often stalk through those
streets, giving stern glances and raising eyebrows
at those she believed weren't following the tenets
of Asmodeus. Eventually, she schemed and plotted
her way to a position in the Midnight Temple,
overseeing the education of new acolytes and being
second-in-command to the elderly Grand High
Priest Septeriano Volus.

During this time, Aspexia married the oldest son
of another minor noble house, Eustate Scarpo. An
accomplished barrister for Egorian's courts, Eustate
was an acceptable match in Aspexia's eyes. Their
two houses joined would hold more prominence
than either could alone, and he was handsome
enough. The wedding was the season's social event,
but if pressed, none of the attendees would say it
was a union of love. Aspexia soon gave birth to a
son, Ludoviro, who's since followed in her footsteps
as a cleric.

Then one cloudless summer night, Septeriano
plummeted from one of the highest towers of the
cathedral, body landing upon the stones of Thrune
Square below. No one knows what happened to the
old priest or why he was even atop the tower so late,
but the very next morning, officials anointed Aspexia
as the next Grand High Priestess of the church. She
immediately purged the rolls of Egorian's upper
priesthood, sending the clerics who'd been loyal to
Septeriano to far-flung corners of the nation and
replacing them with those she could more easily bend
to her will.

In her new role, Aspexia also took control over
the Infernal Inquisition, an organization within the
church that has jurisdiction over suspected subversive
activity against Cheliax's Infernal Compact—the
agreement between Asmodeus and House Thrune.
With her husband's aid, the rate at which citizens
have been arrested and convicted by the inquisition
has nearly tripled.

Aspexia has been the high priestess of the Church
of Asmodeus for over two decades now, but her thirst
for control hasn't yet been sated.

CAMPAIGN ROLE
When Egorian begins transforming into Hell at the
beginning of Chapter 10, Aspexia rules the city as
its seneschal in Abrogail II's absence. Asmodeus
dispatches a small devil army to aid in her tyranny,
though it isn't enough to maintain a total iron grip
on the populace. The PCs can still traverse the city
patrols of devils for even the smallest crime against
the church.
