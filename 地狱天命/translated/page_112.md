从安多兰军营出发之后，玩家角色们可以按任意次序前往四处遭遇地点。开始每场遭遇之前，玩家角色们应决定由谁来指挥**地狱骑士猎手**部队，依常规建立他们的**小规模冲突纽带**。玩家角色们可在每场遭遇中视情况选择是否携部队同行。每两场遭遇之间大约相隔 2 小时，足以让**地狱骑士**部队按上一战结束时残留的小队节数恢复满 HP。此外，本日内玩家角色们可一次性动用**地狱骑士**的预备力量来「治疗」他们的部队，若小队节数有损失，则补回 1 节。

由于今日**沉言林**东缘云层极低，能见度受限——在玩家角色们完成**遭遇 A** 之前，所有遭遇都将在**昏暗光线**下进行。

#### A. 嚎天（HOWLING SKY）　**中等 15**

距边境最近的地点是一片山地空地，地形之陡几乎可称山岳之态。这里是**誓嚎风灵**（oath howlers）的领地——四只来自**风元素位面**的生物，喜爱与开阔的天空和**沉言林**上空辽阔的视野相通。切利亚斯的密探们找上这些生物，许诺只要它们效命于**斯戎家族**永世不变，便给予它们邪秽的契约馈赠；如今这四只已被召集起来，准备对安多兰开战！

**生物**：当玩家角色们从东北方接近时，四只**誓嚎风灵**正分别盘踞各山头休憩。它们性情凶猛，已为奉**斯戎家族**之命的大战做足准备，因此玩家角色们一进入区域便会发动攻击。它们激动至极，誓战至死。

### FIELD RECON

After departing the Andoren army camp with their troops, the PCs can choose to head to the four encounter sites in any order they wish. Before beginning each encounter, the PCs should decide which one of them is commanding any troops of Hellknight hunters, establishing their skirmish bonds as normal. The PCs can decide whether or not to bring their troops with them to each encounter on a case-by-case basis. Roughly 2 hours pass between encounters, allowing the Hellknight troop to heal back to full HP for however many segments it had at the end of a battle during which it was used. Additionally, once during the course of the day, the PCs can draw on Hellknight reserves to "heal" their troop, restoring one segment to its number if it was lost.

Due to extremely low cloud coverage over the eastern Whisperwood today, visibility is limited, resulting in all encounters taking place under dim light until the PCs complete Encounter A.

The closest site to the border is a hilly clearing whose topography is so steep that it's almost mountainous terrain. Cheliax's agents came to these creatures and promised them unholy bargains if they would serve House Thrune in perpetuity, and now these four have been called to make ready for war against Andoran!

Creatures: Four oath howlers rest here, one on each hilltop, as the PCs approach from the northeast. Aggressive and primed for the coming battle in service to House Thrune, they attack as soon as the PCs enter the area. As riled up as they are, the oath howlers fight to the death.
