### 章节结语（Concluding the Chapter）

**奥拉克斯被击败后**——**PC 们已铲除对地狱之口海湾的威胁**。**泽拉米尔承诺**：**他将集结分散在海湾各村庄的同胞**——**带来一支水栖精灵大军**——**协助攻取寇兰廷**。**在泽拉米尔筹集军队期间**——**还有另一项必须处置的威胁**。**在奥拉克斯的遗物中**——**PC 们发现来自埃戈里安的未开封文件**——**附有给这位失宠的独眼巨人的命令**。

**字里行间清楚地表明**：**切利亚斯的兵力捉襟见肘**——**为壮大其阵营**——**切利亚斯的使者向奥拉克斯发出了新的命令**——**给她一个重获恩宠的机会**。**报告暗示**：**卡利市的反抗者**——**这座位于赫斯佩雷斯海峡南岸的拉哈多姆城市数百年前被切利亚斯占领**——**受到寇兰廷动乱的鼓舞**——**在那里掀起了血腥的反叛**——**从切利亚斯人手中夺回了大半座城市**。**奥拉克斯所携带的文件提供了夺回该城与平息反叛的复杂作战计划**。**就在 PC 们离开寇兰廷之时**——**沃蕊·塔尔米纳里也并未闲着**。**她已派出她的副手罗特罗维奥·佩拉瓦利**（Rotrovio Peravali）——**一位阿斯摩蒂斯的嗜虐祭司**——**前往该城**——**屠杀那里的反抗者**——**唤起一支不死大军**——**并与一个名为"咸眼邪教"**（the Brine Eye Cult）**的神秘邪教敲定新结盟**。**这些计划必须被阻止**——**否则 PC 们日后夺取寇兰廷便无任何希望**。

Concluding the Chapter
With Oourax defeated, the PCs have eliminated the threat to the Hellmouth Gulf. Zeramial promises that he'll rally the remainder of his people from villages across the gulf and bring an army of aquatic elves to help capture Corentyn. While Zeramial musters his armies, there's another threat that must be handled. Among Oourax's possessions, the PCs discover unsealed documents from Egorian with orders for the disgraced cyclops.

Reading between the lines, it's clear that Chelaxian forces are stretched thin and, as a way to bolster their ranks, emissaries from Cheliax have issued new orders to Oourax, giving her the opportunity to regain favor. The reports suggest that rebels in the city of Khari—a Rahadoumi city on the southern shore of the Hespereth Strait that Cheliax seized centuries ago—were inspired by the unrest in Corentyn and have triggered a bloody rebellion there, wresting much of the city away from Chelaxian control. The documents carried by Oourax provide complex battle plans for retaking the city and quashing the rebellion. While the PCs were away from Corentyn, Vorrea Talminari hasn't been idle. She has dispatched her second-in-command, Rotrovio Peravali, a sadistic cleric of Asmodeus, to the city to slaughter the rebels there, raise an undead army, and finalize a new alliance with a mysterious cult called the Brine Eye Cult. These plans must be stopped for the PCs to have any hope of capturing Corentyn in the future.
