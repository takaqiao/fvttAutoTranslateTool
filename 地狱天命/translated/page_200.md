## Page 200 — 第十一章：终极天罚 · B1 庭院 · B2 地牢

铁铸要塞中的房间皆如同洞穴一般空旷——然而——由于戈索克莱克的兵力——已分散于整个阿弗纳之原——其中大部分房间——都是空的。因此——本场冒险并不呈现该城堡完整的详细地图——而仅有数处关键区域。若戴狄安与玩家角色同行——他便会带他们前去他认为其儿子灵魂被关押之处。若否——玩家角色的探索——会将他们带至以下地点。

这些区域中的第一处——便在前门之内。

> **B1. 庭院（COURTYARD）　　中等 20**
>
> 「数件已废弃的攻城器械——散落在这座开阔的庭院之中——就在要塞铁制双扇门之内。石制地板上多处刻有划痕——仿佛被巨大野兽的爪所抓挠。上方的天空——不知何故——比外面更暗——也充满更多灰烬。」

这里的攻城武器——是戈索克莱克对其他领域与位面发动攻击的遗存——它们已沉睡于此数十年。眼下——这位**尼萨利**——更关注他城堡的防御。

**生物**：一对**门槛凯鲁卡贡**（threshold kerulkagons）——在庭院中巡逻——迫不及待地撕碎任何能闯到这一步的入侵者。它们若负伤——也无处可退——故而战至殒命。

> **B2. 地牢（OUBLIETTE）　　轻微 或 低 20**
>
> 离开庭院之后——戴狄安急切地领着玩家角色穿过铁铸要塞——来到这处遍布囚笼的房间——他相信自己能感知到其儿子的灵魂。
>
> 「绝望之声——在这宽阔的房室中回荡——憔悴枯瘦的身影——被困于一只只铁笼之中——苦苦哀求——求一场最终之死的解脱。无从分辨他们有多少人在此被关了多久——亦无从分辨他们的族裔——因为岁月与折磨——几近抹去了他们的容貌。尽管满室痛苦——空气中——却只有金属的气味。」

戈索克莱克——偶尔会将一个灵魂放在此处折磨——作为一种扭曲的消遣。他在戈拉里翁的那些岁月里——他允许其地牢——被沃尔加格哈斯用以为戴狄安·鲁尔布下一个陷阱。

**生物**：戴狄安入内时——他冲向其中一座笼子——大声呼喊「**艾里斯（Eris）！艾里斯**！」笼中的人形——可能曾是一位年轻的切利亚斯男子——但他以失明的目光凝视着戴狄安。戴狄安恳求他回忆——片刻之后——那有可能是艾里斯的灵魂——犹豫地说：「**父亲**？」此时——戴狄安开始强行打开笼子——并向玩家角色求助。一位以 DC 40 **神秘**或**宗教**检定成功的玩家角色——能看出艾里斯不太对——他显得有些不真实——仿佛他的形体边缘——是模糊的。任何向戴狄安指出此事的人——都会遭到他愤怒的否定怒喊——他继续用力拉扯笼门。若玩家角色试图进一步说服戴狄安——这位灵魂并非他真正的儿子——或将他从笼边拉开——这位死墓骑士便会愤然出手。他试图杀掉玩家角色——之后再回到他的事中。

另一方面——若玩家角色协助戴狄安——笼门会在多花点力气之下——吱呀一声打开。戴狄安试图扶艾里斯出来——但他一触及那人形——它便随一声叹息——溶化为一汪蜡水。**沃尔加格哈斯**——一只缠绕着寒霜的**缚体魔鬼**——

[**艾里斯·鲁尔之灵立绘**]

The rooms of the Iron Fortress are cavernous, yet
given that Gorthoklek's forces are spread out across
the Avernal plains, most of them are empty. As such,
this adventure doesn't present a detailed map of the
entire citadel, only a few key areas. If Daidian is with
the PCs, he leads them to where he believes his son's
soul is being kept. If not, the PCs' explorations bring
them to the following locations.

The first of these areas is immediately through the
front door.

B1. COURTYARD	MODERATE 20

A few pieces of disused siege equipment are scattered
across this open courtyard, just inside the fortress's
iron double doors. The stony floor is scratched in many
places, as if by the claws of massive beasts. The sky
above appears somehow darker and filled with more ash
than outside.

The siege weapons here are remnants of Gorthoklek's
attacks on other domains and planes, and they've laid
dormant here for decades. Currently, the nessari is
focused more on the defense of his citadel.

Creatures: A pair of kerulkagons patrols the courtyard,
eager to tear apart any intruders that happen to make it
this far. There's no space for them to retreat to should
they become wounded, so they fight to the death.

B2. OUBLIETTE	TRIVIAL OR LOW 20

After exiting the courtyard, Daidian hurriedly leads
the PCs through the Iron Fortress to this room lined
with cages, believing he can sense his son's soul.

The sounds of despair echo through this wide chamber as
emaciated figures trapped in iron cages beg for the release
of a final death. It's unclear how long any of them have
been here or even what their ancestries are, for time and
torment have nearly erased their features. Despite all this
suffering, the air here smells only of metal.

Gorthoklek keeps the occasional soul here to
torment as a twisted pastime. During his years on
Golarion, he gave permission for his oubliette to be
used by Voulgarghas to lay a trap for Daidian Ruel.

Creatures: When Daidian enters, he rushes over to
one of the cages, crying out, "Eris! Eris!" The figure in
the cage could once have been a young Chelaxian man,
but he stares at Daidian with unseeing eyes. After a few
moments of Daidian pleading for him to remember, the
soul that might be Eris hesitantly says, "Father?" At this
point, Daidian begins to force the cage open, asking the
PCs for help. A PC who succeeds at a DC 40 Occultism
or Religion check can tell that something is wrong with
Eris; he doesn't appear quite real, as if the edges of his
form are blurry. Anyone who points this out to Daidian
is met with angered shouts of denial as he continues to
pull at the cage door. If the PCs try to further convince
Daidian that this soul isn't really his son or pull him
away from the cage, the graveknight lashes out in anger.
He tries to slay the PCs before returning to his task.

On the other hand, if the PCs help Daidian, the
