### 魔鬼之舞（DEVIL'S DANCE）

玩家角色已得知**玛伊蕾·费兰**（**Maire Phelan**，破狱者联盟成员）**在「魔鬼之舞」所租公寓的地址**。**埃戈里安西南的这一街区**，**吸引着年轻有志的切利亚斯人**——**他们渴望建立职业人脉**、**借此街区五芒星形的街道格局引动「地狱信奉」魔法**，**抑或仅是借酒麻痹白日所目睹的种种暴行所带来的震惊**。**地狱小鬼也常在此戏闹避难——以脱离它们的差事**——**然而如今屋顶上却异常安静**——**地狱小鬼们或已在见证城市的变迁后纷纷逃离**，**或正与畏惧不已的居民讨价还价**。

**玛伊蕾热情迎接英雄们**——**尤其若彼此从前已经相识**——**并解释她已发现一项仪式**——**可在他们身处此城期间帮助抵御「邪灵之力的影响」**。**坏消息是**，**她只能在午夜星空下施行此仪式**。**好消息是**：**她已找到一个相对偏僻的地点**。**当玩家角色准备就绪**（**且夜幕降临**），**她便可引领他们前往并施行仪式**。

#### 地狱之颚（JAWS OF HELL）　**低 18 级**

**在研究此仪式的过程中**，**玛伊蕾发现了一处荒废的屋顶宴会厅**——**亟需大规模翻修**——**而这一翻修因官僚红头文件而被搁置多年**。**其屋顶的一部分已坍塌**——**透出上方的天空**。**当玛伊蕾把玩家角色带到这里时**，**他们看见她已在屋中央摆出一圈白色蜡烛**。**她向玩家角色保证仪式安全**——**且他们之中无人需要主动参与施法**。**英雄们只需站在圆圈之内**——**而玛伊蕾会在地板上以白垩绘出连线、撒下香气扑鼻的花瓣并吟诵**。**一名玩家角色以一次 DC 35 奥秘或宗教检定成功**，**便能理解此仪式系**向**黛丝娜**（**Desna**）**请援**——**这便是为何需要见到夜空**。**2 小时后**，**玛伊蕾的吟诵升至最高潮……然后她忽然倒地**。

**DEVIL'S DANCE**

The PCs have an address for the flat in Devil's Dance rented by Maire Phelan of the Hellbreakers League. This district in southwest Egorian attracts young, hopeful Chelaxians eager to make professional connections, channel diabolical magic through the neighborhood's pentagram-shaped grid of streets, or just drink to numb the shock of whatever atrocity they witnessed during the day. Imps often cavort and shelter here to escape their duties; however, the rooftops are eerily quiet now, as the imps have either fled after watching the city's transformation or are off bargaining with fearful residents.

Maire greets the heroes warmly, especially if they've met before, and explains that she's uncovered a ritual that will help protect them against fiendish influences while in the city. The bad news is that she can only perform it at midnight under the stars. The good news is that she's found a location that should be relatively secluded. When the PCs are ready (and night has fallen), she can show them and perform the ritual.

**JAWS OF HELL	LOW 18**

While researching this ritual, Maire discovered an abandoned rooftop feasthall in need of serious renovation—renovation that's been delayed for years by bureaucratic red tape. Part of its ceiling has collapsed, offering a view of the sky above. When Maire brings the PCs here, they see that she has already set up a large circle of white candles in the center of the room. She assures the PCs the ritual is safe and that none of them will need to actively participate in the casting. The heroes need only stand within the circle as Maire draws lines of chalk across the floor, spreads sweet-smelling petals around, and chants. A PC who succeeds at a DC 35 Arcana or Religion check understands that the ritual beseeches Desna's aid, hence the reason for the need to view the night sky. After 2 hours, Maire's chanting rises to a crescendo… and then she suddenly collapses.

You can use the ruined side of Pathfinder Flip-Mat: Feasthall for this encounter.
