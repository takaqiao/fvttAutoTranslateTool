#### B4. 反应训练塔（Reflexes Training Tower）　**低 10**

> 一箱箱的小刀、匕首与其他小型刃器靠着墙摆放。一张告示写着："**在刀堆中找到旗帜**"。

碎冠者旗帜藏在其中一只箱子最底层一把被掏空的刀柄里。PC 可通过成功的 DC 31 察觉检定来**搜寻**（Search）找到对的那把刀。每次搜寻尝试耗时 10 分钟。

**危险**：这些箱子里包含艾里尔的私人刀具收藏——她原本筹划一套精巧计划，让这些刀注入闹鬼，从而割伤任何不走运摸到它们的多塔里。然而，她始终未能完全驾驭那些灵体，于是把仍被闹鬼附着的刀丢进了这几只箱子。一旦活物靠近，这些刃片便会自行升起、动起来。

B4. REFLEXES TRAINING TOWER	LOW 10
Crates of knives, daggers, and other small blades sit against the wall. A notice says, "Find the flag in the knifestack."

The Crowncrushers flag is hidden in a hollowed-out knife handle at the bottom of one of the crates. The right knife can be located by a PC who succeeds at a DC 31 Perception check to Search. Each Search attempt takes 10 minutes.

Hazard: The crates include Eryr's personal knife collection, which she assembled as part of an elaborate plan to imbue them with haunts so they would cut any unlucky dottari that touched them. However, she was never quite able to control the spirits, so she discarded the still-haunted knives in these crates. The blades animate whenever a living creature approaches.
