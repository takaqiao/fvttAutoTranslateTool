### 阿斯摩蒂斯式的影响（ASMODEAN INFLUENCES）

效忠切利亚斯王冠的——**邪魔信奉者**（Diabolists）——在寇兰廷——其同侪之中——位列最高。魔鬼——公开地——走在街道上——有时被绑定于一名凡人——有时则自由地游荡。仅有阿斯摩蒂斯教会——在该城内——经营着官方的神庙。任何希望崇拜——另一位神祇的人——都必须——秘密地——做此事——而对那些其他神祇的——可疑街头牧师——进行的公开逮捕——则在民众之中——散播着恐惧。那些——真心实意——将自己——献给阿斯摩蒂斯的人——则可从那份虔诚——获益——例如——有资格——担任一份——与监管贸易船只的魔鬼协作的——行政职务——或者——得到一份——「**地狱所赐的命运**」（hell-blessed destiny）。

虽然——阿斯摩蒂斯教会——曾经容许——对其他神祇——做出名义上的崇拜——但密拉妮信徒——所未遂的起义——招致了一次——严厉的打压。然而——即便在最严酷的环境之中——信仰——依然延续。伪装过的神龛——在后巷之中——时常出现。未经通告的礼拜——则在废墟之中——定期举行——尤其是——那些——请求**密拉妮**——为寇兰廷——引领出新的未来的——祈祷礼仪。

### 贵族（NOBILITY）

在切利亚斯的贵族之中——寇兰廷的贵族——几乎在各方面——都位列最低。许多人——勉强够格——加入贵族行列——他们倚仗着——一些——有时颇为可疑的——与更显赫的家族的——关系——而这些谱系——并不总是——能由公开档案——所佐证。其他人——则因为某种过错——而被流放——到这座摇摇欲坠的城市。鉴于寇兰廷——长期以来——位居帝国的边端——朝廷的——普遍意见认为——只有那些——失了体面、或是喜欢——穷乡僻壤生活的人——才会考虑——在那里——安家立户——而大多数人——都假定——那些去了的人——是不情愿——被发配过去的。这座城市——的确——为某些人——提供了避暑度假的去处——但更常见的是——它被视为——一个——处置——令家族蒙羞之成员——的选择。

具体而言——给家族带来麻烦的——年轻一代——常被送到寇兰廷——以「**额外的训练**」之名——或被送来——觅得——可能的婚配人选。**大异教官夏塔尼翁**的妻子**伊丽蒂利娅**（Iridelia；粗野的女性人类贵族 4 级）——便是——以此种方式——被送到这座城市的。在那之后的——数年间——这一直是一个公开的秘密——他们——分房而睡——并达成一项安排——允许各自——保有——其他关系。然而——当夏塔尼翁——自**大异教官庄园**（Archheathen's Manor）——搬入**大异教官守备宅**（Archheathen's Garrison）之时——伊丽蒂利娅——出钱——把他的物品——送回了庄园——并亲自——把他——接回去。

「**大异教官**」一名——并非得名于「**拱桥**」（Arch）——而是源自——那时——执政的奥罗登信徒——所下的诅咒——称当**邪魔信奉**在此盛行之时——「**大异教徒**」（the arch-heathen）——便会——毁灭此城——大异教官——担任该城的市长。当——一位大异教官——再也无法——任职时——切利亚斯的王冠——便从——寇兰廷家族——之中的贵族——任命一位继任者。这一任命——是这座城市的贵族——少数——升迁通道之一——而许多——饥饿的贵族——都会乐于——把他们——所能获得的——些许权威——施加于他们的同侪——之上。

切利亚斯的主要家族——保有住所——并在寇兰廷——留下——至少一位家族成员——以便——一旦——大异教官之位——与其附带的——税收——有空缺——便能符合资格。夏塔尼翁的统治——近来——颇为微妙——因为——他公开宣称——他从事邪魔信奉——只是出于政治原因——这一点——很快——便变得——与该职位所需——不相容。他对其邪魔顾问**奥隆托斯**（Oronthos；精明的男性仆魔顾问 14 级；《怪物核心》91 页）——的公开宠信——目前——使阿波罗盖的——不悦——得以——暂被压住。

### 切利亚斯海军（CHELAXIAN NAVY）

在与安多兰公开开战——之前——**寇兰廷封锁**（Corentyn blockade）——最初——专注于——关税执行。在那场冲突之前——日子——花在了——追捕走私者——与——为入侵——做训练上。如今——水手们——在海上巡逻——并在海军船坞——修补受损的船只。

ASMODEAN INFLUENCES
Diabolists loyal to the Chelaxian crown rank highest
among their peers in Corentyn. Devils openly walk
the streets, sometimes bound to a mortal, sometimes
wandering freely. Only the church of Asmodeus
operates official temples within the city. Those who
wish to worship another god must do so in secret, and
public arrests of suspected street priests of other deities
spread fear throughout the populace. Those who
genuinely give themselves to Asmodeus benefit from
that devotion, such as qualifying for an administrative
role working with the devils who monitor the trade
ships or receiving a hell-blessed destiny.

While the church of Asmodeus once permitted
nominal worship of other gods, the attempted Milanite
rebellion resulted in a crackdown. However, faith
persists even in the harshest environments. Disguised
shrines regularly appear in back alleys. Unannounced
services occur regularly in ruins, particularly rites
asking Milani to help shepherd a new future for
Corentyn.

NOBILITY
Among the Chelaxian nobility, the Corentyn nobles
rank among the lowest, nearly across the board.
Many barely qualify for membership, drawing upon
sometimes suspect connections to more established
families through genealogies that don't always seem
backed by public records. Others ended up in the
crumbling city as punishment for some transgression.
Since Corentyn has long been at the tip of the empire,
general court opinion holds that only those in disgrace
or who enjoy backwater life would consider taking
residence there, and most assume that those who do
are being sent against their will. The city does serve
as a summer vacation spot for some, but more often,
it's thought of as an option for offloading more
embarrassing family members.

Specifically, young adults causing trouble for their
families are often sent to Corentyn for "additional
training" or potential marriage matches. Archheathen
Charthagnion's wife Iridelia (raucous female human
noble 4) was sent to the city in just such a manner.
In the years since, it's been an open secret that they
sleep in different rooms and have an arrangement that
permits each to maintain other relationships. However,
when Charthagnion moved from the Archheathen's
Manor into the Archheathen's Garrison, Iridelia paid
for his belongings to be returned to the manor and
fetched him herself.

The archheathen—named not for the Arch, but from
a curse from the then-ruling Arodenite priests that the
"arch-heathen" would destroy the city when diabolism
overtook it—serves as the mayor of the city. When an
archheathen can no longer serve, the Chelaxian crown
appoints a replacement from the nobility within the
Corentyn families. This appointment is one of the few
ways for the nobility of the city to advance, and many
hungry nobles would love to lord what little authority
they'd gain over their fellows.

The major houses of Cheliax maintain residences and
leave at least one member of the family in Corentyn,
so as to be eligible in case archheathenship and its
associated tax income ever call. Charthagnion's rule has
been tenuous as of late since his open assertion that he
only engages in diabolism for political reason quickly
grows incompatible with the requirements for the role.
His open favor toward his diabolic advisor Oronthos
(savvy male gylou councilor 14; Monster Core 91) has
kept Abrogail's frustrations at bay for now.

CHELAXIAN NAVY
The Corentyn blockade originally focused on tariff
enforcement prior to the open war with Andoran. Before
the conflict, days were spent chasing smugglers and
drilling to prepare for invasion. Now, the sailors patrol
the seas and repair damaged ships in the Navy Yard.

Many a commoner hopes to rise to a position as
a naval officer. Rising through the ranks offers one
of the few ways to change social standing in the city,
though a separation exists within the military between
those who were born into nobility and commoners.
