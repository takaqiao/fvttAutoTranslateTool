## Page 32 — Ch2 行动开始 + 协助各派

### 行动开始（The Operation Begins）

傲世号被毁（或至少受到严重损伤）之后，寇兰廷陷入凝滞。锁链骑士团与细缕者（如果加入了起义）搬进仓库。在整座城里，碎冠者升级了他们的反抗：突袭发生的次日夜，凯旋与艾里尔在贵族区大门外把一具乌罗·阿登执棒官的稻草人偶斩首示众。需要援助的市民越来越多，而资源日益枯竭，铃花会则把焦点更紧地放在那些遭压迫最久的人身上。仓库的住处弥漫着拥挤的体味与日益累积的焦躁。两周过去，生存的压力磨损着寇兰廷的起义。对一段关系而言，这股紧张来得太早。

**泰莎·微风的抽签好运号带来了救赎。**

> 午夜深处，一名跑腿员抵达仓库。一个面庞稚嫩的男人喊着琳奈塔的名字，手中挥着一份侦察报告，等着她从睡梦中醒来。仓库里所有正在睡觉的人——包括 PC 们与盟友派系的领袖们——都因这一骚动而醒。
>
> "**抽签好运号**就在地平线上，"那张面庞稚嫩的跑腿员喘着气说。"领着一支舰队。数十艘之多。海军战船正在准备起航。**黎明之后开战**。"
>
> 他还没说完，琳奈塔已经系着靴带了。"我们走，"她对着仓库宣告。"带上你在意的一切。我怀疑我们一时半会儿回不来了。**破钥行动现在开始。**"

仓库一愣，吸收着这份宣告，随后变成一片飞奔忙碌的活动——人们打包铺盖、换上衣物，并为这一日做好武器准备。

破钥行动的第一阶段开始之际，琳奈塔把 PC 们与锁链骑士团的地狱骑士们拉到一边。若锁链骑士团没有以派系身份投票加入起义，则只有卡蒂利娅与她同列。琳奈塔概述了对格拉戴斯卡堡垒的潜入计划。此任务原本并不属于破钥行动的一部分，但她长年的导师**厄登·砾掌**在寇兰廷历史博物馆突袭之前数日便被擒获。

厄登领导暮爪在该城的行动逾十年，并担任琳奈塔的导师。当他失踪时，整个行动陷入险境。他原本与**卡利联络组**（Khari contingent）合作解放该城——这件事琳奈塔知之甚少。她不仅出于关心他的安危想要营救厄登，也希望确保两座城市都能获得自由。

她分享了关于格拉戴斯卡堡垒的基本情况：一座只有在潮水最低时的那一小时内才能接近的**岛屿要塞**。它关押着内海最危险的罪犯。然而它同时也关押着阿波罗盖女王想要压制的反叛者与政治对手。如今由镣铐骑士团掌控，其武装只针对切利亚斯帝国的威胁。来自其他国家的囚犯运送均未获批，尽管曾有请求要把活跃威胁交由其严密看押。

潮坪（tidal flats）将在 **2 小时后**变得可通行，并保持**1 小时**的可通行时间。潮水回归后，须再过 **11 小时**任何人才能再次离开堡垒。监狱的安保意味着其周围设有一道**抑制魔法**的区域以及一道**禁止传送**的屏障——但岛屿本身不受这一屏障影响。

若 PC 们有任何疑问，由卡蒂利娅回答。她熟悉格拉戴斯卡堡垒的布局与永久防御。她可以告诉 PC 们执棒官（lictor）与副执棒官（paralictor）大致可能在何处（即各自办公室，区域 A16 和 B5），但她对斯戎家族的任何特工以及分裂后所作的任何调整一无所知。说话间，她为这座堡垒画了张地图；请把第 32 页的**格拉戴斯卡堡垒草图**（Citadel Gheradesca Sketch）作为道具发给 PC 们。

PC 们出发前，琳奈塔会谈及撤离计划。她强调**没有资源能把他们从堡垒中接出来——他们就是那支接应队伍**。此外，她分享了越狱之后的安排：一处废弃的贵族庄园位于北方海岸。持续中的海战会使任何返回寇兰廷的路途变得复杂，因此 PC 们应带着任何被救出的囚犯前往该庄园，并等候她的消息。

**宝藏**：琳奈塔向 PC 们提供了一卷**传送术卷轴**（scroll of teleport）以助他们迅速逃脱，尽管她也承认该卷轴在堡垒之内不会生效。

### 协助各派（Assisting the Factions）

地狱骑士过去对该城所有派系都下过手，每个派系都至少有一名希望从堡垒中救出的囚犯。为支援同道反抗者，琳奈塔汇编了一份**营救目标名单**。把这份名单——包括锁链骑士团的请求，以及任何已加入起义的派系所提出的请求——出示给 PC 们。

#### 铃花会（Bellflower Network）

铃花会强调：**厄登·砾掌是铃花会的朋友，必须先救他**。他们请求在时间允许时，PC 们再救出**露伊西蒂**（Luicity，意志坚定的女性半身人裁缝，4 级）——一位"朋友的朋友"，因在卡利街头公开异议而被捕。

The Operation Begins
After the destruction of the Impervious (or at least its significant damage), Corentyn froze. The Order of the Chain and the Thin Wisps (if they joined the rebellion) moved into the warehouse. Throughout the city, the Crowncrushers escalated their acts of defiance; Triumph and Eryr decapitated a straw effigy of Lictor Uro Adom outside the gates to the Noble Quarter the night after the attack. More citizens needed help while resources dwindled, and the Bellflower Network tightened its focus on those longest oppressed. The warehouse's quarters stink with crowded bodies and mounting frustration. Two weeks in, and the pressure of survival wears on Corentyn's rebellion. It's too early for such strain in a relationship.

Tessa Fairwind's Luck of the Draw brings salvation.

In the dead of night, a runner arrives at the warehouse. A fresh-faced man calls for Linetta, waving a scouting report in the air as he waits for her to rouse from slumber. Those sleeping in the warehouse wake at the disturbance, including the PCs and the leaders of the aligned factions.

"Luck of the Draw on the horizon," the fresh-faced runner pants. "Leading a fleet. Dozens strong. Naval warships preparing for sail. Battle after dawn."

Before he finishes speaking, Linetta is already tying her boot laces. "We're leaving," she announces to the warehouse. "Bring anything you care about. I doubt we'll be back anytime soon. Operation Broken Key begins now."

The warehouse pauses to absorb the announcement, then becomes a flurry of activity as people pack their bedrolls, change clothes, and prepare weapons for the day.

As the first phase of Operation Broken Key begins, Linetta pulls the PCs aside with the Order of the Chain Hellknights. If the Order of the Chain didn't vote as a faction to join the rebellion, only Cathilia stands with her. Linetta outlines the infiltration planned for Citadel Gheradesca. This task wasn't originally part of Operation Broken Key, but her long-time mentor Urdun Gravelhands was captured days prior to the raid on the Corentyn Museum of History.

Urdun oversaw the Twilight Talons' operations in the city for over a decade and served as Linetta's mentor. When he disappeared, it put the entire operation at risk. He'd been working with the Khari contingent on liberating that city, which Linetta knows little about. She not only wishes to rescue Urdun out of concern for his well-being but also to ensure both cities achieve their freedom.

She shares the basics of Citadel Gheradesca: an island fortress accessible only during the hour the tides are at their lowest. It houses the most dangerous criminals of the Inner Sea. However, it also houses the rebels and political opponents Queen Abrogail seeks to suppress. With the Order of the Manacle now in charge, its forces have exclusively targeted threats to the Chelaxian empire. No prisoner transports from other countries have been approved, despite requests for active threats to be placed under their intense security.

The tidal flats will be accessible in 2 hours and remain accessible for 1 hour. After the tides come back, it'll be another 11 hours until anyone can leave the citadel again. The security of the prison means that it's surrounded by a zone that suppresses magic and a barrier that prohibits teleportation, though the island isn't affected.

If the PCs have any questions, Cathilia answers. She knows the layout and permanent defenses of Citadel Gheradesca. She can share where the probable locations of the lictor and paralictor are (their respective offices, areas A16 and B5), but she's unaware of any of the Thrune agents or adjustments made after the schism. While talking, she sketches a map of the fortress; provide the PCs with a copy of the Citadel Gheradesca Sketch handout on page 32.

Before the PCs depart, Linetta talks about the plans to leave. She stresses that there are no resources to extract them from the citadel; they are the extraction team. In addition, she shares the plan for after the jailbreak. An abandoned noble estate waits to the north on the coast. The ongoing naval battle will complicate any return to Corentyn, so the PCs should travel to the estate with any rescued prisoners and wait until they hear from her.

Treasure: Linetta offers the PCs a scroll of teleport to help them make a quick escape, though she recognizes it won't function within the citadel itself.

Assisting the Factions
Hellknights have targeted all the city's factions in the past, so each has at least one prisoner they want rescued from the citadel. To support fellow rebels, Linetta compiled a list of these rescue targets. Present a list of the requests to the PCs, including the Order of the Chain's request and one from any other faction that has joined the rebellion.

BELLFLOWER NETWORK
The Bellflower Network emphasizes that Urdun Gravelhands is a friend of the Bellflowers and must be rescued first. They ask that, if time allows, the PCs also rescue Luicity (strong-willed female halfling seamstress 4), a "friend of a friend" who was arrested on the streets of Khari for public dissent.
