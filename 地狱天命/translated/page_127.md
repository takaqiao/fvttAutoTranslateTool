### 林间伏击（FOREST AMBUSH）　低 16

由于安多兰军方已知切利亚斯人正试图开启燃狱之门，将强大魔鬼引入戈拉里翁世界，他们派出**反仪式者**（counter-ritualists）赶往散布于沉言林各处的**地脉**（ley lines）以阻断其努力。这一情形以一支非战斗仪式者纵队（部队，使用德鲁伊圆环部队的察觉、HP、AC 与豁免数据[《探路者战吼！》第 178 页]）来呈现，玩家角色必须护送他们。不幸的是，沉言林天然的腐泥与污浊已被斯戎家族的爪牙武器化，玩家角色们必须一边推进、一边为护送对象浴血而战！

这是一场**小规模冲突遭遇**，发生于森林地图上。地图中央应有一条 30 至 40 英尺宽的小径或道路贯穿。本遭遇的目标是保护仪式者直至他们能够施行一次仪式赶走围困者，**或者**摧毁所有进攻势力。每回合末，仪式者以 +25 修正对距离最近的进攻部队的意志 DC 尝试一次检定，代表他们的仪式。成功时，目标部队必须以 DC 35 尝试一次**溃逃检定**（即便该部队通常不可被击溃）。极成时，目标部队自动如同极失溃逃检定般逃离战场，即便它有指挥官也不例外。

**生物**：道路一侧由 **3 棵大猪笼草灌木丛**（dezullon thickets）发动攻击，另一侧由 **3 群残渣怪狂潮**（ofalth stampedes）攻击。每翼侧击由 2 位**树歌者**（tree singers）指挥，他们受切利亚斯人重金雇佣以协助行动。

### 横扫蹂躏（LAYING WASTE）　**轻微 16**

切利亚斯部队不是唯一会设伏的一方。忒尔齐纳与玩家角色们分享最近的魔法情报：一支**军团魔军团**（vordine legion）已抵达本地区，正被庄严授予一位新任阿斯摩蒂斯卫士——名为**奥布里马尔**（Obrimar，狂热男性人类战场祭司 12 级）。盟军军情已知此场仪式地点；玩家角色的特种部队可在敌方最脆弱之时发起突击，赶在他们行军至前线、与未必能轻松应对邪秽的安多兰步兵交手之前将其消灭。

这是一场**小规模冲突遭遇**，发生在一片大型林间空地。空地应足够大，让多支军团魔军团能以阅兵阵形（彼此并列）面向奥布里马尔与契约魔列阵。玩家角色与他们的部队应有足够空间于林缘开战。本遭遇的目标是击杀阿斯摩蒂斯卫士、击杀或赶走契约魔，并击溃或消灭场上所有军团魔军团。每死亡或离场一名两位指挥官，所有军团魔军团便在溃逃检定上累计 –1 状况减值（最多 –2）。奥布里马尔可不花费动作或检定承担任何军团魔军团的指挥。

**生物**：当玩家角色与他们的部队进入位置时，一只**契约魔**正在完成一项复杂的仪式，受任奥布里马尔为阿斯摩蒂斯卫士，并接受此人永恒灵魂作为代价，将 **6 支军团魔军团** 与他终身绑定指挥。玩家角色发起突袭时，那人与契约魔分别承担最近军团魔军团的指挥，孤注一掷地与玩家角色那支特种部队抗衡——尽管被完全压制。奥布里马尔战至毙命，但契约魔在被减至 50 HP 或更低时会用**转移术**逃走。

FOREST AMBUSH　LOW 16

Because the Andoren military knows the Chelaxians are trying to open the Inferno Gate and bring forth powerful devils into the world of Golarion, they're sending counter-ritualists to ley lines scattered throughout the Whisperwood to hinder the effort. This is represented by a column of noncombatant ritualists in a troop (use the Perception, HP, AC, and saving throws for a druid circle troop [Battlecry! 178]) whom the PCs must escort. Unfortunately, the Whisperwood's natural muck and filth have been weaponized by House Thrune's servants, and the PCs must fight to defend their charges as they march!

This is a skirmish encounter that takes place on a forest map. There should be a path or road that's 30 to 40 feet wide running down its center. The objective of this encounter is to protect the ritualists until they can perform a rite to drive off the enemies besieging them and the PCs, or to destroy all of the attacking forces. At the end of each round, the ritualists attempt a check with a modifier of +25 against the Will DC of the closest attacking troop, representing their rite. On a success, the target troop must attempt a rout check with a DC of 35 (even if the troop would normally be unroutable). On a critical success, the target troop automatically flees the battle as if it critically failed a rout check, even if it has a commander.

Creatures: One side of the road is attacked by three dezullon thickets, and the other side of the road is attacked by three ofalth stampedes. Each side of the flanking maneuver is commanded by two tree singers, paid well by Cheliax to aid their endeavors.

LAYING WASTE　TRIVIAL 16

The Chelaxian forces aren't the only ones who can set an ambush. Telzinah shares with the PCs recent magical intelligence indicating that a legion of vordines have arrived in the region and are being sworn over to a newly ordained champion of Asmodeus named Obrimar (fanatical male human battlepriest 12). With the location of this ritual known to allied military intelligence, the PCs' special forces can attack this force when it's at its most vulnerable and annihilate it before it can march to the front lines to engage with the Andoren infantry, who might not be as well-suited to handling fiends as the PCs and their troops.

This is a skirmish encounter that takes place in a large forest clearing. The clearing should be big enough so the vordine legions can assemble in parade formation (abreast of one another) facing Obrimar and the phistophilus. The PCs and their troops should have enough room to begin the encounter at the edge of the woods. The objective of this encounter is to kill the champion of Asmodeus, kill or drive off the contract devil, and rout or destroy all of the vordine legions present. All vordine legions take a cumulative –1 circumstance penalty to rout checks for each of the two commanders who have been killed or who have fled the area. Obrimar can assume command of any vordine legion without taking any actions or attempting a check.
