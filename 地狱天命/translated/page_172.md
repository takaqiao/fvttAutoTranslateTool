自斯戎家族登基重写历史以来，**历史一直对斯戎家族的贵族们至关重要**。**此厅收藏了多件来自斯戎家族过往的历史文物**。**每只展柜上的铜牌都自夸地描述其内的物件**。**地狱女皇陛下偶尔会引领宾客与显贵高位者穿过此博物馆**——**借以震慑、威吓他们**。

**生物**：**此厅内的两座金属雕像并非单纯装饰物**。**它们是「精金哨卫」**（**adamant sentinels**）——**被编程用来守护此处的珍宝免遭盗贼**——**这种事在此层楼极为罕见**。**若任何并非斯戎血脉的人试图打开其中一只展柜**——**这些构装体便被激活并对窃贼发起攻击**。**这些哨卫不会追击逃离的生物**——**因为它们无法穿过门洞**。

**战利品**：**其中一只展柜内陈列着一柄「+3 高等银制 高等畏惧 高等强击 高等雷鸣 三叉戟」**——**某位斯戎家族成员曾用它击败一只密谋反对阿斯摩蒂斯的魔鬼**（**至少展牌上是这么写的**）。**另一只展柜则放着一根「上等奥能威力之杖」**——**据传是阿波罗盖·斯戎一世登基之后**——**当时拉哈多姆**（**Rahadoum**）**的领袖赠予她的礼物**。

#### A7. 女王寝宫（QUEEN'S CHAMBERS）　**中等 18 级**

> **一张巨大的天蓬床占据了这间精装卧室的大部分**。**一面带有三面大镜的暮木化妆台占据一角——其台面整洁——只放着一只首饰盒**。**两侧立着相称的衣橱**——**两者皆大到能塞入数人**。**附带的私人盥洗室散发着薰衣草皂香**。

**地狱女皇陛下阿波罗盖·斯戎二世**入睡于符合她身份与财富的奢华环境**。**此处的家具器物价值——超过一位切利亚斯农夫一辈子所见的金币**——**且全部由她的仆从保持整洁有序**。**幸运的是，**阿波罗盖此刻不在此处**——**虽然玩家角色或许会以毁坏她的物品或顺手牵走她的珍宝为乐**。

**危害**：**阿波罗盖在此室内安装了一对陷阱**——**专为她长时间离开皇宫期间所设**。**她传送入地狱前激活了这两个陷阱**——**只要有人踩到散布于此室各处的众多压力板之一**，**两个陷阱便都会激活**。**「带刃之柱」会自标记好的交叉点降下**——**并能在房间内随机移动**——**将任何闯入者撕成碎片**。**若某一柱将要移入某件家具或与另一根柱重叠**——**则重新掷决其移动方向**。

Ever since they rewrote it after their ascent to power, history has been important to the nobles of House Thrune. This chamber houses many historical artifacts from the Thrunes' past. The plaque on each case pompously describes the item within. Her Infernal Majestrix occasionally escorts guests and honored dignitaries through this museum to impress and intimidate them.

**Creatures**: The two metal statues in this chamber aren't simply for decoration. They're adamant sentinels programmed to guard the treasures here from thieves, an incredibly rare occurrence on this floor. If anyone not of Thrune blood attempts to open one of the display cases, the constructs activate and attack the would-be burglars. The sentinels don't pursue fleeing creatures since they can't fit through the doors.

**Treasure**: One of the display cases contains a +3 high-grade silver greater fearsome greater striking greater thundering trident that a member of House Thrune used to defeat a devil who plotted against Asmodeus (or so the attached placard claims). Another display case holds a major staff of arcane might supposedly gifted to Abrogail Thrune I from the then-leader of Rahadoum after she took the throne.

**A7. QUEEN'S CHAMBERS	MODERATE 18**

> A massive canopy bed fills up a large portion of this well-appointed bedroom. A duskwood vanity with three large mirrors occupies one corner, its surface free of clutter with only a single jewelry box. Matching wardrobes stand on either side, both large enough to cram several people within. An attached private washroom smells of lavender soap.

Her Infernal Majestrix Abrogail Thrune II sleeps in the luxury one might expect of someone of her station and wealth. The furnishings here cost more gold than a Chelaxian peasant would see in a lifetime, and they're all kept neat and tidy by her servants. Luckily, Abrogail isn't here at the moment, though the PCs might take some pleasure in destroying her things or pilfering her valuables.

**Hazards**: Abrogail had a pair of traps installed in the room for those few extended periods during which she would be absent from the palace. She activated them before teleporting into Hell, and they're both activated if anyone steps on one of the many pressure plates scattered around the room. The bladed pillars descend at the marked intersections and can move randomly about the room to shred any intruders. If a pillar would move into a piece of furniture or the other pillar, reroll the direction it moves.
