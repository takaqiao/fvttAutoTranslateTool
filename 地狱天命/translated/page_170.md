#### A4. 晨间室（MORNING ROOM）　**中等 18 级**

> **这间舒适的房间内有一张小型圆形餐桌与两把高背椅——置于一对俯瞰「斯戎广场」的窗户旁**。**桌上一只多层银制食盘已空**。**房间他处**，**一张厚实的长沙发面向房间中央——其前则是一张矮咖啡桌**。**数幅油画山水——描绘切利亚斯的核心地带——挂在墙上**。

**当政的斯戎家族成员往往在此用早餐**——**俯瞰此城**，**同时听取关于当日事务与既定日程的简报**。**窗户让晨光透入**——**但被附魔得如同石头般坚硬**——**且从外面看与外墙融为一体**。**一句口令便可让窗户变得不透明——以遮蔽射入的光线**。

**一名玩家角色以一次 DC 40 察觉检定成功**，**可发现通往 A5 区的暗门**——**该门未上锁**。

**生物**：**西佐博塞斯**（**Cizoboseth**）**是一只「安加沃尔魔」**（**ayngavhaul devil**，**异端魔鬼**）——**目前在皇宫担任「大管家」**（**majordomo**）。**他管教仆从——确保「地狱女皇陛下」**（**Her Infernal Majestrix**）**的需求得到满足**。**他也确保她每天清晨能及时获悉需采取行动的重要消息**。**他的契约并不包括「提供建议」**——**但这只异端魔鬼往往按捺不住自我**。**与他寻常那种「禁书宗教文献库」不同**——**西佐博塞斯有一套个人收藏的账簿——其中记着皇宫的财政记录**。

**西佐博塞斯总有三位「看似普通的人类仆从」陪同**。**这些生物实则是「剥皮魔人」**（**ecorches**）——**一种穿着三位已逝者皮肤的亡灵怪物**。**那只魔鬼习惯性地从一位埃戈里安的死灵法师手中「雇用」这些亡灵作为他的私人助手**——**当它们偷来的皮肤变得无法维持时**——**便换用新的**。

当玩家角色进入此室，**西佐博塞斯热情地招呼他们**——**对他们显然是闯入者一事毫不在意**。**他坚信他与他的剥皮魔人助手能应付任何暴力**——**且乐于尝试腐化一两个凡人**。**他询问英雄们为何而来**——**若他们告知**，**他会很高兴得知他们「正在求知」**。**西佐博塞斯描述他在皇宫的职能**——**并主动提出帮助玩家角色找到他们寻求的物事……只要其中一人同意将自己的灵魂交予他**。**若其中一位玩家角色会施神圣法术、或被神圣化为「神圣**」**身份**——**这位大管家便坚持要他的灵魂**。

**玩家角色多半不会接受这种交易**——**这时西佐博塞斯打个响指——剥皮魔人发起攻击**。**这些亡灵在第一轮各自撕下借来的皮**——**而那只魔鬼则退后施法并使用远程攻击**。**剥皮魔人死战不退**——**但若西佐博塞斯的 HP 降至 60 以下**，**他便尝试逃往 A7 区——并在此过程中暴露暗门的位置**。

**然而，若玩家角色同意西佐博塞斯的条件**——**他便从他的收藏中取出一本账簿**——**要那位放弃灵魂的英雄以血签下**。**之后**，**西佐博塞斯告诉玩家角色此室内的暗门以及通往 A9 区的那扇**。**他告诫他们 A8 区的图书馆员**——**并告知 A9 区的「玛蕾希娅」**（**Mareshiah**）**不会让他们随意翻找她的契约**。**他对她没有权柄**——**承认玩家角色得自寻解决之法**——**但他同意确保此层楼的皇宫守卫不会再来打扰他们**。**作为此条件的全部交换**：**下一次签约玩家角色的 HP 降至 0 时**——**他将自动死亡——其灵魂被发配地狱**。**唯有像「祈愿之仪」这样的强大魔法**——**方能对抗此契约**。

**A4. MORNING ROOM	MODERATE 18**

> This cozy room contains a small, round dining table and two high-backed chairs situated near a pair of windows that overlook Thrune Square. A silver-tiered serving tray on the table is empty of food. Elsewhere in the room, a plush sofa faces the center of the room, a low coffee table in front of it. Several oil landscapes of Cheliax's heartland hang on the walls.

The ruling Thrune often takes their morning meal here, viewing the city while they're briefed on the day's issues and scheduled events. The windows let in the morning light, though they're enchanted to be as tough as stone and to resemble the exterior wall from the outside. With a command, the windows can turn opaque to block the incoming light.

A PC can find the secret door to area A5 with a successful DC 40 Perception check, and it isn't locked.

**Creatures**: Cizoboseth is an ayngavhaul devil who currently serves as the palace's majordomo. He keeps the servants in line and ensures that Her Infernal Majestrix's needs are met. He also ensures that she promptly receives any important news that needs to be acted upon each morning. His contract doesn't include offering advice, but this heresy devil often can't help himself. Instead of the usual library of forbidden religious texts, Cizoboseth has a personal collection of ledgers in which he keeps the palace's accounts.

Cizoboseth is always accompanied by three figures who appear to be normal human servants. These creatures are actually ecorches, undead monstrosities wearing the skins of three deceased individuals. The devil routinely "hires" these undead from an Egorian necromancer to act as his personal assistants, getting new ones when their stolen skins become impossible to maintain.

When the PCs enter this room, Cizoboseth greets them warmly, not caring that they're clearly intruders. He firmly believes that he and his ecorche assistants can handle any violence and relishes an attempt to corrupt a mortal or two. He asks the heroes why they're here, and if they tell him, he's delighted to learn they're seeking knowledge. Cizoboseth describes his function at the palace and offers to help the PCs find what they're looking for… if one of them agrees to relinquish their soul to him. If one of the PCs casts divine spells or is sanctified as holy, the majordomo insists on their soul.

The PCs are unlikely to accept such a deal, at which point Cizoboseth snaps his fingers and his ecorches attack. The undead each tear off their borrowed skin during the first round, while the devil falls back to cast spells and use his ranged attacks. The ecorches fight until destroyed, but Cizoboseth tries to flee to area A7 if reduced to fewer than 60 Hit Points, revealing the secret door in the process.

However, if the PCs do agree to Cizoboseth's terms, he retrieves a ledger from his collection and asks the hero who's giving up their soul to sign it in blood. Cizoboseth then tells the PCs about the secret door in this room as well as the one that leads to area A9. He warns them about the librarian in area A8 and informs them that Mareshiah in area A9 won't simply let them root through her contracts. He has no power over her and admits that the PCs will have to find their own way to deal with her, but he does agree to ensure that the palace guard on this floor won't bother them any longer. In exchange for all that, the next time that the signing PC is reduced to 0 Hit Points, they're automatically killed, and their soul is consigned to Hell. Only powerful magic like a wish ritual can countermand this contract.
