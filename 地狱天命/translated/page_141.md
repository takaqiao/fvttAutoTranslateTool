## Page 141 — 第七章：战争即地狱

整场沉言林之战中向切利亚斯西翼推进的成果。如果玩家角色在完成沉言林之战渗透第四阶段之前便累积到 **25 警觉点**的上限，则燃狱之门将开启至**最大孔径**，地狱**倾巢而出**。如果反之玩家角色在达到警觉点上限之前便完成了渗透第四阶段，则燃狱之门只开启至**仅够一名地狱战将逐个跨过**的孔径，给予安多兰部队迎住浪潮的机会。

带着震动整片大气的脉动，燃狱之门周围的五芒星从暗深红色绽放为**亮如猩红的炽光**——足以盖过日辉。整片战场上的厮杀瞬间停滞，两方士兵都为此景所慑而呆住。一只丑陋的巨手从地狱深处伸出，紧接着一根巨大锤状肢体连着同样巨大的手臂，紧抓门缘、将其撑大。伴随着一声让方圆半英里内每一灵魂体内都共振的**深重低音**，那生物的躯体硬挤过传送门，落地时大地为之颤动。**泰坦级的攻城魔鬼——梅尔托雷贡（merztorlegons）——已踏上戈拉里翁的战场**。

本场遭遇中玩家角色所面对的敌人不变，但所获援助因渗透结果而异。**如果渗透成功**，你应描绘攻城魔鬼们正一只一只奋力挤过传送门；同时塔尔曼铎催促鹰骑士向前推进，将魔鬼们在矿坑边缘形成瓶颈、围堵住他们。**如果玩家角色渗透失败**，则传送门打开足够宽，**梅尔托雷贡两只一起轻松涌出**——他们以压倒性势头攻破安多兰前线，将鹰骑士像炮弹一样捞起、抛飞过自己人队伍，使士兵们陷入恐慌、纷乱撤退；即便塔尔曼铎尽力也无济于事。

如果玩家角色**赢得了渗透**，他们将与部队并肩面对这些敌人。此外，**在第一回合开始时及之后每 1d4 回合**，**塔尔曼铎**（第 250–251 页）使用 2 动作援助玩家角色，然后回到自己的战场。如果玩家角色**输掉了渗透**，他们将孤军面对此遭遇——他们的部队也被迫逃离。

**生物**：玩家角色必须面对**几个世纪以来（至少）首次踏上戈拉里翁地表的梅尔托雷贡**。这只超大型巨兽攀越发掘坑毫不费力，把那些层级当作楼梯——且**不视这些层级为困难地形**。此外，此攻城魔鬼借助其**部队运输（Troop Transport）**能力，背负一支**一等军团魔（first-class vordines）**部队，**每隔一只穿过传送门的梅尔托雷贡也如此运载**。**龙兽飞群**（drake flight）在传送门上方空中开战，**一等骑兵队**（first-class cavalry）则在能容下它的、最接近发掘坑的平地空间上开战。**梅尔托雷贡**与其绑定的军队**战至毙命**。

**危害**：梅尔托雷贡贴近燃狱之门，使他们获得保护——地狱之界向外伸出燃焰指节，赋力于其战将。**此危害不直接伤及玩家角色**。

**例行动作（1 动作）**　在其回合中，邪魔生力危害对燃狱之门 200 英尺内的梅尔托雷贡与其绑定军队赋能。它给予攻城魔鬼**再生 20**，并将他们对**圣洁**的弱点从 20 降至 10。军队获得**再生 10**，对范围伤害与溅射伤害的弱点 –5。这些效果持续 1 回合。

Chelaxians' western flank throughout the Battle of the Whisperwood. If the PCs accrued the maximum of 25 Awareness Points before completing Phase 4 of the Battle of the Whisperwood infiltration, the Inferno Gate opens to its maximum aperture, unleashing Hell's full might. If instead the PCs finished Phase 4 of the Battle of the Whisperwood infiltration before reaching the maximum Awareness Points threshold, the Inferno Gate opens just wide enough for one diabolic champion to step through at a time, giving the Andoren forces a chance to stem the tide.

With an atmosphere-shuddering pulse, the pentagram around the Inferno Gate flares from dark carmine to bright scarlet, burning so bright as to eclipse the light of the sun. All warfare stops for a moment as soldiers from both sides are caught in awe of this spectacle. A grotesque hand reaches out from the depths of Hell, followed by a massive hammer-like appendage attached to an equally large arm, gripping the edges of the portal and stretching it wide. With a deep bass sound that reverberates through the bodies of every soul for a half-mile radius, the creature's body shoves itself through the portal, landing with an earthshaking thud. The titanic merztorlegons—siege devils—have now emerged onto the battlefields of Golarion.

The enemies the PCs face in this encounter are unchanged, but the amount of help the PCs receive differs based on the result of their infiltration. If the infiltration succeeded, you should describe the siege devils as struggling to push through the portal one by one while Talmandor urges the Eagle Knights forward to bottleneck the devils at the edge of the excavation pit and hem them in. If the PCs failed the infiltration, however, the portal opens wide enough that the merztorlegons step through two at a time with ease, overwhelming the Andoren front line—picking up Eagle Knights and lobbing them like cannon balls through their own troops, terrorizing the soldiers into a panicked and disorganized retreat despite Talmandor's best efforts.

If the PCs won the infiltration, they face these enemies with their troops alongside them. Additionally, at the beginning of the first round and every 1d4 rounds after, Talmandor (pages 250–251) uses 2 actions to assist the PCs before resuming his own battles. If the PCs failed the infiltration, they face this encounter alone as their troops are also forced to flee.

Creatures: The PCs must face the first merztorlegon to step onto the face of Golarion in centuries (at least). This gargantuan beast has no trouble climbing up the excavation pit, treating them like stairs—and not treating these levels as difficult terrain. In addition, the siege devil carries a first-class vordines troop using its Troop Transport ability, as does every second merztorlegon that passes through the portal. The drake flight begins the combat in the air above the portal, and the first-class cavalry begins the combat in the closest space to the excavation pit where it can fit on flat ground. The merztorlegon and their bound armies fight until destroyed.

Hazards: The merztorlegon's proximity to the Inferno Gate gives them some protection as the realm of Hell reaches out fiery fingers to empower its champions. This hazard isn't directly harmful to the PCs.
