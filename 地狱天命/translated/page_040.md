**宝藏**：厄登的 **+1 重击 强力出击护手缠绕**（handwraps of mighty blows）以及他的投石带（连同 5 颗**冰冻投石弹**）位于东北角的储物柜中。装有试剂的那只储物柜里还包括从路登·马尔迪努斯那里取走的**钥匙**——这是他位于堡垒以外约 8 英里那处庄园的钥匙；他服役期间曾被准许在该处居住。

#### A9. 中央楼梯（Central Stairwell）

> 敞开的楼梯通往堡垒的其他楼层。最后一级台阶因多年磨损而下凹。

这些楼梯最终通向尖塔顶端的**执棒官走廊**（Lictor's Walk），亦通往石下数千英尺的高级监狱。若 PC 们向上攀爬，他们会发现格拉戴斯卡堡垒的更多楼层——全部由残余的镣铐骑士团成员居住。向上走会让 PC 们经过"**地狱骑士住所**"障碍并进入区域 A16。向下楼梯则把 PC 们带入高级监狱的入口楼梯（区域 B1）。

> **地狱骑士住所（HELLKNIGHT QUARTERS）**　**障碍 10**
> 渗透点 2（团队）；克服：DC 28 体技；DC 30 欺瞒或匿踪
>
> 你在攀登楼梯时遭遇正进行日常活动的地狱骑士。诡计行为——例如溜入楼梯井或在隐蔽空间中保持平衡——让你得以避免被注意。或者，一次时机精确的分心也可在地狱骑士的注意力上创造一个空隙。

**奖励**：PC 们首次克服"地狱骑士住所"障碍时，授予 20 XP。

#### A10. 值班室（Duty Chamber）　**严峻 10**

> 两张办公桌在一盏冷峻灯光下相对而置。蹲坐的凳子提供了工作时的座位。守卫值勤的杂物覆盖着桌面。

当值的高级地狱骑士在此进行监狱行政工作，文件井然有序地记录于多个文件柜与抽屉之中。

**文件（Files）**：这些文件详细记录了自骑士团分裂以来格拉戴斯卡堡垒发生的诸多事件。
- 首先，**利刃大师路登·马尔迪努斯**因公开反抗执棒官而被免职，目前被关押在高级监狱等待审判。
- 其次，强大的恶魔**手术刀夫人**（Lady Scalpel）从高级监狱**西翼**逃脱——她在监狱（以及岛屿一侧）撕开了一个洞。结果，该翼已被疏空，原住者被重新分配至**东翼**。这一调动主要通过把原本单人居住的牢房改为双人居住完成。
- **厄登·砾掌**的被捕及其随后被押至格拉戴斯卡堡垒一事被归类为"**重要囚犯转运**"。备注指出他已被转入 **3101 号牢房**（区域 B6），并以星号标注尚待审判。空白处有手写注："**Racid 想亲自处理他**。"
- 最后，名册显示**利亚布里斯·科尔博拉**目前不在堡垒——她正前去寇兰廷的战事增援。当前堡垒中只有一支骨干兵力，由斯戎宫廷派来的特工辅助。

**生物**：目前一名斯戎特工正在整理镣铐骑士团的文件。两名地狱骑士在监督他——主要是确保过程中原件不会神秘消失。若 PC 们进入此室（或以其他方式吸引了其中居住者的注意，例如在区域 A9 制造显著噪音），他们会发起攻击。

A9. CENTRAL STAIRWELL
Open staircases lead to other levels of the citadel. The final step dips with years of accumulated wear.

These stairs eventually lead to the Lictor's Walk at the top of the spire and to the high prison thousands of feet beneath the stone. If the PCs ascend, they discover additional floors of Citadel Gheradesca, all inhabited by the remaining Order of the Manacle Hellknights. Heading upstairs leads the PCs through the Hellknight Quarters obstacle and into area A16. The stairs leading down bring the PCs into the high prison's entrance staircase (area B1).

HELLKNIGHT QUARTERS	OBSTACLE 10
Infiltration Points 2 (group); Overcome DC 28 Acrobatics, or DC 30 Deception or Stealth
You encounter Hellknights engaged in their daily activities as you ascend the stairs. Acts of subterfuge, such as sliding into stairwells or balancing in hidden spaces, enable you to avoid notice. Alternatively, a well-timed distraction creates an opening in the Hellknights' attention.

Reward: The first time the PCs overcome the Hellknight Quarters obstacle, award them 20 XP.

A10. DUTY CHAMBER	MODERATE 10
Two desks face each other beneath a stark light. Squat stools provide a place to sit while working. The detritus of guard duty covers the tabletops.

The ranking Hellknight on duty uses this room for prison administration, documented in well-organized files and drawers of paperwork.

Files: These files detail events in Citadel Gheradesca since the order split. First, the Master of Blades Leuden Mardinus was removed from his post after an open rebellion against the lictor. He's now in detention in the high prison awaiting trial. Second, the powerful demon Lady Scalpel escaped from the western wing of the high prison, where she tore a hole through the prison (and the side of the island). As a result, the wing has been emptied and the inhabitants reassigned to the east wing. This move has mostly been accomplished by doubling up prisoners in what were typically single-person cells.

The arrest of Urdun Gravelhands and his subsequent transport to Citadel Gheradesca is categorized as "notable prisoner transport." It notes that he's been transferred into Cell 3101 (area B6), with an asterisk to indicate that he's still pending trial. A note scrawled in the margin reads, "Racid wants to deal with him personally."

Finally, the roster notes that Liabriz Corborra is currently away from the citadel, lending reinforcements to the ongoing battle in Corentyn. The current contingent in Citadel Gheradesca consists of a skeleton crew aided by agents sent from Thrune's court.

Creatures: One of the Thrune agents is currently sorting through the Order of the Manacle's files. Two Hellknights supervise him, mostly to ensure the records' original copies don't mysteriously disappear during the process. If the PCs enter this room (or if they draw the occupants' attention otherwise, such as by making considerable noise in area A9), they attack.
