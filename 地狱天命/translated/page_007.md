## Page 7 — 战役概览（续）

无论如何，PC 们都是 10 级冒险者——已经身经百战的英雄。他们将援助寇兰廷、号召周边国家一同抗击切利亚斯，并最终把阿维斯坦（Avistan）西南拯救出沉沦的命运。请务必让你的玩家阅读 paizo.com 上免费提供的《玩家指南》（Player's Guide），为本冒险做好准备。

### 子系统（Subsystems）

本战役使用了若干子系统。你需要熟悉胜利点机制（Victory Point mechanics，《主持人核心》GM Core 184），尤其是影响力子系统（influence subsystem，《主持人核心》187）。冒险还会周期性使用《主持人核心》中的其他机制，例如追逐（chases）。

不过最常用的子系统是**前哨战**（skirmish combat），详见《战吼！》（Pathfinder Battlecry!）第 158-161 页。它几乎在每一章中都会出现——有时甚至多次。你将希望在第 28 页的首次前哨战之前就熟悉这些规则。

### 人物与地点（People and Places）

下文是本战役中关键人物与地点的简要介绍。

**阿波罗盖·斯戎二世（Abrogail Thrune II）**：切利亚斯当今的统治者，造诣极深的术士，亦是阿波罗盖一世的后裔——后者于近一个世纪前将切利亚斯与自己的家族签订给了阿斯摩蒂斯。

**奥罗登拱桥（Arch of Aroden）**：巨型石柱托举起这座古老而残破的拱桥，跨越赫斯佩雷斯海峡（Hespereth Strait）的绝大部分水域。其拱孔之大，连大型船只都可在石柱之间通行。

**寇兰廷（Corentyn）**：位于切利亚斯西南边境的港口城市，控制着进出内海的航运。

**多塔里（Dottari）**：对切利亚斯警察的称呼，各城市各自维持自己的多塔里武装。

**鹰骑士团（Eagle Knights）**：安多兰的精锐战士，和平时期他们以小队形式行动，在整个内海地区颠覆暴君。

**埃戈里安（Egorian）**：地处国家中部，切利亚斯首都以彰显斯戎家族权势与其魔鬼盟友的可怖建筑著称。

Subsystems
This campaign uses several subsystems. You'll want to be familiar with Victory Point mechanics (GM Core 184), especially the influence subsystem (GM Core 187). The adventure also periodically uses other GM Core mechanics, such as chases.

That said, the most common subsystem is skirmish combat, detailed on pages 158–161 of Pathfinder Battlecry!. It appears in almost every chapter—sometimes numerous times. You'll want to be familiar with the rules before the first skirmish combat on page 28.

People and Places
The following is a brief overview of key people and places featured in this campaign.

Abrogail Thrune II: Cheliax's current ruler, an accomplished sorcerer, and a descendant of Abrogail I, who first signed Cheliax and her family over to Asmodeus nearly a century ago.

Arch of Aroden: Massive pylons support this ancient, broken bridge that spans most of the Hespereth Strait. Its arches are so wide that even large ships can sail between the columns.

Corentyn: This port city along Cheliax's southwest border controls traffic into and out of the Inner Sea.

Dottari: A term for Cheliax's police, with different cities each maintaining their own forces.

Eagle Knights: Andoran's elite soldiers who (in times of peace) operate in small groups to undermine tyrants throughout the Inner Sea region.

Egorian: Located near the country's center, Cheliax's capital features fearsome architecture
