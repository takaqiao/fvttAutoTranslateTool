## Page 33 — Ch2 协助各派（续）：碎冠者 / 锁链骑士团 / 细缕者

#### 碎冠者（Crowncrushers）

碎冠者的名单上有数个被写下、划掉、又重新写上的名字。剩下的第一个名字并非营救目标，而是一个请求：**执棒官乌罗·阿登**，标注"**必须死**"。后面跟着另一个名字：**焦掌**（Scorchpalm，富于发明的女性牛头人无政府主义者，7 级），被列为碎冠者的"**修补师与炸弹酿造者**"，因纵火未遂被捕。

#### 锁链骑士团（Order of the Chain）

锁链骑士团（视第 1 章中地狱骑士们的投票结果而定，可能只有卡蒂利娅一人）并没有一份要释放的囚犯名单。相反，他们有一份**镣铐骑士团中危险成员**的名单。处死他们并不合适——他们必须被擒拿归案，绳之以法。这些目标是：**执棒官乌罗·阿登**、**副执棒官拉西德·伊利托**（Paralictor Racid Illito）、以及**利亚布里斯·科尔博拉**（Liabriz Corborra）。乌罗曾提及把一位被称为**弃誓者**（the Foresworn）的地狱骑士**死墓骑士**（graveknight）"**放到那些不忏悔者头上**"，卡蒂利娅希望 PC 们能一并阻止此人。若加入了起义，埃默汀会感叹自己无法亲自带队潜入格拉戴斯卡堡垒、把阿登执棒官从其位上除掉。

尽管如此，卡蒂利娅确实知道一名她希望释放的囚犯：80 岁的**利刃大师路登·马尔迪努斯**（Master of Blades Leuden Mardinus，年长的男性人类地狱骑士，8 级）。他遵循正当指挥链程序抗议镣铐骑士团的政权更替，却仍因此被囚。

他们也希望救出所有**对骑士团演变结果心存悔意**的地狱骑士。要重建锁链骑士团，必须允许背离者归来。任何愿意公开谴责镣铐骑士团并希望回归的地狱骑士都可以这样做——但需要先进行一次**自审**（Reckoning）。

PC 们进入格拉戴斯卡堡垒之前，卡蒂利娅警告他们要小心其他囚犯。锁链骑士团并不习惯囚禁无辜者，但**无法仅凭外表判断一个人的道德立场**。

#### 细缕者（Thin Wisps）

随着阿波罗盖·克鲁恩获释，细缕者派系里目前没有成员被关押于堡垒。然而，他们列出了几个名字——全是**过往各场革命的成员**。虽然其中有些革命取得了成功，但这些囚犯都在革命胜利之前被擒。这些囚犯都不是细缕者的成员，但他们希望释放那些**著名反叛者**能鼓舞民众。

CROWNCRUSHERS
The Crowncrushers' list has various names that have been written, scratched out, and then written over again. The first name on the remaining list isn't a rescue but a request: Lictor Uro Adom, noted as "must die." Another name follows: Scorchpalm (inventive female minotaur anarchist 7), listed as the Crowncrushers' "tinkerer and bomb-brewer" and arrested after an arson attempt.

ORDER OF THE CHAIN
The Order of the Chain (or just Cathilia, depending on how the Hellknights voted in Chapter 1) doesn't have a list of prisoners to release. Rather, they have a list of dangerous members of the Order of the Manacle. Death wouldn't be appropriate for them; they need to be rounded up so that they can be brought to justice. These targets are Lictor Uro Adom, Paralictor Racid Illito, and Liabriz Corborra. Uro's made comments about unleashing a Hellknight graveknight known as the Foresworn "on the unrepentant," and Cathilia hopes the PCs can stop this individual as well. If she joined the rebellion, Emertine laments that she can't lead the infiltration into Citadel Gheradesca and remove Lictor Adom from his post herself.

Still, Cathilia does know of one prisoner who she hopes to release, the 80-year-old Master of Blades Leuden Mardinus (elderly male human Hellknight 8). He followed the proper chain of authority to protest the regime change to the Order of the Manacle and was still imprisoned for it.

They also wish to rescue all other Hellknights who regret what became of the order. To rebuild the Order of the Chain, apostates must be permitted to return. Any Hellknight who denounces the Order of the Manacle and wishes to return may do so, though a reckoning will be required.

Before the PCs venture into Citadel Gheradesca, Cathilia warns them to be wary of the other prisoners. The Order of the Chain didn't take to locking up innocents, and it's impossible to tell someone's morals based on their appearance.
