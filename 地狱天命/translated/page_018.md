### 任务一：文物回收（Artifact Recovery）

铃花会在"**邪叉酒馆**"（Wicked Fork）会面——西泡区（West Drenches）一家不起眼的酒馆。它以表演者繁复、酒水浓烈著称，同时也掩藏着穿城而过的地下密道。无声剪影带 PC 们走这些密道，今日便不必与多塔里周旋。进入前，她卸下伪装，以**厄内萨·法兰纳**（Ernetza Farrunner，狡黠的女性半身人盗贼，9 级——酒馆的招待与跑腿）的身份引他们入内。**内尔德·哈瓦萨乌**（Neld Havasavu，话多的男性人类酒馆主人，5 级）冲她示意进入一间内屋，深知她与铃花会的联系。

无声剪影概述博物馆突袭的关键：是的，博物馆藏有被寇兰廷当局判为非法持有之物。这些文物包括建城时期的文献——恶魔教士们想要篡改这些文献并销毁原件；博物馆地下室还藏有最早一代铃花会"耕耘者"（tillers）所制的被毯，她认为那些更多是情感价值而非历史核心。摧毁这些物件即是摧毁历史，理应不能落入多塔里之手。

多塔里已占据博物馆地面层，唯一的进入方式是一场偷天换日的**潜入**！文物回收使用**渗透子系统**（infiltration subsystem，《主持人核心》第 196 页）。无声剪影已安排在博物馆外举行一场抗议作为掩护，PC 们则从后门潜入。PC 们可对每个挑战尝试相应检定，结果如下：

| 结果 | 影响 |
|---|---|
| 大成功 | PC 们获得 2 **渗透点**（Infiltration Points） |
| 成功 | PC 们获得 1 渗透点 |
| 失败 | PC 们累计 1 **警觉点**（Awareness Point） |
| 大失败 | PC 们累计 2 警觉点 |

PC 们靠近历史博物馆时，他们发现多塔里在博物馆四周巡逻甚密。无声剪影与铃花会已安排了一场抗议，但持续不会太久。PC 们仅有 **1 小时**进入博物馆、深入地下室、找到文物、撤出。每渗透轮代表 10 分钟。

#### 准备（Preparation）

由于历史博物馆的突袭仍在持续，没有时间为这次回收做准备！代以每名 PC 从此前与多塔里突袭的互动中获得 1 **优势点**（Edge Point）。

#### 目标一：进入博物馆（Objective 1: Into the Museum）

每名 PC 必须设法进入博物馆——攀越围墙、分散守卫注意，或绕过展品潜行。

> **越墙（OVER THE WALL）**　**障碍**
> 渗透点 1（个人）；克服：DC 25 运动或体技，或 DC 29 匿踪。
> 博物馆正门是抗议与多塔里施暴的现场，唯一进入方式是翻越博物馆的后墙。

> **分散守卫（DISTRACTING THE GUARD）**　**障碍**
> 渗透点 2（个人）；克服：DC 25 交涉或欺瞒，或 DC 27 表演。
> 大多数多塔里已转向去镇压前方的抗议，馆内只剩下骨干人手。

> **穿越展厅（THROUGH THE EXHIBITS）**　**障碍**
> 渗透点 1（个人）；克服：DC 27 社会、DC 29 制作，或 DC 31 察觉。
> 从建筑背面进入时，展厅迷宫几乎完全摸不着头绪。从结构与流线考虑，反而能洞悉地下室入口的所在。

Artifact Recovery
The Bellflower Network meets in the Wicked Fork, an unassuming tavern in the West Drenches. It boasts elaborate performers and strong drinks while concealing subterranean tunnels that lead through the city. Silent Silhouette leads the PCs through these tunnels, so they don't contend with the dottari today. Before entering, she removes her disguise and leads them in as Ernetza Farrunner (sneaky female halfling rogue 9), the tavern's server and delivery runner. Neld Havasavu (talkative male human tavern keeper 5) waves her into a back room, aware of her connection to the Bellflower Network.

Silent Silhouette outlines the problem with the museum raid: Yes, the museum had been keeping artifacts deemed illegal to own by the Corentyn government. These artifacts included documents from the founding of the city that the diabolists sought to have revised and the originals destroyed. Several quilts from the first generation of Bellflower tillers were also kept in the museum's basement, though she considers those more sentimental than vital pieces of history. Destruction of these objects are destruction of history, so it's only right they avoid falling into the hands of the dottari.

The dottari have overtaken the museum grounds, so the only way in is through a heist! Artifact Recovery uses the infiltration subsystem (GM Core 196). Silent Silhouette has arranged for a protest to occur outside the museum as a distraction while the PCs sneak in through the back. The PCs can attempt an appropriate check to overcome each challenge, with the following results.

Critical Success The PCs gain 2 Infiltration Points.
Success The PCs gain 1 Infiltration Points.
Failure The PCs accrue 1 Awareness Point.
Critical Failure The PCs accrue 2 Awareness Points.

As the PCs approach the Museum of History, they find the dottari heavily patrolling both the grounds. Silent Silhouette and the Bellflower Network have arranged for a protest, but it won't last for long. The PCs have only 1 hour to enter the museum, access the basement, find the artifacts, and get out. Each infiltration round represents 10 minutes.

PREPARATION
With the ongoing raid on the Museum of History, there's no time to prepare for the recovery! Instead, each PC gains 1 Edge Point from their previous interaction with the dottari raid.

OBJECTIVE 1: INTO THE MUSEUM
Each PC must access the museum, either by scaling the wall, distracting guards, or sneaking past the exhibits.

OVER THE WALL	OBSTACLE
Infiltration Points 1 (individual); Overcome DC 25 Athletics or Acrobatics, or DC 29 Stealth
With the front gate a site of protests and dottari brutality, the only way into the museum is over its back wall.

DISTRACTING THE GUARD	OBSTACLE
Infiltration Points 2 (individual); Overcome DC 25 Diplomacy or Deception, or DC 27 Performance
While most of the dottari have moved to quash the protest out front, a skeleton crew remains in the museum.

THROUGH THE EXHIBITS	OBSTACLE
Infiltration Points 1 (individual); Overcome DC 27 Society, DC 29 Crafting, or DC 31 Perception
The maze of exhibits makes little sense when approached from the rear of the building. Considering the structure and the flow of the building provides insight into where the door to the basement might be.

ARRESTED!
During this adventure, the dottari try to arrest the PCs. If the authorities manage to drag off any of the PCs, they're brought to Dhoffram Keep, one of the fortresses of Corentyn. Linetta Seacarver springs the PCs from their cells before the end of the night. There might be a condition as part of this escape, especially if the players don't consider the arrests to be serious. For example, a PC might lose a valuable piece of equipment during the jailbreak, or an allied faction might become displeased with how lightly the PCs are taking the revolution.
