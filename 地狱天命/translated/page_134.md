5 个任务时段一旦过去，一组耀目烟火从安多兰军方此刻所在位置直冲低空大气层。这组烟火炸成一只**鹰焰**（eagle flare）——以蓝色与黄色火星拼出的展翅之鹰图样，鹰喙与鹰爪指向沉言林东部的某一处。这便是玩家角色与他们的特种部队一直等待的信号。他们必须迅速汇入主战场，自西方带兵从切利亚斯军后翼包抄！

### 沉言林之战（Battle of the Whisperwood）

本章未将穿越沉言林的行军以及战场上的接战逐一作为多场连续的小规模冲突遭遇来呈现，而是借助《GM 核心》第 196–199 页**渗透子系统**规则的一个变体来呈现这些挑战。你应提前熟悉此子系统，但请留意下方各**障碍**所做的细微调整。

与一般渗透不同的是，**没有准备阶段**。取而代之，玩家角色因近几日的胜绩获得**战场点**（Battlefield Points，**BP**）奖励。你应**计算玩家角色可用的 BP 总数**，并向玩家解释如何使用这些 BP。

- 玩家角色每**击败下列敌人之一**——第六章中的**誓嚎风灵**、**燔林精**、**斯戎暴龙**——便获得 **1 BP**。但是，若他们放走了斯戎暴龙，便不获得该 BP。
- 玩家角色每**击杀阿瑟沃克斯**获得 **1 BP**，或**招揽该龙加入己方**获得 **2 BP**。
- 玩家角色若在第六章中从**伊蒂·乔吉努斯将军**那里**获得了炮兵协助包**（precision artillery package），且未在第七章任务期间使用，则获得 **1 BP**。
- 玩家角色每位**在第六章最终影响遭遇中获得 7 影响点的其他 NPC**，便各获得 **1 BP**。
- 玩家角色在**第七章完成的任务**奖励 **1–2 BP**，最高 **7 BP**。

玩家角色花费战场点时，你应使用第 6–7 章中的接触点来增强故事感。花费 1 BP 可能意味着被救出的地狱骑士预备队涌入战场压制守军；或意味着同盟的阿瑟沃克斯自空中俯冲、为玩家前方地形进行碾压；或意味着安多兰仪式者帮玩家解除危险危害。**沉言林之战的所有部分都是相互联系的**，玩家角色应感到自己是带动整个轮盘转动的关键齿轮。**强烈建议**通读所有渗透与遭遇部分后再继续。

Battle of the Whisperwood

Rather than play out the march through the army-filled Whisperwood and battlefield engagements as numerous skirmish encounters one after the other, this chapter presents these challenges using a variation of the infiltration subsystem rules presented on pages 196–199 of GM Core. You should familiarize yourself with this subsystem in advance, but pay heed to the slight changes made to the individual obstacles presented below.

Unlike a normal infiltration, there are no preparation phases. Instead, the PCs are awarded Battlefield Points (BP) for their victories over the last few days. You should calculate the number of BPs available to the PCs and explain how they're used to the players.

- The PCs receive 1 BP for each of the following that they defeated: the oath howlers, the marrmoras, and the thruneosaurus rex during Chapter 6. However, they don't receive a BP if they let the thruneosaurus rex escape alive.
- The PCs receive 1 BP for slaying Athervox, or 2 BP for recruiting the dragon to their side.
- The PCs receive 1 BP for earning the artillery package from General Edei Georginus during Chapter 6 (if they didn't use it earlier during their Chapter 7 missions).
- The PCs receive 1 BP for each of the other NPCs that they earned 7 Influence Points with during the final influence encounter in Chapter 6.
- The PCs receive 1–2 BPs for the missions they accomplished during Chapter 7, to a maximum of 7 BP.

When the PCs spend their Battlefield Points, you should use touchpoints from Chapters 6–7 to enhance the story. One BP spent might entail rescued Hellknight reserves streaming into the area to overwhelm defenders, or an allied Athervox swooping in from overhead to lay waste to the terrain ahead of the PCs, or Andoren ritual casters helping defuse dangerous hazards for them. All parts of the Battle of the Whisperwood are connected, and the PCs should feel like a key cog, turning this wheel. You're strongly encouraged to read all parts of the infiltration and encounters before proceeding.
