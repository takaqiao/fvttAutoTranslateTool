#### 满口尘灰（Ashes in the Mouth）　**低 12**

**找到煤烬姊妹相对容易**——**她们的住所在城中废墟中格外显眼**——**位于最东边的城门附近**。

> **与周围灰烬般的城市废墟形成鲜明对比的**——**是这条街上的一座保养得宜的客栈**——**屋顶修缮一新**、**窗板漆成红色**。**前门上方有一块招牌**——**写着"冒险者的休憩"**（The Adventurer's Rest）。**门附近的鹅卵石上插着近十支长枪**——**每支顶端**——**串着一颗鱼形类人生物的断头**。

**客栈内里看上去是一间整洁却朴素的居所**——**有几张桌子与一处熊熊燃烧的壁炉**。**它比表面更脏**——**一道幻象场景让建筑微弱的火光显得更亮**——**并掩盖了家具因年代久远而变得多么单薄**。**该幻象能承受随便的互动**——**PC 可使用"搜索"动作刺破这一幻象**（DC 36）——**多半会首先注意到沾染在各种表面上的盐、风干的糖浆与油脂残渣**。

**生物**：**这里居住着一群强大的蜜鬼婆**（sweet hags）**结成的巫团**——**维持着"已退休的年长冒险者"之伪装**——**彼此互称"煤姊妹"（Sister Cinder）、"烬姊妹"（Sister Ember）与"灰姊妹"（Sister Ash）**。**定期会有几只伪装成半身人的红帽精**（redcaps，Monster Core 第 290 页）**前来送货、看护房产、清运垃圾**。

**一般情况下**，**这些鬼婆会向旅人提供款待并闲谈**——**借此机会**——**评判她们的新客人**——**并决定如何最好地戏弄他们**——**从拿好食物把他们撑死、到信口胡说、到（极少时）把一名类人生物烤进派里**——**应有尽有**。**她们奖励那些"愉快地忍受她们的甜食、把戏与偶尔折磨"的人**——**为他们提供健康的餐食与安全的住处**。**若有人识破她们的伪装（DC 36）**——**鬼婆们会有所失望**——**但只要客人不举起指控或剑指主人**——**她们就不会怪罪客人那双敏锐的眼睛**。**当地的塞达克西人很快就开始往她们身上扔长枪**——**这导致鬼婆们与他们这群鱼形邻居之间持续不断的敌意**。

**影响**：**PC 们可在一次为期 3 个社交回合的影响遭遇**（influence encounter，GM Core 第 187 页）**中赢得姊妹们的合作**。**PC 们抵达时**——**鬼婆们邀请他们入内**——**愉快地宣布："**我们正煮今天的大餐**——**有客人来真是开心**。"**屋内**——**朴实的面包正在散热**——**蔬菜汤在炉边咕嘟**——**一根野猪后腿正在火上烤**——**小烤炉则散发着肉桂卷的香味**。

**在每个影响回合开始时**——**鬼婆们闲聊、询问 PC 的旅行**——**并为客人端上又一道丰盛的菜肴**。**每一回合**——**鬼婆们都会夹带一两种"恶作剧食物"**——**目标是让每位 PC 在整个遭遇过程中至少品尝过其中之一**。**PC 可以选择不吃**——**但会让主人不悦**——**并在该回合的刺探与影响检定上承受 -2 环境减值**。

**腻甜款待（Cloying Treat）**：**PC 吃到一种味道宜人的东西**——**但其风味最终会让感官超载**。**此食物受鬼婆的"蜜糖与砒霜"（Poisoned Candy）能力影响**——**让 PC 暴露于魅惑术**（charm，DC 32）。

**令人作呕的款待（Loathsome Treat）**：**PC 吃到一种令人难以下咽之物**（**例如一只满是活蛆的饺子**、**一块装满变质蛋奶酱的酥皮**、**或一杯随每一口都变得更可怕之辣的汤**）——**其真实本质被幻象所掩盖**。**PC 必须尝试一次 DC 32 强韧豁免**——**失败时变为反胃 1**（**大失败时变为反胃 2**）——**直到该影响回合结束**。

ASHES IN THE MOUTH	LOW 12
Locating the Sootcinder sisters is relatively easy, and their dwelling stands out from the ruins of the city and is located near the easternmost gate.

In contrast to the ashy ruins of the surrounding city, a well-kept inn with a repaired roof and red-painted shutters stands on the street here. A sign above the front door reads, "The Adventurer's Rest." Nearly a dozen spears are driven into the cobblestones near the door, each with the severed head of a fishlike humanoid impaled on top.

On the inside, the inn appears to be a tidy but simple abode with several tables and a roaring fireplace. It's dirtier than it appears; an illusory scene brightens the building's anemic fire and hides just how flimsy the furniture has become with age. The illusion withstands casual interaction, and a PC can Seek to pierce the illusion (DC 36), likely first noticing the residue of salt, dried syrup, and grease that stains various surfaces.

Creatures: A coven of powerful sweet hags live here, maintaining the guise of elderly retired adventurers who refer to each other as Sister Cinder, Sister Ember, and Sister Ash. Periodically, a few redcaps (Monster Core 290) disguised as halflings visit to deliver supplies, tend the property, and haul away refuse.

In general, the hags offer hospitality to travelers and engage in chitchat, using the interactions to judge their new guests and determine how best to toy with them—anything from stuffing them with good food to doling out misinformation to (rarely) baking a humanoid into a pie. They reward those who cheerfully endure their sweets, trickery, and occasional torments by sharing healthful meals and providing a safe place to sleep. While the hags are disappointed if someone sees through their disguises (DC 36), they don't fault the stranger's keen eyes so long as their guest doesn't level accusations or swords at their hosts. Local sedacthies were quick to throw spears, leading to the hags' ongoing animosity toward their fishy neighbors.

Influence: The PCs can win the sisters' cooperation through an influence encounter (GM Core 187) over 3 social rounds. When the PCs arrive, the hags invite them inside, cheerfully announcing that they're cooking the big meal of the day and would be delighted to have guests. Inside, hearty bread is cooling, vegetable soup simmers at the hearth, a boar haunch roasts over the fire, and the small oven smells of cinnamon rolls.

At the beginning of each influence round, the hags chat, inquire about the PCs' travels, and serve another hearty course for the guests. Each round, the hags include one or two mischievous foods, aiming to expose each PC to one of these foods at least once during the encounter. A PC can choose not to eat, but upsets their hosts and takes a –2 circumstance penalty to Discovery and Influence checks that round.

Cloying Treat: The PC eats something that tastes delightful, though the flavors eventually overload the senses. This food is affected by the hags' Poisoned Candy ability, exposing the PC to charm (DC 32).

Loathsome Treat: The PC eats something unpalatable (such as a dumpling full of live maggots, a pastry filled with spoiled custard, or a cup of soup that becomes more frightfully spicy with each bite) whose true nature was hidden by an illusion. The PC must attempt a DC 32 Fortitude save, becoming sickened 1 on a failure (sickened 2 on a critical failure) until the end of the influence round.
