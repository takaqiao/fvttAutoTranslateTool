## Page 47 — Ch2 B6 续（厄登/3101 牢房）+ 西翼穿行障碍引子

3101 号牢房在格拉戴斯卡堡垒中有着**传奇般的历史**。它专为**主管副执棒官想要监视的囚犯**而设。挑选的依据**或许是名望**——例如弃誓者被带入格拉戴斯卡堡垒时便是如此；**或许是个人恩怨**——正如厄登眼下的处境；但也可以**只是因为副执棒官想看着此人崩溃**，或**只是因为此人让她想起一位故友**，又或者**只是因为此人曾试图越狱**。

无论起因为何，被关进 3101 号牢房的囚犯都会发现：**自己其实还远未被格拉戴斯卡堡垒折服**。锁链强迫他们**只要副执棒官愿意，便须始终站立**；玻璃牢墙则**剥夺其残存的一切隐私**。拉西德**尤其乐于绘声绘色地评论囚犯神情的变化**——她要看着他们扭动挣扎，意识到**连一声叹息都逃不过她的关注**。她对她的"试验品"会逐渐厌倦，每隔数周便**换上一位新人**。

这间牢房的门并未上锁，但锁链已被**焊死在厄登的四肢周围**——需要一次 DC 25 的制作检定来切开它们，或一次 DC 28 的运动检定**强行开启**。

**生物**：厄登**尚未崩溃**。他相信**暮爪一定会前来救他**，所以这段日子里他一直**在观察拉西德工作**。这种行为让她不安，尽管她假装觉得有趣。如今他已在脑海笔记中加上了她**所偏爱的毒药、武器，以及她讨厌的助手**等条目。

当 PC 们解救厄登时，他会立即因疲惫而瘫倒；但在**休息 10 分钟后**他便能恢复。若 PC 们告诉他**琳奈塔派他们前来**，他便提出与他们结盟。若 PC 们接受，他便能为他们**尝试"安置囚犯"机遇**（高级监狱内适用）。厄登还会**分享他从观察拉西德实验室中获悉的监狱内情**：他告诉 PC 们一位名为**泽拉米尔**（Zeramial）的**水栖精灵王子**被关押在**深层监狱**之中。地狱骑士将泽拉米尔扣作**人质**——以此让**地狱之口海湾**（Hellmouth Gulf）的精灵**不敢造反**。厄登提供路线，指引 PC 们**穿越西翼**前去寻找他。

**奖励**：当 PC 们救出**厄登·砾掌**时，授予 80 XP。

Cell 3101 has a storied history within Citadel Gheradesca. It's reserved for a prisoner whom the overseeing paralictor wishes to watch. The selection might be based on fame, such as when the Foresworn was brought to Citadel Gheradesca. Sometimes, it's based on personal animus, as in Urdun's current circumstance. However, it can just as easily be a prisoner who the paralictor wishes to watch break, or one who reminds them of an old friend, or one who attempted to escape from the citadel.

Whatever the case, the prisoner of cell 3101 finds that they hadn't yet been fully humbled by Citadel Gheradesca. The chains force them to stand as long as the paralictor wishes, and the glass cell wall strips away any remaining privacy. Racid particularly enjoys colorfully commenting on changes in the prisoner's demeanor, so that she can watch them squirm and realize that they can't even sigh without her notice. She does tire of her subjects and replaces them every few weeks.

The door to this cell is unlocked, but the chains are welded shut around Urdun's limbs, requiring a DC 25 Crafting check to break them or DC 28 Athletics check to Force Open.

Creatures: Urdun hasn't yet broken. He believes that the Twilight Talons will rescue him, so he's been watching Racid as she works. This behavior disturbs her, though she pretends it's amusing. Already, he's added to his mental notes about her preferred poisons, weapons, and the assistants who annoy her.

When the PCs free Urdun, he immediately collapses from fatigue. However, he recovers after resting for 10 minutes. If the PCs mention that Linetta sent them, he offers to ally with them. If they accept, he can attempt the Secure Prisoner opportunity for them while in the high prison. Urdun also shares with the PCs what he learned about the prison while watching Racid's lab. He tells them of Zeramial, an aquatic elf prince being held in the deep prison. The Hellknights imprisoned Zeramial as collateral so the elves of Hellmouth Gulf won't revolt. Urdun provides the PCs with instructions to travel through the west wing to find him.

Reward: When the PCs rescue Urdun Gravelhands, award them 80 XP.
