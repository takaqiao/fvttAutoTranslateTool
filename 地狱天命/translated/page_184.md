### 集结神圣大军（Gathering a Divine Army）

阶梯向下延伸数段——直至埃戈里安下水道之下——通往一处由未经雕琢的岩石所构的洞穴大厅。近百具石像——皆形若艾奥梅黛——身披坚实板甲——手持其柄首镌有承嗣者圣徽的长剑——一排排成行肃立。柔光弥漫整间房——每具石像皆微微焕发着光辉。

这些石像便是海莉雅·贝伦内为应对紧急情况所聚集的「**神圣守卫**」（divine wardens）。它们已然待命——只待合适之人、为合适之事——将其唤醒。此处共有 4 支构装体**部队**。一位玩家角色——可立于厅中央——高声讲出他们的名字、他们的目的、与待解决之难——之后再向艾奥梅黛作一段祷告——便能将它们全部激活。假定一位玩家角色解释清楚切利亚斯眼下所发生之事——石像们的双眼便都会以神圣能量焕亮——这些石像便可被指挥——每位玩家角色一支。若一位玩家角色同时以 DC 38 **宗教**检定成功——他便能立即与他所指挥的部队建立**小规模冲突纽带**（《**战吼**！》161 页）。一位以此检定大成功的玩家角色——能令所有玩家角色皆建立小规模冲突纽带。一位被艾奥梅黛**祝圣**的玩家角色——在此项检定上获得高一级的成功度。

Gathering a Divine Army

The stairs descend several flights beneath Egorian's
sewers to a cavernous chamber of unworked stone.
Almost a hundred stone statues that resemble Iomedae,
wearing sturdy plate mail and carrying longswords
whose hilts bear the Inheritor's religious symbol, stand
in serried rows. A soft light suffuses the entire room,
as each statue glows slightly.

These statues are the divine wardens Helia Belunne
was gathering in case of an emergency. They stand
ready, waiting only to be awoken by the right people
for the right cause. There are four troops of constructs
here. A PC can activate them all by standing in the
center of the chamber and loudly speaking their name,
their purpose, and the problem to be solved, followed
by a prayer to Iomedae. Assuming a PC explains
what's happening to Cheliax, the statues'
eyes then all glow with holy energy, and the
statues can be commanded, one for each PC.
If a PC also succeeds at DC 38 Religion check,
they can immediately form a skirmish bond
(Battlecry! 161) with the troop they command. A
PC who critically succeeds at this check allows all of
the PCs to form skirmish bonds. A PC who's been
sanctified by Iomedae gets one degree of success
higher on this check.
