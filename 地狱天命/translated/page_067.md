> **此处坍塌的城市街道让位于一处宽阔的广场**。**三道 12 英尺高的泛红合金门户立于广场远端的升起台座之上**。**每道拱门的门都已敞开**——**门内有一道黄色能量漩涡在旋转**。**广场中央**——**紫色奥术符文在鹅卵石中闪烁**——**形成一个近 20 英尺宽的圆环**。

**抵达纳罗纳后不久**——**奥拉克斯在此广场上建立了一处大型召唤圆环**——**带有一条通往地狱的永久链接**。**每夜**——**该传送门自动召出 1d4 只萨格拉贡魔进入该城**——**她用以扩充其大军兵力**。**PC 们必须解除此圆环以切断她的援军供应**（若不解除——**则向 F 区的"地狱浪潮分队"加入精英调整**[Monster Core 6]）。

**召唤圆环**：**此召唤圆环从广场上三道门户的每一道汲取力量**。**要永久解除它**——**PC 们必须通过 3 次 DC 35 奥术、神秘学、宗教或自然检定来打断能量流向圆环**。

**泛红门户**：**纳罗纳神秘门户中的三道立于此广场**。**每道都已被奥拉克斯激活**——**让它们成为任何走入其中之人的危害**。

D. SUMMONING CIRCLE	MODERATE 12
The crumbling city streets give way here and open to a wide plaza. Three twelve-foot-tall doorways made from red-tinged alloy stand upon raised daises on the far side of the clearing. The doors of each archway are thrown open, and a vortex of yellow energy swirls within. In the center of the clearing, purple arcane runes glitter in the cobblestone, forming a circle nearly twenty feet wide.

Shortly after arriving in Narona, Oourax constructed a large summoning circle with a permanent link to Hell in this square. Each night, the portal automatically conjures 1d4 sarglagon devils to the city, which she uses to swell the ranks of her army. The PCs must disable this circle to cut off her flow of reinforcements (if they don't, add the elite adjustment [Monster Core 6] to the infernal tide troop in area F).

Summoning Circle: This summoning circle draws power from each of the three gateways in the plaza. To permanently disable it, the PCs must succeed at three DC 35 Arcana, Occultism, Religion, or Nature checks to interrupt the flow of energy to the circle.

Red-Tinged Doorways: Three of the mysterious doorways of Narona stand in the city clearing. Each has already been activated by Oourax, making them hazardous to any who journey inside.
