……向下。**温暖的海风让雷玛斯苦不堪言**——**汗水与融冰从他身上滴落**。**PC 们的被掳同伴尚活着**——**悬在平台之外的开阔空气中**。

**雷玛斯宁愿栖于一座冰冻山巅做苦行冥思**——**也不愿留在这座炽热可怕的岛屿**——**几十年来不停的出汗已激出了他残酷的一面**。**他愿意在 PC 们寻找掩蔽或在一阵冰镖之雨下登塔时进行谈判**——**但他最初的要求是骇人听闻的**——**例如要求 PC 们再交出几名船员供他吞食**——**作为他放行通过的代价**。**话虽如此**——**通过一次 DC 31 察觉检定察觉动机的 PC** 可以**看出雷玛斯比起邪恶更多是"易激惹"**。**大成功时**，**PC 可辨明**：**温暖天气与漫长隔绝合在一起让他暴躁**——**这提示**：**字面意义上"降低温度"，或弄清楚是什么把他困在这里**，**可能为 PC 们在谈判中提供一个优势**。

**PC 们可以胁迫**（Coerce）**雷玛斯**——**让他暂停攻击足够长的时间以让 PC 们进一步交谈**，**并可能释放俘虏**。**话虽如此**——**这只雪鬼很可能会在事后猎杀他们**——**因被强迫顺从而羞愧**。**PC 也可以建立印象（Make an Impression）**（**雷玛斯偶尔会发起远程攻击**）来**赢得他的勉强休战**。**这两种可能性都给 PC 一个机会**——**揭开甚至毁掉藏匿的豆罐**（见**第 61 页的边栏**）。

### 浪沫之城艾尔莉维亚（Elryvia, City of Foam）

**PC 们终于抵达泽拉米尔的故乡之城**——**它位于离岸数英里之处**——**在水线之下**——**藏身于一片尖锐岩礁的迷宫中**。**泽拉米尔帮助 PC 们安全地穿越迷宫至其中心**——**在那里**——**岩石危害让位于一处布满辉光的深坑**——**坑壁上凿出数百座珊瑚屋**。**此即艾尔莉维亚**——**地狱之口海湾最大的水栖精灵之城**。

**一队水栖精灵很快从水中浮起**——**示意 PC 们随他们而行**——**并按需提供水中呼吸（water breathing）魔法以便利此行**。**他们前往一座充气的王厅**——**那里他们被艾蕊涅**（Aereene）**所接见**——**艾蕊涅即泽拉米尔的母亲、艾尔莉维亚的女王**。**为感谢 PC 们营救她的儿子**，**她举办了一场宴会与战争会议**——**以 PC 们为主宾**。**在这一晚的进程里**，**PC 们应得知如下事实**：

- **水栖精灵正与地狱之口海湾的塞达克西人**（sedacthies）**交战**——**且处境不利**。**三只海蛇正辅助塞达克西人**——**已数次扭转战局**。
- **精灵们从俘虏处得知**：**塞达克西人与一位来自切利亚斯的独眼巨人恶魔教士奥拉克斯**（Oourax）**结盟**。**看来她操控着这些可怕的海蛇**——**且为换取力量将自己的灵魂卖给了不止一只**——**而是两只不同的魔鬼**。
- **奥拉克斯在海岸边一座名为纳罗纳**（Narona）**的废墟之城中筑起堡垒**——**她似乎正搜寻该城以发掘古代遗物**。**他们不知遗物的确切功用**——**但侦察兵报告说该城水域已变得污浊**——**而精灵们担心**：**奥拉克斯在纳罗纳所制造的、不论是何种瘟疫**，**最终都将蔓延至海湾**。

**进展**：**宴会过程中**，**一项新计划开始成形**。**泽拉米尔需要数日时间才能集结足够兵力强攻纳罗纳**——**因此他请 PC 们先于他前往那里**——**侦察该城**——**并在可能之处削弱敌军**。**一旦定位到奥拉克斯及其大军所居之处**——**他将率足够兵力赶到**——**攻击她的阵地并杀死她**。**PC 们抵达后**——**海精灵建议 PC 们首先向"煤烬姊妹"**（Sootcinder sisters，第 62 页）**寻求情报**——**她们是已退休的冒险者**——**始终关注着纳罗纳的动向**——**对恶魔教士毫无好感**——**且愿意与访客谈判**。**海精灵知道**：**"消融者"雷玛斯……**

Rematsu would rather dwell atop a frozen mountain in ascetic contemplation than dwell on this dreadfully hot island, and decades of relentless sweating have brought out his cruel side. He's willing to negotiate as the PCs Take Cover or ascend under a hail of ice darts, but his initial demands are horrific, such as insisting the PCs surrender several more of their crew for him to devour in exchange for safe passage. That said, a PC who succeeds at a DC 31 Perception check to Sense Motive can tell that Rematsu is more irritable than evil. By critically succeeding, a PC discerns that the combination of the warm weather and long isolation have made him grouchy, suggesting that literally bringing down the temperature or determining what traps him here might provide the PCs an edge in negotiation.

The PCs can Coerce Rematsu to stop fighting them long enough to speak further and possibly release the captive. That said, the oni likely hunts them down later, ashamed of being browbeaten into compliance. A PC might also Make an Impression (while Rematsu makes occasional ranged attacks) to earn his grudging truce. Both possibilities give the PCs a chance to uncover and even destroy the hidden bean jar (see sidebar on page 61).

Elryvia, City of Foam
The PCs at last reach Zeramial's home city, located a few miles off the coast and within a maze of sharp rocks lying just below the waterline. Zeramial helps the PCs safely navigate the maze to its center, where the rocky hazards give way to a deep pit filled with glowing lights and lined with hundreds of coral houses carved into the rock. This is Elryvia, the largest aquatic elf city in the Hellmouth Gulf.

A contingent of aquatic elves soon surfaces and bids the PCs to follow, providing water-breathing magic to facilitate the journey, as needed. They travel to an air-filled royal hall, where they're greeted by Aereene, Zeramial's mother and queen of Elryvia. In thanks for rescuing her son, she hosts a feast and war council, with the PCs as the guests of honor. Over the course of the evening, the PCs should learn the following facts.

• The aquatic elves are at war with the sedacthies of Hellmouth Gulf and faring poorly. Three sea serpents aid the sedacthies and have turned the tide of several battles.

• The elves have learned from captives that the sedacthies are allied with a cyclops diabolist from Cheliax named Oourax. It seems that she has control of the fearsome serpents and has sold her soul to not one, but two different devils in exchange for power.

• Oourax has fortified herself within the ruined city of Narona on the coast, where she appears to be exploring the city for ancient relics. They don't know the exact function of the relics, but scouts report that the waters of the city have become polluted, and the elves worry that whatever sickness Oourax is creating in Narona will eventually spill into the gulf.

Development: Over the course of the feast, a new plan begins to take shape. It'll take several days for Zeramial to marshal enough forces to assault Narona, so he asks the PCs to journey there ahead of him to scout the city and weaken the enemy forces where possible. Once they've located where Oourax and her armies reside, he'll arrive with enough troops to assault her position and kill her. Once the PCs arrive, the sea elves suggest the PCs first seek information from the Sootcinder sisters (page 62), retired adventurers who stay informed of activity in Narona, have no love for diabolists, and are willing to bargain with visitors.
