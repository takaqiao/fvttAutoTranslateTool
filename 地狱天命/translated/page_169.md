#### A3. 战利品厅（TROPHY ROOM）　**中等 18 级**

> **此宽敞房间陈列着数件标本作品**——**从一只普通的棕熊到某类小龙**。**多个野兽头颅沿墙挂列**。**一对舒适的填充座椅面向已点燃的壁炉——其中一把椅背沾着长长的爪痕——抓出了里面的填料与弹簧**。

历代斯戎统治者皆以猎大型猎物制成标本陈列于此为豪。**阿波罗盖未见此室有何大用**——**故此处不如此层的其他地方那般精心维护**。

**生物**：**阿波罗盖容许一群「高等地狱猫」（greater hellcats）住在此处**。**它们大部分时间都在炉火旁取暖**——**或在家具与标本上磨爪**。**当玩家角色进入时**，**这些地狱猫以心灵感应要求被喂食**——**但很快意识到英雄们并非阿波罗盖女王**——**便发起攻击**。**当两只地狱猫已被击败、或当所有地狱猫的 HP 都低于 40 时**——**它们开始在角落瑟缩**——**以心灵感应乞求宽恕**。**玩家角色是否给予宽恕则由他们决定**。

**A3. TROPHY ROOM	MODERATE 18**

> This expansive room contains several pieces of taxidermy, ranging from an ordinary brown bear to some manner of drake. Several bestial heads are mounted along the walls. A pair of comfortable stuffed chairs face a lit fireplace, one of their backs covered in long scratches to reveal stuffing and springs.

Previous Thrune rulers took great pride in hunting large game to stuff and display here. Abrogail hasn't seen much use for such a room, so it isn't as well-maintained as the rest of the floor.

**Creatures**: Abrogail allows a pack of greater hellcats to live here. They spend most days basking by the fire and scratching up the furniture and taxidermy. When the PCs enter, the hellcats telepathically demand to be fed but quickly realize that the heroes aren't Queen Abrogail and attack. When two of the hellcats have been defeated or if all of them have fewer than 40 Hit Points, they start to cower in the corner, telepathically begging for mercy. Whether the PCs grant that mercy is up to them.
