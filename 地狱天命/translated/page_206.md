## 切利亚斯境内的冲突（Conflicts in Cheliax）

战争或许永恒不变——但它是一股能在其毁灭尾迹之中——使万物为之蜕变的强大变易之力。心灵与肉体——既被磨砺、亦被粉碎；纽带——既被锻结、亦被斩断；城邦——既车水马龙、亦烈火焚烧；国家——既蓬勃扩张、亦土崩瓦解。需要与绝望——孕育出在科技上的飞跃突进——抑或——孕育出残忍恶毒与自我求存的暴行。这是一个考验生民魂魄的时代。「**地狱火危机**」——不仅仅是一场意识形态与政治之争——更是一场穿越「**伟大彼方**」（Great Beyond）的形而上学战役。在地狱的眼中——每一个在精神层面被发现存有不足的凡人参战者——皆是迈向终极胜利的又一步。

随着战火蔓延——平民与贵族之间的关系——亦愈发紧绷。当平民选择阵营、或为避免暴力而挣扎之时——暴民式的私刑——亦愈发频繁。贵族——雇佣额外的护卫陪同他们出入宴会、歌剧、与酒庄——而雇佣兵需求的激增——也将「**内海**」（Inner Sea）周边——粗鲁好斗之徒——带入了切利亚斯——这是一种供他国势力与境外利益的间谍——以伪造身份证明渗入境内——再完美不过的方式。许多派系都在切利亚斯境内进行间谍活动：「**探路者社团**」（Pathfinder Society）公开支持切利亚斯的敌人——并利用这场混乱去夺取危险的遗物——同时将学者与他人护送过境；像「**燎原党**」（Firebrands）这样的抵抗团体——则试图借助混乱推翻这些压迫性的暴君；而斯戎家族的审判官——则在民众中——搜捕异见者。间谍藏身于最意想不到的伪装与场所之中——而渗透与暗杀的威胁——也在精英与权贵头上——投下了挥之不去、令人疑神疑鬼的阴影。

战争对精神的腐蚀——正中地狱下怀。严苛敕令的拟订与执行——令魔鬼们大喜过望——它们设计这些限制——以碾碎民众的精神——把他们磨碾入一种愤懑而怨怼的服从之中。每一道宵禁、每一处关卡——都迫使守卫——执行残忍的命令——也迫使市民——忍受非人的待遇——令切利亚斯之民——在每一桩相继而来的不公之中——其言行举止与所感所受——皆变得愈发恶劣。这种对人心与魂魄的粗化——令阿斯摩蒂斯无比满意——而那群邪魔官僚——则雀跃地汇报创纪录数量的「**残屑魔**」（ort，《怪物核心》86 页）——正涌入地狱的列阵——一切都好让切利亚斯的前线进一步得到补足——并让战争更长久地拖延下去。

然而——魂魄中那份英雄性的本质——仍能找到办法——超越战争的混乱而崛起。那些怀着高尚意图——其德行尚未完全屈从于战争之需求的人们——抓住机会去挫败切利亚斯——并令其计划脱轨——无论他们是渴望终结斯戎家族政权的切利亚斯人——还是已做好准备、要将「**邪魔信奉**」之污渍——自**格拉里安**（Golarion）的面庞上抹去的外邦士兵。战争的混乱呈现出种种弱点与机遇——它们在和平时期——绝无可能被加以利用——尽管这般环境——也提升了风险与代价。**拉沃努尔**境内自由战胜暴政的捷报——仍在鼓舞人心——而「**辉煌光复**」（Glorious Reclamation）所留下的余烬——只要条件合适——也仍能被吹拂成熊熊火焰。那些曾在阿波罗盖二世与斯戎家族残酷的拇指之下苟活的人们——已做好准备——冒着死亡的风险——以摆脱长期以来——压迫他们的魔鬼与邪魔信奉者——而活出自由。

战争——确实——为战斗提供了无尽的机会。玩家不必去掷出随机遭遇——他们也许会发现偏远的补给站——或与一支运载工具与补给品的车队——擦肩而过。对军中显要成员的精准打击——也能充当快速、以战斗为核心的一次性支线任务。从外部看——战争或许显得混乱无序——但 GM 能为玩家提供清晰的机会——让他们投入大规模战斗中——那种以目标为核心的战术。

下文呈现了一些超出本战役系列主要事件之外的冲突——以作为额外的目标、场景设置——以及种子——好让你讲述属于自己的、关于地狱火危机的英雄故事。战争——既在战场与街巷之上、也在粮仓与实验室之中——同样地被打响与赢得——它们既造就了私密的人际戏剧、也造就了广袤的史诗战场。此处的故事种子——能为你提供灵感与指引——以提升你的牌桌体验。

Conflicts in Cheliax

War might be unchanging, but it's a potent
transformative force that alters everything in its
destructive wake. Minds and bodies sharpen and
shatter; bonds are forged and broken; cities bustle
and burn; nations expand and crumble. Necessity and
desperation coax great leaps in technology—or vicious
acts of cruelty and self-preservation. It's a time that
tries peoples' souls. The Hellfire Crisis isn't only an
ideological and political conflict, but a metaphysical
campaign waged through the Great Beyond. In
Hell's eyes, every mortal combatant found spiritually
wanting is another step toward the ultimate victory.

As war rages, relations between common folk and
the aristocracy fray. Mob justice becomes increasingly
frequent as civilians choose sides or struggle to avoid
violence. The aristocracy hires additional guards
to escort them to parties, operas, and wineries, and
the surge in mercenary hiring brings rough-and-tumble types from around the Inner Sea into the
country—a perfect way for agents of foreign powers
and outside interests to infiltrate with false papers.
Many factions spy in Cheliax: the Pathfinder Society
openly supports Cheliax's enemies and uses the chaos
to seize dangerous relics while ushering academics
and others across borders; resistance groups like the
Firebrands try to capitalize on the chaos to overthrow
their oppressive tyrants; and the inquisitors of House
Thrune seek dissidents among their population. Spies
hide in the most unlikely guises and places, and the
threat of infiltration and assassination casts a constant
paranoid pall over the elite and powerful.

War's spiritual decay plays directly into Hell's plans.
The crafting and enforcement of draconian edicts
thrills devils, who design restrictions to crush the
spirits of the populace and grind them into a festering
and resentful obedience. Every curfew and checkpoint
forces guards to enact cruel orders and citizens to
suffer inhumane conditions, making the people of
Cheliax act and feel worse with each passing injustice.
This roughening of people's minds and souls pleases
Asmodeus to no end, and the devilish bureaucracy
gleefully report record numbers of ort (Monster Core
86) filling the ranks of Hell—all the better to bolster
Cheliax's front lines and drag the war on further.

Still, the heroic nature of the soul can find a way
to rise above even the chaos of war. Those with noble
intent, whose morals haven't entirely given way to the
necessities of war, seize opportunities to thwart Cheliax
and derail its plans, whether Chelaxians who long to
end the regime of House Thrune or foreign soldiers
prepared to wipe the stain of diabolism from the face
of Golarion. The chaos of war presents weaknesses
and opportunities that could never be exploited in
peacetime, even as the circumstances heighten the
risks and consequences. The victories of freedom over
tyranny in Ravounel continues to inspire, and the
Glorious Reclamation left embers that might still be
coaxed into flame with the right conditions. Those
who suffered life under the cruel thumb of Abrogail
II and House Thrune are prepared to risk death to
live free of the devils and diabolists who've oppressed
them for so long.

War truly presents endless opportunity for battle.
Rather than rolling for random encounters, players
might discover remote depots or cross paths with
caravans of tools and supplies. Surgical strikes on
prominent members of the military can serve as quick,
combat-focused one-shot side quests. War might seem
chaotic and random from the outside, but a GM can
provide clear opportunities for players to get into the
objective-focused tactics of large-scale combat.

The following presents conflicts that arise outside
this Adventure Path's main events to serve as
additional objectives, set pieces, and seeds to tell your
own heroic stories of the Hellfire Crisis. Wars are
fought and won as much in granaries and laboratories
as on the battlefield and in the streets, and they create
both intimate interpersonal drama and sprawling epic
theaters of combat. The story seeds here can provide
you with inspiration and guidance to enhance your
table's experience.
