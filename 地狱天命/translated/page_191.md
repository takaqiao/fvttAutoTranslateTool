> **B7. 神殿圣物殿（TEMPLE RELIQUARY）　　中等 19**
>
> 「这间形状奇特的房间——被一道内墙——分为两半。两半之中——各有一座漆为黑红、门上饰有五芒星的大型木柜。它们威逼之姿——似要充满整间房。北半部的东侧——通向一道门——而南半部——则有石阶向下延伸。」

阿斯摩蒂斯信仰的重要圣物——皆存放于此处的柜中——但其中真正为**神器**者寥寥。每一座柜皆已上锁——**撬锁**需要四次成功的 DC 38 **贼活**检定；金邪魔姊妹们的钥匙打不开这些柜子——但**阿斯佩西娅**身上携有一把可以打开它们的钥匙。

石阶向下——通往区域 B8。

**生物**：这些圣物还由一对**元素精怪**进一步守护——它们藏身于异维度空间之中——直至需要时方才显现。当一位玩家角色穿过分隔两半房间的门廊——或试图打开北侧的柜子时——两只**苦痛地狱火**（anguished hellfires）——由大魔王创造、用作守卫的、火焰拟人化的元素精怪——便会出现在房间中。一只显现于阶梯顶端——另一只显现于通往东侧的门前——以阻断逃路。若没有空间供地狱火显现——它便出现在最近的可行方格中。

B7. TEMPLE RELIQUARY	MODERATE 19

This oddly shaped room is split into two sections by an
internal wall. Both halves contain a large wooden cabinet
painted in black and red, featuring a pentagram on its
doors. Their imposing presence seems to fill the room. A
door leads out to the east in the northern half, while stone
stairs lead down from the southern half.

Important relics of Asmodean faith are stored in the
cabinets here, though only a few are actual artifacts.
Each cabinet is locked, requiring four successful DC
38 Thievery checks to Pick the Lock; the Golden Fiend
Sisters' keys don't open the cabinets, but Aspexia
carries a key that does.

The stairs lead down to area B8.

Creatures: The relics are further protected by a pair
of elementals that hide within an extradimensional
space until needed. When a PC passes through
the doorway separating the room's two halves or
attempts to open the northern cabinet, two anguished
hellfires—fiery, anthropomorphic elementals created
by archdevils and used as guardians—appear in the
room. One materializes at the top of the stairs and the
other in front of the eastern door to block escape. If
there isn't room for a hellfire to materialize, it appears
in the closest possible space.

B8. TREASURY LAIR	SEVERE 19

This massive underground chamber is lined with alcoves
that once held the many treasures pilfered by the Church
of Asmodeus over the centuries. These relics of other
faiths, antiquities of other cultures, and keepsakes of old
battles have been removed from their places of display and
piled into messy heap at the far end of the room. Smoke
trailing from several lit torches by the alcoves rises up and
collects at the top of the vaulted ceiling.
