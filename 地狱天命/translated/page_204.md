# 战役之外（Beyond the Campaign）

「**地狱天命**」战役系列——是对更广阔的「**地狱火危机**」（Hellfire Crisis）作出收尾的一环——其它部分则在其他「探路者」产品中——例如《**探路者 战吼**！》、「**地狱破狱者**」（Hellbreakers）战役系列、《**探路者 失落奥秘：地狱火急讯**》（Pathfinder Lost Omens Hellfire Dispatches）、各类小说、探路者社团情境本等——皆有探索。下文呈现新现状的一部分——聚焦于玩家角色到访过、并直接影响过的地区。《**探路者 失落奥秘：切利亚斯——地狱遗产**》（Pathfinder Lost Omens Cheliax, Infernal Inheritance）以远更细致的篇幅——探索了这些地区（以及其它许多地方）。

此处亦列出供玩家角色继续其冒险的引子——从人道主义任务、到一劳永逸消灭阿波罗盖——皆有所涵盖。第 205-209 页——呈现更多冒险可能性——以亲身探索地狱火危机的其他侧面——也许是与本战役系列中的玩家角色一同——或以其他任意等级的角色——进行一些简短的冒险。

## 余波（The Aftermath）

戈索克莱克之死——并未立即终结这场战争——但它确实迫使**阿波罗盖二世**——向**安多兰**伸出和平条款。在玩家角色打断她的仪式之前——阿波罗盖二世已将其新获之力——用以对付她的敌人——重创了来犯的陆军与海军。即使玩家角色的行动——已击碎切利亚斯继续作战的意志——但安多兰的军力同样伤亡惨重——锐气大挫——而其军事侦察兵与顾问们——也对切利亚斯各地如今显现的地狱地形——发出了严峻的警告。距离英雄们封印「**世界裂痕**」（Worldwound）——如今的「**萨科里斯之疤**」（Sarkoris Scar）——尚不足十年——安多兰的将军们——也不愿冒险——将兵力带入又一处未知的地狱景观之中。

数日之内——一位携和平条款的魔鬼代表——出现在**阿尔玛斯**（Almas）的「**主拱石**」（Archstone）之上。「**至高执政**」（Supreme Elect）**安蒂拉·马鲁塞克**（Andira Marusek）与之会面——审阅了条款——并与**人民议会**讨论各种选项——而后——勉强地签下了停战协议。至此——地狱火危机——便逐渐画下句号——切利亚斯周边各方交战势力——纷纷自行谈判其条款——划定不断变化的边界——并迫切地研究切利亚斯——以理解斯戎家族的地狱契约——对全球的连锁反应。

## 变更的版图（A Changing Map）

地狱火危机期间所流的血——并非徒劳；与切利亚斯接壤的各国——要么夺取了它的大片领土——要么宣告自其斯戎统治之下独立。尽管这些新成立之邦的长期归属仍不明朗——但它们至少在一点上是一致的——那便是反对它们共同后院里那片切利亚斯地狱景观。

### 地狱遗产（THE INFERNAL INHERITANCE）

阿波罗盖二世的仪式——将余下的切利亚斯——变易为地狱的第十层。话虽如此——这场变易本应是全面的——并影响切利亚斯历代所辖的全部国土——但因玩家角色的介入——在这两方面都被强行打断。在一些地方——切利亚斯仍像它战前的样子——包括起伏的农田、与配备着凡人手造的、阴森建筑的繁忙城市。然而——即便在那些地方——天空也常常带有一种令人毛骨悚然的绛红色——而地形上——则星罗棋布着地狱要塞、自地里破出的锯齿地貌、与偶现的不灭火坑。变易——在埃戈里安周围最为显著——仪式的效应——随距离递减。阿波罗盖的领域是否会随时间扩张（以及可作出何等抵抗）——尚有待观察。

所幸——少数居民——并未被其家园所受影响那般剧烈地变易。一些平民——自发地化为**奈非利姆**——尤其是当地狱特征显现之时他们正在近旁。然而——大多数人——身形未变——但已彻底动摇。若有机会——大多数人都会想要逃走——但除了**多塔里**与亲斯戎的地狱骑士之外——一群魔鬼公开在切利亚斯巡逻——强制人服从。**铃花会**与其他爱好自由的派系——前方还有大量工作待做。

### 安多兰（ANDORAN）

代价惨重——但战争期间——安多兰的军队占据了切利亚斯东部的大片土地——而这些土地几乎未被阿波罗盖的仪式所触动。自此——安多兰已经接纳了**伊塞德河**（River Iseld）以南的国土——并将「**席尔米姆平原**」（Sirmium Plains）——以及**瑞麦西纳**、**欧斯坦索**、**布拉斯特沃克**（Brastlewark）等城市——划入版图。住在那里的大多数人——欢迎这一变化——他们已厌倦了斯戎的赋税与暴政。话虽如此——欧斯坦索及其海军基地——长期以来支持切利亚斯的野心——安多兰也预料到——未来数年——那里会有争议。

The Hell's Destiny Adventure Path concludes the wider
Hellfire Crisis, explored in other Pathfinder products like
Pathfinder Battlecry!, the Hellbreakers Adventure Path,
Pathfinder Lost Omens Hellfire Dispatches, novels,
Pathfinder Society scenarios, and more. The following
presents part of the new status quo, focusing on regions
the PCs visited and affected directly. Pathfinder Lost
Omens Cheliax, Infernal Inheritance explores each of
these regions (and many others) in far greater detail.

There are also hooks for the PCs to continue their
adventures, ranging from humanitarian missions to
destroying Abrogail once and for all. Pages 205–209
present further adventure possibilities to explore other
aspects of the Hellfire Crisis firsthand, perhaps with
this Adventure Path's PCs or by using other characters
of any level in short adventures.

The Aftermath

Gorthoklek's death doesn't end the war immediately,
but it does push Abrogail II to extend peace terms
to Andoran. Before the PCs interrupted her ritual,
Abrogail II had channeled her newfound power against
her enemies, devastating the invading army and navy.
Even though the PCs' actions have broken Cheliax's
will to keep fighting, not only have Andoran's forces
taken severe losses that sapped their momentum, but
the military's scouts and advisors have voiced dire
warnings about the infernal terrain now appearing
across Cheliax. Less than a decade has passed since
heroes sealed the Worldwound—now the Sarkoris
Scar—and Andoran's generals aren't willing to risk
marching troops into another unknown hellscape.

Within days, a devil representative bearing peace
terms appears in Almas atop the Archstone. Supreme
Elect Andira Marusek meets them, reviews the terms,
and discusses options with the People's Council before
reluctantly signing the armistice. With that, the Hellfire
Crisis gradually concludes, as warring factions around
Cheliax's periphery negotiate their own terms, chart
changing boundaries, and desperately study Cheliax
to understand the global repercussions of House
Thrune's infernal deal.

A Changing Map

The blood shed during the Hellfire Crisis wasn't in
vain; nations bordering Cheliax have either seized
much of its territory or declared their independence
from Thrune's rule. While these states' long-term
allegiances are uncertain, they're at least united in
their opposition to the Chelaxian hellscape in their
collective backyard.

A map of post-war boundaries appears on page 206.

THE INFERNAL INHERITANCE

Abrogail II's ritual transformed what remains of
Cheliax into the tenth layer of Hell. That said, the
transmogrification was supposed to be comprehensive
and affect all Cheliax's historically held lands—only to
be cut short in both respects by the PCs' intervention.
In some places, Cheliax still resembles its pre-war state,
including rolling farmlands and bustling cities outfitted
with mortal-made, macabre architecture. Yet even
there, the sky often has a hauntingly crimson hue, and
the landscape is dotted with infernal fortresses, jagged
terrain bursting from the ground, and the occasional
pit of unquenchable fire. The transformation is most
apparent around Egorian, with the ritual's effects
diminishing with distance. It remains to be seen
whether Abrogail's domain will expand over time (and
what might be done to stop it).

Fortunately, few inhabitants were affected as dramatically as
their homes. Some civilians
spontaneously became nephilim, especially if they were
nearby when a hellish feature manifested. However,
most remain unchanged yet thoroughly shaken. If
given a chance, most would flee, but in addition to its
dottari and pro-Thrune Hellknights, a host of devils
openly patrol Cheliax and enforce obedience. The
Bellflower Network and other freedom-loving factions
have their work cut out for them.

ANDORAN

At great cost, Andoran's army occupied large swaths
of eastern Cheliax during the war, and these lands were
virtually untouched by Abrogail's ritual. Andoran has
since welcomed the lands south of the River Iseld,
annexing the Sirmium Plains and cities like Remesiana,
Ostenso, and Brastlewark. Most of the people living
there welcome the change, having tired of Thrune
taxation and tyranny. That said, Ostenso and its naval
base have long supported Cheliax's ambitions, and
Andoran expects disputes there in the coming years.
