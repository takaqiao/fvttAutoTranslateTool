Gather Information—Streets: The recent war compounded with generations of Chelaxian xenophobia have made Westcrown's citizens wary of gossiping with strangers. Even so, a PC can get civilians talking with careful encouragement (DC 38 Diplomacy), a bit of menace (DC 34 Intimidation), or gossiping about the ongoing war (DC 34 Warfare Lore or DC 36 Society).

The typical civilian knows a little about the disappearing nobles but doesn't care too much if some soused aristocrat accidentally fell into the harbor. Beyond that, civilians report the Westcrown economy remains stagnant, even with the war increasing demand. The only goods making anyone any money are luxury fabrics. The PC can learn about some of the major clothiers, and Living Gold is known for the most expensive outfits.

The more pressing issue involves Asmodean priests; the clergy have cracked down on festivals, unsanctioned activities, gossiping, and such far more strictly than normal.
