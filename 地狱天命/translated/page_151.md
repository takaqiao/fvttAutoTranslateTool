## Page 151 — 第八章：裁缝与叛徒

"**战前**，**刑架骑士团曾推测西冠城可能正崛起一股新的犯罪势力**——**也许甚至是新一届「盗贼议会」（Council of Thieves）**——**他们使用这些符号**。**线索零星**，**刑架团把这些副本发给其他骑士团求助**——**最终无果**。**最近我从一名线人那里收到了新报告**。"

他取出一封更新的信件，**上面有更多设计纹样**。"**显然，这些设计正在演变，且出现在贵族服饰上**。**时尚本来不是我会关心的事**……**直到穿戴某种特定风格的人开始失踪**。**我不知道这一切如何串在一起**——**但其中正在发生的事情，代表着西冠城防线上一道我们可以利用的裂缝**。**或许你们会发现这是一个绑架团伙，救出被绑贵族，再说服他们背叛斯戎家族**。**也或许是某种嗜食地狱贵族的野兽**，**你们可以将其收为盟友**。**又或是某种沉睡特工**，**正在等待信号被唤醒**——**你们能识别出信号并加以利用**。

"**不管是哪种情况**，**我们都需要在西冠城半岛上为那座传送法阵确保一处地点**。**我们的部队可以夺取大陆侧的城区**，**而岛区也将很快归降**。"

托尔西斯**将纹样样本分享给玩家角色**——**每幅都以一个圆形元素（像硬币、眼睛或戒指）为核心**，**外围环绕着各种漩涡、羽毛状装饰与其他点缀**；**每一处装饰在不同样本之间略有差异**。**研究这些设计的玩家角色可尝试各种检定以了解更多**。

**切利亚斯学识（DC 34）或社交学（DC 36）**：**检定成功，玩家角色识别出这些纹样并非切利亚斯或邻近国家常见**——**暗示该设计要么从更远地方引入，要么是西冠城本地艺术家的独创**。**极佳成功**：**玩家角色注意到一些漩涡的弧度类似地图上的等高线**。**鲁瓦多恩执棒官拿出一张西冠城的地图**，**确认其中一些纹样的确像是复刻出该城特定街道、区界以及其他特征**。**这些城市特征为何被复刻其中，尚不清楚**。

**工艺（DC 34）**：**检定成功，玩家角色注意到要刺绣出特定的图案需要相当高的技艺**。**但变化看似有意为之，并非有人在尝试复制设计时粗心所致**。**极佳成功**：**玩家角色注意到漩涡装饰（类似从蕨叶茎部生出的单根细枝）的刺绣方式不是按惯例从"茎"开始向外绣至尖端**——**反而像是裁缝反向作业**，**仿佛"每条漩涡的终点"含有某种意义**。

**宗教（DC 36）**：**检定成功，玩家角色注意到这些纹样类似与奥罗登（Aroden）相关的旧符号**——**尤其是眼形设计**。**极佳成功**：**玩家角色还识别出一些漩涡与装饰类似魔鬼语（Diabolic）草体变体**——**仿佛有人将地狱信息（甚至可能是潜意识层面）编码入了传统的奥罗登信仰纹饰之中**。**这种文字似乎与任何特定大魔王或其他强大邪魔无关**。

**极差失败**：**玩家角色错把这一设计联系到玛门（Mammon）——地狱第三层艾尔伯斯的主宰**。**这位大魔王曾通过「盗贼议会」干预西冠城政治数十年**——**直到该机构在大约 4710 ar 崩塌**。**这或许暗示玛门的影响力正在抬头**。

**这些发现合在一起**，**便能帮助玩家角色识别出**：**其中有一套刺绣密码值得调查**。

### 西冠城的麻烦（WESTCROWN'S TROUBLES）

**作为切利亚斯长达数百年的首都**，**西冠城曾是该国南部沿海一座繁华的大都市**。**正是在这里**，**预言声称奥罗登（Aroden）将于 4606 ar 重返戈拉里翁，开启新时代**——**结果他却众目睽睽下陨落，由此开启了「失兆时代（Age of Lost Omens）」**。**这场悲剧让西冠城蒙羞**——**许多人都把它与那位神祇之死及随之而来的一切苦难联系在一起**。**几十年后，斯戎家族赢得内战后，将切利亚斯首都迁至埃戈里安**。**西冠城的大部分财富与残存声望都随之而去**——**让这座城市沦为人口稀薄、部分残破的旧日辉煌之影**。

**坦白说**，**当与安多兰的战争爆发时**，**阿波罗盖二世对西冠城几乎没有指望**。**该城为数不多的兵力**，**用于守卫港口、抵御任何从西边寇兰廷或东边欧斯坦索绕过的袭击者，更为合宜**。**因此**，**民众大多忽略了这场战争**。**仅有少数贵族带着家臣骑赴前线**——**想借战功复振家声，多半是徒劳之举**。

**凯森提娅的邪教（Kaisentia's Cult）**：**奥罗登的信徒蒙受重创**——**他们发现自己被切断了与曾经不朽的赞助神祇及其魔法的联系**。**大多数另寻新赞助人**——**只有日渐稀少的少数人仍紧抱他们陨落的信仰**。

**其中之一便是凯森提娅（Kaisentia，受惑女性艾乌瓦林人类祭司 17 级）**——**一位由奥罗登祭司养大的孤儿**。**她在奥罗登陨落前数周才宣誓加入祭司团**。**她固执地拒绝接受她那位赞助神祇的陨落**。**在随后的几十年里**，**她遍历冒险**，**寻找奥罗登隐居之处的任何迹象**——**毕竟，她推论：他必定是诈死，或施了别的什么精妙手段**。

Tolsis shares the pattern samples, each of which features a round element (resembling a coin, eye, or ring) surrounded by various whorls, feather-like designs, and other flourishes; each embellishment differs slightly between the sample patterns. A PC studying these designs can attempt various checks to learn more.

Cheliax Lore (DC 34) or Society (DC 36): With a successful check, the PC recognizes that these patterns aren't common in Cheliax or nearby nations, suggesting the design is either imported from farther away or is a unique creation of Westcrown's artists. With a critical success, the PC realizes some of the whorls curve like contours on a map. Lictor Ruvadorn pulls out a map of Westcrown and confirms that some of the patterns seem to recreate specific streets, district borders, and other features from the city. Why exactly these city features are recreated isn't clear.

Crafting (DC 34): With a successful check, the PC notes that embroidering a specific design would require considerable skill. However, the variations appear purposeful, not sloppy mistakes by someone trying to replicate the designs. With a critical success, the PC notes that the whorl decorations (akin to individual fronds growing from a fern's stem) weren't embroidered by starting at the "stem" and working outward to the tip, as would be common. Instead, it seems the tailor worked in reverse, as if the points at which each whorl ends holds some significance.

Religion (DC 36): With a successful check, the PC notices the patterns resemble old symbols associated with Aroden, especially an eye design. With a critical success, the PC also identifies that some of the whorls and flourishes resemble a cursive variant of Diabolic script, as if someone was encoding infernal (possibly even subliminal) messaging into traditional Arodenite motifs. It doesn't seem like the script is associated with any specific archdevil or other powerful fiend.

With a critical failure, a PC mistakenly connects the design to Mammon, ruler of Hell's third layer. The archdevil meddled in Westcrown politics for decades via the Council of Thieves before that institution was shattered around 4710 ar. This might suggest Mammon's influence is on the rise.

Collectively, these discoveries help the PCs recognize that there's an embroidered code to investigate.

WESTCROWN'S TROUBLES
The capital of Cheliax for centuries, Westcrown was once a bustling metropolis along the country's southern coast. Here, prophecy insisted that Aroden would return to Golarion to usher in a new age in 4606 ar, only to infamously perish and begin the Age of Lost Omens. The tragedy shamed Westcrown, which many associated with the god's death and all the suffering that followed. Decades later, House Thrune relocated Cheliax's capital to Egorian after winning the civil war. Most of Westcrown's wealth and remaining prestige followed, leaving the city a depopulated and partly ruined shadow of its former glory.

Frankly, Abrogail II expected little from Westcrown as war erupted with Andoran. The city's few troops were better used defending its harbor against whatever raiders might slip past Corentyn to the west or Ostenso to the east. As a result, the populace has mostly ignored the war. Only a handful of nobles and their retainers rode to the fronts, hoping to win spoils and glory in a vain attempt to restore their old fortunes.

Kaisentia's Cult: Aroden's faithful were especially hard-hit, finding themselves cut off from their once-immortal patron and his magic. Most adopted new patrons, and only a rapidly dwindling fraction clung to their fallen faith.

Among them was Kaisentia (deluded female aiuvarin human priest 17), an orphan raised by Aroden's priests and who'd taken her own vows to join the priesthood weeks before Aroden's death. She stubbornly refused to accept her patron's demise. In the following decades, she adventured extensively, seeking any sign of where Aroden had sequestered himself; after all, she reasoned, he must have faked his death or executed some other canny ploy.</details>
