### E2. 沉眠之穴（THE SLEEPING DEN）	中 14

此地原是大厅，如今则成为枭鬼们救助自幼起便收养的几只**修兰**的巢穴。这些被驯化的生物住在此处，用堡垒里坚硬矿物制成的家具来磨牙。如果玩家角色们是被护送通过此区域的，修兰们会好奇地嗅闻他们但不会进攻。否则，它们便守住此地。如果堡垒住户正在沉睡，夜行性的修兰也在打盹，允许玩家角色们以 DC 30 隐匿检定成功而潜行通过此室。只要超过一人未失败检定，修兰们就不会醒来。

**生物**：这些修兰过着深居简出的安逸生活，缺乏战术意识。当一只修兰被压到 45 HP 以下时，它会尖叫、钻地逃走，且至少一天内不会回来。

### E3. 空栖巢

这座三层高的塔，是枭鬼们用来聚集与休憩的栖巢之一。各层由螺旋阶梯相连，顶层是一处侦察哨——它探出堡垒之上，可观望**魔鬼栖**山脉西南许多英里之外，并提供登顶通道（见第 99 页「**飞越屋顶**」）。中层即是栖巢，是玩家角色们在外面遭遇的那三名守卫的卧息处。底层地面散落着羽毛与碎石——这是他们闲暇时尝试重制矮人石工技艺的痕迹。

### E4. 下栖巢	中 14

这座塔与「**空栖巢**」（E3）几乎一致，只是中层目前住着四名枭鬼。

**生物**：玩家角色们接近堡垒时，这四位住户多半正在沉睡——除非梅兰迪亚通报全堡警醒或巡查。如果玩家角色们不发出大声响，他们也不易醒来。但若堡垒住户已醒或正巡视，塔内枭鬼会奋战以保卫栖巢，生命降至 50 HP 或以下时撤退或投降。

### E2. THE SLEEPING DEN	MODERATE 14

Once the great hall, this area now houses a nest for shulns whom the strix rescued as pups. The tamed creatures live here, sharpening their teeth on the fortress's hard mineral furniture. If the PCs are escorted through this area, the shulns sniff at them curiously but don't attack. Otherwise, the shulns defend the area. If the fortress's occupants are asleep, the nocturnal shulns are napping, allowing the PCs to sneak through this room with successful DC 30 Stealth checks. As long as no more than one PC fails this check, the shulns don't wake up.

**Creatures**: The shulns have lived sheltered lives and have little tactical acumen. When a shuln is reduced to fewer than 45 HP, it screeches, flees by burrowing, and doesn't return for at least a day.

### E3. EMPTY ROOST

This three-story tall tower is one of the roosts used by the strix to congregate and sleep in. Connected by spiral staircases, the top floor is a lookout post that pokes above the fortress to view the southwestern part of the Devil's Perch range for many miles around and provides rooftop access (see Up and Over on page 99). The middle floor is the roost, the sleeping quarters for the three guards whom the PCs encountered outside. Feathers and broken bits of stone cover the ground floor—evidence of them trying to recreate dwarven stonework during downtime.

### E4. LOWER ROOST	MODERATE 14

This tower is identical to the empty roost (area E3), with four strix currently occupying the middle floor.

**Creatures**: When the PCs approach the keep, the four occupants are likely sleeping, unless Melandiar has alerted everyone inside to be awake or patrolling. If the PCs don't make any loud noises, they won't easily wake up. But if the keep's occupants are awake or patrolling, the strix inside fight to defend their roost from intruders, fleeing or surrendering if reduced to 50 HP or fewer.
