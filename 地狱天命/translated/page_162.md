## Page 162 — 第九章：荆棘之中

### 埃戈里安 · 荆棘之城（Egorian, City of Thorns）

一旦进入埃戈里安城内，**玩家角色便可在与各位线人见面的过程中较少受阻地穿行此城**。**埃戈里安远离任何前线**，**斯戎家族在此地竭力削弱战争的影响并维持市民的政治支持**。**然而这并不代表民众对战事毫不知情**。**当玩家角色穿城而过时**，**他们会看到数以百计的民族主义旗帜颂扬切利亚斯军队的强盛**，**店铺橱窗里贴着拥军告示**，**屋顶与塔尖飘扬着比平日更多的旗帜**。**只要玩家角色不引人注目、也不佩戴明显的反切利亚斯情感符号**，**多塔里**（**dottari**）**与狂热的爱国市民都不会找他们的麻烦**。

埃戈里安**又称「荆棘之城」**——**自 4640 ar「斯戎登基」（Thrune Ascendancy）以来便是切利亚斯的首都**。**当第一位阿波罗盖崛起掌权时**，**她将国家的权力中枢自西冠城的贵族手中迁出**，**移至此座临湖小城**，**并开启一项大规模城市更新计划**——**修建大量哥特式建筑**，**也即如今构成埃戈里安天际线的主体**。**自内海而来的贸易经阿迪维安河（Adivian River）溯流而上、穿过埃戈里安**——**最终经由自哀愁湖（Lake Sorrow）向北延伸的其他水道分送至全国各地**。**因此**，**商人与贸客始终络绎不绝**——**只是受战争影响**，**近来商贸略有掣肘**。

此城划分为 **23 个官方与非官方街区**。**玩家角色清楚他们要在何处见各位线人**（**见下文摘要**）——**且通过查阅各街角张贴的城图**，**可以被指向这些会面的正确方向**。

- **议和圆环**（**Parley Circle**，**见下文**）：**厄登·砾掌每日数次巡视此中产阶级广场**。**若玩家角色在附近的某间餐馆用餐**，**他应会注意到他们并悄然取得联系**。
- **长市集**（**Long Market**，**第 162 页**）：**琳奈塔·海凿从事经济谍报与破坏行动**。**玩家角色已掌握她所运作的某座仓库的地址**。
- **魔鬼之舞**（**Devil's Dance**，**第 163 页**）：**玛伊蕾·费兰**租下了一间小公寓，**以追踪埃戈里安城内魔法发展动向**。
- **地狱猎犬丘**（**Hellhound Hill**，**第 165 页**）：**作为较新近被招募的间谍**，**盖里乌斯·亨德塞恩常出没于「地狱猎犬丘」这一极尽奢华的街区**——**借以从该城贵族口中尽可能多打听些消息**。**他是玩家角色寻得通往皇宫之路的最佳资源**。

### 议和圆环（PARLEY CIRCLE）

玩家角色预定在**议和圆环**与厄登·砾掌会面——**那是一处中上阶级街区，其主要特色便是同名的圆环**。**该区中央矗立着一座以尖锐哥特式装饰物点缀的喷泉**，**周围则环绕着茶馆与餐厅**。**他们到此不到一小时**，**厄登便从一条巷子里冲出来**——**身后还拽着一位切利亚斯青年**。**「来不及解释了，」**他略喘着说，**「当地的多塔里正紧追我们不放，要找的就是这个小子。」**厄登**刚刚从附近的一处警卫所里把这名曾任皇宫仆从、后转扒手的青年救出**——**就在他即将被转移至更高戒备的设施之前**。

#### 摆脱多塔里（ESCAPE THE DOTTARI）　**中等 18 级**

**此遭遇采用《GM 核心规则》第 192 页所示的「追逐子系统」**。**玩家角色必须通过抵达最终障碍（或领先多塔里至少三个障碍）来摆脱追击的多塔里**——**同时还要尽力不被人看见**。**一旦玩家角色抵达「翻车事故」障碍**，**多塔里便加入追逐**——**在下一轮开始时抵达「议和圆环人群」障碍**。**之后多塔里每轮开始推进 1 个障碍**。**厄登与他的新负责对象始终跟随玩家角色**。

**若多塔里与玩家角色同时身处同一障碍**，**每位玩家角色须做一次 DC 36 潜行检定以避免脸被看见**（**此检定不影响其追逐点**）——**视你的裁量**，**可用欺骗等技能替代——例如玩家角色已乔装时**。**该检定可承受失败若干次**（**次数等于玩家角色人数的一半**）**而无后果**——**若多塔里多次追上**，**可能需多次此检定**。**进一步失败将使玩家角色被列为「受关注人物」**（**persons of interest**）——**即便他们成功完成追逐**。**此身份会让皇宫内所有守卫对玩家角色的察觉检定（用以观察并掷先攻）获得 +2 状况加值**。

**若某一轮结束时多塔里与玩家角色处于同一障碍**，**则多塔里已追上并将玩家角色逼入死角**。**鉴于玩家角色等级颇高**，**他们不太可能被长时间扣留**——**毕竟他们身怀绝世战技、可借魔法逃脱、舌灿莲花……**。**若被逮**，**一名玩家角色可尝试以下检定缓解局面**：**DC 34 法律学识检定、DC 36 交涉检定**（**若玩家角色不具备「群体印象」技能专长或类似能力**，**则 DC 38**）**或 DC 38 欺骗检定**。

- **极佳成功**　多塔里在离去前严厉警告玩家角色一番。
- **成功**　多塔里向玩家角色处以 **200 gp** 罚款——以惩其所致骚乱。
- **失败**　**同「成功」**，**但玩家角色将被进一步盘问**——**使他们成为后续遭遇中的「受关注人物」**。

**Egorian, City of Thorns**

Once inside Egorian, the PCs can move about the city with few impediments as they meet their contacts. Egorian is far from any of the fronts, and House Thrune has worked hard to soften the impact of the war here and maintain citizens' political support. However, that doesn't mean the populace is unaware of the fighting. As the PCs traverse the city, they spot hundreds of nationalistic banners extolling the strength of the Chelaxian armies, signs in shop windows that declare their support for the troops, and rooftops and spires flying more flags than usual. As long as the PCs don't do anything to draw attention to themselves or wear obvious symbols of anti-Cheliax sentiment, neither the dottari nor zealously patriotic citizens bother them.

Egorian, also known as the City of Thorns, has been the capital of Cheliax since the Thrune Ascendancy in 4640 ar. When the first Abrogail rose to power, she moved the country's seat of power away from the nobles of Westcrown to this lakeside town and began a major urban renewal program to construct the many gothic buildings that now constitute Egorian's skyline. Trade from the Inner Sea flows up the Adivian River and through Egorian, eventually moving to the rest of the country via the other waterways that extend northward from Lake Sorrow. As such, traders and merchants are a constant presence, though with the war, commerce has been a bit constrained lately.

The city is divided into 23 official and unofficial districts. The PCs know where to meet their contacts—summarized below—and can be pointed in the right direction for these meetings by consulting city maps posted at various street corners.

**Parley Circle (below)**: Urdun Gravelhands checks on this residential plaza several times a day. If the PCs dine at one of the nearby restaurants, he should notice and contact them discreetly.

**Long Market (page 162)**: Linetta Seacarver is engaged in economic espionage and sabotage. The PCs have the address of a warehouse she operates out of.

**Devil's Dance (page 163)**: Maire Phelan has rented a small apartment from which she's tracking magical developments in Egorian.

**Hellhound Hill (page 165)**: A more recently recruited spy, Garius Henderthane frequents the extravagant neighborhood of Hellhound Hill to learn what he can from the city's aristocracy. He's the PCs' best resource for finding a way into the Imperial Palace.

**PARLEY CIRCLE**

The PCs are scheduled to meet Urdun Gravelhands in Parley Circle, an upper-middle-class district whose main feature is the eponymous circle. The area features a central fountain with spiky gothic ornamentation surrounded by tea shops and restaurants. The PCs are there for less than an hour when Urdun comes rushing out of an alley with a young Chelaxian man in tow. "No time to explain," he says, a bit out of breath. "The local dottari are right on our trail and looking for this young fellow." Urdun just broke out this former Imperial Palace servant-turned-pickpocket from a nearby watchhouse right before he was to be moved to a more secure facility.

**ESCAPE THE DOTTARI	MODERATE 18**

This encounter follows the rules for the chase subsystem presented on page 192 of GM Core. The PCs must escape the pursuing dottari by reaching the final obstacle (or by being at least three obstacles ahead of the dottari) while also trying to remain unseen. Once the PCs reach the Cart Accident obstacle, the dottari enter the chase, reaching the Parley Circle Crowd obstacle at the beginning of the next round. The dottari then advance one obstacle at the beginning of each subsequent round. Urdun and his new charge remain alongside the PCs.

If the dottari are ever at the same obstacle as the PCs, each PC must attempt a DC 36 Stealth check to avoid having their faces seen (this doesn't impact their Chase Points); at your discretion, skills like Deception might be substituted, such as if a PC is disguised. The PCs can fail this check without consequence a number of times equal to half the number of PCs, potentially having to attempt this check multiple times if the dottari catch up to the PCs more than once. Further failures make the PCs "persons of interest" even if they succeed at the chase. This status grants all guards in the Imperial Palace a +2 circumstance bonus to their Perception checks to observe and roll initiative against the PCs.

If the dottari and the PCs are at the same obstacle at the end of a round, the dottari have caught up to and cornered the PCs. Given the PCs' high level, they're unlikely to be detained for long. After all, they have extraordinary combat skills, some access to magical escape, unmatched persuasiveness, and more. If caught, a PC can attempt to defuse the situation by attempting a DC 34 Legal Lore check, DC 36 Diplomacy check (DC 38 if the PC doesn't have the Group Impression skill feat or a similar ability), or DC 38 Deception check.

**Critical Success** The dottari give the PCs a stern warning before departing.

**Success** The dottari fine the PCs 200 gp for the disruptions they caused.

**Failure** As success, but the PCs are questioned further, making them "persons of interest" for future encounters.
