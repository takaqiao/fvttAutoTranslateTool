**LONG MARKET**

With the information given to them by their Andoren allies, the PCs can easily find the empty warehouse where they're to meet Linetta Seacarver at the edge of Long Market, an eclectic collection of stalls and shops in the northern quarter of the city. The market is very busy throughout the day, even during this time of war, with citizens buying and selling goods of all kinds. If the PCs need any common equipment, they can likely find it here. At night, the district is much quieter with all the shops closed and stalls shuttered. The designated warehouse is marked with a sign that reads "Condemned by order of the Lord Mayor" and bears a complicated stamp, but a PC who succeeds at a DC 35 Perception or Society check determines that it's a forgery.
