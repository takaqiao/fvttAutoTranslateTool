## Page 20 — Ch1 B. 碎冠者长廊（试炼场）

### B. 碎冠者长廊（Crowncrushers Gauntlet）

碎冠者不耐花哨言辞或长远谋略——那是给打算活下去的人留的。他们的计划只是熬到合适的时机砍下蛇头：**杀掉异端魁首**（archheathen）。可惜这位异端魁首早有防备。在等待合适时机之际，他们磨练身手，偶尔抢劫银行或占领某个政府办事处。

**凯旋**与**艾里尔**——碎冠者那对青少年双胞胎领袖——邀请 PC 们参加一场"骨之试炼"。**成功意味着碎冠者将加入这场联合起义**；**失败则意味着 PC 们的骨头将被碾碎**，正如他们打算碾碎切利亚斯王冠那般。

试炼在黄昏时分进行，观战的碎冠者可观看激战而不致引来附近守卫太多注意。要完成此试炼，PC 们必须**集齐三面带有碎冠者破裂王冠徽记的红旗**。若 PC 们需要撤退，碎冠者最多重置试炼 2 次。

试炼建在**贝尔维里奥城堡**（Castle Belverio）的残壳之中，位于高贵区西缘。中央处隆起着数座部分坍塌的塔楼，顶部建有平台（高 40 英尺）。西侧 60 英尺高处悬挑着一个观战平台，可让两打碎冠者俯瞰整条试炼线路。城堡的幕墙仍存，足够高到挡住路人窥视。本区域地图见第 12 页。

#### 碎冠者的起哄（Crowncrushers' Heckling）

碎冠者要的是刺激。若 PC 们采取过多"安全"选项，看客会起哄。**安全选项**包括：飞行远程攻击战斗者、连续多场遭遇停留在墙顶、对同一招式反复使用两次。受起哄时，PC 们在基于听觉的察觉检定与基于察觉的先攻骰上承受 -2 状况减值。当某位 PC 尝试一个足够冒险的招数时，起哄即停止。

#### B1. 起跑线（Starting Line）

> 残破的拱门将碎冠者长廊的石塔连接起来，开阔的平台之间仅以狭窄的通道相连。越过起跑线的边缘不远处，一处平台危险地架在一座更高的结构上。数十名观众挤在那里争抢最佳视野。欢呼与起哄声夹杂着传来，他们正等着试炼场上发生什么有趣的事情。
>
> 在试炼场中心高高升起的杆顶，一面带有"破裂王冠"徽记的鲜红旗帜随风飘扬。要塞的高墙挡住了杆基。

试炼起点提供两条选择：一条**看起来安全**的通道通向区域 B2；另一条则是绷紧的绳索（tightrope），连接到一个平台（也可凭 12 英尺的跳跃抵达）——通向区域 B3。

#### B2. 暗设栈道（Rigged Causeway）　**琐碎 10**

一条**看似平常**的栈道从起跑线连向下一座塔。

**危险**：路线其他部分会随碎冠者们的兴致而变，但这条栈道自最早设置以来一直没变。它被设计为：当生物踩在上面时随机坍塌，**借此告诫 PC 们不要走"轻松路线"**。如果某位 PC 踏入地图上标记的陷阱区域，看客们会陷入一片死寂，而一旦陷阱触发，他们立刻爆发出大笑。若所有 PC 都安然走过陷阱，看客们便开始起哄。

B. Crowncrushers Gauntlet
The Crowncrushers have no use for fancy words or long-term strategy. That's for those who intend to live. Their plan is to survive long enough to cut off the head of the snake: to kill the archheathen. Unfortunately, the archheathen is prepared for such attacks. While they wait for the right opportunity, they hone their skills and occasionally rob a bank or take over a government office.

Triumph and Eryr, the Crowncrushers' twin teenage leaders, invite the PCs to a "test of bones." Success means the Crowncrushers will join the allied rebellion. Failure means that the PCs' bones will be crushed, just as they intend to crush the Chelaxian crown.

The gauntlet takes place at dusk, so spectating Crowncrushers can watch the action without drawing too much attention from nearby guards. To complete the gauntlet, the PCs must collect three red flags with the Crowncrushers' broken crown symbol. If the PCs need to retreat from the gauntlet, the Crowncrushers reset it up to 2 times.

The gauntlet is built in the husk of Castle Belverio on the western edge of the High Quarter. At the center rise, several partially collapsed towers with platforms built atop them (40 feet high). A cantilevered platform sits 60 feet above the ground to the west so that two dozen Crowncrushers can watch with a clear view of the entire course. The castle's curtain wall survives and is tall enough to block passersby from seeing the action.
The map for this area is on page 12.

CROWNCRUSHERS' HECKLING
The Crowncrushers want excitement. If the PCs take too many "safe" options, the onlookers heckle them. Safe options include flying to attack the combatants at range, staying atop the wall for multiple encounters, or using the same trick twice. While heckled, the PCs take a –2 circumstance penalty to hearing-based Perception checks and to initiative rolls using Perception. The heckling stops after a PC attempts an appropriately risky maneuver.

B1. STARTING LINE
Crumbling arches connect the stone towers of the Crowncrushers gauntlet, offering only narrow passages between the more open platforms. Just past the edge of the starting line, a platform balances precariously on a higher structure. Dozens of spectators jostle for the best view. Cheers and heckling mix as they wait for something interesting to happen on the course.

Rising above the center of the course, a bright red flag emblazoned with a broken crown waves from the top of a pole. The fortress's tall wall obscures the base of the pole.

The gauntlet begins with a choice between a safe-looking passage to area B2 and a tightrope attached to a platform that's also reachable with a 12-foot jump (which leads to area B3).

B2. RIGGED CAUSEWAY	TRIVIAL 10
A normal-appearing causeway leads from the starting line to the next tower.

Hazard: While the rest of the course changed over time based on the Crowncrushers' whims, this causeway has stayed the same since it was first introduced. The causeway is set to collapse randomly when creatures walk on it, which serves as a lesson to avoid the easy way though the course. If a PC steps into the area of the trap marked on the map, the spectators become dead silent, only to burst into laughter if it's triggered. If all the PCs safely walk over the trap, the spectators start heckling.
