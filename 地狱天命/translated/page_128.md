### A. 河上复仇（RIVER REVENGE）　中等 16

**伊塞尔德河**（Iseld River）是一条贯穿沉言林中部的关键水道，从**米萨利亚斯城**（Misarias）一路延伸至切利亚斯港口**瑞麦西纳**（Remesiana）。随着安多兰军方的战事不断推进，斯戎家族的援军源源不断地由河驳船运来。通常情况下，伊塞尔德河两岸在沉言林东缘附近过于陡峭，无法大批量登岸，但在一位新上任的指挥官调度下，斯戎家族的后勤部队搭建了一座**临时栈桥**，让士兵们能下船登岸。

在海战中，玩家角色曾让**副伯爵船长沃蕊·塔尔米纳里**（Paracountess Captain Vorrea Talminari）颜面扫地，迫她弃旗舰逃回大陆。如今降职去负责部队后勤、不再担任前线军事指挥的她正怒火中烧、伺机复仇。玩家角色们的到来——可能正给了她这个机会。

A. RIVER REVENGE　MODERATE 16

The Iseld River is a critical waterway that runs through the middle of the Whisperwood, reaching from the city of Misarias all the way to the Chelaxian port of Remesiana. As the battle with the Andoren military continues to evolve, House Thrune's reinforcements steadily stream in by river barge. Normally, the Iseld's banks are too steep to disembark large numbers of troops this close to the Whisperwood's eastern border, but under a new commanding officer, Thrune's logistics corps have built a makeshift jetty to get their soldiers off the ships and onto shore.

During their naval campaign, the PCs disgraced Paracountess Captain Vorrea Talminari, forcing her to abandon her flagship and flee back to the mainland. Now demoted to being in charge of troop logistics rather than a frontline military command, she seethes, hoping for a chance at revenge. With the PCs' arrival, it's possible she'll get it.

This is a skirmish encounter. The objective is to destroy the river jetty (Hardness 10, 80 HP, object immunities) and kill Vorrea Talminari and her bodyguard. When the jetty is destroyed, it slides into the water, taking any troops with it who are standing on it. Once the Chelaxian commander and the cannoneer are slain with the jetty destroyed, any remaining non-devil troops flee the area. GMs should describe the sight of other river barges laden with troops approaching the area to encourage their players to deal with the makeshift dock as soon as possible.

Creatures: When the PCs and their troops arrive in the wooded area near the makeshift dock, Vorrea Talminari and two of her lieutenants are overseeing four Chelaxian infantry troops being disembarked from river barges.
