### E. 沉言林之战的遭遇（Battle of the Whisperwood Encounters）

随着玩家角色穿越沉言林不断演变的战场，他们将被迫陷入更直接的冲突。处理这些遭遇所花的时间**不计入**渗透回合末警觉点的累积。此外，玩家角色每次遭遇之后可花 10 分钟原地休整、不受惩罚。**若在此基础上多滞留 10 分钟**，便每段额外的 10 分钟累积 **1 AP**。

如果玩家角色获得了由**乔吉努斯将军**提供的**精确炮击协助包**所代表的战场点（第 133 页），他们可以选择将任何已累积的 BP 用于在这些遭遇中召唤炮击（如第 125 页所述），**每场遭遇最多召唤一次**。

由于渗透子系统的本质偏抽象，**建议**你以最合理的方式将玩家角色的位置串联起来——让他们在**第一阶段末抵达 E1 区**、**第二阶段末抵达 E2 区**、**第三阶段末抵达 E3 区**、**第四阶段末抵达 E4 区**。但如果玩家角色在完成第四阶段前便达到了 **25 警觉点**的上限，则**战斗失败**——玩家角色将被迫**孤军面对**遭遇 E4，没有部队与塔尔曼铎的支援，孤立无援地困在敌阵之中。

E. Battle of the Whisperwood Encounters

As the PCs progress through the evolving battlefields of the Whisperwood, they're forced to engage in more direct confrontations. Time spent resolving these encounters don't count toward the accrual of Awareness Points at the end of infiltration rounds. Additionally, the PCs can take 10 minutes to rest and recuperate after each encounter without penalty. For each additional 10 minutes that the PCs tarry beyond this period, however, they gain 1 AP.

If the PCs earned the Battlefield Point from General Georginus represented by the precision artillery package (page 133), they can choose to use any of their accumulated BP to call in artillery strikes during these encounters (as described on page 125), to a maximum of one artillery strike per encounter.

Because the nature of the infiltration subsystem is somewhat abstract, you're encouraged to tie the PCs' location together in the way that makes the most sense to them so that they arrive at area E1 at the end of Phase 1, at area E2 at the end of Phase 2, area E3 at the end of Phase 3, and area E4 at the end of Phase 4. If the PCs reach the maximum of 25 Awareness Points before completing Phase 4, however, the battle is lost, and the PCs are forced to face encounter E4 alone without any support from their troops or Talmandor, trapped behind enemy lines.

E1. TANGLED BRIARS　LOW TO MODERATE 16

Fertilized by the blood spilled by the armies fighting in the Whisperwood, foul plant creatures controlled by House Thrune guard the northern edge of the fields surrounding the Inferno Gate. When the PCs and their troops enter this area, a wall of rock and earth suddenly shoots out of the ground, separating the heroes from their special forces units and forcing the PCs to engage in this battle by themselves. The terrain in this area is relatively flat earth, surrounded to the northwest and southeast by thick trees. The remnants of a decades-old military encampment can be seen half-buried in the ground.

Creatures: Two wemmuths emerge from either side of the woodland areas to flank the PCs. These huge creatures silently close in on the PCs, intent on their destruction. If the PCs tarried too long during their infiltration and reached 10 Awareness Points before completing Phase 2 of the infiltration, there are three wemmuths here to attack them instead of just two. All wemmuths fight until destroyed.
