### 玛琳获救

无论以何种方法获救，玛琳都感激玩家角色们的搭救。但在哥哥巴林同样安全之前，她不会道出所知的一切。如果玩家角色们尚未把他救出来，她便先回**漂白鲸**等候他们归来。如果玩家角色们已经把巴林带回了漂白鲸，那么她将加入他们。此时，继续阅读第 98 页「**晶莹清明**」（Sparkling Clarity）。

如果玩家角色们以非暴力手段把玛琳从总督府邸救出，他们获得 2 调查点；通过杀死梅利亚·阿鲁克图斯将她救回，则获得 1 调查点。如果玩家角色们攻打驻军，则不获调查点；如果他们另外花时间洗劫了堡垒，扣 1 调查点。

### 地下听众（UNDERGROUND AUDIENCE）

要与**阿玛丽娅·拉克斯顿**对话，玩家角色们须先找到「**地穴**」（The Burrows）的入口。若他们见过老麦琪与她的面条推车，则可直接前往（并获得 1 调查点）。否则，玩家角色们须设法搞清地穴在哪里。

传言地穴的入口被魔法所遮蔽。要找到它，玩家角色们必须先以小队为单位选择佩札克的哪一片来搜——把这座城分作三份（港口与北段、南段、西段）。然后每位玩家角色可尝试一次 DC 32 奥秘、自然、神秘、宗教或求生检定。若玩家角色们成功 2 次或以上，他们便知道自己是否走对了路。地穴真正的入口在镇东南缘一家蘑菇农场——「**贝瑞布莱克的地下美味**」（Berryblack's Underground Delights）之下。每当玩家角色们（作为整队）搜查港口与北段或西段时，扣 1 调查点。

一旦玩家角色们成功搜查到镇南段，其中一人必须以一次 DC 34 察觉检定成功，才能跟随正确的路径到「**地穴**」而非误入「**腑肠**」（the Guts；佩札克底下相互交错的走私者隧道群）。如果所有玩家角色此次检定都失败，他们多扣 1 调查点，但可以再试。

最终，他们抵达一座朴素而经加固的剧场，与**阿玛丽娅·拉克斯顿**相会。这位剧作家无意与玩家角色们交战，却也并不信任他们。和谈期间被刺客杀害的**哈巴·柯尔**，正是她的好友与亲密盟友，那次刺杀彻底破坏了佩札克各派系互相寻得喘息的机会。要说服她听他们说话，玩家角色们必须以对抗切利亚斯的功绩动之以情——可尝试一次 DC 30 表演检定、DC 32 交涉或宗教检定，或 DC 34 欺骗或社交检定。只要至少两名玩家角色成功（或一名玩家角色大成功），她便愿意听他们讲述，玩家角色们获得 1 调查点。如果玩家角色们未能说服她，她会客气但严厉地请他们离开。

如果地穴是玩家角色们的第一站、且他们成功让阿玛丽娅愿意交谈，她会向他们透露关于布鲁克斯的事——内容与玩家角色们若去**桶市**做过消费打听到的情报相同。她还知道忠党派正软禁玛琳为人质，但不明所以——尤其是她原以为**达让·陶拉诺尔**比他那位已故的父亲更有荣誉感。

### Marynn Rescued

Marynn is thankful to the PCs for her rescue regardless of method, but she isn't willing to share all she knows until her brother Barynn is also safe. If the PCs haven't yet recovered him, she returns to the Bleached Whale to wait for their return. If the PCs have already brought Barynn back to the Bleached Whale, she joins them. At this point, proceed to Sparkling Clarity on page 98.

The PCs receive 2 Investigation Points if they rescued Marynn from the Governor's Manse without resorting to violence and 1 Investigation Point if they retrieved her by slaying Meliar Aructus. The PCs receive no Investigation Points for attacking the garrison and lose 1 Investigation Point if they take the extra time to loot the fortress.

### UNDERGROUND AUDIENCE

To speak with Amalia Wraxton, the PCs need to find The Burrows. If they've met Old Madge and her noodle cart, they can go straight there (and gain 1 Investigation Point). If not, the PCs need to figure out where it is.

Rumors say that the entrances to The Burrows are magically concealed. To find them, the PCs must first decide which part of Pezzack to search as a group, dividing the city into thirds (harbor and north, south, and west). Each PC can then attempt a DC 32 Arcana, Nature, Occultism, Religion, or Survival check. If the PCs succeed at two or more checks, they know if they're on the right track. The true entrance to The Burrow is beneath Berryblack's Underground Delights, a mushroom farm on the southeastern edge of town. Each time the PCs (as a group) search either the harbor and north or western sections of town, they lose 1 Investigation Point.

Once the PCs successfully search the southern section of town, one of them must succeed at a single DC 34 Perception check to follow the correct path to The Burrows and not down into "the Guts" (a series of interconnected smuggler's tunnels that crisscross underneath Pezzack). If all the PCs fail this check, they lose 1 more Investigation Point, but they can try again.

Eventually, they reach a modest, reinforced theater and are met by Amalia Wraxton. The playwright has no interest in fighting the PCs but also doesn't trust them. It was her friend and close ally, Habar Curl, who was killed by the assassins at the peace talks, disrupting the chance for Pezzack's factions to find a detente with one another. To convince her to listen to them, the PCs must ply her with the tale of their deeds against Cheliax by attempting a DC 30 Performance check, a DC 32 Diplomacy or Religion check, or a DC 34 Deception or Society check. So long as at least two PCs succeed (or one PC critically succeeds), she hears them out, and they gain 1 Investigation Point. If the PCs fail to convince her, she politely but sternly asks them to leave.

If The Burrows is the PCs' first stop and they get her to speak with them, she shares things about Brucks as though the PCs made purchases in Tubmarket to gain information there. She also knows that the loyalists are holding Marynn hostage but isn't sure why, especially since she thought Darjan Tauranor to be a more honorable man than his father, the late governor.
