#### A5. 隐藏拷问室（HIDDEN TORTURE CHAMBER）　**中等 18 级**

> **此阴沉室内**，**干涸血腥味与恐惧气息浓重弥漫**。**各种致死与拷问的器具**——**从拉刑架到铁处女**——**散布四处**。**入口对面的墙上挂着数件手持拷问器具——以及看似一片片皮肤的物事**。

**虽然皇宫地牢自有其拷问室——专为那些（多半涉政治）不幸被关在那里的囚徒——但此层楼当初也建有一间较小的「隐藏房间」——以行同样残忍的活动**。**阿波罗盖女王维持了这一残忍的传统**——**且以她的私人拷问室对待非常特殊的「贵客」**——**那些曾亲身冒犯过她、或掌握有重大国情资讯的人物**。**目前此处没有任何囚徒在受拷**。

**生物**：**阿波罗盖向她北方的奈多邻邦请来一位「维斯特拉克魔」（velstrac）作为她私人的施刑者**。**作为对其传授「精妙痛楚」之能的回报**——**这位「隐修者」（eremite）**得允**以其受害者进行扭曲的「血肉实验」**。**它最近的一位「贵客」便是塔尔曼铎**——**那位天界使对大部分拷问手法皆有抗性**——**故它正急于将一些凡人皮肤嫁接到它那狰狞的躯壳之上**。**当玩家角色进入时**，**它正在南墙的钩子旁**——**示意英雄们靠近**——**轻声低语着许诺将「升华改良」他们的躯体**——**之后随即发动攻击**。**该隐修者死战不退**。

**A5. HIDDEN TORTURE CHAMBER	MODERATE 18**

> The smell of dried blood and terror hangs heavily in this dingy chamber. Various devices of death and torture, from the rack to the iron maiden, are scattered about. The wall opposite the entrance features several hooks that bear a few handheld torture implements and what look like shreds of skin.

Though the palace dungeon holds its own torture chamber for those (usually political) prisoners unfortunate enough to be incarcerated there, this floor was constructed with a smaller, hidden room for similarly depraved activities. Queen Abrogail has maintained this cruel tradition and uses her personal torture chamber for very special victims who have either personally slighted her or who hold information of national importance. No prisoners are currently being tortured here.

**Creatures**: Abrogail requested the services of a velstrac as her personal torturer from her Nidalese neighbors to the north. In return for their ability to deliver exquisite pain, this eremite is allowed to use their victims for their twisted experiments of flesh. Their last "guest" was Talmandor, and the celestial proved resistant to most torture practices, so they're eager to graft some mortal skin onto their hideous frame. They are near the hooks on the southern wall when the PCs enter and beckon for the heroes to come closer with whispered promises of "transcendental improvement" of their forms before attacking. The eremite fights until destroyed.
