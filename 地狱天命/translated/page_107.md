# 第六章
# 矛尖（Tip of the Spear）

对抗切利亚斯的「西方同盟」刚刚结成，玩家角色们却无暇为这份功绩自满。新同盟成立后短短片刻，**忒尔齐纳**——一位前**锁链骑士团**的地狱骑士信使、现任**破狱者**成员——便冲过满堂权贵直奔队伍而来，闯入了那场议事会。他仍然身披地狱骑士铠甲、头戴信使盔，但其上的图记已被划损：彰显他虽以从前的功绩为荣，却已弃绝自己的军衔与称号。即便议题如此急迫，**忒尔齐纳**（他坚持让玩家角色们称呼他「**忒尔**」）仍带着几分友善。不过，他并非完全坦诚以告。

**忒尔**告诉玩家角色们：切利亚斯正在**切利亚斯东部的沉言林**（Whisperwood）一带集结大军，安多兰也正在向前线调遣自己的军队。**破狱者**希望由他们信得过的人在地面战开打之前替军中领袖们出谋划策，故此**忒尔齐纳**奉命前来邀请玩家角色们，并尽快把他们带去**沉言林**。

**忒尔**所说大体属实。安多兰确实在寻求援手，却并未点名要玩家角色们。**忒尔**担心安多兰对这场仗准备得严重不足，他相信玩家角色们将至关重要；这位地狱骑士还担心玩家角色们若收到不算热情的接风礼，恐怕不愿乖乖随他前往。他便想出这一半真半假的说辞，作为一种向自己内心妥协的折中——他对真相与秩序的执念，至今仍残留着他在地狱骑士岁月里塑成的痕迹。如果一名玩家角色通过 DC 35 察觉检定**察觉动机**，便能看出他有所保留，但也能看出他确实相信玩家角色们是胜利的关键。此外，他们还能辨明：**忒尔**对玩家角色们心怀最高敬意，并未要把他们引入陷阱。若被当面戳穿，他会迟疑片刻，最终承认：安多兰

# Tip of the Spear

The PCs have no time to rest on their laurels following the formation of the Western Alliance against Cheliax. Just moments after the new alliance's formation, Telzinah, a former Hellknight signifer from the Order of the Chain and current member of the Hellbreakers, arrived at the council meeting, pushing past dignitaries to reach the party. He still wears his Hellknight armor and signifer helm, but its iconography has been defaced, indicating that while proud of his accomplishments, he's abandoned his rank and titles. Despite the desperate topic of conversation, Telzinah (who insists the PCs call him "Tel") is friendly. He isn't, however, entirely forthright.

Tel informs the PCs that Cheliax is massing forces near the Whisperwood in eastern Cheliax and that Andoran is dispatching its armies to the front as well. The Hellbreakers want people they trust to help guide the military leaders before the ground war begins, so Telzinah was sent to retrieve the PCs and bring them to the Whisperwood as quickly as possible.

Tel mostly speaks true. Andoran is looking for aid, but they didn't call for the PCs specifically. Tel fears that Andoran is woefully unprepared for the fight and believes the PCs will be crucial; the Hellknight also fears that the PCs won't simply follow him into an unwelcome reception. He devised this half-truth as a compromise to his internal need for truth and order, still remaining from his time as a Hellknight. With a successful DC 35 Perception check to Sense Motive, a PC discerns that he's holding something back but also that he truly does believe the PCs are key to victory.
