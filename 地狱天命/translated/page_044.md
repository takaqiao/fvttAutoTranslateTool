#### B2A. 1036 号牢房（Cell 1036）

**生物**：当 PC 们找到**路登**时，他几乎因怒气而颤抖。

**路登·马尔迪努斯**——昔日锁链骑士团的利刃大师——在他的牢房中来回踱步。当执棒官宣告骑士团的忠诚方向改变时，路登抵抗了。锁链骑士团、地狱骑士之路（Path of the Hellknight）以及**准绳与锁链**为他这个出身于贫困的童年的人创造了一种生活。他曾被生命可能的去向所淹没，地狱骑士那循规蹈矩的确定性赋予了他意义。

锁链骑士团的教诲让他理解了世界与他所经历的恐怖。他终于明白这些事为何会发生在他身上，为何花了那么久才相信"更好的生活是可能的"，又为何在他重获自由之后寻求规则来约束自己。**抛弃这些原则将是异端，将是个人的背叛**。

他的反抗只持续了短暂却辉煌的 **30 分钟**。乌罗的演说之后，路登径直走入执棒官走廊并占据了那里。虽然他没有自封为执棒官，但他声明乌罗已**背弃了锁链骑士团**。路登将作为剩余的"利刃大师"接管，凡仍想留在"锁链"麾下的人都可向他报到。他聚集了半打支持者，直到执棒官乌罗·阿登抵达并亲自将他逮捕。

如今，他被关进了他曾经守卫的监狱。乌罗甚至没有把他放进加固牢房——一个持续的挑衅，他相信路登无力进行一次简单的越狱。一看到 PC 们，他便苦笑出声；他们能渗透格拉戴斯卡堡垒，恰恰证明乌罗已**失去控制**。他对这一认识的笑声听起来或许像是被环境击垮，但其实是一个**终于得到平反的人**的笑声。凭借他对堡垒的了解，他知道哪里可以藏匿……（接下页）

Leuden Mardinus, the erstwhile Master of Blades of the Order of the Chain, paces his cell. When the lictor announced that the order's loyalties were shifting, Leuden resisted. The Order of the Chain, the Path of the Hellknight, and the Measure and the Chain created a life for him after an impoverished childhood. He'd been overwhelmed by the possibilities of where his life could take him, and the routine certainty of the Hellknights provided meaning.

The lessons of the Order of the Chain allowed him to make sense of the world and the horrors he experienced. He finally understood why it'd happened to him, why it took him so long to believe in the possibility of a better life, and why he sought rules to govern himself after he became free. To leave those principles behind would be heresy. It would be personal betrayal.

His resistance lasted for a brief yet glorious 30 minutes. After Uro's speech, Leuden marched into the Lictor's Walk and occupied it. While he refrained from declaring himself lictor, he did state that Uro had abandoned the Order of the Chain. Leuden would take charge as the remaining Master of Blades, and those who wished to remain with the Chain could report to him. He gathered a half-dozen supporters before Lictor Uro Adom arrived and personally arrested him.

Now, he's been thrown into the prison that he once guarded. Uro didn't even place him in one of the reinforced cells, a constant goad that he believes Leuden incapable of a simple prison break. Upon seeing the PCs, he laughs bitterly; their ability to penetrate Citadel Gheradesca proves that Uro has lost control. His laughter at this realization might sound like he's broken under the circumstances, but it's that of a man who's finally been vindicated.
