### 战役总览

当切利亚斯颁布戒严令、PC 们被困寇兰廷之际，他们与安多兰间谍**琳奈塔·海凿**（Linetta Seacarver）携手联合反抗派系——不仅是为了解放寇兰廷，更要打破切利亚斯的海上封锁，好让镣铐群岛（Shackles）的海盗能够援助安多兰。PC 们必须先帮助每一个派系，赢得他们的信任。随后他们将在镣铐骑士团（Order of the Manacle）令人不寒而栗的总部——格拉戴斯卡堡垒——发起一场惊心动魄的越狱行动；琳奈塔的导师厄登（Urdun）连同无数潜在盟友都被关押于此。

海盗抵达后，与切利亚斯海军陷入僵持。为了在切利亚斯增援赶到之前争取更多盟友打破封锁，PC 们与一位被解救的囚犯——一位水栖精灵贵族（aquatic elf noble）——一同跨海西行；这位贵族承诺，只要 PC 们护送他回归故土，他的族人就会出手援助。在劫得一艘船、引导精灵盟友驶向寇兰廷之后，PC 们还挫败了一位切利亚斯恶魔教士（diabolist）那狂妄的图谋——他妄图唤醒远古邪恶以助切利亚斯一臂之力。

随后，PC 们集结、招募切利亚斯西部周边的更多盟友，建立联盟开辟第二战线。首先，PC 们协助解放卡利——一座切利亚斯数百年前从拉哈多姆夺取的城市。接着，PC 们挫败邪恶的阿扎克缇（azarketis）的魔法阴谋——他们已将一件海底阿兹兰提（Azlanti）神器化作武器。最后，PC 们协助动员拉沃努尔的军队，并将潜在的枭鬼（strix）盟友从恶魔的控制中解放出来。

安多兰对此印象深刻，邀请 PC 们加入即将在切利亚斯东部展开的攻势。即便如此，安多兰的部分将领仍对这些异军突起的英雄心存疑虑，于是 PC 们出手清除切利亚斯从附近邪恶的沉言林（Whisperwood）所征召的威胁。

证明自身实力后，PC 们加入了由安多兰守护圣灵亲自统领的精锐军团：塔尔曼铎。然而切利亚斯早有准备——他们用一台地狱机械擒获了塔尔曼铎，将其押回阿波罗盖·斯戎处，并冲散了他的部队——包括陷入敌后的 PC 们！PC 们通过魔法与安多兰领袖们协作，受命潜入切利亚斯沿海各城，在城内布设传送法阵（teleportation circles），让安多兰士兵得以绕过这些港口的防御直取城市。诸将军希望切利亚斯会因此调动其剩余预备队，让埃戈里安几近空虚。届时，PC 们便可渗透敌方首都、解救塔尔曼铎。

计划得以顺利推行——直到 PC 们抵达埃戈里安。在那里，他们才知道阿波罗盖二世为何要活捉塔尔曼铎：她意欲与阿斯摩蒂斯（Asmodeus）签订一份新契约，并以这位天界领主（empyreal lord）作为对价。与她祖先那份将切利亚斯绑定于地狱的契约不同，这份新契约将把切利亚斯**化为**地狱，而阿波罗盖二世将摇身一变成为其大魔王（archdevil）！PC 们必须铲除统治者的下属、查明阻止阿波罗盖二世的方法，同时还要在埃戈里安部分区域陷入地狱化变形之际保护市民。最终，他们必须强袭地狱本身、解救塔尔曼铎、与女王的尼萨利（nessari）统帅决战，并打断仪式。

Hell's Destiny

ADVANCEMENT TRACK
"Hell's Destiny" is designed for four characters.

10  The PCs begin this adventure at 10th level.
11  The PCs should be 11th level after raiding the Imperious.
12  The PCs should be 12th level after escaping Citadel Gheradesca.
13  The PCs should be 13th level before assisting Khari's liberation.
14  The PCs should be 14th level when they travel to Ravounel.
15  The PCs should be 15th level by the time they meet with Andoran's generals.
16  The PCs should be 16th level upon joining Talmandor's battalion.
17  The PCs should be 17th level after the Battle of the Whisperwood.
18  The PCs should be 18th level upon reaching Egorian.
19  The PCs should be 19th level after exploring Abrogail's palace.
20  The PCs should be 20th level by the time they enter Hell.

Trapped in Corentyn when Cheliax imposes martial law, the PCs join the Andoren spy Linetta Seacarver in uniting rebel factions—not just to liberate Corentyn, but also to break Cheliax's naval blockade so Shackles pirates can aid Andoran. The PCs must aid each of the factions to earn their trust. The PCs then stage a daring prison break at Citadel Gheradesca, the foreboding headquarters of the Order of the Manacle Hellknights, where Linetta's mentor Urdun is imprisoned alongside myriad other potential allies.

The pirates arrive and fight Cheliax's navy to a stalemate. Hoping to secure more allies to break the blockade before Chelaxian reinforcements arrive, the PCs travel the seas to Cheliax's west with one of the liberated prisoners, an aquatic elf noble who promises his people's aid if they escort him home. After stealing a ship and directing their elven allies toward Corentyn, the PCs also thwart a Chelaxian diabolist's bold schemes to awaken ancient evils to aid Cheliax.

From there, the PCs rally and recruit additional allies neighboring western Cheliax, creating a coalition that opens a second front. First, the PCs help liberate Khari, a city Cheliax seized from Rahadoum centuries ago. The PCs then thwart the magical schemes of evil azarketis who have weaponized an undersea Azlanti artifact. Finally, the PCs help mobilize Ravounel's army and free potential strix allies from diabolical control.

Impressed, Andoran invites the PCs to join an upcoming offensive in eastern Cheliax. Even so, some of Andoran's generals remain skeptical about these upstart heroes, so the PCs neutralize threats that Cheliax has recruited from the sinister Whisperwood nearby.

Having proven themselves, the PCs join an elite battalion led by the celestial patron of Andoran himself: Talmandor. However, Cheliax prepared for this possibility. Using a hellish machine, Cheliax captures Talmandor, carries him back to Abrogail Thrune, and scatters his forces—including the PCs, who are trapped behind enemy lines! Collaborating with Andoran's leaders via magic, the PCs are directed to infiltrate coastal Chelaxian cities and establish teleportation circles inside so that Andoren soldiers can bypass these ports' defenses and seize the cities. The generals hope that Cheliax will deploy its remaining reserves, leaving Egorian nearly undefended. From there, the PCs can infiltrate the enemy's capital and free Talmandor.

The plan works—at least until the PCs reach Egorian. There, they learn why Abrogail II wanted Talmandor alive: she intends to sign a new deal with Asmodeus and offer the empyreal lord as payment. Unlike her ancestor's deal that bound Cheliax to Hell, this new pact will turn Cheliax into Hell, with Abrogail II as its archdevil! The PCs must neutralize the ruler's subordinates and learn how to stop Abrogail II, all while shielding citizens as parts of Egorian undergo hellish transformation. Ultimately, they must storm Hell itself, save Talmandor, confront the queen's nessari general, and interrupt the ritual.
