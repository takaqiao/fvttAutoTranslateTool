## Page 143 — 第八章：裁缝与叛徒

尽管安多兰或许在沉言林之战中拿下战术胜利，**切利亚斯却收下了一场战略胜利**——他们叫停了安多兰的攻势、俘获其天界守护神、并将玩家角色及其地狱骑士盟友从主力部队中切离。**为避免被擒**，**托尔西斯·鲁瓦多恩执棒官**带领玩家角色与他们幸存下来的部队**深入沉言林**。在那里，他们能重整队伍，并联络安多兰的将军们以协调下一步行动。

这给玩家角色**一日休整时间**，地狱骑士则负责侦察并收容更多幸存者——一位或多位玩家角色可尝试**DC 31 侦察学识检定、DC 34 战争学识检定、DC 36 医疗检定，或 DC 36 察觉检定**协助此过程。若至少有一位玩家角色成功，他们便判明切利亚斯部队并未乘胜追击——他们只追了安多兰部队几英里以确保撤退完成。**切利亚斯军已分成数大群**，其中一部分散布乡间以恢复秩序；另一支**重要分队已撤出并向西移动，可能开往埃戈里安**。若至少有一位玩家角色**极佳成功**，他们还能从一具被击败的魔鬼斧上摘下一枚**精良獠牙刀符箓**（greater toothy knife talisman，《探路者战吼！》第 137 页）。

随着各路侦察情报陆续传来，托尔西斯请求与玩家角色会面，**商讨下一步**。

---

> "**结果惨淡**，" 托尔西斯·鲁瓦多恩执棒官一边浏览铺在一截老树桩上的几张地图，一边坦言。"**我们打得很勇**——这一点毋庸置疑。但有时候啊，**那头野兽就是会比你更强**——任你带多少长矛上阵也没用。" 他扭了扭脖子，随后总结道："**我们必须适应**，并把握**这场新近降临的厄运**为我们带来的**任何机会**。"

# 裁缝与叛徒（TAILORS AND TRAITORS）

<details><summary>English Source</summary>

Though Andoran might have won a tactical victory at the Battle of the Whisperwood, Cheliax achieved a strategic victory by halting Andoran's offensive, capturing its celestial patron, and separating the PCs and their Hellknight allies from the main army. To avoid capture, Lictor Tolsis Ruvadorn leads the PCs and their surviving troops deeper into the Whisperwood. There, they can regroup and contact Andoran's generals to coordinate their next moves.

This affords the PCs a day's rest as the Hellknights reconnoiter and gather more survivors—a process one or more PCs can aid by attempting a DC 31 Scouting Lore check, DC 34 Warfare Lore check, DC 36 Medicine check, or DC 36 Perception check. If at least one PC succeeds, they determine Cheliax's forces haven't pressed the advantage, having chased Andoran's forces only a few miles to ensure the retreat. Instead, Cheliax's army has split into several large groups, with some of these troops fanning out across the countryside to restore order. A large contingent has withdrawn and moved westward, likely toward Egorian. If at least one PC critically succeeds, they also recover a greater toothy knife talisman (Battlecry! 137) attached to a defeated devil's axe.

As he receives scouting reports, Tolsis asks to meet with the PCs to discuss their next steps.

"A grim outcome," admits Lictor Tolsis Ruvadorn as he reviews several maps lying atop an old tree stump. "We fought bravely—no doubt about that. But sometimes the beast gets the better of you, no matter how many spears you bring." He cracks his neck before concluding, "We have to adapt and seek whatever opportunities this fresh misfortune has delivered to us."
