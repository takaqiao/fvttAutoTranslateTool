> "好吧，**如果**大家能和睦相处，那才算是革命！"琳奈塔大喊一声，随后双臂交叉、就地坐下。

在琳奈塔的发作之后，革命者们羞愧地安静下来。等到吸引了他们的注意，她宣布此次聚会的目标：学习如何合作以解放寇兰廷。她询问他们是否愿意助她一臂之力，并向同意者承诺会告知全部细节。

每一位代表都在思考这一提议，但对他们而言各有更紧迫的任务，使他们不能立刻应允。当借口与请求纷纷涌向琳奈塔时，她转向 PC 们寻求援助，提供以下几项任务。这些任务**可按任意顺序完成**，完成其一便能赢得所属派系的信任。

- **无人无辜（None Are Innocent）（第 22 页）**：四分五裂的锁链骑士团必须重新整合，尽管成员之间充满紧张。卡蒂利娅在多数同意之前不会加入起义。
- **拱与钥之下（Under Arch and Key）（第 26 页）**：细缕者的头领失踪了，目前该派的重心是把他（或他的尸首）从关押他的监狱中救回来。

### 戒严令（Martial Law Decrees）

PC 们已成通缉犯。短短数小时内，街墙上张贴的告示便宣告了新的法律、宵禁与警示，包括 PC 们的画像，旁注"**危险罪犯**（DANGEROUS CRIMINALS）"与"**多塔里杀手**（DOTTARI KILLERS）"等字样。博物馆袭击事件后，以下新法令现已生效：

- **宵禁与出行限制（Curfew and Limitation on Travel）**：本法令生效期间禁止天黑后出行。未持有获批准的通行文件，不得进入贵族区或离开寇兰廷。
- **举报危险罪犯与活动之义务（Obligation to Report Dangerous Criminals and Activities）**：此令威胁称，PC 与反抗派系若在公开场合被发现，将立即被捕。
- **武器与盔甲不得出鞘（Prohibition of Drawn Arms and Armor）**：此令要求武器入鞘、收纳并系紧。非多塔里者在城中通行时不可亮出武器，遭遇战斗时通常需在战斗开始时才能拔武器。
- **未授权魔法之禁（Prohibition of Unauthorized Magic）**：本令生效期间，仅多塔里允许使用魔法，所有其他施法皆遭禁止。
- **物资配给以供战时（Ration of Supplies for the Cause）**：此令禁止市民购买超出严格配给量的物品，购买需出示官方配给卡。PC 可作为闲暇活动（downtime activity）尝试**制作伪造品**（Create a Forgery）（DC 27），需 1 天时间制成一张配给卡，授权购买若干消耗品或一件至 10 级的非消耗品。PC 可将 DC 提高至 32，以制作能购买任意 1 件任意等级常见物品的配给卡。

### 抗拒、闪避、服从（DEFY, EVADE, OBEY）

每名 PC 在穿越城市时必须从以下探索活动中选择一项。

> **抗拒（DEFY）**　**探索**
> 你公然违抗城邦的法令，毫不遵守其规则。在嘲弄多塔里之际，尝试 DC 29 的运动、威吓或社会检定。
> **成功** 你公然违抗多塔里，毫无波折地抵达目的地。
> **失败** 你激起了多塔里的愤怒。在之后的"抗拒"尝试上，你受到 -2 状况减值。
> **大失败** 你被一支多塔里逮捕巡逻队抓住。

> **闪避（EVADE）**　**探索**
> 你避开多塔里巡逻。尝试 DC 27 的体技、欺瞒或匿踪检定，或 DC 29 的察觉检定。
> **成功** 你避开多塔里，毫无波折地抵达目的地。
> **失败** 你险险避过多塔里巡逻，他们对你的行动知晓得更多。在之后的"闪避"尝试上，你受到 -2 状况减值。
> **大失败** 你被一支多塔里逮捕巡逻队抓住。

> **服从（OBEY）**　**探索**
> 你尽职尽责地遵守多塔里法令。当"举报危险罪犯与活动之义务"法令生效时，你**不能**选择"服从"。一旦你停止遵守法令，必须改选"抗拒"或"闪避"作为你的探索活动，并立即尝试其技能检定。

Martial Law Decrees
The PCs have become wanted criminals. Within hours handbills plastered to the walls repeat new laws, curfews, and warnings, including sketches of the PCs accompanied by text declaring them "DANGEROUS CRIMINALS" and "DOTTARI KILLERS." The following new decrees are in place after the raid on the museum.

Curfew and Limitation on Travel: While this decree is in effect, travel is prohibited after dark. Travel into the Noble Quarters and outside of Corentyn is prohibited without approved travel documents.
Obligation to Report Dangerous Criminals and Activities: This decree threatens the PCs and rebel factions with immediate arrest if spotted in public.
Prohibition of Drawn Arms and Armor: This decree demands weapons be sheathed, stowed, and bound. Non-dottari can't have their weapons drawn when traveling through the city, typically requiring weapons to be drawn at the start of combat encounters.
Prohibition of Unauthorized Magic: Only the dottari are permitted to use magic while this decree is in effect, and all other spellcasting is prohibited.
Ration of Supplies for the Cause: This decree prohibits citizens from buying anything beyond a strict ration, which requires an official ration card. A PC can Create a Forgery (DC 27) as a downtime activity, taking 1 day to create a ration card that authorizes the purchase of several consumables or one non-consumable item up to 10th level. The PC can increase the DC to 32 to create a ration card for the purchase of any one common item of any level.

DEFY, EVADE, OBEY
Each PC must choose one the following exploration activities when traveling through the city.

DEFY	EXPLORATION
You openly defy the decrees of the city and don't abide by their rules. Attempt a DC 29 Athletics, Intimidation, or Society check as you taunt the dottari.
Success You openly defy the dottari and arrive at your destination without complication.
Failure You provoke the ire of the dottari. On further attempts to Defy, you take a –2 circumstance penalty.
Critical Failure You're caught by a dottari arrest patrol.

EVADE	EXPLORATION
You avoid dottari patrols. Attempt either a DC 27 Acrobatics, Deception, or Stealth check or a DC 29 Perception check.
Success You evade the dottari and arrive at your destination without complication.
Failure You narrowly avoid the dottari patrols, and they learn more about your movements. On further attempts to Evade, you take a –2 circumstance penalty.
Critical Failure You're caught by a dottari arrest patrol.

OBEY	EXPLORATION
You dutifully follow the dottari decrees. You cannot Obey if the Obligation to Report Dangerous Criminals and Activities decree is in effect. If you ever stop obeying the decrees, you must select Defy or Evade as your exploration activity and immediately attempt its skill check.
