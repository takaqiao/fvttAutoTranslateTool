## Page 99 — Ch5 阿玛丽娅给信 + 晶莹清明（漂白鲸）+ E. 一切闪光的（闪光岩堡 IP 等级）

如果玩家角色们是在见过达让之后再来此处，并提到他的爱意，阿玛丽娅在受宠若惊、为未来剧目记笔记、与戏谑批评达让陷入了"潜在情人因战火而结合"的桥段之间反复摇摆。她回应了他的爱意，并愿意为玩家角色们写一封信交付达让；信中她请他释放玛琳，并请他与她相约会面，共商俩人与佩札克的未来。

她还有一件刺客之一遗下的怪异物品要赠予玩家角色们——这是一柄 **+2 强击长矛**，由**刚玉**所制；若玩家角色们尚未做过鉴定，可在此识别其材质属性（第 92 页）。

### 晶莹清明（SPARKLING CLARITY）

玩家角色们救出玛琳与巴林后，可以回到「**漂白鲸**」——这家客栈整间皆由白色鲸骨建成。双胞胎感谢玩家角色们，并请他们品尝店里的招牌——**香料炽印达**：一种传统枭鬼烈酒，由李子浓缩液所酿。双胞胎打趣说，多数切利亚斯人嫌炽印达太烈而要兑水，但他们会让玩家角色们自行选择"配方毒"——喝原汁或不要也行。

巴林与玛琳简要解释道：他们作为**断牙**（Broken Tusk）兽人部落出身的商人，搬到佩札克之前与**魔鬼栖**许多枭鬼部族都有友好联系。其中包括一群与兽人通商的炽印达酿酒师们。当他们在佩札克开店时，希望借这些人脉缓和紧张关系，开辟绕过切利亚斯封锁的空中贸易航路。

他们记得在和谈遇袭的刺客们持有形貌怪异的武器，但他们当时正在奔逃保命，没能仔细看清。如果玩家角色们出示自己可能已经得到的**刚玉**匕首或长矛（或描述出武器形貌），双胞胎会立即认出它们属于一个名为**闪光岩**（Glittercrag）的偏远枭鬼部族——他们因占据一处古老矮人要塞「**闪光岩堡**」而得名。

巴林与玛琳指出，该部族比大多数枭鬼更隐居避世，不自酿烈酒，但他们既热爱亲手工艺，也喜爱传统枭鬼讲故事。他们憎恨切利亚斯人侵入其领地，并曾参与在 4710 AR 几乎把佩札克烧成废墟的「**第二次余烬**」（Second Ashes）事件。巴林认为他们可能是对抗斯戎家族的有力盟友，玛琳则不那么确信。

如果玩家角色们没提到那些刚玉武器，双胞胎主动表示愿意联系他们的枭鬼酿酒契机以获取更多情报，但提醒可能要花上一天左右。他们让玩家角色们免费在漂白鲸过夜，次日清晨再来找他们，带着相同的揭秘——是**闪光岩枭鬼**协助了在瑞瓦诺与佩札克下手的刺客！

不论如何，双胞胎都会向玩家角色们提供前往堡垒的路线，并送上两瓶各值 1,000 gp 的传统炽印达，作为对救援的酬谢。

玩家角色们离开佩札克、前往闪光岩堡时，每持有一件刚玉武器都获得 1 调查点。

### E. 一切闪光的（All That Glitters）

从佩札克到**闪光岩堡**的路线，是深入**米纳多山脉**「**魔鬼栖**」一带共 20 英里的艰苦跋涉，沿途尽是困难地形。请参阅《玩家核心》第 438 页以决定队伍跨越此距离所需时间。玩家角色们每多花一天到达目的地，便扣 1 调查点。

在金塔戈与佩札克的两次行刺背后，真正的元凶其实是一只名叫**梅兰迪亚**（Melandiar）的**诡谎魔**（belier devil；第 235 页）。她不正式隶属于切利亚斯，正在米纳多山脉与瑞瓦诺试图建立自己的领地，且能避免危及那份**金塔戈契约**（infernal Kintargo Contract）。她目前附身于**玛拉拉**（Marala；凶悍的女性枭鬼酋长 15 级）——通常深居简出的**闪光岩**部族领袖——的身体。由于她最初尚未察觉这群历战的玩家角色已抵达本地区，梅兰迪亚为巢穴遭袭做了多少准备，取决于玩家角色们在佩札克调查时的功力。

- **9–10 调查点**：梅兰迪亚正在沉睡，毫无察觉。入口处的守卫足够放松，愿意与玩家角色们攀谈。堡垒内住客非眠即静观其变，不主动挑事。
- **7–8 调查点**：梅兰迪亚正在沉睡，但已指示守卫保持谨慎，因此他们不会未经一战便接纳外人。堡垒内住客非眠即息，除非察觉到入侵者。
- **4–6 调查点**：梅兰迪亚已醒，担心被人发现。入口守卫不会未经一战便接纳外人。堡垒内住客已醒，处于警觉状态。
- **0–3 调查点**：梅兰迪亚已醒，且对受袭一事心怀偏执。前门守卫已得到加强，不会未经一战便接纳任何人。堡垒内住客高度警戒，定期巡视，并在内部发生冲突时彼此驰援。

### SPARKLING CLARITY

After the PCs rescue Marynn and Barynn, they can return to the Bleached Whale, an inn made entirely from white whale bones. The twins thank the PCs and offer them the house specialty: spiced shyinda, a traditional strix liquor made from plum concentrate. The twins joke that most Chelaxians find shyinda too strong and water it down, but they let the PCs pick their own proverbial poison; they can have it straight or not.

Barynn and Marynn briefly explain that as merchants from the Broken Tusk orc tribe, they had friendly contacts with many of the Devil's Perch strix tribes before they moved to Pezzack. Among them were a collective of shyinda brewers who traded with orcs. When they opened up shop in Pezzack, they hoped to use these connections to ease tensions and open up aerial trade routes to circumvent Cheliax's blockade.

They remember that the assassins at the peace talks were armed with strange-looking weapons, but they didn't get a good look at the arms while running for their lives. If the PCs present the corundum dagger or spear they might have obtained (or describe the weapons), the twins immediately identify them as belonging to the remote Glittercrag strix tribe, named for their occupancy of an old dwarven fortress called Glittercrag Keep.

Barynn and Marynn note that this tribe is more reclusive than most, don't brew alcohol of their own, and enjoy crafting with their hands almost as much as they enjoy traditional strix storytelling. They hate Chelaxian incursions into their lands and also took part in the Second Ashes event that nearly burned Pezzack to the ground in 4710 ar. Barynn thinks they could be valuable allies against House Thrune, but Marynn isn't so sure.

If the PCs don't mention the corundum weapons, the twins offer to reach out to their strix brewing contacts to try and get more information but warn that it might take a day or so. They let the PCs stay at the Bleached Whale free of charge overnight and come to them the next morning with the same revelation—that the Glittercrag strix aided the assassins who attacked Ravounel and Pezzack!

Regardless, the twins provide the PCs with directions to the keep and also gift them two bottles of traditional shyinda, each worth 1,000 gp, as thanks for their rescue.

For each corundum weapon that the PCs still have with them when they leave Pezzack and head for Glittercrag Keep, they gain 1 Investigation Point.

### E. All That Glitters

The way from Pezzack to Glittercrag Keep is a 20-mile trek into the Devil's Perch region of the Menador Mountains. The journey is arduous through difficult terrain. Consult page 438 of Player Core to determine how long it takes the party to traverse this distance. The PCs lose 1 Investigation Point for each total day of travel it takes them to reach their destination.

The true source of the assassination attempts in both Kintargo and Pezzack is a belier devil (page 235) named Melandiar. Not officially part of Cheliax, she's trying to set up her own demesne in the Menador Mountains and Ravounel, and she can do so without jeopardizing the infernal Kintargo Contract. She's currently possessing the body of Marala (fierce female strix chieftain 15), leader of the normally reclusive Glittercrag tribe. As she's unaware of the war-hardened PCs' presence in the region at first, how much Melandiar prepares for an assault on her lair depends on how proficient the PCs were in their investigation at Pezzack.

- **9-10 Investigation Points**: Melandiar is asleep and unaware of any scrutiny. The guards at the entrance are relaxed enough to entertain conversation. The fortress's inhabitants are either asleep or passive unless provoked.
- **7-8 Investigation Points**: Melandiar is asleep but has instructed the guards to be cautious, so they won't admit outsiders without a fight. The fortress's inhabitants are either asleep or resting unless they notice intruders.
- **4-6 Investigation Points**: Melandiar is awake and worried about being caught. The guards at the entrance won't admit outsiders without a fight. The fortress's inhabitants are awake and alert.
- **0-3 Investigation Points**: Melandiar is awake and paranoid about being attacked. The guards at the front entrance have been reinforced and won't admit anyone without a fight. The fortress's inhabitants are on high alert, patrol regularly, and reinforce each other when fights break out internally.
