#### B. 焚林空地（BURNING GLADE）　**中等 15**

玩家角色们必须勘查的另一处地点是一片林间空地——它曾被**沉言林**的德鲁伊与精类奉为圣地，称作「**圣林空场**」（nemeton）。这处圣地被一群**燔林精**（marrmoras）所亵渎——它们在**斯戎家族**信使的怂恿下渗入此地，将原本的居民活活烧成焦炭。

**生物**：犯下这些暴行的两只**燔林精**如今正在烧成焦土的空地上嬉戏，围绕着曾是圣林中心的那株巨橡兜圈子。这株橡树以惊人的顽强抵御了它们炽热的利爪与烈焰喷射。由于它们对这棵树的痴迷与分心，玩家角色们（与若同行的部队）可在这些火系精类察觉之前抵达空地边缘。**燔林精**性情反复又残忍，会战至阵亡，只为以烈焰吞噬活体生灵——尤其是任何木质或植物类生物。战斗中它们彼此完全不协调。

**燔林精（2）　生物 15**
《Pathfinder 怪物核心 2》220 页
先攻 察觉 +27

**剧情发展**：这两只**燔林精**被击败后，空地正中的那株神圣橡树会张开自己的枝叶，露出**燔林精**所害的几具尸体——一位年迈的德鲁伊与数名年幼的精类。橡树本想将他们的遗体藏起，免遭进一步亵渎。如果玩家角色们将这些遗体在空地上妥善安葬，橡树会再张开一处隐蔽的枝叶，将德鲁伊的装备交予他们。

如果玩家角色们没自行想到这一点，一名玩家角色通过 DC 34 自然或求生检定可领悟到：这株神圣橡树正握有对他们而言贵重的物品，只要他们以应有的敬意对待它的故友。

此外，**燔林精**伏诛之后，原本损害士气的烈焰与浓烟也已消散。

**战利品**：若玩家角色们妥善安葬了遗体，橡树将赠予玩家角色们一柄**精良暴雨杖**（major staff of the tempest；《玩家核心 2》308 页）、一具**精良太初槲寄生**（greater primeval mistletoe），以及一颗**萌芽宙之石**（sprouting aeon stone）。

#### B. BURNING GLADE　MODERATE 15

Another site the PCs must investigate is a glade that was once considered sacred to druids and fey of the Whisperwood, known as a nemeton. This place was desecrated when a group of marrmoras, encouraged by House Thrune's messengers, infiltrated the area and killed its original inhabitants, burning them to a crisp.

Creatures: The two marrmoras responsible for the atrocities now frolic in the burnt-out glade, circling the gigantic oak tree that was the focus of the sacred nemeton. The tree has proven stubbornly resistant to their fiery claws and flame jets. Because of their fascination and distraction with this tree, the PCs (and their troop, if it accompanies them) can reach the edge of the glade before the fiery fey notice them. Capricious and cruel, the marrmoras fight to the death in an attempt to consume living creatures—especially any wood- or plant-based creatures, in fire. They don't even remotely coordinate with one another during combat.

MARRMORAS (2)　CREATURE 15
Pathfinder Monster Core 2 220
Initiative Perception +27

Development: When the fiery fey have been defeated, the sacred oak tree in the center of the glade opens its boughs and reveals the corpses of an elderly druid and several young fey whom the marrmoras murdered, their bodies hidden by the tree so they couldn't be desecrated further. If the PCs give these bodies a proper burial in the glade, the tree opens another hidden bough and provides them with the druid's gear.

If the PCs don't think to do this on their own, a PC who succeeds at a DC 34 Nature or Survival check will realize that this sacred oak tree holds something valuable for them, if they show its former loved ones respect in turn.

Further, with the marrmoras dead, the flames and smoke that had been hurting morale are now gone.

Treasure: If the PCs properly buried the bodies, the oak tree gives the PCs a major staff of the tempest (Player Core 2 308), greater primeval mistletoe, and one sprouting aeon stone.
