## Page 19 — Ch1 文物回收（续）/ 地下室 + 撤离 + 复杂情况

### 博物馆地下室（The Museum Basement）

PC 们进入地下室后，按以下顺序面对这些障碍。若仍有时间，PC 们也可顺手取回无声剪影想要的被毯。

> **寻找文献（FINDING THE DOCUMENTS）**　**障碍**
> 渗透点 4（团队）；克服：DC 25 生存或 DC 29 察觉。
> 泽菲罗在突袭前藏起了最珍贵的文物。话虽如此，他忘了调查员可能注意到那些缺货留下的无尘空格。

> **保养处理（PRESERVATION TREATMENTS）**　**障碍**
> 渗透点 4（团队）；克服：DC 27 制作或巧手，或 DC 29 奥秘。
> 泽菲罗藏起这些典籍时，它们正在接受保养处理。在移动它们时不致损伤，需要灵巧的双手，或者熟悉所涉及的炼金与奥术材料。

> **铃花会被毯（BELLFLOWER QUILTS）**　**机遇**
> 要求：PC 们在渗透中还剩超过 10 分钟。
> 你在地下室搜寻铃花会的被毯。每名 PC 可尝试 DC 29 制作、自然或社会检定来鉴别被毯。成功时，他们识别出年代、密语图样或花卉设计正确的、属于铃花会的那几张。

### 离开博物馆（Leaving the Museum）

当 PC 们在博物馆中已经待了 50 分钟（5 个渗透轮），他们听到外面的口号声变了——这是撤离的信号。每名 PC 必须通过完成下列障碍中至少一项来离开博物馆。

> **借暗夜潜行而出（SNEAK OUT UNDER COVER OF DARKNESS）**　**障碍**
> 渗透点 1（个人）；克服：DC 27 匿踪。
> 博物馆花园提供充足掩护，剩下只需善加利用。

> **再次翻墙（BACK OVER THE WALL）**　**障碍**
> 渗透点 1（个人）；克服：DC 27 体技或运动。
> 多塔里已清除墙上可攀爬的杂物，但总还有别的着手点可用。

> **混入抗议人群（SLIP INTO THE PROTESTS）**　**障碍**
> 渗透点 2（个人）；克服：DC 25 欺瞒、DC 27 社会，或 DC 29 交涉或表演。
> 博物馆入口处的抗议正被多塔里驱散。然而，借人群悄然离去仍是一个选项。

### 复杂情况（Complications）

> **流离的多塔里（STRAY DOTTARI）**　**复杂情况**
> 触发：PC 们首次累计 5 警觉点。
> 克服：DC 27 威吓、DC 29 匿踪，或 DC 31 欺瞒或交涉。
>
> 一名多塔里特工从抗议现场误入博物馆。在他与某位入侵的 PC 面面相对之前数瞬，他的脚步声已先一步响起！
> **成功** 你闪躲或巧言脱身。
> **失败** 多塔里意识到你不该在这里。队伍累计 1 AP。
> **大失败** 同失败，但队伍累计 2 AP。

### 警觉（Awareness）

随着 PC 们累计警觉点（AP），他们会引来执行突袭的多塔里的注意。以下情况会在这些 AP 阈值发生：

- **5 AP**：触发"流离的多塔里"复杂情况。
- **10 AP**：所有障碍 DC 提高 1。
- **15 AP**：多塔里发觉博物馆窃取行动正在进行。渗透失败。

### 在邪叉酒馆复盘（Debrief at the Wicked Fork）

回到邪叉酒馆，无声剪影接管所有被回收的文物。若 PC 们成功取回文献（同时完成"寻找文献"与"保养处理"两项障碍），她同意让铃花会加入起义。若 PC 们完成了"铃花会被毯"机遇，她会向他们致谢，并恭敬地端详取回的被毯。

若铃花会加入起义，**"武器与盔甲不得出鞘"** 法令将在一位高级多塔里队长被"动之以理"之后被解除。

**宝藏**：若 PC 们取回了文献，泽菲罗将把他的**高级工匠目镜**（greater crafter's eyepiece）赠予他们；他认为自己即将被偷渡出城，已无需此物。若他们取回了铃花会被毯，无声剪影会赠予他们一枚 **+2 武器潜能符文**。

**奖励**：若铃花会加入起义，授予 PC 们 60 XP。

THE MUSEUM BASEMENT
Once the PCs enter the basement, they face these obstacles in order. If there's still time, the PCs might also collect the quilts Silent Silhouette seeks.

FINDING THE DOCUMENTS	OBSTACLE
Infiltration Points 4 (group); Overcome DC 25 Survival or DC 29 Perception
Zefiro hid the most precious artifacts before the raid. That said, he forgot that investigators might notice the dustless spaces left behind by the missing goods.

PRESERVATION TREATMENTS	OBSTACLE
Infiltration Points 4 (group); Overcome DC 27 Crafting or Thievery, or DC 29 Arcana
These texts were receiving preservation treatments when Zefiro hid them. Moving them without causing damage requires careful fingers or knowing the alchemical and arcane materials involved.

BELLFLOWER QUILTS	OPPORTUNITY
Requirements The PCs have more than 10 minutes remaining in the infiltration.
You search the basement for the Bellflower Network's quilts. Each PC can attempt a DC 29 Crafting, Nature, or Society check to examine the quilts. On a success, they identify those with the correct ages, quilt codes, or flower designs to be from the Bellflower Network.

LEAVING THE MUSEUM
When the PCs have spent 50 minutes in the museum (5 infiltration rounds), they hear the chanting outside change—their sign to leave. Each PC must escape the museum by completing at least one of these obstacles.

SNEAK OUT UNDER COVER OF DARKNESS	OBSTACLE
Infiltration Points 1 (individual); Overcome DC 27 Stealth
The museum's garden offers plenty of cover to hide in, and it's only a matter of taking advantage of it.

BACK OVER THE WALL	OBSTACLE
Infiltration Points 1 (individual); Overcome DC 27 Acrobatics or Athletics
The dottari cleared any climbable debris from the walls, but there are always other handholds available.

SLIP INTO THE PROTESTS	OBSTACLE
Infiltration Points 2 (individual); Overcome DC 25 Deception, DC 27 Society, or DC 29 Diplomacy or Performance
The protests at the museum's entrance dissolve as the dottari break them up. However, slipping away through the crowd is still an option.

COMPLICATIONS
STRAY DOTTARI	COMPLICATION
Trigger The PCs earn 5 Awareness Points for the first time.
Overcome DC 27 Intimidation, DC 29 Stealth, or DC 31 Deception or Diplomacy
A dottari agent wanders into the museum from the protest. Their footsteps sound moments before they come face-to-face with one of the intruding PCs!
Success You dodge or sweet-talk your way out of the encounter.
Failure The dottari realizes you aren't supposed to be here. The party accrues 1 AP.
Critical Failure As failure, but the party accrues 2 AP.

AWARENESS
As the PCs accumulate Awareness Points (AP), they attract the attention of the dottari conducting the raid. The following happens at these AP thresholds.
5 AP: The stray dottari complication occurs.
10 AP: Increase the DCs for all obstacles by 1.
15 AP: The dottari realize that the museum heist is occurring. The infiltration fails.

DEBRIEF AT THE WICKED FORK
At the Wicked Fork, Silent Silhouette takes custody of any retrieved artifacts. If the PCs retrieved the documents (by completing both the finding the documents and preservation treatments obstacles), she agrees for the Bellflower Network to join the rebellion. If the PCs completed the Bellflower quilts opportunity, she thanks them while reverently studying the recovered quilts.

If the Bellflower Network joins the rebellion, the decree Prohibition of Drawn Arms and Armor is lifted after a "persuasive argument" to a high-ranking dottari captain.

Treasure: If the PCs recover the documents, Zefiro awards them his greater crafter's eyepiece; he figures he won't need it now that he's preparing to be smuggled out of the city. If they recovered the Bellflower quilts, Silent Silhouette gifts them a +2 weapon potency rune.

Reward: If the Bellflower Network joins the rebellion, award the PCs 60 XP.
