### 塔贡堡之围（THE SIEGE OF TAGGUN HOLD）

矮人采矿城镇——已设防的「**塔贡堡**」（Taggun Hold）——选择了一条不与切利亚斯战争努力合作的道路——并对城堡中的少数切利亚斯效忠者——发动了一场小规模反叛。这座孤立的要塞——靠它多年以来——用于横越**奈多**边境运送货物的走私隧道——而得到加固。斯戎家族的军队——若不试图饿垮这座坚韧的堡垒——便已是举步维艰——而地狱骑士团内部的政治——则为他们送上了一个不寻常的机会。在「**笞鞭骑士团**」（Order of the Scourge）——拒绝向王座——屈膝低头——并撤入他们位于**巴罗林**（Barrowood）——几近完工的城堡之后——埃戈里安——便派出特使——去与笞鞭骑士团与皇室——的宿敌——「**骨轴骑士团**」（Order of the Crux，曾被驱逐的伪地狱骑士团）——这些不死异端——及其周身环绕毒雾的死墓骑士——「**执棒官肖克尼尔**」（Lictor Shokneir；残忍的男性死墓骑士指挥官 15 级）——进行谈判。他们结盟的条款——很简单：骨轴骑士团——在击破塔贡堡的反叛者之后——将得到赦免——并被切利亚斯——承认为一支正式的地狱骑士团。

肖克尼尔——是一位敏锐的战术家——拥有不死之物——所特有的耐心。在数月的时间里——他的不死矿工——一寸一寸地——向山脉深处——挖凿——直至他们达成了执棒官的第一个目标：与「**幽暗地域**」（Darklands）——建立了接触。通过——与一个早已被开采作业的声响——吸引而来的**地底矮人**（hryngar）王国——结盟——肖克尼尔——得以——将塔贡堡从——四面八方——切断。一面——地底矮人的部队——使用半训练好的**洞穴蠕虫**（cave worms；《探路者怪物核心》54 页）——封锁隧道——并自下方——突击该城市——另一面——执棒官肖克尼尔与他的另外两名同伴死墓骑士（《探路者怪物核心》179 页）——则以一支庞大的军队——围困这座城市——而他们强大的**附鬼**（**dybbuk**；《探路者怪物核心》136 页）盟友——则附身于守军、并恐吓他们。地底矮人——是机会主义的懦夫——一旦他们的洞穴蠕虫被击败——他们便会逃回他们位于深处的堡垒——但那些不死生物——会战斗——直至附鬼——与至少一名死墓骑士——皆被击败。倘若围攻显然——即将瓦解——肖克尼尔——也完全乐意——做出战术性的撤退——以保全他的部队——但玩家角色——可能会忍不住——尾随他——回到他位于不死要塞「**基斯迪诺堡**」（Citadel Gheisteno）的本据——在他的力量之座——与他决一死战。

无论塔贡堡之围——以何种方式收场——通往幽暗地域的新入口——都将令该地区——暴露于一种由**食尸鬼**与他们麾下的**地蜥人**（xulgath）打手——所组成的邪魔邪教——的劫掠之下——这些家伙——开始侵扰先前的走私隧道——并在夜间——出没于山间——寻找猎物。更糟的是——一旦阴影笼罩的「**黑三角**」（Black Triune）——意识到——一条通往深邃地域的通道——几乎就坐落在**奈多**的门阶之上——他们——便会派遣一支代表团——进入幽暗地域——以将**宗-库山**（Zon-Kuthon）的教诲——带给——任何愿意接受其手段的居民——并获得地下领域——额外的力量。这种邪恶的外交——会如何发展——尚有待观察——但它——绝不会——是地表诸国的——好消息——尤其是附近的塔贡堡——它正在那场灾难性的围城之后——艰难地——重建。

THE SIEGE OF TAGGUN HOLD
The fortified dwarven mining town of Taggun Hold
chose the path of noncooperation with the Chelaxian
war effort, staging a minor rebellion against the
few Chelaxian loyalists in the keep. This solitary
stronghold is bolstered by the smuggling tunnels
they've used to pass goods across the Nidalese border
for years. Thrune's military is hard-pressed enough
without trying to starve out the resilient redoubt,
but Hellknight politics presented them with a unique
opportunity. After the Order of the Scourge
refused to bend the knee to the throne and
retreated to their nearly finished keep in the
Barrowood, envoys from Egorian were sent
to negotiate with old enemies of the Scourge
and the crown: the undead heretical Order
of the Crux and their poison-shrouded
graveknight Lictor Shokneir (merciless
male graveknight commander 15). The
terms of their alliance are simple; the
Order of the Crux would be pardoned
and recognized as a Hellknight order of
Cheliax after breaking the rebels of Taggun Hold.

Shokneir is a keen tactician with the patience of
the unliving. Over the course of months, his undead
miners tunneled deeper and deeper into the mountain
until they achieved the Lictor's first goal: contact with
the Darklands. By allying with a kingdom of hryngar
who'd been attracted by noise from the mining
operation, Shokneir is able to cut Taggun Hold off
from all directions. While hryngar troops employ
half-trained cave worms (Monster Core 54) to
seal off tunnels and assault the city from below,
Lictor Shokneir and his two fellow graveknights
(Monster Core 179) surround the city with a
massive army as their powerful dybbuk
ally (Monster Core 136) possesses and
terrorizes the defenders. The hryngar are
opportunistic cowards who flee to their
deep keeps once their cave worms are
defeated, but the undead fight until the
dybbuk and at least one graveknight have
been defeated. If the siege is clearly about to break,
Shokneir is perfectly willing to stage a tactical retreat
in order to protect his forces, but the PCs might be
tempted to follow him back to the undead fortress of
Citadel Gheisteno and fight him in his seat of power.

No matter how the Siege of Taggun Hold goes,
the new entrance into the Darklands opens the area
to the predations of a demonic cult of ghouls and
their beholden xulgath brutes who begin to infest the
former smuggling tunnels and prowl the mountains at
night seeking victims. Worse, once the shadowy Black
Triune realizes that a passage to the deep regions is
sitting practically on Nidal's doorstep, they send a
delegation into the Darklands to bring Zon-Kuthon's
teachings to any denizens receptive to his methods and
to obtain the additional powers of the subterranean
realms. How this nefarious diplomacy develops
remains to be seen, but it can't mean good news for the
surface lands—particularly the nearby Taggun Hold as
it struggles to rebuild after the catastrophic siege.
