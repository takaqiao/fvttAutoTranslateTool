### 地狱猎犬丘（HELLHOUND HILL）

**地狱猎犬丘是埃戈里安一些最富有的市民之家**——**位于该城东南方**。**哥特式宅邸俯瞰街道**——**彼此之间几乎没有什么空地**。**玩家角色预定与他们的线人盖里乌斯·亨德塞恩在一间名为「邪桶」**（**Wicked Keg**）**的高档酒馆会面**——**这间店几乎就在「斯戎宅邸」**（**Thrune Manor**）**的阴影下**。

走进昏暗的酒馆内，**英雄们在建筑后端一处角落桌旁找到等候着的盖里乌斯**。**他是个相貌堂堂、看似人类的青年男子**，**有着红棕色的头发**，**戴着一副线框眼镜**，**穿着一双柔软的皮手套**。**他在玩家角色靠近时鬼祟地张望四周**，**挥手示意他们入座**。**盖里乌斯从一只大酒瓶中为英雄们斟出几杯酒**。

**盖里乌斯压低声音说话**——**始终留意着前门**。**他说他的家族曾是切利亚斯最为强盛的家族之一**——**直到不到 20 年前**——**时任族长艾因马奇·亨德塞恩**（**Einmarch Henderthane**）**因「阴谋之罪」被处死**。**作为家族支系的旁系堂兄弟**，**盖里乌斯一脉的亲族得以在随后的「清洗」中幸存**。**他在对斯戎家族的深深怨恨中长大**——**不愿意有任何份儿参与到阿波罗盖如今所策划的勾当之中**。**他在某一刻摘下一只手套——露出一片红鳞般的肌肤与尖锐的黑指甲**。**「我是奈非利姆**，」**他解释道**，**「切利亚斯人惯于役使地狱**——**而非与其混血**——**所以许多人对我避而远之**。**我相信若在斯戎统治下的地狱里**，**我的下场不会太妙**。**」**

随后**他从内襟里抽出一小叠羊皮纸**——**上面胡乱画着一张地图**。**地图上并无文字**——**所以盖里乌斯不得不口头描述那几处地标**。**「别让人在你们身上发现这个，」**他警告道，**「但若真发现了**，**立刻销毁**。**」**

经盖里乌斯的解说，**玩家角色可借此地图找到一处秘密入口**——**通向一条被遗忘的地下通道**——**那条通道直通皇宫的后厅**。**借助某条仆从通道楼梯**，**玩家角色可抵达阿波罗盖的私人寝宫与书房**——**他们希望能在那里查得更多关于她所主持仪式的情报**。

盖里乌斯对皇宫布局知之甚少——**他解释他不久前才查到这条秘密通道的迹象，故从未亲身使用过**。**「依你们的样貌看来**，**你们已准备好应付那里可能遇到的一切**，**」**他向英雄们如此保证**。**对话结束之后**，**盖里乌斯建议他们不要同行离开**——**以免引人怀疑**。**说罢**，**他起身走出酒馆**。

**HELLHOUND HILL**

Home to some of the wealthiest of Egorian's citizens, Hellhound Hill is in the southeast section of the city. Gothic manors loom over the streets with not a lot of land in between them. The PCs are scheduled to meet their contact, Garius Henderthane, in an upscale tavern called the Wicked Keg that sits almost in the shadow of Thrune Manor.

Inside the dimly lit tavern, the heroes find Garius waiting for them at a corner table near the rear of the building. A handsome, seemingly human young man, he has reddish-brown hair and wears a pair of wire-rimmed spectacles and supple leather gloves. He looks around furtively when the PCs approach and waves for them to join him. Garius pours glasses of wine for the heroes from a large bottle.

Garius speaks in hushed tones, keeping constant watch on the front door. He says that his family was once among the most powerful in all of Cheliax until then-patriarch Einmarch Henderthane was executed on charges of conspiracy less than 20 years ago. Being a distant cousin of that branch of the family tree, Garius's relatives were spared a subsequent culling of the house. He grew up around deep resentment for the Thrunes and doesn't want any part of what Abrogail is planning. At one point, he removes one of his gloves to reveal a patch of red-scaled skin and sharp, black nails. "I am nephilim," he explains. "Chelaxians are comfortable controlling Hell, not hybridizing with it, so many shun me. I don't believe I would fare well in a Hell ruled by a Thrune."

He then pulls a small sheaf of parchment from his tunic, on which is scrawled a crude map. It has no writing on it, so Garius has to describe the map's few landmarks. "Don't get caught with this," he warns. "But if you do, destroy it right away."

With Garius's explanations, the PCs can use the map to find a secret entrance to a forgotten underground tunnel that leads into the back rooms of the Imperial Palace. By using one of the servants' staircases, the PCs can reach Abrogail's personal chambers and libraries, where they can hopefully find more information about the ritual she's conducting.

Garius doesn't know much about the layout of the palace and explains that he only recently found evidence of this secret tunnel so has never used it. "By the looks of you, you're prepared to deal with whatever might be in there," he assures the heroes. Once the conversation ends, Garius suggests that they not leave together, so as not to arouse suspicions. With that, he stands and exits the tavern.

**A. Imperial Palace**

Using the map given to them by Garius, the PCs can locate a disused tailor's shop a few blocks south of the Imperial Palace off of Thrune Square in central Egorian. A dusty trap door in a back room opens onto a set of stone stairs that lead down to a cramped basement storage area. A trace of a breeze coming from behind a collection of ancient dressing dummies indicates the presence of a secret door that's been wedged shut by age. A PC who succeeds at a DC 36 Athletics check can Force Open the door, revealing a dark tunnel.

**SECRET TUNNEL	LOW 18**

> A decrepit tunnel stretches into the darkness in both directions. The style of the brickwork dates back hundreds of years, and though some of the mortar has turned to dust, the tunnel seems structurally sound. It was constructed at the same time as the palace, but its existence was forgotten several decades ago, and it remained undiscovered until now. The tunnel is devoid of light sources.
