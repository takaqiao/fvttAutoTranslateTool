**所有由斯戎、斯戎亲族、或斯戎党羽签下的地狱契约的副本——皆保存于此典藏之中**。**每份契约残留的神圣邪秽魔法与其守护者的长期存在——已赋予这些契约一种动物般的智识**。**它们行为如同被驯化的鸽子——栖息在各自的小格之中**。**与真正的鸟类不同——它们不需要进食——但有时也会装作要被喂食的样子——自栖处扑翅而下——察看、或骚扰房间内的任何人**。

**生物**：**一只名为玛蕾希娅**（**Mareshiah**）**的「契约魔」**（**contract devil**）**守护此典藏**——**确保未经授权之人无法接触这些契约**——**并在执职最初几年之后——开始像驯鸽一般训练它们**。**毁坏这些副本并不会使契约失效**——**因为原件被安然保存于地狱**——**但玛蕾希娅知道这些契约的条款若落入斯戎家族敌人之手——将是足以撼动世界的资讯**。**故而**，**她极其认真地对待此职**。**任何想要冒充斯戎家族成员的魔法都不太可能蒙骗她**——**她对该家族的每位在世成员皆能一眼认出**。

玛蕾希娅以高傲的语气要求玩家角色「**即刻**」离开她的视线——**威胁若他们不走，便将他们的骨骸化为灰烬**。**她不接受任何借口、谎言或贿赂**。**若一名玩家角色提出与她签下契约——以自己的灵魂为代价**——**她拒绝该请求**——**理由是「这位玩家角色的灵魂不过是琐屑之物——不值得她以任何东西交换」**。**若玩家角色拒不离开**——**她便发动攻击**——**死战不退**。

**玛蕾希娅通常单独行事**——**但目前典藏内还有一对「缚体魔鬼」**（**belier devils**）。**它们是戈索克莱克派来的——在阿波罗盖身处地狱时**——**为典藏提供额外的安保**。**它们对被分派如此「体力活」感到不快**——**也不会干涉玩家角色尝试与玛蕾希娅进行的任何（无论多么短暂的）对话**。**它们仅在房间的一端伫立逼视——直至显然要爆发暴力为止**。**其中一只会尝试附身一位玩家角色**——**另一只则用其自体法术发动攻势**。**它们亦死战不退**。

Copies of each infernal contract signed by a Thrune, Thrune relative, or Thrune lackey is kept in this archive. The residual divine unholy magic of each contract and the constant presence of their caretaker has granted the contracts a kind of animallike intelligence. They act like domesticated pigeons, resting in their individual cubbyholes. Unlike real birds, they don't need to be fed, but they sometimes act like they do, fluttering down from their perches to inspect or annoy anyone in the room.

**Creatures**: A contract devil named Mareshiah maintains this archive, ensuring no unauthorized people access these contracts and, after the first few years of her assignment, training them like pigeons. There's no danger of nullifying these contracts by destroying these copies, as the originals are kept safely in Hell, but Mareshiah knows that the terms of these contracts would be world-shaking information to enemies of House Thrune. As such, she takes her job very seriously. It's unlikely she would be fooled by magic used by someone trying to impersonate a Thrune, and she knows every living member of the family by sight.

Mareshiah imperiously demands that the PCs leave her sight "this instant," threatening to reduce their bones to ash if they don't. She accepts no excuses, lies, or bribes. If a PC offers to sign a contract with her for their soul, she denies the request, citing that the PC's soul is "naught but a trifle" and unworthy of anything she would exchange for it. She attacks the heroes if they refuse to leave and fights until destroyed.

While Mareshiah generally works alone, a pair of belier devils is currently in the archive. They've been sent by Gorthoklek to provide additional security for the archive while Abrogail is in Hell. They're unhappy to be assigned such a physical duty and don't interfere in any conversations, however brief, the PCs try to have with Mareshiah. They simply loom at one end of the room until it becomes clear that violence is imminent. One tries to possess one of the PCs while the other uses their innate spells offensively. They also fight until destroyed.
