**本遭遇使用第 72 页的地图**。

**生物**：**两支卡利守卫分队当前守卫着这些关键门户**——**但若它们倒下**——**便没有几名战士能守卫卡利**。**保留其"硫磺军团"**（Brimstone Corps，**精英切利亚斯战士**）**作为预备**——**罗特罗维奥派出两支骷髅部队强攻门户**。**当 PC 们抵达时**——**让他们指挥两支卡利步兵分队**——**这两支分队各自驻于两道门户中**。**让 PC 们将自己剩余的部队**（**船员、弓箭兵团、纯净军团小队**）**按其意愿沿地图东缘任意位置部署**。

**除直接击败敌人外**——**若两名 PC 与其指挥的分队占据两道门户至少 2 个完整回合**——**PC 们可获得一个优势**。**感到败局已定**——**阿斯摩蒂斯的神卫们便会逃跑**——**导致他们的骷髅分队崩溃**。**硫磺军团立即尝试一次溃散检定**。**罗特罗维奥会一直作战**——**直到他绝无获胜可能**——**届时他便尝试逃跑或投降**。

**为阻止 PC 们控制门户**——**罗特罗维奥将兵力一分为二**——**派一位神卫带硫磺军团至一道门户**——**另一位神卫带两支骷髅分队至另一道门户**。**他骑着他的梦魇坐骑飞往任何需要他的地方**——**以其强大法术在敌军中炸开缺口**。

C. BATTLE AT THE WALL	SEVERE 13
Having recruited allies in eastern Khari, the PCs should now have enough forces to directly attack the main Chelaxian force, which is attempting to breach two key gateways leading into western Khari.

The clash and flash of steel fill the plaza before a large sandstone wall, its two arched gateways separating Khari's eastern and western halves. Skeletal hordes to the east surge forward relentlessly, while two squads of Rahadoumi soldiers hold the gates with grim resolve.

Use the map on page 72 for this encounter.

Creatures: Two troops of Khari defenders currently hold these key gates, but if they fall, there are few warriors remaining to defend Khari. Keeping his Brimstone Corps (elite Chelaxian warriors) in reserve, Rotrovio has sent two units of skeletons to assault the gates. When the PCs arrive, give them command of the two units of Khari infantry, which begin in each of the two gates. Allow the PCs to position their remaining troops (ship crew, archer regiment, and Pure Legion squad) anywhere they like along the map's eastern edge.

In addition to defeating the enemy outright, the PCs can gain an advantage if two PCs and their commanded troops occupy both gates for at least 2 full rounds. Sensing defeat, the Asmodean champions flee, causing their skeleton troops to crumble. The Brimstone Corps immediately attempts a rout check. Rotrovio keeps fighting until he has absolutely no chance of victory, at which point he tries to escape or surrender.

To prevent the PCs from controlling the gates, Rotrovio splits his forces in two, sending one champion with the Brimstone Corps to one gate and the other champion with two skeleton troops to the other. He rides atop his nightmare mount and flies wherever he's needed, using his powerful spells to blast holes in the enemy forces.
