> **B5. 宗教审判监室（INQUISITION HOLDING CHAMBER）　　中等 19**
>
> 「微弱的锁链碰击声——回响在这处阴森的房室中——自天花板悬下的绞刑笼中传出。数个其他覆有狞恶尖刺的大型笼子——靠墙堆放。所幸——这些刑具皆为空——尽管其中大部分——已被陈年血迹微微锈蚀。此地散发着金属与绝望的气味。」

这里是「**地狱审判庭**」（Infernal Inquisition）的一处羁押室——专为等候审讯与刑罚的罪犯所设。当阿波罗盖前往地狱举行其仪式之际——阿斯佩西娅清空了这些笼子与绞刑笼（藉由低调而无据的处决）——为这位**大高阶女祭司**所预期的、在切利亚斯地狱变易之后将会发声反对的众多埃戈里安市民——预留位置。玩家角色赶在任何不幸之人被带至此处之前——便将阿斯佩西娅制止——大多数市民在过去数日中都瑟缩在自家之中——故而这些笼子如今为空。然而——这并不意味着这些笼子无害。

**生物**：一只**叛教魔**——近期被召至此地以监管即将涌入的新囚犯——正在此守候。它已因无所事事而略感无聊——但它的契约不允许它擅自走动。它并不会立即攻击玩家角色——起初它以为他们是来送几个囚犯。它很快意识到自己的错误——但仍佯装友好——试图引诱其中一位玩家角色靠近那座**活体笼陷阱**。该叛教魔战至殒命——因这会让它的契约失效——但它无法追击进入其他房间的玩家角色。

**危害**：房内东墙上那只大铁笼——已被部分赋予一种残忍的恶意。尽管不具神智——它几乎显出迫不及待——要以它的锁链擒住受害者——将其拖入其中——并榨干其生命。

B5. INQUISITION HOLDING CHAMBER	MODERATE 19

The faint clinking of chains echoes through this bleak
chamber, emanating from the gibbets hanging from the
ceiling. Several other large cages covered in vicious
spikes are pushed up against the walls. Thankfully,
these restraints are empty, though most of them have
rusted slightly from old bloodstains. The area reeks of
metal and despair.

This is one of the Infernal Inquisition's holding cells
for offenders awaiting questioning and punishment.
Aspexia cleared out the cages and gibbets (via quiet
and unwarranted executions) when Abrogail left for
Hell to perform her ritual, in preparation for the many
Egorian citizens the grand high priestess anticipated
would voice dissent following the country's infernal
transformation. The PCs stopped Aspexia before any
unfortunate people were brought here—most citizens
have cowered in their homes over the past few days—
so the cages are currently empty. However, that doesn't
mean that the cages are harmless.

Creatures: A deimavigga, recently summoned to
oversee the influx of new prisoners, waits here. They've
grown a little bored from the inaction, but their
contract doesn't allow them to wander. They don't
immediately attack the PCs, at first assuming they're
here to deliver some captives. They quickly realize
their mistake but remain seemingly friendly in an
attempt to lure one of the PCs closer to the animated
cage trap. The deimavigga fights until destroyed, as
that voids their contract, but they can't pursue PCs to
other rooms.

Hazard: The large iron cage on the eastern wall
of the room has been partially animated with a cruel
malevolence. Though not sentient, it almost seems
eager to ensnare victims with its chains, pull them into
itself, and crush the life from them.
