#### 围攻庄园（Sieging the Manor）　**严峻 12**

**强攻夺取庄园**对 PC 们而言是个**令人却步的前景**。**将其视为一场遭遇战**，**目标是夺取庄园本宅的中央广场**。**若 PC 们能让一支或多支分队占据该区域**，**亵典与她剩余的部队便会放弃战斗、撤退**。**PC 们可从囚犯中招募四支佣兵团**用于此次强攻。

**生物**：**亵典、她的四只科阿提魔、以及外侧两支愤懑教团**（详见 A3 区描述）**守卫庄园**。**第二回合开始时**，**剩余的门徒在 A4 区集结成第三支愤懑教团**加入战斗。

**PC 分队**：**PC 们护送的囚犯与其他反抗者**可**集结成乌合分队**（ragtag troops）。**PC 们可看出**：**这些分队是可战之兵**，**但相对亵典的部队仍处于轻微劣势**——**故应慎防鲁莽部署**。

#### 会见亵典（Meeting Apocriphany）

若 PC 们愿意谈判，**亵典会愉快地飘出**迎接他们：

> 一只穿着橙黑长袍的**雌性魔鬼**坐在一座**漂浮的王座**上飘近。**典籍与卷轴慵懒地绕着她转**，**半开或被书签夹着**。她带着**戏谑的笑容**喊道："**呵呵，看看这儿来的是谁？**我可没想到自己的讲座之夜会有访客来访——**不过开明之士永远受欢迎**。"

虽然她**是一个可怕的敌人**，但**亵典觉得战斗很乏味**——**更喜欢腐化凡人**。她可以**回答 PC 们以下问题**：

**你是谁？** "我是**亵典**——一位**勉强算是学者**之人。我**通常住在更温暖的地方**，但**时不时**也喜欢**潜入凡人之界**，**散布几粒甜美的知识小食**。"

**你为何在此？** "我目前**正进行一项私人任务**，**而这座庄园正好是个避开窥探之眼的绝佳之地**。我**怀疑你们来这里也是出于同样的原因**。**你知道**，**多年前**——**在他失去理智之前**——**我曾认识这座庄园的主人**。**真是憾事一桩**。"

**你为谁效力？** "**真是个味道甜美的问题**。不过**这个秘密我恐怕不能透露**。但是我**可以告诉你们**：**我可不是斯戎家族的朋友**。**事实上**——**如果你们有兴趣**——**我今晚正要进行几场讨论**，**正是为了把斯戎家族打下来一两层**。**欢迎诸位加入**。"

**我们可以共用庄园吗？** "**恐怕不行**。**我的追随者是热切的小学习者**，但**他们和外人合不来**。**既然我们是先来的**，**讲点礼数**——**你们应该自己跑去别处**。**当然了**，**我们也可以做笔交易**……"

**我们要打你**——或——**你必须离开**。"**哦，武力的威胁——多么没想象力**。**不如我们做笔交易吧**？**今晚之后**，**我那些小学习者们就要被派往切利亚斯各地执行任务**。**我本打算留在这里赶上点儿读书**，但**或许可以被说服搬迁**。**何不今晚出席我的讲座**，**贡献你们自己的见解**呢。**若我喜欢我所听到的**，**早晨我便离开**。"

#### 异端讲座（A Lecture on Heresy）　**严峻 12**

**若 PC 们同意亵典的条件**，**她便愉快地呵呵笑，邀请他们进入庭院**——**而她仍要完成准备**。**出于安全考虑**，**她坚持要 PC 们的囚犯主力留在庄园之外**（**但仍处于遮蔽帷幕的庇护之内**）。

SIEGING THE MANOR	SEVERE 12
Taking the manor by force is a daunting prospect for the PCs. Treat this as a skirmish encounter with the objective of seizing the central square of the manor house. If the PCs can occupy this area with one or more troops, Apocriphany and her remaining forces give up the fight and retreat. The PCs can recruit four mercenary bands from the prisoners for the assault.

Creatures: Apocriphany, her four coartis, and the two rancorous priesthoods outside defend the estate (as described in area A3). At the beginning of the second round, the remaining disciples assemble to create a third rancorous priesthood inside area A4 and join the fight.

PC Troops: The prisoners and other rebels the PCs are escorting can assemble into ragtag troops. The PCs can tell that these troops are capable combatants yet will be at a slight disadvantage against Apocriphany's forces, so they should be wary of deploying these troops recklessly.

MEETING APOCRIPHANY
If the PCs wish to negotiate, Apocriphany cheerfully floats out to greet them.

A feminine devil wearing orange and black robes drifts close while seated on a floating throne. Tomes and scrolls lazily orbit her, half-opened or bookmarked. With an amused grin, she calls out, "Ho, ho, what have we here? I didn't expect visitors to my lecture night, but those with an open mind are always welcome."

Although a fearsome foe, Apocriphany finds combat mundane and prefers corrupting mortals instead. She can answer the following questions for the PCs.

Who are you? "I am Apocriphany, a scholar of sorts. I typically reside in somewhat warmer locales, but from time to time, I like to pop into the world of mortals to spread a few delicious morsels of knowledge."

Why are you here? "I'm on a private mission currently, and this manor house is the perfect location to avoid prying eyes. I suspect you came here for the same reason. I used to know the owner of the manor, you know—years ago, before he lost his wits. Such a terrible shame."

Who do you work for? "Isn't that a yummy question? I'm afraid I can't divulge that secret. But I can tell you that I am no friend of House Thrune. In fact, if you're interested, I'm having some talks this evening to discuss taking Thrune down a peg or two. You are welcome to join."

Can we share the manor? "I'm afraid not. My followers are avid little learners, but they don't play nicely with others. Since we were here first, the courteous thing would be for you to scamper off somewhere else. Of course, we could make a deal…"

We will fight you or You need to leave. "Oh, the threat of force—how unimaginative. How about we make a deal instead? After this evening, my little learners will be off on missions throughout Cheliax. I was planning to stay here and catch up on some reading, but I might be convinced to relocate. Why don't you attend my lectures this evening and lend your own perspectives. If I like what I hear, I will leave in the morning."

A LECTURE ON HERESY	MODERATE 12
If the PCs agree to Apocriphany's terms, she chuckles happily and invites them into the courtyard while she finishes preparations. For security, she insists that the bulk of the prisoners remain outside the manor (but still within the shelter of the obscuring veil).
