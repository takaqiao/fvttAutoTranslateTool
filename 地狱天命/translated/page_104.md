### E11. 陷落神龛（FALLEN SHRINE）	中 14

这处供奉**石血神系**（Stone's Blood pantheon）的神龛，曾经在很久以前发生过一次令其遭诅咒的背叛事件。事件具体经过已湮没于历史，但其灵性残余仍盘桓不散。当玩家角色们踏入此处时，被困其中的亡灵会苏动并发动攻击。

**生物**：一只**狄布克**（dybbuk）与两位矮人**墓骑士**被矮人诸神的意志束缚于此神龛。它们厌憎活物的存在，战斗直至毁灭——或敌人逃出它们能企及的范围。**狄布克**只要先附身一名活生物，便能脱离它原本的禁锢区域；它会每有机会就试图这样做，但仍会先杀光所有其他活生物，再溜走。

**墓骑士（2）	生物 10**

**剧情发展**：玩家角色们可轻易感觉到这处神龛已被污秽。他们可花 1 小时操劳并施行一道简单仪式，将此地重新奉献给原本的诸神——**石血神系**，以重新净化此地。所有参与的玩家角色须分别尝试一次 DC 34 检定，例如以运动清扫、以交涉为他们刚刚清除的亡灵之灵祈祷、以神秘擦除特定符文、以宗教献上恰当的祭品，或以社交说出正确的祷词。若一半以上玩家角色检定成功，他们便祓除了神龛中的邪秽，也由此永久消灭了墓骑士，并使其铠甲变得安全可触可穿。

一名玩家角色若以 DC 37 察觉检定成功**搜寻**，能发现一扇暗门，可从此室通往 E13 区域。

**战利品**：构成这处神龛的，是十枚以红宝石雕成的宗教神符——石血神系中每位神祇各一枚。每枚价值 500 gp，可安全地从神龛中取走。

### E12. 石板典藏（STONE ARCHIVES）	中 14

这座古老的典藏室中藏着众多石板——闪光岩堡原矮人创造者留下的记录。枭鬼们用不上石板，便保留此室不动。然而最近来了一位访客——是他们到栖巢做客时显示出对此处的兴趣，玛拉拉便在被附身之前允许此人入内查阅。

**生物**：**黑鸦爵 Cotrila**（一只**黑鸦爵**，vilderavn）正在典藏室内研读古矮人文本，不愿被打搅。她非常清楚玛拉拉正被地狱所附身，但她并不在意。她正在研究闪光岩堡的原始居民及其制作高品质**刚玉**武器与神器的方法——这是当前居住者所不掌握的秘密。她语气尖刻地要求任何入侵者立刻离开她；若进一步打扰，她会以致命武力作答。与闪光岩堡内其他遭遇不同，没有任何其他住户会前来支援黑鸦爵——本地枭鬼都惧怕她。当其生命降至 100 HP 以下时，Cotrila **变身**为一只巨鸦飞走，等到入侵者离开此地区域后才回到此处继续她的研究。

### E11. FALLEN SHRINE	MODERATE 14

This shrine to the Stone's Blood pantheon was the site of a betrayal long ago that left it cursed. The exact nature of that incident has been lost to history, but its spiritual remains linger. When the PCs enter this space, the undead that have remained trapped in here animate and attack.

**Creatures**: A dybbuk and two dwarven graveknights are bound to this shrine by the will of the dwarven gods. They seethe at the presence of living creatures, fighting until destroyed or until their enemies flee beyond their reach. The dybbuk can escape the limitations of their bound area if they first possesses a living creature; they try to do so at every opportunity, though they still seek to kill all other living creatures before fleeing.

**GRAVEKNIGHTS (2)	CREATURE 10**

**Development**: The PCs can easily sense that this shrine has been tainted. They can work to reconsecrate the area by spending 1 hour of work and performing a simple ritual to rededicate it to its original deities, the Stone's Blood pantheon. All PCs taking part must attempt a DC 34 Athletics check to clean, Diplomacy check to pray for the souls of the undead they've just purged, Occultism check to scrub away specific sigils, Religion check to make the proper offerings, or Society check to say the right words. If more than half of the PCs succeed at their checks, they purge the evil from the shrine, which also permanently destroys the graveknights and renders their armor safe for handling and wearing.

A PC who succeeds at a DC 37 Perception check to Search discovers a secret door that goes from this room to area E13.

**Treasure**: Ten religious symbols carved from ruby, one for each god in the Stone's Blood pantheon, make up this shrine. Each one is worth 500 gp, and these icons can be safely taken from the shrine.

### E12. STONE ARCHIVES	MODERATE 14

These ancient archives contain stone tablets—the records of Glittercrag Keep's original dwarven creators. The strix had no use for stone slabs and left this room alone. Recently, however, a visitor to their roosts showed an interest, and their chieftain allowed them access just prior to her becoming possessed.

**Creatures**: The vilderavn Cotrila is in the archive studying the ancient dwarven texts and doesn't wish to be disturbed. They're well-aware of Marala's current infernal possession, and they don't care. They're researching the original inhabitants of Glittercrag Keep and their methods for creating high-quality corundum weapons and artifacts, a secret the current inhabitants don't possess. They sharply demand that any intruders leave them alone and, if disturbed further, responds with deadly force. Unlike the other encounters in Glittercrag Keep, none of the other inhabitants come to Cotrila's aid, as the local strix are afraid of the vilderavn. If reduced to fewer than 100 HP, Cotrila Changes Shape into a great raven and flies away, waiting until the invaders have left the region before returning to continue their research.
