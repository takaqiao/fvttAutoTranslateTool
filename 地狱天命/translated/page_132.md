### C. 通讯断绝（COMMS BLACKOUT）　中等 16

斯戎家族的兵力此刻正向燃狱之门进军，与安多兰大军展开血腥的混战；他们还沿伊塞尔德河上溯派出部队、协调远程炮击、并准备多项邪魔仪式。这一切都需要协调、指挥与控制。其关键节点之一是一座位于前线视野内的石塔——已被一只强大魔鬼占用——该魔鬼在塔上建立了一座魔法通讯枢纽，得以心灵感应数英里、瞬间为切利亚斯将军和前线指挥官来回传达命令。

这套系统的真正弱点是**操作者本身**。负责此处运营的**叛教魔**（deimavigga），如所有叛教魔鬼一般，厌恶与他人合作、坚持独自工作。这位特殊的魔鬼只接受一名贴身护卫——来自**镣铐骑士团**（Order of the Manacle）的地狱骑士副执棒官，名为**蕾恩**（Rayhnne，复仇女性精灵哨兵 14 级）。

塔是一座简单的石质结构，高 60 英尺。两架长长的木梯通向其顶部——一架自西侧、一架自南侧的城垛口。塔顶中央平台之上悬浮着一团形似眼睛的地狱火球，发出沉闷低吼，让叛教魔得以将其心灵感应扩展至数英里之外。这只眼同时也起到**精良炽焰旗（major blazing banner）**（《战吼！》第 140 页）的作用——已经安置好并处于激活态。在玩家角色对塔发起攻势之前，**忒尔齐纳**在远处忧伤地认出了**蕾恩**，并道：在斯戎家族分裂锁链骑士团之前，这两人是恋人。他询问玩家角色能否设法**留她性命**，让他试着劝她回头，但他也理解他们如做不到。和解之路并不轻松、更非一蹴而就，忒尔齐纳与蕾恩之间发生什么交由 GM 定夺，玩家角色也可能给忒尔齐纳出主意。他还指出，这场任务又是玩家角色必须把部队留在外围的一次——通往塔的入口与塔身本身都太狭窄，无法容纳他们的全部兵力。

**生物**：**叛教魔**站在塔楼东北缘，朝战场方向眺望。在蕾恩拉响警报或玩家到达塔顶之前，他们对玩家的接近都不会留意。**蕾恩**则有些百无聊赖，绕着那团火焰小步巡逻。如果玩家使用梯子，且当时她在 15 英尺以内，他们必须对她的察觉 DC 尝试隐秘检定，否则便有被她发现并拉响警报的风险。蕾恩出于职责必须守护此处，但魔鬼并不与她协同行动——认为这位凡人精灵不足挂齿。她会在第一时间使用她铠甲的**反魔法属性符文**，预料任何瞄准她的法术皆为强力法术。一旦战斗打响，两人将战至毙命或失去意识。

C. COMMS BLACKOUT　MODERATE 16

House Thrune's forces are currently marching on the Inferno Gate, engaging in a bloody mass melee with Andoran's armies, sending troops up the Iseld River, coordinating long-range artillery strikes, and preparing numerous diabolical rituals. All of that takes coordination, command, and control. One of their key nodes to accomplish this is a stone tower within sight of the front lines that has been appropriated by a powerful devil, who's set up a magical communications hub so that they can telepathically reach out over miles to instantaneously relay orders to and from the Chelaxian generals to the frontline commanders.

The only real weakness of this system is the operator themself. The deimavigga who runs this operation, like all apostate devils, loathes teaming up with others and insists on working alone. This particular devil accepted only a single bodyguard, a Hellknight paralictor from the Order of the Manacle named Rayhnne (vengeful female elf sentinel 14).

The tower is a simple structure made of stone and 60 feet tall. Long wooden ladders lead up to its top from both its western and southern crenelations. Hovering over the center of its flat top is a ball of hellfire shaped vaguely like an eye that gives off a dull roar, allowing the deimavigga to extend their telepathy to a range of several miles. This eye also functions as a major blazing banner (Battlecry! 140), already affixed and active. Before the PCs begin their assault on the tower, Telzinah mournfully spots Rayhnne from a distance and remarks that the pair were lovers before House Thrune splintered the Order of the Chain Hellknights. He asks if the PCs can somehow find a way to take her alive so he might get her to see reason but understands if they're unable to do so. The path to reconciliation isn't easy or quick, and what happens between Telzinah and Rayhnne is up to the GM, though the PCs might have their own advice for Telzinah. He remarks that this mission is another in which the PCs will have to leave their troops behind, as the tower and its approach are too small to accommodate their full forces.

Creatures: The deimavigga stands at the northeastern edge of the tower, looking out toward the battlefield. They aren't paying attention to the PCs' approach until Rayhnne raises the alarm or the PCs reach the top of the tower. Rayhnne, on the other hand, is slightly bored and walking in small patrol circles around the flame. If the PCs use the ladders, they must attempt Stealth checks against her Perception DC if she's within 15 feet of their location, or else they risk her detecting them and raising the alarm. Rayhnne is duty bound to protect this position, but the devil doesn't coordinate their actions with her, thinking that the mortal elf is beneath their notice. She uses her armor's antimagic property rune at the first opportunity, suspecting that any spell she's targeted by will be a potent one. Once combat ensues, the pair fight until slain or knocked unconscious.
