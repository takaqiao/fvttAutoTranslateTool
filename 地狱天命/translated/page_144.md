Hinji
The port city of Hinji sits on the coast of the Inner Sea, approximately halfway between Corentyn and Westcrown. Its well-tended public park and carefully planned streets greet the PCs with a sense of order. However, if a PC attempts to Gather Information (DC 36), they learn that Hinji has been struggling with mismanagement for the past several years, as Lord-Mayor Tareth Ivici (pompous male human status seeker 10) focuses on his standing among the nobility instead of day-to-day city business. A critical success additionally reveals that he's almost always accompanied by his diabolic advisor Ivirno (page 147). With a DC 32 Underworld Lore check to Recall Knowledge, a PC knows that some suspect he's purposefully ignoring the city's underworld due to ties with the ruling Sons of the Tear cartel, and a critical success reveals that the cartel is based out of the District of Ashes.
