## Page 31 — 第 2 章：破钥行动（开篇）

> 赫斯佩雷斯海峡（Hespereth Strait）附近的海面翻涌不安，为一把钥匙的转动而准备。泰莎·费尔温的**抽签好运号**（Luck of the Draw）正从镣铐群岛向北航行。镣铐骑士团（Order of the Manacle）请求斯戎家族派出特工以补充其有限的兵力。在格拉戴斯卡堡垒（Citadel Gheradesca）岩石之下的高级地牢深处，**厄登·砾掌**（Urdun Gravelhands）正等候救援。
>
> 两周之后，船帆终于抵达。战斗在午夜深处打响。从格拉戴斯卡堡垒，执棒官乌罗·阿登（Lictor Uro Adom）命令**利亚布里斯·科尔博拉**（Liabriz Corborra）率领地狱骑士前往寇兰廷增援陆上前哨战。一名信使将战事消息送达暮光之爪安全屋。
>
> 当起义联军开始行动时，琳奈塔揭开了**破钥行动**（Operation Broken Key）的真貌：一项胆识超群的计划，意在把寇兰廷与卡利从切利亚斯手中解放。第一阶段意图削弱切利亚斯海军，并打破对赫斯佩雷斯海峡的封锁。铃花会、碎冠者与细缕者将被派往码头，破坏任何潜在的撤退路线。锁链骑士团与 PC 们则要利用这场混乱，去攻打一个更具诱惑力的目标——**格拉戴斯卡堡垒**。
>
> 这座堡垒是锁链骑士团历史上的据点，但在分裂之后已成为镣铐骑士团的财产。在寇兰廷，自其落成以来便一直俯视着这座城市。来自整片大陆的囚犯都被押解至此：那些反抗切利亚斯王冠的政治目标、其他国家无法稳固关押的危险俘虏、异界威胁，以及只在低声细语中提及的恐怖之物。被送进其高级监狱的人极少返回。即便返回的人，也已破碎不堪，性格被彻底改造。

> ## 第 2 章：破钥行动（Chapter 2: Operation Broken Key）

<details><summary>English Source</summary>

The seas near the Hespereth Strait roil with movement, preparing for the turning of a key. Tessa Fairwind's Luck of the Draw sails north from the Shackles. The Order of the Manacle requests agents from Thrune to swell their limited numbers. In the high dungeon beneath the rock of Citadel Gheradesca, Urdun Gravelhands waits for rescue.

Two weeks pass before the sails arrive. The battle begins in the dead of night. From Citadel Gheradesca, Lictor Uro Adom orders Liabriz Corborra to lead Hellknights to Corentyn as reinforcements for the land skirmish. A courier reaches the Twilight Talons safe house with news of the fight.

As the combined rebellion moves, Linetta unveils Operation Broken Key: a bold plan to liberate Corentyn and Khari from Cheliax. The first phase hopes to weaken the Chelaxian navy and destroy the blockade of the Hespereth Strait. The Bellflower Network, Crowncrushers, and Thin Wisps will be sent to the docks to sabotage any potential retreats. The Order of the Chain and the PCs are to take advantage of the chaos for a more tempting target: Citadel Gheradesca.

The citadel is the Order of the Chain's historical stronghold, though it became the property of the Order of the Manacle after the schism. In Corentyn, it's loomed over the city since its commissioning. Prisoners from across the continent were brought to it: political targets who rebelled against the Chelaxian crown, dangerous captives other countries couldn't keep contained, extraplanar threats, and horrors only whispered about. Those who disappear into its high prison rarely return. Those who do return are broken, wholly transformed in personality.
