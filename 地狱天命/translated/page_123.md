## Page 123 — 第七章：战争即地狱（Chapter 7: War Is Hell）

经过一整天的武装战场侦察，玩家角色们获得了短暂的休整时间，在安多兰军营中央那几顶舒适的私人帐篷里安歇。他们有时间将这一日缴获的装备与物资变卖给营中的军需官（见第六章「派克长枪骑士团」一节），也可以采购所需，再美美地睡上一觉。次日清晨，整个军营都因黎明时分一场天界异象的传言而沸腾——玩家角色们甚至还未来得及完成每日的日常准备，便被请去伊蒂·乔吉努斯将军（General Edei Georginus）的指挥帐。

帐外围着一群高低军衔的军官，个个仰头惊叹。玩家角色们一进帐内，便明白了原因：**塔尔曼铎**（Talmandor，自信男性天界使 20 级）亲自降临这片战场，要协助安多兰军队对抗切利亚斯！

军官们让开通路，将玩家角色们引入指挥帐。营帐中央，将军与上校们环绕着一道辉煌的存在。一只长着白色与金色羽翼的鸟人天界生灵伫立其间，那双翼自行散发着柔和光辉。他半边身躯披着精心安排的天界锁子甲、缀着几片黄金板甲，确保那双巨翼活动自如。这便是塔尔曼铎，安多兰的守护天界使。他面容端庄又不失慈祥，散发着勇毅、明断与坚韧之气——仿佛这些品质是众人皆可如沐正午阳光般吸纳的灵光。他头顶羽毛上扬，自然形成一顶王冠，衬托着那方正的下颌。

英雄们一踏入帐中，塔尔曼铎便结束了与指挥幕僚的谈话，径直走向他们，伸手与他们一一握手——

Following a day of armed field reconnaissance, the PCs receive time to rest in comfortable private tents near the center of the Andoren military encampment. They have time to sell any gear and materials they acquired to the camp's quartermaster (see Pike's Place in Chapter 6), as well as make purchases, before getting a good night's sleep. The next morning, the whole camp is abuzz with rumors of a celestial event taking place at the stroke of dawn, and the PCs are invited to the command tent of General Edei Georginus before they've even made their daily preparations.

The tent is surrounded by a throng of junior and senior officers who stare on in wonder, and the PCs soon find out why. Once they're admitted inside, they discover that Talmandor (confident male agathion 20) himself has come to this very battlefield to help the army in its efforts against the forces of Cheliax!

The officers part to allow entrance to the command tent, revealing the generals and colonels gathered around a shining presence. At the center of the tent stands an avian celestial being with white and gold wings that radiate light of their own volition. He wears celestial chainmail armor over half of his body with a few carefully arranged golden plates, ensuring full mobility for those mighty wings. This is Talmandor, the celestial patron of Andoran. His face appears regal yet kind, exuding courageousness, judiciousness, and resilience like they were auras everyone near him could absorb as rays from the noonday sun. The feathers on his head angle upward, forming a natural crown that helps frame his square jaw.
