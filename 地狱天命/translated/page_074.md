#### B. 解放市场广场（Liberating the Market Square）　**低 13 或 中等 13**

> **沙岩建筑环绕着这处宽阔的市场广场**——**广场里满是一排排五颜六色的帐篷与摊位**。**广场诡异地安静**——**只偶尔传来从沿广场北缘临时围栏中关押的数百名俘虏所发出的悲嚎**。**广场中央已被清理出空地**——**地面上涂着血色符号**。

**沃蕊·塔尔米纳里知道她需要更多资源以守卫寇兰廷**——**便派罗特罗维奥率一小支部队前来征服卡利**——**并以任何手段补充其兵力**。**罗特罗维奥乐于从命**。**毕竟**——**他不仅理解控制卡利的战略意义**——**也急于通过将那些不愿为他作战之人化为不死生物**——**来磨练自己的死灵学造诣**。**本遭遇使用第 72 页的地图**。

**生物**：**罗特罗维奥派他的副手——一位名为旺达·喷毒鞭的阿斯摩蒂斯神卫**（Vanda Spitelash，champion of Asmodeus）——**负责本街区并复活那些顽固俘虏**。**她与她的锈铁魔同伴一起监督这场行动**——**其中包括**：**定期在广场中央以一场亵渎仪式祭献并复活约 20 名不驯居民**。**她最新创造的骷髅步兵分队帮助管理囚犯**。

**旺达在广场中央立起了一架画架**——**在魔鬼的细心指导下**——**正用被屠杀平民的鲜血绘制一幅虔诚的阿斯摩蒂斯画像**。**骷髅战士们在市场外围以顺时针圈巡逻**。

**解救囚犯**：**部分囚犯是士兵**——**如被释放将乐意协助 PC 们**。**机智的 PC 或可在与旺达交战之前先解救囚犯**——**但这样做需要潜行**。**PC 们必须避开巡逻的骷髅分队才能抵达围栏**。**围栏门**（**硬度 8、HP 90、强行开启 DC 33**）**装有一把优良锁**（**需要 5 次成功的 DC 30 巧手检定来开锁**）。**钥匙搁在旺达的画架上**——**除非她被分心或被引开**——**否则难以盗取**。

B. LIBERATING THE MARKET SQUARE	LOW OR MODERATE 13
Sandstone buildings surround this wide market square filled with rows of colorful tents and stalls. The square is eerily silent save for the occasional wail from the hundreds of captive citizens held in a makeshift stockade along the square's northern edge. Square's center is cleared out, the ground painted with bloody symbols.

Knowing she would need additional resources to defend Corentyn, Vorrea Talminari dispatched Rotrovio with a small force to subjugate Khari and replenish her forces by any means necessary. Rotrovio happily obliges. After all, he not only understands the strategic importance of controlling Khari, but also he's eager to hone his necromancy by transforming anyone into undead if they won't fight for him willingly. Use the map on page 72 for this encounter.

Creatures: Rotrovio placed his second-in-command, a champion of Asmodeus named Vanda Spitelash, in charge of this neighborhood and reanimating stubborn captives. She and her rust devil companion oversee the operation, which includes periodically sacrificing and reanimating about 20 unruly citizens in an unholy ritual at the center of the square. The latest skeleton infantry troop she created helps manage the prisoners.

Vanda has set up an easel at the square's center and, under the devil's careful instruction, is painting a devoted image of Asmodeus made from the blood of slain civilians. The skeleton warriors patrol the market's outer edge in a clockwise circle.

Freeing the Prisoners: Some of the prisoners are soldiers who would happily aid the PCs if freed. Clever PCs might try to free the prisoners before fighting Vanda, although doing so requires stealth. The PCs must evade the skeleton troop on patrol to reach the stockade. The stockade door (Hardness 8, 90 Hit Points, Force Open DC 33) is secured with a good lock (requiring five successful DC 30 Thievery checks to Pick the Lock). The key rests on Vanda’s easel, making it difficult to steal unless she's distracted or lured away.
