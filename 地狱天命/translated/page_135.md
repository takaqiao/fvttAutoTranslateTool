## Page 135 — 第七章：战争即地狱

除了玩家角色获得 BP 的方式，**未完成的任务则会强化切利亚斯方**——表现为本场遭遇起始**警觉点（Awareness Points，AP）**的增加。玩家角色每**未完成**第七章中一项任务，便将起始 AP 数 +1，最高 +4。

战场渗透分为 **4 个阶段**，每个阶段代表玩家角色通往最终目标——**燃狱之门**——途中的不同部分。**障碍**仅在其所属阶段内可用。**与标准渗透不同的是，下方所列的「个人障碍」选项中，玩家角色只需击败其中之一即可——不必两项都过**——以便玩家发挥自身长处。**团体障碍**则与标准渗透相同。

请记住，**每个渗透回合结束时，队伍累积 1 警觉点**。此外，**玩家角色总是可以选择跳过一项检定**，避免在自己的回合潜在地累积更多 AP。**每个阶段结束时**，玩戏从渗透规则切换为遭遇式玩法（详见下方）。

**每个渗透回合代表游戏世界中 15 分钟**。

---

### 第一阶段：行进中（PHASE 1: ON THE MOVE）

玩家角色一旦收到安多兰将军的信号，便必须组织部队、迅速归入主战场。然而，在他们组织部队并启动的过程中，会被来自两翼的切利亚斯侦察兵与小规模冲突兵骚扰——他们从路两侧用箭雨袭来。

**第一阶段结束时，进入遭遇 E1。**

---

**急步行军（QUICK STEP MARCH）　障碍 16**
**渗透点** 2（团体）；**克服** DC 35 表演以将特种部队列入行军阵形、DC 37 强韧豁免以承受跟部队同步的急速行军、或 DC 39 交涉以在面对未卜的命运时维持士气

玩家角色将部队列为狭窄纵队穿越沉言林蜿蜒小径，以鹰焰为林间星标导引方向。

---

**行军中的小规模冲突（SKIRMISH EN ROUTE）　障碍 16**
**渗透点** 2（团体）；**克服** DC 35 森林学识或侦察学识，赶在敌人对特种部队侧翼形成跨射位置之前击溃其机动；DC 37 求生，借地形对抗敌人、抢占高地；或 DC 39 自然，利用对沉言林的深度认识合围那些小规模冲突兵。

数支切利亚斯小规模冲突分队在玩家与他们的部队赶往战场途中发起攻击，试图拖慢推进。这些敌方游侠必须被击败，以保护部队的生命，使他们能完整抵达最终战场。

---

### 第二阶段：突破（PHASE 2: BREAKING THROUGH）

玩家角色到达曾建有**阿雷戈堡（Fort Arego）**的区域，那里已被发掘至天际开放。**安多兰与切利亚斯**两军交战的主战线就在 E 区东北方不远处，斯戎家族的指挥官与预备队散布于地图全境。玩家角色有机会与他们的盟军会合——只要能穿越敌方战线。

**第二阶段结束时，玩家角色把他们的部队带至南侧。运行遭遇 E2。**

---

**深入敌后（BEHIND ENEMY LINES）　障碍 16**
**渗透点** 1（个人）；**克服** DC 35 隐秘以悄悄引导一支特种部队穿越林木、避开侦测；DC 37 欺骗以让部队行军时假冒切利亚斯人、瞒过观察者；或 DC 39 察觉，找出守军巡逻的破绽，从空隙中冲过部队。

玩家角色让部队穿越敌线的一种方式，是凭借**隐秘与狡诈**，从守军身边悄悄滑过，将气力保留给即将到来的更多战斗。

---

**警戒线突击（PICKET CHARGE）　障碍 16**
**渗透点** 1（个人）；**克服** DC 35 威吓以让特种部队突袭切利亚斯警戒线、令其惧而弃守；DC 37 战争学识以在守军与瞭望发出警报之前压制他们；或 DC 39 运动，以特种部队简单粗暴地砸穿、斩杀挡在前方的每一名切利亚斯士兵。

如果潜伏不在玩家角色的字典里，那便永远还有**暴力**这条路可走。这毕竟是战争。借助速度强冲切利亚斯战线，与隐秘一样行之有效。

---

### 第三阶段：避开险境（PHASE 3: SIDESTEPPING DANGER）

虽然玩家角色已突破切利亚斯警戒线、看到了燃狱之门，他们仍要面对一些危险。在这一节点，安多兰士兵已将切利亚斯人逼退，玩家角色终于有机会与盟军会合——只要他们不被**战雾**所困。

In addition to how the PCs earned BP, tasks left undone by the PCs strengthen the Chelaxian side, reflected by an increase to the starting Awareness Points (AP) for this encounter. For each mission during Chapter 7 that the PCs didn't accomplish, increase the starting number of AP by 1, to a maximum of 4.

The battlefield infiltration is broken down into four phases, each one representing a different part of the PCs' journey toward the end goal—the Inferno Gate. Obstacles are available only during their indicated phases. Different from standard infiltration rules is that when individual obstacles are presented below, the PCs have to defeat only one of them—not both—allowing the PCs to play to their strengths. Group obstacles are unchanged from the standard infiltration rules.

You should remember that at the end of each infiltration round, the party accrues 1 Awareness Point. Additionally, PCs can always choose to skip a roll to avoid potentially accruing more Awareness Points on their turns. At the end of each phase, play shifts from the infiltration rules to encounter-based play, as detailed below.

Each infiltration round encompasses 15 minutes of in-world time.

PHASE 1: ON THE MOVE

Once the PCs have received the signal from the Andoren generals, they must organize their troops and hastily rejoin the main battle. While organizing their troops and getting them moving, however, they're opposed by Chelaxian scouts and skirmishers who pepper them with arrows from either side of their path.

At the end of Phase 1, run encounter E1.

QUICK STEP MARCH　OBSTACLE 16
Infiltration Points 2 (group); Overcome DC 35 Performance to form the special forces units up into marching formation, DC 37 Fortitude save to endure a march alongside the troops, or DC 39 Diplomacy to keep everybody's spirits up as they march toward an uncertain doom

The PCs form their troops up into narrow columns to traverse the winding paths of the Whisperwood, using the eagle flare as a north star through the tree line.

SKIRMISH EN ROUTE　OBSTACLE 16
Infiltration Points 2 (group); Overcome DC 35 Forest Lore or Scouting Lore to outmaneuver the opposition before they can take up defilading positions around the special forces units, DC 37 Survival to use the terrain against the attackers and seize the high ground, or DC 39 Nature to leverage advanced knowledge of the Whisperwood and encircle the skirmishers

Squads of Chelaxian skirmishers attack the PCs and their troops while they rush toward the battlefield, trying to slow down their progress. These enemy rangers must be defeated to protect the lives of the troops so they can reach the final battlefield intact.

PHASE 2: BREAKING THROUGH

The PCs reach the area that used to house Fort Arego, which has now been excavated to the open air. The main line of the battle between Andoran's and Cheliax's armies is just to the northeast of area E, with House Thrune's commanders and reserves spread throughout the mapped area. The PCs have a chance to link up with their allied army, if they can make it through the lines.

At the end of Phase 2, the PCs bring their troops down to the south. Run encounter E2.

BEHIND ENEMY LINES　OBSTACLE 16
Infiltration Points 1 (individual); Overcome DC 35 Stealth to lead a special forces unit through the trees quietly and avoid detection, DC 37 Deception to march the troops as though they were Chelaxian to throw off observers, or DC 39 Perception to find gaps in the watch patterns of the defenders and rush the special forces units through the opening

One way the PCs can get their troops through the lines is through stealth and guile, sneaking past the defenders and saving their strength for the battles yet to come.

PICKET CHARGE　OBSTACLE 16
Infiltration Points 1 (individual); Overcome DC 35 Intimidation to rush the Chelaxian picket line with the special forces unit and scare them away from their posts, DC 37 Warfare Lore to quickly overwhelm the defenders and lookouts before they can raise the alarm, or DC 39 Athletics to simply smash through and slay every Chelaxian soldier standing in the way

If subtlety isn't in the PCs' vocabulary, there's always the way of violence. This is war, after all. Using speed to rush through the Chelaxian lines is just as good as stealth in this situation.

PHASE 3: SIDESTEPPING DANGER

Despite breaking through the Chelaxian picket line and emerging within sight of the Inferno Gate, the PCs still have hazards to face. At this juncture, the Andoren soldiers have pushed the Chelaxians back, and the PCs have a chance to link up with their allies at long last, so long as they don't get caught up in the fog of war.
