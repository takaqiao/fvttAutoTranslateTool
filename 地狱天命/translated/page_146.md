## Page 146 — 第八章：裁缝与叛徒

——透过敞开的中央天窗洒下温暖的日光。**新换的盆栽**让入口透出一股**葱郁有机的气息**。

**中庭从地面到天花板高 25 英尺**——同时包含**超高的一层（前 15 英尺）**和**标准高度的二层（后 10 英尺）**。**塔雷斯每天下午用他的 3 环警报术法杖**（**wand of alarm**），**将其 20 英尺爆发范围以楼梯与二层走廊交接处为中心**，设定的触发条件为：**有人进入法术范围且既非塔雷斯、伊维诺，也未佩戴伊维奇徽章**。**法术持续时段被定为从晚餐后开始，至清晨结束**。**家政与公务人员被要求作为职责一部分佩戴伊维奇徽章**——**那些未佩戴（从而触发警报术）的人则会被痛骂**。

**一扇通往 A6 衣帽间的暗门**，可由一位**搜寻** DC 40 察觉检定成功的玩家角色发现。

### A2. 办公室（OFFICES）

**四张洁净的书桌占据房内**，每张桌子上**放着一盏绿色玻璃油灯**，**桌面别无他物**。**角落里有一座壁炉**，**温暖着三把覆有薄尘的椅子**。

大公市长办公室的**专业职员们**在此办公。**他们都尽量避免在这片空间里工作**，**反倒找种种"现场"的理由去社区中各处**——**比如港务总管办公室**。

**每张桌子都用上等锁（superior lock）锁住**（**开锁 DC 40**；**需要六次成功才能解锁**），**且都被装上机关——如果开锁尝试出现极差失败，桌内文件便被销毁**。这些职员各负责辛吉的不同辖区，**主要职责是监视贿款及压下投诉**。

**港务总管督察（西北桌）**：**这张桌子的文件已被加密**，需要一位玩家角色**社交学 DC 36 解译文书检定成功**（通常是探索期活动）才能阅读。**解密后，文件详述大公市长一直纵容港务总管收受贿赂以逃避税务**。**作为回报，塔雷斯则要求港务总管允许「泪痕之子」将自己的走私货物（一种名为"冰泪"［ice tears］的强效毒品）输出辛吉**。

**慈善督察（东北桌）**：**这张桌子里有对一些"慈善机构"的投诉**——这些机构开在已被烧毁的「灰烬区」内，**实为有组织犯罪的门面**。**所有与「泪痕之子」相关的文件均被标注为"调查完毕"，但实际上没有任何调查记录存在**。

**公共美化督察（东南桌）**：**有关伊维奇公园的资料整齐归档于此**——**详细规定卫兵未经大公市长明确许可，不得在伊维奇公园内进行任何逮捕**。**对塔雷斯魔鬼顾问伊维诺的投诉，则被列为"美化障碍"**。

**海军督察（西南桌）**：**这张桌子里的公函讨论格林辛上将的领导力**——**尤其指她对塔雷斯似乎抱有敌意，且不愿被贿赂**。

**羞辱点**：**如果玩家角色至少收集到上述两组文件，他们获得 1 MP**；**如果他们收集到全部四组，则总共获得 2 MP**。

**战利品**：**港务总管督察的桌内有上个月的贿款——一袋 50 枚铂金币**。

### A3. 吸烟温室（SMOKING CONSERVATORY）

**沙发围着一张精致的咖啡桌**，**桌上放着几只烟灰缸**——**那便是空气中浓厚雪茄气味的来源**。**一辆金色推车停在一旁**，**车上摆着几只玻璃醒酒瓶**。

**塔雷斯在这间房里度过大量时间，于此招待访客**。**多数会面都围绕他炫耀自己的雪茄、葡萄酒与烈酒收藏**。但**这套收藏并非他所宣称的那般**——**几十瓶酒中只有六瓶是真品**，**其余都是他用更廉价替代年份酒灌注的假酒瓶**。**一位玩家角色可凭借工艺 DC 34 检定成功识破此手脚**。

**搜寻：DC 38 察觉检定成功**，玩家角色会发现**其中一瓶葡萄酒少了一杯之量**。**鉴定该瓶酒需要工艺 DC 38 检定成功**——**辨明这瓶酒被掺了一剂"王之安眠"（king's sleep）毒**。

**羞辱点**：**找出毒酒瓶并辨明所掺毒种**，**玩家角色获得 1 MP**——**因为格林辛上将能借此将塔雷斯与数月前一位来访商人的死亡联系起来**。

**战利品**：**塔雷斯的酒与雪茄收藏总价值 100 gp**。

A2. OFFICES
Four pristine desks occupy the room, each with a green glass oil lamp sitting atop otherwise empty desktops. A fireplace in the corner warms three chairs covered in a thin layer of dust.

The professional staff of the lord-mayor's office maintain their offices here. They avoid working in the space, instead finding reasons to be "on-site" in the community, such as the harbormaster's office.

Each desk is locked with a superior lock (DC 40 to Pick the Lock; six successes required to unlock) and is rigged to destroy the documents within if an attempt to Pick the Lock critically fails. The staff were assigned to oversee a different area of Hinji, which mostly required monitoring bribes and suppressing any complaints.

The Harbormaster's Regulator (northwest desk): The documents in this desk are encoded, which requires a PC to succeed at a DC 36 Society check to Decipher Writing (typically an exploration activity) to read. Once decoded, the documents detail that the lord-mayor has been allowing the harbormaster to accept bribes to avoid taxes. In return, Tareth required the harbormaster to allow the Sons of the Tear to smuggle their own goods (a potent drug called ice tears) out of Hinji.

The Charity Regulator (northeast desk): This desk contains complaints about "charities" in the burnt-out District of Ashes that are fronts for organized crime. All documents related to the Sons of the Tear are marked as "Investigation Complete," even though no record exists of the investigation.

The Public Beautification Regulator (southeast desk): Information about Ivici Park is neatly organized in this desk, which details that no guards are to make arrests in Ivici Park without the lord-mayor's express permission. Complaints about Ivirno, Tareth's devil advisor, are listed as "barriers to beautification."

The Naval Regulator (southwest desk): Missives in this desk discuss Admiral Grinceen's leadership, particularly that she seems to be hostile to Tareth and unwilling to be bribed.

Mortification Points: If the PCs collect at least two of the sets of documents, they gain 1 MP. If they collect all four, they gain 2 MP total.

Treasure: The desk of the harbormaster's regulator contains last month's bribe, a pouch of 50 pp.

A3. SMOKING CONSERVATORY
Couches circle a fine coffee table with several ashtrays resting on it, the source of the heavy cigar odor that fills the air. A golden cart sits to one side and holds several decanters.

Tareth spends significant time in this room, where he entertains visitors. Most of these meetings focus on him bragging about his cigar, wine, and liquor collections. However, the collection isn't what he says it is. Of the dozens of bottles, only six are genuine. The rest are bottles that he has filled with far cheaper replacement vintages. A PC can identify this trick with a successful DC 34 Crafting check.

With a successful DC 38 Perception check to Search, a PC finds that one of the wine bottles is missing a single glass of liquid. Identifying this bottle requires a successful DC 38 Crafting check, which discerns that the wine is poisoned with a dose of king's sleep.

Mortification Points: Finding the poisoned decanter and identifying its poison earn the PCs 1 MP, as Admiral Grinceen can tie Tareth to the death of a visiting merchant several months prior.

Treasure: The contents of Tareth's booze and cigar collection are worth 100 gp.
