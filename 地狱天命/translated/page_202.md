### 战役总收尾（Concluding the Adventure）

戈索克莱克殒命之后——他的躯体便向后飞——撞回他的王座之上。墙壁与天花板上的锁链——将王座层层缠绕——挤压成一个铁球——继而无声爆开。在它原本所在之处——出现一道闪光的门户——透过它——玩家角色可以看到格拉利昂各处景观的影像——每一处都以不同方式陷入天罚之中：熔岩柱在城市中喷涌、魔鬼们在丘陵上嬉舞、天空染上一片愤怒的红色。铁铸要塞震动了八次——每次震动皆比上一次更快——每次都让门户中的一处地狱景象——被涤净于其地狱之影——除了切利亚斯之外。在最后一次震动之时——一位**塔尔曼铎**的灵相——遍体血污、奄奄一息——冲入铁铸要塞——他已自地狱第九层中阿波罗盖二世的掌握之下脱逃。他停留片刻——对玩家角色致以认可的颔首——而后将他们驱入那道门户——离开诸大魔王的领域。

该门户将玩家角色送至「**阿斯波丹之墙**」（the Aspodean Wall）的残墙之上。从此处——他们可俯瞰切利亚斯腹地——其中大半已成焦土与灰烬。远处——巨大无比、不该有那么高的铁塔——自各大城市拔地而起。地狱已临格拉利昂——但玩家角色的介入——已限制了它的生长。他们身后——的国土未受变易——安多兰的大军——正不解地站在原地——而残余的切利亚斯军力——则在绝望中投降。

更多关于切利亚斯这一变更——以及阿波罗盖命运的资讯——可见第 203 页的「**战役之外**」（Beyond the Campaign）一文。

Concluding the Adventure

When slain, Gorthoklek's body flies backward onto his
throne. Chains from the walls and ceiling wrap around
the chair, crushing it into an iron ball that silently
explodes. In its place is a shimmering portal through
which the PCs can see images of various landscapes of
Golarion, each damned in different ways: plumes of
lava erupt in cities, devils cavort across the hills, and
the sky turns an angry red. The Iron Fortress shudders
eight times, each impact occurring quicker than the
last, each causing one of the portal's hellscapes to be
purified of its hellish influence—all except for Cheliax.
With the last impact, an apparition of Talmandor—
bloodied yet barely clinging to life—rushes into the
Iron Fortress, having escaped Abrogail II's clutches on
Hell's ninth level. He pauses long enough to give the
PCs an approving nod before driving them through
the portal and away from the archdevils' domain.

The portal deposits the PCs on the remnants of the
Aspodean Wall. From there, they can view Cheliax's
heartland, much of it scorched earth and ash. In the
distance, impossibly tall iron towers rise from the
major cities. Hell has come to Golarion, yet the PCs'
intervention has limited its growth. Behind them,
the land is unchanged, and Andoran's armies stand
around confused while what's left of the Chelaxian
forces surrender in despair.

More about this alteration to Cheliax and Abrogail's
fate can be found in the Beyond the Campaign article
on page 203.
