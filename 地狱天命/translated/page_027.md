### 计票（Tallying the Votes）

至少有 **3 名地狱骑士**投票支持破钥行动，本提案才能通过。若失败，卡蒂利娅会被埃默汀逐出骑士团，并离开此城。若通过，锁链骑士团加入这场新生的起义——其首项行动便是针对**傲世号**（见第 28 页"乘风起义"）。

**宝藏**：若地狱骑士加入起义，他们会向 PC 们提供物质支持。
- 若塞贝尼奥投赞成，他将赠予 PC 们他的 **+1 地狱骑士胸甲**（《珍宝密库》10）与一枚**阴影信戒**（shadow signet）。此外，他与异端魁首协作，把 PC 们从最危险通缉名单上移除（使"举报危险罪犯与活动之义务"法令不再被执行）。
- 若乌贝诺投赞成，他将赠予 PC 们他的 **+2 韧能 标准品级 晨曦银地狱骑士板甲**（《珍宝密库》10）——因为他打算把打造新铠装作为自己自审仪式的一部分。
- 若埃默汀投赞成，她将赠予 PC 们四副优良镣铐、两把优良门锁，以及两段 10 英尺长的标准品级精金（adamantine）锁链。
- 若整体加入起义的投票通过，卡蒂利娅向 PC 们致谢，并把她手头所有余钱（100 pp）赠予他们。

**奖励**：每有一位地狱骑士支持该提案，授予 PC 们 20 XP（上限 60 XP）。

### 任务四：拱与钥之下（Under Arch and Key）

当细缕者的头领、表演者**阿波罗盖·克鲁恩**（Abrogail Croon，张扬的性别流动半精灵（aiuvarin）人类喜剧演员，7 级）召集组织时，他们并未打算采取直接行动。他们只是希望"**发表声明**"和"**对正在发生的可怕事情做点什么**"。结果，他们却发现自己成了国家的敌人——在余下的细缕者抗议多塔里突袭之际即遭即时逮捕。其余的细缕者担心自己"是下一个失踪的人"，许多人甚至考虑放弃政治行动。要让细缕者继续团结到加入起义，便须救出他们的领袖。琳奈塔相信阿波罗盖被关押在一处镌入奥罗登拱桥之中的**恶魔教士监狱**里。

TALLYING THE VOTES
At least three Hellknights must vote in favor of Operation Broken Key for the measure to pass. If it fails, Cathilia is exiled from the order by Emertine and leaves the city. If it succeeds, the Order of the Chain joins the nascent rebellion in its first action against the Impervious (see Rebellion on the Winds on page 28).

Treasure: If the Hellknights join the rebellion, they provide material support to the PCs. If Sebenio votes yes, he gives the PCs his +1 hellknight breastplate (Treasure Vault 10) and a shadow signet. Additionally, he works with the Archheathen to remove the PCs from the most wanted list (making it so the decree of the Obligation to Report Dangerous Criminals and Activities is no longer enforced). If Ubenor votes yes, he gives the PCs his +2 resilient standard-grade dawnsilver Hellknight plate (Treasure Vault 10), as he intends to craft new armor as part of his Reckoning. If Emertine votes yes, she gives the PCs four sets of good manacles, two good locks, and two 10-foot lengths of standard-grade adamantine chain. If the overall vote to join the rebellion succeeds, Cathilia thanks the PCs and gives them what money she has to spare: 100 pp.

Reward: For each Hellknight that supports the proposal, grant the PCs 20 XP (maximum 60 XP).

Under Arch and Key
When the Thin Wisps' leader, the performer Abrogail Croon (outrageous genderfluid aiuvarin human comedian 7), convened the organization, they weren't planning on direct action. No, they were hoping to "make a statement" and "do something about the terrible things happening." Instead, they found themself an enemy of the state and summarily arrested while the rest of the Thin Wisps were protesting the dottari raid. The rest of the Thin Wisps fear that they'll disappear next, and many are considering giving up political action. Keeping the Thin Wisps together long enough to join the rebellion means rescuing their leader. Linetta believes that Abrogail is being kept in a diabolist prison that's carved into the Arch of Aroden.
