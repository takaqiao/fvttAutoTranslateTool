> **A. 斯戎广场（A. THRUNE SQUARE）　　中等 19**

> 麾下有神圣守卫大军——玩家角色便可挥师**午夜神殿**——前去与阿斯佩西娅对决。在街区巡逻的魔鬼们——会远远地为英雄们让路——它们知道——自己会被这些神圣兵团轻易碾碎。在**斯戎广场**——玩家角色将面对更强的对抗——这里曾是一处宏伟广场——如今已被阿波罗盖的仪式彻底毁坏。鹅卵石大半破裂——其间错落分布着硫磺蒸汽口与地狱熔岩池。这些特征——具有以下效果：
>
> **蒸汽口**：每轮结束时——尝试一次 DC 8 硬性检定。大失败时——下一轮中——没有蒸汽口（地图上标记为「V」）开启。失败时——下一轮中——其中一处蒸汽口（随机选定）开启；该区域如同受**雾术**法术影响——但任何在蒸汽中结束其回合的生物——必须以 DC 38 **强韧**豁免成功——否则便陷入**反胃 1**（大失败时为**反胃 2**）。硬性检定成功时——下一轮中——有两处蒸汽口（随机选定）开启。大成功时——所有蒸汽口皆开启。
>
> **熔岩池**：进入或在熔岩池（地图上标记为「L」）内开始回合的生物——承受 15d6 火焰伤害——离池后再承受 5d6 持续火焰伤害。熔岩池为困难地形。

A. THRUNE SQUARE	MODERATE 19

With their divine warden armies, the PCs can march on
the Midnight Temple to confront Aspexia. The devils
patrolling the neighborhoods give the heroes a wide
berth, knowing they would be easily crushed by the
holy regiments. The PCs encounter greater opposition
at Thrune Square, a once grand plaza thoroughly
devastated by Abrogail's ritual. The cobblestones are
mostly broken and dotted with sulfurous steam vents
and pools of infernal lava. These features have the
following effects.

Steam Vents: At the end of each round, attempt
a DC 8 flat check. On a critical failure, none of the
steam vents (marked as "V" on the map) are active in
the next round. On a failure, one of the steam vents
(chosen randomly) is active in the next round; the area
is affected as if by the mist spell, except that a creature
that ends its turn in the steam must succeed at a DC 38
Fortitude save or become sickened 1 (sickened 2 on a
critical failure). If the flat check is a success, two of the
steam vents (chosen randomly) are active next round.
On a critical success, all of the steam vents are active.

Lava Pools: A creature that enters or begins its turn
in a lava pool (marked as "L" on the map) takes 15d6
fire damage and then 5d6 persistent fire damage after
it exits the pool. A lava pool is difficult terrain.

Skirmish: This is a skirmish encounter (Battlecry!
158). The objective is to defeat Aspexia Rugatonn;
if the PCs can kill her, knock her unconscious, or
otherwise remove her from the fight, any remaining
infernal forces on the battlefield will scatter into the
city long enough for the PCs to enter the Midnight
Temple. Each PC has command over a divine warden
army (page 183). The PCs can enter Thrune Square
from the southern or western edges as they see fit.
