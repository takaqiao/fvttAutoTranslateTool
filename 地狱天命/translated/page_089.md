## Page 89 — 第五章 招纳盟友（开篇）

寇兰廷脱离切利亚斯掌控后，玩家角色们终于有了片刻喘息。城中钟声彻夜回荡，狂欢的市民涌上街头庆祝；少数残存的斯戎家族忠党则趁夜色掩护悄然溜走，启程返回艾格利安。玩家角色们走进寇兰廷的市集、酒馆与客栈，发现自己被当作英雄迎接，得以好好享受一个酬劳之夜的欢愉与放松。

翌日清晨，旭日把寇兰廷染成一片血红，迷信的水手与码头工人窃窃私语，称这是斯戎家族震怒的凶兆。琳奈塔的使者找到玩家角色，请他们到港口一聚。她再度感谢英雄们的辛劳，但告诉他们：对抗该死的斯戎家族的战争，还需要他们再出一次力。

琳奈塔领玩家角色们走向一支混杂的船队，其中包括几艘载着夜里赶到的厄尔芬荣誉追寻者与佣兵的长船。她将这支舰队交予玩家角色们指挥，请他们到他国去寻觅愿意挺身抗击斯戎家族的盟友。拉沃努尔便是潜在盟友之一——它在数年前脱离了切利亚斯。虽然某条地狱契约的漏洞令拉沃努尔免受直接入侵，但其人民对昔日母国毫无眷恋，因此琳奈塔托付玩家角色们去开辟一条针对切利亚斯的新西线战场！

随玩家角色北行的船只载着兵员与军需物资，意在向拉沃努尔政府表达善意——他们不会让拉沃努尔人民独自作战。此举也是为了把斯戎家族的注意力从寇兰廷引开，因为玩家角色们在那里立下的功绩已让他们成了一个巨大的目标。

**第五章：招纳盟友**

After setting Corentyn free from Chelaxian control, the PCs finally have a little bit of time to rest. The city's bells ring throughout the night as revelers take to the streets in celebration, while the few remaining Thrune loyalists slip out under cover of darkness, setting off Egorian. The PCs find that Corentyn's markets, taverns, and inns welcome them as the heroes they are, allowing them to enjoy a well-deserved evening of fun and relaxation.

The next morning's sunlight bathes Corentyn in a red glow that causes superstitious sailors and dockworkers to mutter and claim it's an omen of Thrune's ire. Messengers from Linetta find the PCs and ask them to come to the docks. Thanking the heroes anew for their efforts, she tells them that they're needed again in the war against thrice-damned House Thrune.

Linetta walks the PCs over to a motley collection of vessels, including some longships that carry Ulfen glory seekers and mercenaries who arrived during the night. Linetta places this flotilla of ships under the PCs' command and asks them to find allies in other nations who might be willing to take a stand against House Thrune. Among these potential allies is Ravounel, who broke from Cheliax several years ago. While protected against direct invasion by a loophole in an infernal contract, its people hold no love for their former nation, so Linetta charges the PCs with unleashing a new western front against Cheliax!

The ships sailing north with the PCs carry troops and war materiel, intended as a gesture of good faith to persuade Ravounel's government that their citizens won't be expected to fight alone. This act is also a way to draw House Thrune's eye away from Corentyn, since the PCs' accomplishments there have made them a big target.
