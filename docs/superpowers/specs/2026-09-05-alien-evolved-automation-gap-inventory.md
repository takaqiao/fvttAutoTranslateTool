### `P0` Dice-pool floor, stress-pool integrity, NPC/synthetic modes, and the vanishing GM roll · 📤上游PR

### `P1` Push bound to the card it was rolled on, with synthetic and multi-push handled honestly
- `push-correctness` · 价值 5/5 · 成本 3/5 · 现状 **做了一半**
- **规则要求**：Pushing costs +1 Stress Level, adds one stress die and re-rolls everything not already showing a 6; kept 6s still count. A roll may be pushed only once, unless a talent (Nimble on Agility, True Grit on Strength, Inquisitive on Wits) allows one further push at +1 Stress. Synthetics do not suffer stress and do not push.
- **今天要人手做**：Players are told not to push an older card because it will re-roll someone else's dice. A synthetic PC with the option on clicks a Push button that does nothing and gets no message. A player with Nimble has to ask the GM to tick the untyped multi-push box, which is neither talent- nor attribute-gated — simultaneously the only way to use the talent and a way for anyone to break the once-only rule.
- **接入点**：Replacement renderChatMessageHTML binding of button.alien-Push-button reading ChatMessage flags instead of game.alienrpg.rollArr (superseding module/alienrpg.mjs:464-494)
- **风险**：Suppressing the system's own listener without removing it double-binds the click (two pushes, two stress). Either OVERRIDE the hook registration or stop propagation on the module's own mount; verify with a second push on one card. The synthetic decision is a judgement call that reverses the Starter Set's flat wording — surface it as a setting so a table that reads the rule strictly can force suppression regardless of synthstress. renderChatMessage disappears in V15, so a module that only overrides it inherits the breakage; bind the HTML variant. ### `P1` The Broken state, the automatic critical injury, and the Death Roll clock
- `broken-state-and-death-roll` · 价值 5/5 · 成本 4/5 · 现状 **完全没有**
- **规则要求**：At 0 Health a character is BROKEN: they roll a critical injury immediately, may take only one move action per round, cannot gain Stress and never make Panic Rolls, and stay broken until rallied or treated. Health never goes below 0, but each further hit while Broken inflicts another critical injury. A fatal critical injury forces an un-pushable STAMINA Death Roll with no stress dice after its time limit (Shift > Stretch/Turn > Round); each failure steps the limit down and failing at Round kills, each success stabilizes and restores 1 Health per success.
- **今天要人手做**：The GM notices Health hit 0, declares the PC Broken out loud, remembers to press Roll Crit, remembers the character is action-limited and immune to Stress and Panic, tracks a Shift/Stretch/Round clock in their head, calls for the STAMINA roll, remembers it takes no stress dice and cannot be pushed, decrements the step on each failure, counts successes toward three, and clicks + on Health once per healing success. This is the single most consequential moment in an Alien session and none of it is wired.
- **接入点**：A module-owned `broken` CONFIG.statusEffects entry plus Hooks.on('preUpdateActor') on system.header.health.value, with Hooks.on('createItem') on fatal critical-injury items driving the death clock
- **风险**：The Death Roll cadence needs a time-unit clock the system does not have (`grep -rniE 'worldTime|advanceTime|stretch' module/` is empty), so either this feature owns a minimal clock or the cadence degrades to a manual 'advance the clock' button — do not claim automatic Shift timing without one. Health is also written by any other module or a GM dragging the pip, so preUpdateActor must tolerate arbitrary deltas including jumps past 0. Unlinked tokens sharing a base actor will all go Broken together unless the hook resolves per-token. Applying core's `dead` interacts with yze-combat's tracker and with the system's duplicateCombatantOnCombatStart setting (module/alienrpg.mjs:503), where one actor owns two combatants.
- 合并自：broken-state-and-rally, broken-and-dead-states, broken-state-and-death-rolls, broken-state-and-death-clock

### `P1` Cross-actor panic and stress dispatch: witnesses, cascades, and component damage
- `panic-trigger-bus` · 价值 5/5 · 成本 4/5 · 现状 **完全没有**
- **规则要求**：A Panic Roll is forced when you witness a PC become Broken, first see a horrifying Xenomorph, have a terrifying one come Adjacent, or witness a PC's panic response. Scream forces an immediate panic roll on every friendly PC in the zone; Frenzy does the same for everyone who witnesses the rampage. Stress rises from a nearby NPC panicking, a second or third full-auto burst, becoming Fatigued, and — in space combat — from minor component damage, with major damage or a disabled ship forcing panic rolls instead.
- **今天要人手做**：Every panic roll begins with the GM saying 'everyone who saw that, roll panic' and each player clicking their own button. On every component-damage draw the GM walks the crew list clicking each person's Stress +. Nothing notices a downed ally, a first sighting, an adjacent xenomorph, a cascading Scream or Frenzy, a second full-auto burst, or that a character just became Fatigued.
- **接入点**：A module trigger bus fanning out to alienrpgActor.prototype.rollStress, entered from Hooks.on('createActiveEffect'), Hooks.on('createItem') on spacecraft-crit, and the shared rollStress wrapper
- **风险**：Zone membership is the load-bearing unknown — the ruleset is written in zones and the system models none, so any automatic radius is a guess that will sometimes panic the wrong people; keep the GM in the loop by default. Line-of-sight checks against token.vision are expensive and unreliable for unlinked tokens and for GM-hidden tokens. Cascades can recurse (a Scream triggers a panic that rolls another Scream) and must be depth-limited. First-sighting flags stored per actor break when a GM re-uses one creature actor for many tokens. The full-auto stress gain needs the burst-count model from the combat theme; without it this feature can only charge a flat per-roll cost.
- 合并自：stress-and-panic-triggers, panic-triggers-and-contagion, witness-panic-cascade, panic-zone-cascade, ship-crew-stress-panic-chain

### `P1` Rest to shed Stress, and a real way out of panic · 📤上游PR

- **规则要求**：Each stretch of rest in a safe area reduces Stress Level by 1 and lets the character remove one stress response of their choice per point removed; no skill rolls may be made while resting and an interrupted rest does not count. - **今天要人手做**：Recovery between scenes is entirely off-system: the player clicks the stress minus button the agreed number of times and right-clicks off whichever response icons the group decides, with nothing enforcing one-per-point. 'Safe area' and 'interrupted' are GM calls with no system representation; enforcing them automatically will misfire, so keep them declarative. checkAndEndPanic's early return for non-characters means synthetics and crewed craft need the module's own path rather than a wrapper. A stretch timer without a clock is vapour — gate that half behind the clock landing.

The governing attribute can be swapped by rule, GM or talent.
- **今天要人手做**：To roll Strength + MANIPULATION the player works out the difference between two attribute mods in their head and types it into Base Mod. Marking a helper's action spent touches the combat tracker and can collide with token-action-hud-alien or yze-combat. The negotiating-position table is GM-judgement content — over-automating it will annoy tables that adjudicate it narratively, so keep every factor a checkbox with a visible running total, never an auto-detected value.

- **规则要求**：Evolved (primary): nine ordered steps — 2D6 human or android; base attributes 2/2/2/2 with androids adding +3 to two; Origin (2D6 super-nation then D6 twice, plus 3 random general skill points); Early Life; Life Event; 2 pre-career attribute points placed before applying; career application (unpushable, 0 rejected / 1 recruit / 2+ a Favor, with Pull A Fast One after a failure); 1-3 terms, each beyond the first costing 1 attribute point, Kid capped at one; psych evaluation; final touches. 1e (branch): ten steps with a career key attribute reaching 5, three key skills reaching 3, 14 attribute points, 10 skill points, one starting talent from three, a buddy and a rival, and a career cash die.
- **今天要人手做**：The whole life path is run on paper against the imported journal: click each draw link one at a time, keep running attribute/skill/cash/psych totals on scratch paper, count terms out loud, subtract 1 per extra term with nothing warning if Empathy drops to 1, then hand-type four attributes and twelve skills into a sheet that opens with every value at 0 — and paste the narrative results into a Notes box because there is no field for them.
- **接入点**：ApplicationV2 wizard launched from Hooks.on("renderActorDirectory"), committing with one actor.update() + createEmbeddedDocuments and a lifePath setFlag
- **风险**：Once-per-campaign flow, so the cost/benefit is the worst in the theme — it must not be built before the registries it consumes. Holding a half-built PC in wizard memory means a browser refresh loses everything unless state is checkpointed to a flag. Seeding from preCreateActor conflicts with any other module that seeds actors. The 1e/Evolved branch keys on a SYSTEM setting the user may flip mid-campaign, which would change the wizard's rules under existing characters. Babele translates every drawn result, so the whole wizard is only as safe as the id-keyed payload registry beneath it.

### `P3` Career gear tables and money that can actually be added up · ⚠方案存疑
- `career-gear-and-cash` · 价值 2/5 · 成本 3/5 · 现状 **只有字段**
- **规则要求**：On recruitment roll once on your career's gear table, re-rolling anything you already have; each term end repeats one gear roll plus one paycheck roll on the career's formula, from D6x$50 to D6x$500, added to the PC's money. Every weapon arrives with two full reloads; every PC also gets clothing, a signature item that is tiny and never encumbers, and the career's starting cash.
- **今天要人手做**：The player rolls on a printed gear table, hunts each item by name in the compendium and drags it in, tracking duplicates themselves. Then they roll a d6, multiply by the career's rate from the book, and retype the cash total by hand — parsing '$1,234.00' back into a number in their head first, because the formatter already mangled the field.
- **接入点**：Module-authored RollTables with type:"document" results feeding createEmbeddedDocuments("Item"), plus a numeric cash shadow flag round-tripping through the sheet's currency formatter
- **风险**：Authoring nine gear tables by hand against pack content is transcription work that rots on a corerules update. The cash shadow flag and the string field WILL diverge the moment a user hand-edits the box; the shadow must re-sync from the parsed string on read, treating the visible field as authoritative when they disagree. A StringField→NumberField upstream migration would break the module's shim, so the version probe must cover it. Dedupe by source UUID fails for items the user created by hand rather than dragging from the pack.
- 合并自：career-gear-and-paycheck, career-chargen-tables, career-data-registry-and-awards (award half)
