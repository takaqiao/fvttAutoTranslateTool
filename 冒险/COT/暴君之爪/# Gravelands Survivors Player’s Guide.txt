# Gravelands Survivors: Player’s Guide

# Introduction

You will be playing Gravelands Survivors, a survival adventure set in the undead infested Gravelands. In this guide you will find some background information on the location and adventure, as well as suggestions for your character options. If you have any questions or need help, please ask your Dungeon Master (DM)!

# Where and When on Golarion?

Our adventure takes place in Yua’s Hope, a hamlet in the country formerly known as Lastwall. Lastwall was a nation founded some 900 years ago at the end of the Shining Crusade with a singular purpose: to be the shield against evil. The brave people of Lastwall were ever vigilant towards the Orcs in the lands to the north, and most importantly kept watch over the imprisoned lich-king Tar-Baphon, who they also call the Whispering Tyrant, and his undead hordes.

For over 900 years their tireless watch endured, keeping the orcs and undead at bay, and the Whispering Tyrant locked in his tower of Gallowspire. Until it all came crashing down some 6 years ago, when the Whispering Tyrant was finally able to break free. After he destroyed Vigil, the capital city of Lastwall, his undead hordes washed over the country like a tidalwave of death, transforming what was once the noble state of Lastwall into the haunted wasteland now known as *the Gravelands*.

Yua’s Hope met the same fate as so many other unfortunate settlements. You remember that night well. Less than 12 hours after the first panicked survivors had arrived at Yua’s Hope bringing stories not even your worst nightmares could rival, the undead reached your once peaceful town. You, along with several others, fled into the crypts underneath your local temple of Iomedae, sealing the secret door behind you.

Here, in these cramped crypt chambers, you and your group of survivors have held out for over 6 years now, making what passes these days for a life in the Gravelands. Only very rarely do any of you venture outside, since secrecy is the only thing that is keeping your group safe, and you go many months without seeing the sun. Your town of Yua’s Hope is now a silent ruin, and only the dead walk there now.

# The Survivors

Thankfully you are not alone. Your little community in the crypt consists of the following people:

**Dame Talia Gilder** (human woman). This 70 year old is a former captain of the Knights of Ozem, now long retired. It has been her steadfast courage and experienced guidance that has helped keep your group together and keeps people from losing hope that one day they might be rescued, and she is a natural leader for the group.

![dametalia.png](attachment:f11315cb-c410-4bf0-a9ed-5172e5ccdabf:dametalia.png)

![constan.png](attachment:4cb5c914-ea28-42c1-abb1-3b8bb9c4af3b:constan.png)

**Constan Russi** (human man). It is because of Constan that you all survived. Not only did he lead you all to the secret crypts beneath his temple, but as a devout cleric of Iomedae he has been using his divine magic to create food and water for the survivors for over 6 years. While not the most flavourful food, it’s better than starving or braving the undead to try to scavenge in the wasteland.

**Rokvel Hardbrow** (dwarf woman). In happier days, Rokvel was the town’s blacksmith, specializing in the crafting of breastplates for crusading knights. Of all the survivors she’s the least bothered about living underground. She enjoys logic puzzles but has little patience for those who can’t (or won’t) carry their own weight.

![rokvel.png](attachment:2b9561b6-a06f-4833-bdab-503449fa0ab6:rokvel.png)

![garaag.png](attachment:9949403a-c492-4293-b4cc-770a998c20fd:garaag.png)

**Garaag Sunbrand** (half-orc man). He left his tribe in the orc lands of the Hold Of Belkzen many years ago, though the people of Lastwall always treated him with some suspicion. He is known for his standoffishness and short temper (a trait made worse by constant hunger). Since entering the crypts, he has struggled to connect with the other survivors. However, the they have come to rely on Garaag’s strength whenever heavy lifting needs doing, which brings him great pride.

**Jono, Yiluva, Retsnav** and **Retsnov Brushpaw** (Kitsune family). Jono and his life partner Yiluva have ranged Lastwall all their life. When Tar-Baphon’s forces arrived they and their children warned as many settlements as they could, including Yua’s Hope. For their bravery they were offered sanctuary with your group. They spend most of their time in their fox forms in order to consume less food and communicate primarily by crude sign language.

![jono.png](attachment:928ab445-f5b3-45c0-bb34-d80d46c14680:jono.png)

![marko.png](attachment:96078ff4-a10c-424a-931b-eb47ebad5a56:marko.png)

**Marko Blightender** (human man). Both of his parents were Knights of Ozem who were in the field when the Whispering Tyrant’s forces laid siege to Lastwall. While most of you assume the worst, he still holds out hope that one day he will see them again. He was just 10 when you entered the crypt, but now he is a broody teenager.

**The Querulous Quartet: Alyzo, Crasian, Kleb** and **Bicky** (group of performers). This traveling troupe happened to be playing in Yua’s Hope when the undead attacked. Alyzo is a human man (singer-songwriter), Crasian a halfling man (vocalist and flutist), and Kleb (banjoist) and Bicky (percussion) are both goblin women. Their signature melodies have buoyed your group’s spirits over the years, though they have to be careful not to play too often lest their music betrays their location to the undead roaming aboveground.

![troupe.png](attachment:d3ba6530-7b66-4cef-adc5-7fd4e34c63a4:troupe.png)

![hurdauw.png](attachment:bd9beb42-9434-4745-ba0a-c39c374940ce:hurdauw.png)

**Hurdauw** and **Jystolo Pulsoma** (human woman + male child). The former chef at the Hope’s Breath Inn, she discovered her no-good, cheat of a husband had run off with another woman the same night Tar-Baphon’s forces attacked. Not long after, she realized she was pregnant. Six years on, Hurdauw still grapples with the fact that the father of her son Jystolo is likely dead. Though she is a kind and hard-working individual, her nervous energy often rubs off on the other survivors.

# Character Options

Below follow some guidelines regarding the various character options you have. As with all things, if you want to choose something with the Uncommon or Rare trait, check with your DM first. Character builds that appear to be pro-undead are not an option for this adventure.

You will start at level 1, but halfway through the adventure you will level up to level 2. We will use the Free Archetype variant rule, meaning you get 1 extra feat slot at level 2 which you can only use for an Archetype Feat.

## Ancestries & Heritages

Any of the common ancestries and heritages are acceptable. For uncommon or rare ancestries, check with the DM. The only ancestries that are a clear no-go are any undead related ancestries or heritages (such as Damphir or Skeleton). The people of Yua’s Hope are simple townsfolk and the town is at least 60 miles from the coast, so very unusual or aquatic options are likely a bad choice.

- https://2e.aonprd.com/Ancestries.aspx

## Backgrounds

Any of the common backgrounds should work for this adventure.

- https://2e.aonprd.com/Backgrounds.aspx

## Classes

This adventure features a mix of roleplay, combat and exploration so all classes are appropriate. Some classes will do particularly well, such as classes that excel at navigation and foraging (Survival) like Rangers or Druids, or classes that are extra effective against undead like Clerics.

- https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx

## Faiths

The people of Lastwall followed a variety of faiths and gods. The main faith of this region was the faith of Iomedae, the goddess of Honor, Justice, Rulership and Valor. Pharasma, goddess of Birth, Death and Fate was also a popular choice due to her hatred of the undead. Of course it is also OK if your character isn’t particularly devout or interested in religion.

Deities or faiths that are friendly to undead such as Urgathoa are not acceptable choices.

## Skills

At least one player character should be trained in Survival, and at least one should be trained in Diplomacy. You’ll encounter a lot of undead, so Undead Lore or Religion are good choices as well if you want to know what you’re up against.

- https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx

## Archetypes

We will be using the Free Archetype variant rules, meaning you get one extra feat slot at level 2 which can only be used for Archetype feats (including Dedication feats). In some cases (like the Flexible Spellcaster) the archetype must be chosen at level 1, in which case you can use your 2nd level Free Archetype feat slot for it.

- https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx

## Equipment

You were able to flee with some of your own possessions, so you start with a total wealth of 15 gold pieces. You can assume your character has spent some or all of that on equipment such as weapons, armor, consumables or other items prior to going into hiding. Any gold you don’t spend you can keep, though this adventure does not feature any shopping opportunities. Keep in mind you did your shopping 6 years ago, so any perishable items have long since expired.

For any items you choose, make sure they do not exceed your level, and if they are Uncommon or Rare you must first check with your DM.

- https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx

# Fleshing Out Your Character

Consider for yourself the following questions:

- Were you born and raised in the town of Yua’s Hope? Or were you simply a visitor caught in the wrong place at the wrong time?
- How old were you the night the undead came to Yua’s Hope? What circumstances allowed you to take refuge in the crypt? Were you forced to leave anyone behind?
- Do you carry a special keepsake to remind you of your life before the undead arrived? Or did you have to leave it behind in the rush to get to safety? What is it?
- What is your relationship with the other survivors? Which one of them do you like the most? The least?
- What do you look forward to doing once all this is over? Or have you already given up hope of ever seeing the sun again?
