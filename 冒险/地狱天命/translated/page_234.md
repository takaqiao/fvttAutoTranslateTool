## Page 234 — 小规模冲突纽带（续）

#### 秘奥操练（ESOTERIC DRILLS）　[1 个动作]

**秘奥之物 · 操控 · 小规模冲突**

**先决条件** 你具有——「**利用弱点**」（Exploit Vulnerability）——动作。

你已训练你的部队以识别无数生物的弱点为他们装备他们自己的秘奥之物并操练他们使用这些。如果你已成功对一只生物——「**利用弱点**」——你部队的攻击性能力便会以你的一击所做的方式施加你的——「**死灭弱点**」（mortal weakness）——的弱点。本纽带不能与——「**共享弱点**」（Share Weakness，《探路者暗黑典藏》45）——同时使用。这一益处在你的——「**利用弱点**」——结束之时或者你再次使用——「**秘奥操练**」之时终止以先发生者为准。

#### 调查小队（INVESTIGATIVE SQUAD）

**先决条件** 你具有——「**拟定计谋**」（Devise a Stratagem）——动作。

你已在调查与演绎的精妙艺术上操练你的部队。该部队已学会发现与你的案件相关的线索并在你所采用的计谋之中追随你的领率。你的部队总是共享你自——「**追查线索**」（Pursue a Lead）——所获的加值。此外如果你——「**拟定计谋**」——并选择攻击计谋直至你下一回合开始目标对你部队所使用的任何攻击性能力的豁免承受一项——−1 状态惩罚并且这些攻击性能力无视等同于你等级一半的目标抗性。如果你——「**拟定计谋**」——但选择技能计谋你的部队对其在你下一回合开始之前下一次智力、感知或魅力为基的技能检定或者涉及目标的——「**察觉**」检定获得同样的加值。

#### 防毒卫士（POISON GUARD）

**先决条件** 「**强韧**」豁免大师

你已给予你的部队一场应对毒素以及疾病的速成课并以特殊餐食补充以增强免疫力。你的部队获得一项——+2 状态加值于抗疾病以及毒的豁免以及等于你等级一半的毒抗。如果你在——「**工艺**」之中受训这毒抗增加 1；若为专家则增加 2；若为大师则增加 3；若为传奇则增加 5。

#### 第二层皮肤（SECOND SKIN）

**先决条件** 任一类盔甲或——「**无甲防御**」——之中的大师熟练

你已操练你的部队成员以最好维持穿戴以及在其盔甲之中机动的方式尤其是在与其他部队队形交锋之时。通过花费 10 分钟调整并修缮这一群人的盔甲你的部队获得等同于你等级的临时生命值持续 8 小时。此外这部队对其——AC——以及对抗其他部队的攻击性能力所造成的钝击、穿刺或挥砍伤害的反射豁免获得一项——+2 物品加值。

#### 风格挑衅（STYLISH GAMBIT）　[1 个动作]

**虚张声势 · 小规模冲突**

**先决条件** 你具有——「**潇洒**」——职业特性。

你已教导你的部队以风范与优雅可以使最莽撞的战士脱颖而出。他们已获得一点你的虚张声势以及利用——「**潇洒**」——的有限能力。你尝试一项你具有——「**虚张声势**」特征的动作以你的部队与你共行；这应当是——GM 裁量之下一支部队可合理完成的事。只有你需要尝试任何相关的检定但如果这动作涉及移动你的部队便与你一同移动。其 DC——必须至少等同于你部队等级的一项标准 DC。如果你成功你不只获得——「**潇洒**」——你的部队也获得——「**潇洒**」——授予它与——「**潇洒**」——授予你的速度同样的状态加值。此外你的部队所使用的下一项——1 个或——2 个动作的攻击性能力造成由——「**精准一击**」（precise strike）——授予你的额外精准伤害（如有）。一旦动作完成你的部队便失去——「**潇洒**」。你的部队不能每回合超过一次添加这额外精准伤害。

#### 不畏不惧（UNFLINCHING）

**先决条件** 「**意志**」豁免大师

你已操练你的部队以抵御他们敌人所能拿出的最差的东西而岿然不动；你的惊人意志力已濡染他们使他们能抖落恐惧以及其他试图情绪化影响他们的尝试。如果你的部队在抗一项情绪效应的豁免上掷出成功它反而极佳成功。如果你的部队在抗一项情绪效应的豁免上掷出极佳失败它反而只是失败。这些修正也适用于溃散检定。在其回合结束之时这部队把其——「**惊惧**」状态的值减少 2（而非 1）。

#### 难挡之潮（UNSTOPPABLE TIDE）

**先决条件** 「**运动**」大师

你已让你的部队经常进行严酷的徒手健身操以及其他锻炼也为他们装备攀爬装备以及类似之物；没有任何屏障能阻止他们墙壁、护城河以及类似之物只是他们通过机动的又一种战场地形。你的部队获得 10 英尺的攀爬以及游泳速度或者如果已经具有这些速度把其增加 5 英尺。

#### 警惕守卫（VIGILANT GUARDS）

**先决条件** 「**察觉**」专家

你已训练你的部队以警觉任何危险以便它总是警戒威胁并一声招呼便能准备战斗。只要你领率你的部队你除了你所选的任何其他探索战术之外也获得——「**侦察**」（Scouting）——的益处并为先攻骰授予一项——+2 状况加值（而不是通常的——+1）。

ESOTERIC DRILLS [one-action]
ESOTERICA
MANIPULATE
SKIRMISH
Prerequisites You have the Exploit Vulnerability action.

You've trained your troops to recognize countless creatures'
weaknesses, outfitted them with esoterica of their own,
and drilled them in their use. If you've successfully
Exploited Vulnerability against a creature, your troop's
offensive abilities now apply the weakness from your
mortal weakness the same way your Strikes do. This
bond can't be used at the same time as Share Weakness
(Pathfinder Dark Archive 45). This benefit ends when your
Exploit Vulnerability ends or you use Esoteric Drills again,
whichever comes first.

INVESTIGATIVE SQUAD
Prerequisites You have the Devise a Stratagem action.

You've drilled your troop in the finer arts of investigation
and deduction. The troop has learned to spot clues related
to your cases and take after your lead in stratagems you
employ. Your troop always shares your bonuses from
Pursue a Lead. In addition, if you Devise a Stratagem
and choose the attack stratagem, until the beginning of
your next turn, the target takes a –1 status penalty to
saving throws against any offensive ability your troop
uses, and the offensive abilities ignore an amount of the
target's resistance equal to half your level. If you Devise a
Stratagem and instead choose skill stratagem, your troop
gains the same bonus to its next Intelligence-, Wisdom-, or
Charisma-based skill check or Perception check involving
the target before the start of your next turn.

POISON GUARD
Prerequisites master in Fortitude saves

You've given your troop a crash course in dealing with
toxins and diseases, supplemented with special meals
to boost immunity. Your troop gains a +2 status bonus
to saving throws against disease and poison as well as
poison resistance equal to half your level. The poison
resistance increases by 1 if you're trained in Crafting, 2
if you're an expert, 3 if you're a master, and 5 if you're
legendary.

SECOND SKIN
Prerequisites master proficiency in any type of armor or
unarmored defense

You've drilled your troop units in how best to maintain, don,
and maneuver in their armor—especially when clashing with
other troop formations. By spending 10 minutes adjusting
and repairing the group's armor, your troop gains temporary
Hit Points equal to your level for 8 hours. In addition,
the troop gains a +2 item bonus to its AC and to Reflex
saves against other troops' offensive abilities that deal
bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage.

STYLISH GAMBIT [one-action]
BRAVADO
SKIRMISH
Prerequisites You have the panache class feature.

You've taught your troop that style and grace can make
the most reckless combatants come out on top. They've
gained a bit of your bravado and the limited ability to take
advantage of panache. You attempt an action that has the
bravado trait for you, with your troop acting alongside you;
this should be something that a troop could reasonably
accomplish at the GM's discretion. Only you need to
attempt any associated check, but if the action involves
movement, your troop moves along with you. The DC must
be at least equal to a standard DC of your troop's level.
If you succeed, not only do you gain panache, your troop
also gains panache, granting it an identical status bonus to
its Speeds that panache grants you. In addition, the next
1- or 2-action offensive ability your troop uses deals an
additional amount of precision damage granted to you by
precise strike (if any). Once the action is completed, your
troop loses panache. Your troop can't add this additional
precision damage more than once per round.

UNFLINCHING
Prerequisites master in Will saves

You've drilled your troops to stand steadfast against
the worst their foes can bring to bear; your incredible
willpower has rubbed off on them, allowing them to shrug
off fear and other attempts to emotionally influence them.
If your troop rolls a success on a saving throw against an
emotion effect, it critically succeeds instead. If your troop
rolls a critical failure on a saving throw against an emotion
effect, it fails instead. These modifications also apply to
rout checks. At the end of its turn, the troop reduces the
value of its frightened condition by 2 (rather than 1).

UNSTOPPABLE TIDE
Prerequisites master in Athletics

You've put your troop's units through intense calisthenics
and other exercise regularly as well as outfitted them with
climbing gear and the like; no barrier can stop them, with
walls, moats, and the like being just additional battlefield
terrain for them to maneuver through. Your troop gains
climb and swim Speeds of 10 feet or increases each of these
Speeds by 5 feet if they already have it.

VIGILANT GUARDS
Prerequisites expert in Perception

You've trained your troop to be alert to any dangers so that it's
always on the lookout for threats and ready for combat at a
moment's notice. As long as you lead your troop, you also gain
the benefits of Scouting in addition to any other exploration
tactic you choose, and you grant a +2 circumstance bonus to
initiative rolls (instead of the usual +1).
