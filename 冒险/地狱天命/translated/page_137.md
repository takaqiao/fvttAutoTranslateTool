### 空中防御（AERIAL DEFENSES）　复杂情况

**触发** 玩家角色首次达到 15 AP。
**克服** DC 35 杂技以闪避来袭的火血浪潮；DC 37 求生以利用自然地形作为对抗空中突袭的屏障；或 DC 39 隐秘，在巨龙之眼俯视之下硬是隐身。

燃狱之门最为强悍的守卫之一是一对**邪魔之龙**。当玩家角色触发了切利亚斯将军们足够强的警觉，这对龙便升空、对玩家角色与他们的部队喷出**龙焰**洪流。
- **极成**　玩家角色躲避空袭的本事甚为高超，两只龙在半空相撞，其中一只直接砸在了切利亚斯将军指挥所上。玩家角色获得 **1 BP**。
- **成功**　火焰偏离目标，只起到威吓作用。玩家角色与他们的部队避开火焰，未受伤害。
- **失败**　火焰暴露了玩家角色的位置。队伍累积 **1 AP**，玩家角色与他们的部队各受 **8d6 火焰伤害**（DC 36 基础反射豁免）。
- **极失**　火焰焚烧并照亮玩家角色，让全场都看到。队伍改累积 **2 AP**，玩家角色与他们的部队各受 **16d6 火焰伤害**（DC 36 基础反射豁免）。

### 警戒线挑战（PICKET CHALLENGE）　复杂情况

**触发** 玩家角色首次达到 5 AP。
**克服** DC 35 切利亚斯学识或地狱学识以给出正确口令完全绕过警戒哨；DC 37 欺骗以让守卫相信他们是从沉言林其他地方回来防御此地的小规模冲突兵；或 DC 39 社交或战争学识以伪装为来自伊塞尔德河方向的非正规增援。

玩家角色突然撞上一个加强警戒岗，那里设有**警铃号角**系统。攻击守卫易获成功，但他们会在被打死前吹响号角警示切利亚斯人玩家角色的接近。**此处需要更细致的方法**，否则必定引发更多怀疑。
- **极成**　玩家角色技艺高超，守卫向前方示意更易放行玩家进入切利亚斯营区其他区域。玩家角色获得 **1 BP**。
- **成功**　玩家角色顺利通过警戒岗，可继续前行。

AERIAL DEFENSES　COMPLICATION
Trigger The PCs reach 15 AP for the first time.
Overcome DC 35 Acrobatics to dodge out of the way of the incoming wave of fire and blood, DC 37 Survival to use the natural terrain as a shield against the aerial onslaught, or DC 39 Stealth to simply hide from view despite the draconic eyes boring down upon the area

Among the most puissant of the Inferno Gate's defenders are a pair of diabolic dragons. When the PCs raise too much of an alarm from the Chelaxian generals, this pair launches into the air and strafes the PCs and their troops with gouts of dragonfire. 

Critical Success The PCs are so proficient at avoiding the aerial raiders that the two dragons experience a collision in midair, one of them crash-landing on a Chelaxian general's command station. The PCs gain 1 BP.
Success The flames are off-target and only serve as an intimidating display. The PCs and their troops avoid the flames and take no damage.
Failure The flames highlight the PCs' position. The party accrues 1 AP, and each PC and their troops take 8d6 fire damage (DC 36 basic Reflex save).
Critical Failure The flames burn and illuminate the PCs for all to see. The party instead accrues 2 AP, and each PC and their troops take 16d6 fire damage (DC 36 basic Reflex save).

PICKET CHALLENGE　COMPLICATION
Trigger The PCs reach 5 AP for the first time.
Overcome DC 35 Cheliax Lore or Hell Lore to give the proper code phrases to bypass the guard station entirely, DC 37 Deception to convince the guards that they're skirmishers back to defend the area from elsewhere in the Whisperwood, or DC 39 Society or Warfare Lore to pass as irregular reinforcements from the Iseld River

The PCs suddenly run into a reinforced guard post that has a system of alarm horns. Attacking the guards would be easily successful, but not before they sounded their horns to alert the Chelaxians to the PCs' approach. A more subtle approach is required here or else more suspicion will surely be raised.

Critical Success The PCs are so proficient that the guards signal ahead that the PCs are to be given access more readily to other areas of the Chelaxian camps. The PCs gain 1 BP.
Success The PCs bypass the guard post without incident and can continue on their way.
