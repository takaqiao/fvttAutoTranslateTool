### 地狱骑士龙骑兵分队（Hellknight Dragoon Squad）

某些逃犯是如此危险或人数众多以至于需要一整支能骑着追逐猎物并在拥挤的定居点之中下马发起追击的地狱骑士分队。这些地狱骑士使用链枷一如其使用恐惧的熟练以两者制伏并拘束敌人。

#### 地狱骑士龙骑兵分队（HELLKNIGHT DRAGOON SQUAD）　【**生物 9**】

**少见 · 庞大 · 人类 · 类人 · 部队**

**察觉** +18
**语言** 通用语、魔鬼语
**技能** 运动 +20，威吓 +20，宗教 +18，社会 +14，求生 +16

**力 +7，敏 +1，体 +4，智 +2，感 +3，魅 +2**

**AC** 28；**强韧** +21，**反射** +15，**意志** +18；抗恐惧效应豁免——+2 状况

**HP** 150（4 段）；**阈值** 100（3 段），50（2 段）；**弱点** 区域伤害 6，飞溅伤害 6

**断其弱链**（Break the Weak Link）（灵光、情绪、恐惧、心智）10 英尺。只要龙骑兵分队至少保有 3 段灵光之内所有敌方部队对抗情绪效应的豁免承受一项——−2 状况惩罚。

**部队防御**（《探路者 战吼！》216）

**以镣相缚**（Bind Them in Chains）　[反应]；**触发** 一只与龙骑兵分队相邻的生物或部队获得——「**惊惧**」状态或者一支相邻部队降至 2 段；**效果** 感知到其敌人士气动摇龙骑兵以其镣铐猛甩。这触发的生物必须尝试一项——DC 25 反射豁免。

**极佳成功** 该生物不受影响。

**成功** 该生物——「**笨拙** 1」——直至分队下一回合结束。

**失败** 该生物——「**笨拙** 2」——直至分队下一回合结束并且——「**禁制**」——直至它——「**脱出**」（DC 25）。

**极佳失败** 一如失败但——「**束缚**」——替代——「**禁制**」。

**速度** 25 英尺；部队移动（《探路者 战吼！》217）

**箭矢齐射**（Arrow Volley）　[2 个动作]（魔法） 龙骑兵抽出弓并以一道远程攻击的形式发射箭矢。这齐射是——80 英尺内的一道——10 英尺爆裂造成 4d8 穿刺伤害（DC 25 基础反射豁免）。当这部队降至 2 段或更少之时这区域缩减为一道——5 英尺爆裂。

**畏惧锁链**（Fear the Chain）　[1 个动作]（专注、情绪、恐惧、心智）；**频率** 每回合一次；**效果** 龙骑兵尝试对一道——15 英尺飞散之内所有敌人——「**威慑**」——以一项威吓检定的结果与每一目标的意志 DC——比较。该分队若未与一名目标讲同一种语言不承受惩罚。龙骑兵分队可能对每一目标获得不同程度的成功而一名目标仅在分队对其的检定极佳失败之时对本能力暂时免疫。

**链枷突击**（Flail Assault）　[1 个动作] 至 [3 个动作]（魔法）；**频率** 每回合一次；**效果** 龙骑兵以他们的链枷猛甩以对一道——5 英尺飞散之内每一敌人发起一次近战攻击（DC 25 基础反射豁免）。伤害取决于动作数量。

### 链锁断裂（BROKEN CHAINS）

「**锁链骑士团**」——近来经历了一场分裂大约半数在地狱火危机开端之时这骑士团在——「**阿尔泰雷因要塞**」（Citadel Altaerein）——的溃败之后表示效忠于切利亚斯。异议者们坚持他们的组织保持一支非政治化的秩序之力遂脱离。他们目前在寇兰廷力争立足。同时——「**镣铐骑士团**」（如忠于切利亚斯的一派目前自称）——较少追猎逃犯而是专注于擒获安多兰的战士并以链枷与锁链折断其勇气。

Hellknight Dragoon Squad
Some fugitives are so dangerous or numerous that they warrant a whole
Hellknight squadron who can ride after quarry and dismount to give
chase in crowded settlements. These Hellknights wield flails as capably as
they wield fear, using both to subdue and restrain foes.

HP 150 (4 segments); Thresholds 100 (3 segments), 50 (2 segments); Weaknesses

Break the Weak Link (aura, emotion, fear, mental) 10 feet. As long as the dragoon
squad has at least 3 segments, all enemy troops within the aura take a –2
circumstance penalty to saves against emotion effects.

Troop Defenses (Pathfinder Battlecry! 216)

Bind Them in Chains [reaction] Trigger A creature or troop adjacent to the dragoon squad
gains the frightened condition, or an adjacent troop is reduced to 2 segments;
Effect Sensing their foes' faltering morale, the dragoons lash out with their
manacles. The triggering creature must attempt a DC 25 Reflex save.

Critical Success The creature is unaffected.

Success The creature becomes clumsy 1 until the end of the squad's next turn.

Failure The creature becomes clumsy 2 until the end of the squad's next turn
and is immobilized until it Escapes (DC 25).

Critical Failure As failure, but restrained instead of immobilized.

Speed 25 feet; troop movement (Pathfinder Battlecry! 217)

Arrow Volley [two-actions] (magical) The dragoons draw bows and fire arrows as a
ranged attack. This volley is a 10-foot burst within 80 feet that deals 4d8
piercing damage (DC 25 basic Reflex save). When
the troop is reduced to 2 or fewer segments, this
area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

Fear the Chain [one-action] (concentrate, emotion, fear, mental)
Frequency once per round; Effect The dragoons
attempt to Demoralize all foes in a 15-foot
emanation, comparing the result of a single
Intimidation check to each target's Will DC.
The squad doesn't take the penalty if they
don't speak the same language as a target.
It's possible for the dragoon squad to get a
different degree of success for each target, and
a target is temporarily immune to this ability only
if the squad critically fails the check against them.

Flail Assault [one-action] to [three-actions] (magical) Frequency once
per round; Effect The dragoons lash out with their
flails in a melee attack against each enemy in a 5-foot
emanation (DC 25 basic Reflex save). The damage
depends on the number of actions.
[one-action] 1d8+2 bludgeoning
