### A. 盐源庄园（Brinemidden Manor）

这座**古老的庄园**曾属于**琳奈塔·海凿**的**舅公梅赫兹·武林**（Myhez Wryn）——**一位成功的商人兼藏书家**。在他的**晚年**，梅赫兹**愈发古怪**，并**花费了大量积蓄为他的庄园下魔法附魔**。他过世之后，**庄园声名渐传为闹鬼**，**最终被弃**。然而琳奈塔知道，**庄园上的某道魔法守护可屏蔽一切侦测魔法**——这让此地**成为逃亡者藏身的理想之所**。

Having defeated Liabriz, the PCs and prisoners are briefly safe, as reinforcements take several days to mobilize. Silent Silhouette greets them with grim tidings upon their return: Tessa Fairwind's initial assault on Corentyn has devolved into a stalemate. Her swift attack stymied, she's blockaded the port. In isolation, this strategy could work, but Cheliax will eventually send reinforcements to break the siege. Silent Silhouette congratulates the PCs on their prison break but bitterly admits it will mean nothing unless they can secure more allies.

Luckily, the PCs rescue of Zeramial presents a new opportunity. The royal elf pledges that if the PCs escort him home, he'll rally an army to aid Corentyn. Urdun sees promise in this plan and urges the PCs to escort Zeramial north rather than return to Corentyn immediately.

Cheered by this ray of hope, Silent Silhouette leaves immediately to bring news of the new plan to Linetta. She urges the PCs to be swift, however, for she guesses that the stalemate will last for two months at most.

With business settled, Urdun urges everyone to rest. Tomorrow is another long march to reach Brinemidden Manor, which is the prearranged hideout Linetta chose to shelter the escaped prisoners for a few weeks.

A. Brinemidden Manor
This ancient manor once belonged to Linetta Seacarver's great-uncle Myhez Wryn, a successful merchant and book collector. In his golden years, Myhez became increasingly erratic and spent much of his savings to enchant his estate. After he passed away, the manor gained a haunted reputation, and the estate was abandoned. However, Linetta knows that one of the magical wards on the manor shields it from all kinds of detection magic, making it an ideal place for fugitives to lie low.
