# 鲜肉藏身处（FRESH MEAT HIDEOUT）

**「泪痕之子」集团的新近招募者，居住在灰烬区一栋残存建筑的躯壳中**。**他们的头目，泽兰·灰齿（Zeran Graytooth，邪恶魔鬼裔奈非利姆人类游荡者 12 级）**，**以如下方式训练新人**：**让他们挑选一栋房屋蹲点，并在一个月内不被卡特尔其他成员发现地存活下来**。**这种挑战不仅锻炼新人的偷摸本事**，**还逼着他们彼此迅速结下亲密的纽带**。**泽兰借此经验向他们灌输：「泪痕之子」便是他们一直渴望却从未拥有的家**——**并依赖这些纽带把他们困在组织里**。

**找出泪痕之子**，**需要一位玩家角色尝试 DC 38 检定来收集信息、用地下世界学识对该团体回忆知识、或追踪（Track）**。**若失败，玩家角色可再次尝试找出泪痕之子**。**掌握关于这一团体的情报可使定位他们更容易**。**每运用下列信息之一搜索泪痕之子，DC 减 1**（**此 DC 减值可叠加**）：

- **泪痕之子与塔雷斯·伊维奇有关联**。
- **泽兰·灰齿领导这一团体**。
- **最近有一批新近招募的成员**。
- **该卡特尔位于灰烬区**。

Fresh Meat Hideout
The new recruits of the Sons of the Tear collective live in the remaining husk of a building in the District of Ashes. Their leader, Zeran Graytooth (nefarious hellspawn nephilim human rogue 12), trains recruits by challenging them to choose a building to squat in and survive for a month without being spotted by another member of the cartel. Not only does this challenge hone the recruits' skulduggery, but it also pressures them to quickly form close connections with each other. Zeran uses this experience to claim that the Sons of the Tear are the family that the members always needed and leans on these connections to trap them in the organization.

Finding the Sons of the Tear requires a PC to attempt a DC 38 check to Gather Information, Recall Knowledge about the group with Underworld Lore, or Track. PCs can attempt to find the Sons of the Tear again if they fail. Knowing information about the group makes it easier to locate them. For each piece of the following information used while searching for the Sons of the Tear, reduce the DCs by 1 (this DC reduction is cumulative).

- The Sons of the Tear are connected to Tareth Ivici.
- Zeran Graytooth leads the gang.
- There's a recent set of new recruits.
- The cartel is located in the District of Ashes.
