### 塔尔曼铎（Talmandor）安多兰的天界守护神

承以华美羽毛的双翼塔尔曼铎是一位强大的天界使具有类人与禽鸟混合的特征。他大部分时间花在遨游——「**涅槃**」（Nirvana）——之上周期性地转向其他位面以探索、调停争端或为——「**埃拉斯提尔**」（Erastil）、「**艾奥梅黛**」（Iomedae）以及——「**席琳**」（Shelyn）——一类的神圣神祇担任使节。那些强大到足以被视为——「**天界至尊**」（empyreal lord）——的天界使常乐于他们准神圣的地位采纳关切的领域、新的职责以及一群崇拜者。塔尔曼铎不然。尽管强大到足以授予法术即便是一丝神祇与门徒关系的暗示对他来说也感觉过于专制。相反他为凡人提供建议相信每个人都应当有机会讨论他们的挑战并为自己寻找解决方案。

这种哲学也塑造了塔尔曼铎如何指引安多兰这不足为奇。被——「**加尔特**」（Galt）——自切利亚斯分立之时的自由呼喊所吸引塔尔曼铎对随之而来的仇恨暴力望而却步。即便如此他重整并阅读了诗人——「**达尔·朱班尼奇**」（Darl Jubannich）——的作品（他的文字激发了加尔特的反叛）——相信这些同样的文字若被适当地引导便能和平地解放一个国家。当安多兰的读者们一页页地翻阅达尔·朱班尼奇的出版物并思索一个自切利亚斯解放的未来之时塔尔曼铎便拜访他们并把他们的愤怒稍加温和以使他们能想象的不是报复而是一个更光明的未来。

塔尔曼铎在安多兰的影响不容小觑。虽然他对崇拜的回避阻止安多兰人为崇拜他而形成邪教但许多宣扬安多兰的自由、正直以及民主的美德的著作都以他的名字命名或者以纪念他而创作。例如安多兰在——「**阿卡迪亚海洋**」（Arcadian Ocean）——之上构筑于——「**阿兹兰特**」（Azlant）废墟之上的雄心勃勃的殖民地被称为——「**塔尔曼铎之赐**」（Talmandor's Bounty）。（塔尔曼铎是否意识到这座岛屿边疆的存在尚未明朗。）他与猛禽的联系以及他自己猛禽般的容貌不仅是安多兰的——「**鹰骑士团**」——的灵感来源也是其派系——「**钢猎隼**」（Steel Falcons）——与——「**暮光之爪**」（Twilight Talons）——的灵感来源。

#### 战役角色（Campaign Role）

历史上塔尔曼铎大约每年拜访安多兰一次以惊叹于这个国家的进步、就困难的挑战向其领袖提建议并保证安多兰在宇宙之中绝不孤独。即使如此他拒绝担任任何除导师以及偶尔的吉祥物之外的角色拒绝任何把他神圣化或崇拜他的尝试。

在——《地狱天命》之中安多兰面对着前所未有的战争。先前塔尔曼铎不希望参与凡人的战斗他认识到切利亚斯的超凡雇佣兵以及战争的重大赌注是如此巨大以至于若没有他的帮助安多兰可能会被摧毁。即便如此大规模队形的挤压对他来说过于拘束所以塔尔曼铎帮助集结并领率敏捷的突击队为安多兰的军队开路。他的参与是一个藏不住的秘密并迅速成为安多兰的一种集结呼号它欣喜地在本探险路线的第 7 章获得他的直接援助。

不幸的是切利亚斯已为这一意外做了准备。在第 7 章的末尾塔尔曼铎已被擒获并被送给阿波罗盖二世她打算在与地狱做出一份新的交易之时把他作为她的筹码！解救塔尔曼铎成为——PC 的一个新的目标因为这不只是拯救一位正直的存在它也会剥夺切利亚斯把这个国家转化为地狱的一个新层域所必需的东西。

Talmandor
Borne on magnificently feathered wings, Talmandor
is a powerful celestial with a mix of humanoid and
avian features. He spends much of his time traveling
Nirvana, periodically veering into other planes to
explore, mediate disputes, or act as an ambassador
for holy deities like Erastil, Iomedae, and Shelyn.
Those celestials powerful enough to be considered
empyreal lords often relish their quasi-divine status,
adopting areas of interest, new responsibilities, and
a coterie of worshippers. Not Talmandor. Though
powerful enough to grant spells, even the faintest hint
of a deity-disciple relationship feels too autocratic to
him. Instead, he offers advice to mortals, believing
that everyone should have opportunities to discuss
their challenges and find solutions for themselves.

It's no surprise this philosophy also shapes how
Talmandor has guided Andoran. Intrigued by the
cries for freedom in Galt as it seceded from Cheliax,
Talmandor balked at the hateful violence that
followed. Even so, he recovered and read the works
of the poet Darl Jubannich (whose writing inspired
Galt's rebellion), believing these same words could
liberate a nation peacefully if guided properly. As
Andoren readers paged through Darl Jubannich's
publications and contemplated a future free from
Cheliax, Talmandor visited them and tempered their
rage enough that they could imagine not revenge, but
a brighter future.

Talmandor's influence in Andoran can't be
underplayed. While his aversion to veneration
prevents Andorens from forming cults to worship
him, numerous works that promote Andoran's
virtues of freedom, integrity, and democracy bear his
name or have been created in his honor. Andoran's
ambitious settlement in the Arcadian Ocean built
upon the ruins of Azlant is called Talmandor's
Bounty, for example. (It's unclear if Talmandor is
even aware of the existence of this island frontier.)
His association with birds of prey and his own
raptorlike visage is the inspiration for not only
Andoran's Eagle Knights, but also its factions the
Steel Falcons and Twilight Talons.

Campaign Role
Historically, Talmandor has visited Andoran roughly
once a year to marvel in the nation's progress, advise
its leaders about difficult challenges, and assure
Andoran that it's never alone in the cosmos. Even
then, he refuses to act as anything other than a
mentor and occasional mascot, denying any attempt
to deify or worship him.

In Hell's Destiny, Andoran faces a war like never
before. Where previously Talmandor wanted no part in
fighting mortals' battles, he recognizes that Cheliax's
otherworldly mercenaries and the war's monumental
stakes are so considerable that Andoran could be
destroyed without his help. Still, the press of massed
formations is too restrictive to him, so Talmandor
helps assemble and lead agile strike teams to clear
the way for Andoran's armies. His participation is a
poorly kept secret and quickly becomes a rallying cry
for Andoran, which is thrilled to have his direct aid
during Chapter 7 of this Adventure Path.

Unfortunately, Cheliax has prepared for this
contingency. By the end of Chapter 7, Talmandor
has been captured and delivered to Abrogail II, who
intends to use him as her bargaining chip when
making a new deal with Hell! Liberating Talmandor
becomes a new goal for the PCs, for not only would
it save a righteous being, but it would deny Cheliax
what it needs to transform the country into a new
layer of Hell.
