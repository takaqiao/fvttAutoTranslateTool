## Page 224 — 切利亚斯后裔 · 典型职业专长（续）

#### 贵族武艺（ARISTOCRATIC ARMS）　【**4 级**】

**典型职业**

**先决条件** 切利亚斯后裔入选

通过持续的（而且常常是惩罚性的）——练习你已学会如何使用你家族所偏好的武器。你具备以下之一的熟练度：你所选择的一种非寻常武器；自你家族的偏好武器列表中你所选择的两种寻常或非寻常武器；或者自你家族的偏好武器列表中你所选择的一种高级武器。出于熟练度目的把这些选项之中任何为非寻常武器者视为寻常武器并把任何为高级武器者视为非寻常武器。如果你在所选武器之一上至少为专家级你以该武器获得重击之时便获得该武器的重击专精效果。

**夏塔尼翁**：具有「**缴械**」（disarm）或「**格档**」（parry）特征的武器
**埃利恩多**：徒手武器以及小刀类
**亨德尔森**：锤以及镐
**杰加雷**：单手剑
**勒朗**：棒以及盾
**纳里科普卢斯**：弓
**萨里尼**：链枷
**斯戎**：棒以及十字弓

此外你获得——「**为我的家族！**」（For My House!）——动作。

**为我的家族！** [自由动作]　**频率** 每分钟一次；**效果** 你宣布你的家族归属既可以大声宣告（赋予此能力——「**听觉**」特征）——也可公开展示你的家族纹章（赋予此能力——「**视觉**」特征）。直至你下一回合的开始你的武器一击以及法术对任何目睹了这一展示的生物造成额外的 1 点伤害。如果你在——「**切利亚斯学识**」（Cheliax Lore）、「**谱系学识**」（Genealogy Lore）、「**纹章学识**」（Heraldry Lore）、「**表演**」（Performance）或者——「**战争学识**」（Warfare Lore）——上为专家级这额外伤害便增至 2 点。如果你在该项技能上为大师级这伤害便增至 3 点或者如果你在该项技能上为传奇级则增至 4 点。

#### 资助艺术（PATRONIZE THE ARTS）　[反应] 【**4 级**】

**典型职业**

**先决条件** 切利亚斯后裔入选

**触发** 距你——30 英尺内的一名同伴在一次攻击骰或技能检定上极佳成功（critically succeed）。

一名真正的贵族懂得如何鼓舞他们最为优秀的雇佣人员确保他们的忠诚以及未来的卓越表现。你赞扬此触发动作的同伴授予他们以下益处之一。该同伴便对此能力免疫——1 天。

**热忱**（Eagerness）：受赞扬所激励该同伴可作为一项反应使用「**佯攻**」（Feint）、「**与附近物品互动**」（Interact）、「**跳跃**」（Leap）、「**装填**」（Reload）或「**移步**」（Step）。

**坚持**（Persistence）：在——1 分钟之内该同伴获得等同于你等级的一半加上你魅力修正的临时生命值。

**确证**（Validation）：如果该同伴具有——「**潇洒**」（panache）——职业特性他们获得潇洒。

#### 坚执如地狱（STOIC AS HELL）　[反应] 【**6 级**】

**典型职业**

**先决条件** 切利亚斯后裔入选；「**威吓**」（Intimidation）受训；「**意志**」（Will）专家

**频率** 每分钟一次

**触发** 你被一项情绪效应或一项会给予你——「**受控**」状态的效应所目标。

一如大魔王——**狄斯佩特**（Dispater）——你即便在不可能的压力之下也保持一种不可撼动的风度。你对此触发动作的豁免或者你抵御此触发效应的——「**意志 DC**」——获得一项——+2 状况加值。如果你在豁免上极佳成功（或者如果这效应的源头在其技能检定上极佳失败例如对你使用——「**威慑**」（Demoralize））——你便获得等同于你等级一半的临时生命值直至该效应源头下一回合结束。如果该效应源自一个生物你可在你下一回合以一项自由动作对该生物——「**威慑**」——一次；如果该生物曾对你——「**威慑**」——的尝试暂时免疫它便失去那一免疫。

ARISTOCRATIC ARMS
LEVEL 4
ARCHETYPE
Prerequisites Chelaxian Scion Dedication

Through consistent (and often punishing) practice, you've
learned how to use your house's preferred weapons.
You have familiarity with either one martial weapon
of your choice; two simple or martial weapons of your
choice from your house's preferred weapons list; or one
advanced weapon of your choice from your house's
preferred weapons list. For the purpose of proficiency,
treat any of these selections that are martial weapons
as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons
as martial weapons. If you're at least an expert in one of
the selected weapons, you get the critical specialization
effect of that weapon when you get a critical hit with it.

Charthagnion: Weapons with the disarm or parry trait
Elliendo: Brawling weapons and knives
Henderthane: Hammers and picks
Jeggare: One-handed swords
Leroung: Clubs and shields
Narikopolus: Bows
Sarini: Flails
Thrune: Clubs and crossbows

In addition, you gain the For My House! action.

For My House! [free-action] Frequency once per minute; Effect You
announce your house affiliation, either by declaring it
aloud (giving this ability the auditory trait) or openly
displaying your house heraldry (giving this ability the
visual trait). Until the beginning of your next turn, your
weapon Strikes and spells deal an additional 1 damage
to any creature that witnessed the display. If you're an
expert in Cheliax Lore, Genealogy Lore, Heraldry Lore,
Performance, or Warfare Lore, the additional damage
increases to 2. The damage increases to 3 if you're a
master in the skill or 4 if you're legendary in the skill.

PATRONIZE THE ARTS [reaction]
LEVEL 4
ARCHETYPE
Prerequisites Chelaxian Scion Dedication

Trigger An ally within 30 feet of you critically succeeds at
an attack roll or skill check.

A true aristocrat knows how to encourage their best
hirelings, ensuring loyalty and future excellence. You praise
the triggering ally, granting them one of the following
benefits. The ally is then immune to this ability for 1 day.

Eagerness: Invigorated by praise, the ally can Feint,
Interact with a nearby object, Leap, Reload, or Step as a
reaction.

Persistence: For 1 minute, the ally gains temporary
Hit Points equal to half your level plus your Charisma
modifier.

Validation: If the ally has the panache class feature, they
gain panache.

STOIC AS HELL [reaction]
LEVEL 6
ARCHETYPE
Prerequisites Chelaxian Scion Dedication, trained in
Intimidation, expert in Will

Frequency once per minute

Trigger You're targeted by an emotion effect or effect that
would give you the controlled condition.

Like the archdevil Dispater, you maintain an unshakable
poise even under impossible stress. You gain a +2
circumstance bonus to the triggering saving throw or to
your Will DC against the triggering effect. If you critically
succeed at the saving throw (or if the source of the effect
critically fails its skill check, such as to Demoralize you),
you gain temporary Hit Points equal to half your level
until the end of the effect's source's next turn. If the
effect originated from a creature, you can Demoralize
that creature once as a free action on your next turn; if
the creature was temporarily immune to your attempts to
Demoralize it, it loses that immunity.
