## Page 216 — 寇兰廷 · 街区（续）+ 要塞开篇

——寻找有价值或难寻之物的人在商贸区会有最好的机会。

#### 海军坞与船坞（NAVY YARD AND SHIP YARD）

所有的船在入港之时都会停泊于这两处视其为军用还是民用船只而定。船坞的设防与寇兰廷其余各处一致而海军坞的设防则更甚于此。这些防护同时把不受欢迎的船拒之在外并确保被扣押的船留在内。

#### 贵族区（THE NOBLE QUARTERS）

一道墙纵贯贵族区把它分为西半与东半。对那些其庄园已在此驻留数个世纪的家族而言两个街区之间的差别单纯只是彰显他们的传承。而对居于其外的人来说东半区则略新且略胜一筹。

#### 仓库区（WAREHOUSE DISTRICT）

方形的石砌建筑与宽阔的街道便于货物的运输与存放。这一区在傍晚时分大多空荡除了那些试图借夜幕掩护潜行而过的人。

#### 西沼（WEST DRENCHES）

那些付不起东沼居住费用的艺术家筑起了它的姊妹街区西沼。这里夜生活繁盛尽管多塔里的定期突袭阻碍了一种真正的自由之感蓬勃滋长。

### 要塞（Forts）

尽管冠以——「**九堡之城**」（City of Nine Forts）——之名寇兰廷实拥有无数抵御侵略与围攻的防御工事。旅行作家与政府官员会汇编列表记录哪些要塞算作所列九堡之一但这些列表会更换因为更古老的结构会化为废墟而新的工事则在新市政工程的大张旗鼓之中拔地而起。

#### 1. 异端魁首守备宅（ARCHHEATHEN'S GARRISON）

坐落于该城行政建筑群的正中异端魁首守备宅更多地发挥着法庭与私人城堡而非常备要塞的功用。其下一片巨大的隧道网络纵横蔓延通往遍布贵族区的各座建筑。它们通向贵族常去之处既包括意料之中的捷径（例如通往私人庄园）——也包括其他更隐秘的区域例如一处废弃的仓库或——「**邪叉酒馆**」（the Wicked Fork）——的地下室。只有通往——「**异端魁首庄园**」——与——「**多弗拉姆要塞**」（Dhoffram Keep）——的隧道被标注出来其余的则需要对这一网络的了解或熟练的通路找寻才能通过。

#### 2. 奥罗登要塞（ARODEN'S REDOUBT）

奥罗登要塞自城外墙附近的——**奥罗登拱桥**——升起。两者都遭受了同样的命运倾塌。砌筑其石材的灰浆大多在盐雾空气中已溶解。它的一座岗哨塔曾俯瞰海峡并驻扎着港口军队的主要驻防后来坠入其下的海中。其长满藻苔的巨大躯体在海峡之中造成了一处瓶颈。定期会有提案递到异端魁首的案头请求将这道屏障自水中移除但迄今为止他尚未找到一个合适的时机封闭海峡以便进行这一工事。

NAVY YARD AND SHIP YARD
All ships dock in these locations when they come into
port, depending on if they're a military or civilian
vessel. The Ship Yard is as fortified as the rest of
Corentyn, and the Navy Yard even more so. These
protections simultaneously keep unwanted ships out
and ensure seized ships stay in.

THE NOBLE QUARTERS
A wall runs down the Noble Quarters, separating it
into a western and an eastern half. To the families
whose estates have resided here for centuries, the
difference between the neighborhoods simply speaks
to their heritage. To those who live outside it, the
eastern quarter is slightly newer and nicer.

WAREHOUSE DISTRICT
Boxy stone buildings and wide streets allow for the
easy transport and storage of goods. The district is
mostly abandoned in the evenings, except for those
trying to sneak by under the cover of darkness.

WEST DRENCHES
Artists who couldn't afford to live in East
Drenches built up the sister neighborhood
of West Drenches. Nightlife flourishes, though
regular dottari raids prevent a true sense of
freedom from flourishing.

Forts
Despite bearing the title of the City of Nine Forts,
Corentyn contains countless fortifications against
invasion and siege. While travel writers and
government officials compile lists of which forts
count as one of the titled nine, the lists change as
older structures fall into ruin and new ones rise with
the fanfare of new civic works.

1. ARCHHEATHEN'S GARRISON
Sitting in the center of the city's administrative
buildings, the Archheathen's Garrison functions
more as a courthouse and personal castle than
an active fort. A massive tunnel network sprawls
beneath it that leads to buildings throughout the
Noble Quarters. They access places that the nobility
frequent, including expected shortcuts (such as to
personal estates), and other more clandestine areas,
like an abandoned warehouse or the basement of the
Wicked Fork. Only the tunnel to the Archheathen's
Manor and Dhoffram Keep are marked, requiring
knowledge of the system or skilled navigation for
the rest.

2. ARODEN'S REDOUBT
Aroden's Redoubt rises from the Arch of Aroden
near the city's outer wall. Both have suffered the
same fate: collapse. The mortar used in laying the
stones of both largely dissolved in the salt-strewn
air. One of its sentry towers, which once watched
over the strait and housed the primary garrison of
the port's military, fell into the sea beneath it. Its
algae-coated bulk creates a bottleneck in the strait.
Regular proposals come to the archheathen's desk
asking for the barrier to be removed from the water,
but so far, he hasn't found a suitable time to close
the strait for the work.
