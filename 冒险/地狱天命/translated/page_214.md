## Page 214 — 寇兰廷 · 切利亚斯海军（续）+ 平民 + 半身人下层 + 街区开篇

寇兰廷的所有公民都面临强制征召他们必须在 16 至 25 岁之间服役两年。随着战事的持续一项提案已推进拟将必要的兵役延长至三年。最年轻的入伍者会谈论他们的希望希望被分派到一支战斗巡逻队并赢得足够的声望以便在军中拾级而上但只有最具军事天赋者才能实现那份梦想。然而所有被征召者都将被测试其驾驭魔鬼的资质。许多**邪魔信奉者**（diabolist）——最初便是在寇兰廷的「**秘理院**」（Esoterium）——一所专注于施法的海军军官俱乐部接受了他们的训练。

由这座切利亚斯城市自己的海军所进行的长达数个世纪的占领惹怒了该城反权威的一部分人口。近来——**马尔卡·斯威夫顿**（Marka Swifton；造反的男性半身人法师 3 级）——在服完他的兵役之后发起了一场揭露运动试图揭露腐败与失政。秘理院的领袖——**齐尔图拉斯·德梅斯特里奥**（Zilthuras Demestrio；威严的男性人类邪魔信奉者）——公开焚毁了马尔卡的法术书并对其捉拿悬出了赏金。

### 平民（COMMONERS）

寇兰廷的平民几乎全是人类他们以些许的自由与自我表达勉强换取稳定。然而随着他们从贸易业所得到的微薄的利润空间日渐缩水风气正转而反对统治阶层。愿意任由多塔里在自家桌前免费畅饮的酒馆老板越来越少而愿意在街上停留在魔鬼身旁的则更少。虽说寇兰廷加强了对非阿斯摩蒂斯崇拜的治安管理但对其他众神的信仰已愈发深沉。几乎每一户平民家庭都藏着一座他们必须瞒着邻居隐匿的非法神龛。

寇兰廷的迷信曾经认定最重要的莫过于不要招人注目然而过去这一年却给——**西沼**（West Drenches）——带来了一场整个街区参与的修缮行动。

### 半身人下层（THE HALFLING UNDERCLASS）

凡是此类阶层壁垒昌盛之处便必有一个下层阶级劳作维系这壁垒。半身人占该城人口近三分之一使这里成为阿维斯坦半身人最大的聚居地之一。其中大多数人曾被切利亚斯人类奴役。那些仍在贵族庄园与葡萄园工作的人会发现奴役的滋味并未从他们雇主的舌尖褪去。半身人社群之中一大部分靠正规雇用之外勉强糊口他们转而使用社群互助与犯罪化活动的网络来维持果腹。非人类血裔常与他们结盟尤其是那些也曾被切利亚斯奴隶贸易所裹挟的人。那些曾参与把半身人从奴役中解放出来的人例如——「**无声剪影**」（Silent Silhouette；复仇心切的女性半身人游荡者 9 级）——如今正致力于保护半身人社群的安全。

### 街区（Neighborhoods）

街区在寇兰廷至关重要这座城市封闭、不轻信的本性意味着一些市民会一辈子都活在同样的几个街区之中。

#### 下港区（DOWNDOCK）

只有最贫困的平民才住在下港区这里曾经居住着被奴役的劳工。虽然这些贫民窟无人打理但它们为船坞附近提供了廉价的住所。

#### 东沼（EAST DRENCHES）

东沼街区是浪漫主义者们的驻地这里一间小店可能只卖一种款式的陶制汤匙架却仍能招来一群常客。少于 10 个座位的咖啡馆比比皆是同样也有画家在日复一日地创作成系列的户外写生画作。这片以店主居多的街区会留意街上人的着装与举止是否得体。他们会迅速地把衣着不当者驱往——**仓库区**（Warehouse District）。

#### 东城（EASTTOWN）

那些发了一笔小财的殷实商人与平民倾向于居住在东城。这里的街道较之城中别处的陡峭坡势是缓缓爬升更宜于夜间散步。这里街区对园艺的共同兴趣也更增这一氛围。

#### 绞架之墙（GALLOWS WALLS）

寇兰廷的所有废墟之中——「**绞架之墙**」——或许是受损最严重的。**奥罗登拱桥**——的持续坍塌大致每月都会给这里带来额外的损害。古怪的声响则定期自其门内传出尤其是在夜间。

#### 高地区（HIGH QUARTER）

有志于改善自身境况的平民聚拢在高地区这里手艺人与文官混居一处。多塔里监管着这一熙攘并为权贵提供额外的保护。隐秘的神龛则藏于光天化日之下伪装为店面与画室的构件。

where they must serve for two years between the ages
of 16 and 25. With the ongoing war, a proposal has
advanced to extend the required military service to
three years. The youngest enlisted speak of their hope
to be assigned to a combat patrol and earn enough
notoriety to advance within the ranks, but only the
most martially talented achieve that dream. However,
all conscripts are tested for their aptitude at working
with devils. Many diabolists first receive their training
in Corentyn's Esoterium, a naval officer's club focused
on spellcasting.

The centuries-long occupation of a Chelaxian city
by its own navy angers the antiauthoritarian segment
of the city's population. Recently, Marka Swifton
(rebellious male halfling wizard 3) began an exposure
campaign after completion of his conscription,
seeking to reveal corruption and mismanagement. The
Esoterium's leader, Zilthuras Demestrio (austere male
human diabolist) publicly burned Marka's spellbooks

COMMONERS
Corentyn commoners are almost all human and
begrudgingly trade away some measure of freedom
and self-expression for stability. However, as the
slim margins they receive from the trading industry
shrink, attitudes shift against the ruling class. Fewer
tavern owners welcome the dottari to drink for free
at their tables, and fewer still will stay near a devil
in the streets. While Corentyn increased its policing
of non-Asmodean worship, belief in other gods has
deepened. Nearly every commoner's home contains an
illicit shrine they hide from neighbors.

While Corentyn superstitions once held that it was
most important to avoid drawing attention to oneself,
the past year brought a neighborhood-wide repair
effort in the West Drenches.

THE HALFLING UNDERCLASS
Where such stratification thrives, so must an underclass
toil to maintain it. Halflings make up nearly a third
of the city's population, making it one of the largest
concentrations in Avistan. Most were once enslaved by
Chelaxian humans. Those who still work in the noble
estates and vineyards find that the taste of slavery
hasn't left their employers' tongues. A sizable portion
of the halfling community scrapes together a life
outside of formal employment, instead using networks
of community support and criminalized activity to
remain fed. Non-human ancestries often ally with
them, especially those who were also formerly part
of the Chelaxian slave trade. Those who'd once been
involved in freeing halflings from slavery, such as
Silent Silhouette (vengeful female halfling rogue 9),
now work to keep halfling communities safe.

Neighborhoods
Neighborhoods matter deeply in Corentyn, where
the city's insular, untrusting nature means that some
citizens spend their whole lives in the same few blocks.

DOWNDOCK
Only the poorest commoners live in Downdock, which
once housed enslaved laborers. While these slums are
unmaintained, they provide cheap housing near the
shipyards.

EAST DRENCHES
The East Drenches neighborhood is the site of
romantics, where a shop might sell only one style of
ceramic spoon rests and still attract a regular customer
base. Cafes with fewer than 10 seats are common as
well as painters creating daily series of plein air works.
The shopkeeper-heavy neighborhood monitors those
on the street for proper dress and behavior. They
quickly shepherd the underdressed into the Warehouse
District.

EASTTOWN
The well-off merchants and commoners who've made
a small fortune tend to reside in Easttown. The streets
slope gradually in comparison to the steep pitch
elsewhere, which makes for pleasant evening strolls.
A neighborhood interest in gardening also assists this
atmosphere.

GALLOWS WALLS
Of all the ruins in Corentyn, Gallows Walls is perhaps
the most damaged. The continued collapse of the Arch
of Aroden causes additional damage, roughly on a
monthly basis. Strange sounds regularly come from
within its gates, particularly at night.

HIGH QUARTER
Commoners interested in advancing their fortune
crowd into High Quarter, where tradespeople and
civil servants mingle. The dottari oversee this bustle
and provides additional protection for the powerful.
Circumspect shrines hide in plain sight, disguised as
parts of shopfronts and studios.
