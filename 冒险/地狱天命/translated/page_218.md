#### 11. 历史博物馆（THE MUSEUM OF HISTORY）

寇兰廷的历史博物馆记述着该城的历史始于其于 1520 ar——作为一座塔尔多军队的防御工事之奠基经由切利亚斯崛起为一个国家直到它作为一个枢纽性港口城市在当今所扮演的角色。其馆长——**泽菲罗·巴林杰**（Zefiro Balinger；狡黠的男性人类历史学家 7 级）——主持馆藏与保护的优先事项不断抵制异端魁首施加的务必专注于——「**地狱帝国**」（Infernal Empire）——荣光之压力。虽然尚无人能找到（或捏造）——他规避审查的证据但泽菲罗仍位列异端魁首的亲自监视名单。

#### 12. 邪叉酒馆（THE WICKED FORK）

老板兼经营者——**奈尔德·哈瓦萨乌**（Neld Havasavu；多嘴的男性人类酒馆主 5 级）——通过把所有明显的违法行为汇报给多塔里以维持邪叉酒馆的安保。他更加在意的是保守其地下室的秘密因为他一直允许——**铃花会**（Bellflower Network）——为自身的需要使用这一空间。穿行于一楼熙攘的酒吧的额外的人手并不会引人注意。一旦有人注意到一些进入的半身人并未离开他便指向二楼的客房并眨眨眼他无权干涉一场在其酒吧的偶遇。

#### 13. 海军干船坞（NAVAL DRY DOCK）

海军船只的修补在干船坞进行。通常服其强制兵役的寇兰廷市民会被培训作为这些任务的主要工人。然而战事所需的士兵多于船坞工人。任何穿着一件船坞工人外套的人都可能溜近切利亚斯的船只只要他展现足够的自信。

#### 14. 鬼火戏院（WILL-O'-THE-WISP PLAYHOUSE）

鬼火戏院其表演入场每人只收 1 铜币主打喜剧表演与流行音乐。其新任女主人著名的——**阿波罗盖·克鲁恩**（Abrogail Croon；恣肆的双性流人类艾乌瓦林喜剧演员 7 级）——曾被人告知她若不提供一场传统歌剧便无法做到收支平衡但她夸口她的重心是为平民提供娱乐。这些尝试包括大量使用焰火而他们直接自——**阿尔肯斯塔**（Alkenstar）——进口焰火。

然而即便满座也无法收取足够的票房以抵一场演出的成本这引出了种种猜测这戏院可能是一家有组织犯罪的行会的幌子或者阿波罗盖·克鲁恩曾经是一位被流放至寇兰廷之前的富裕贵族。无论如何他们的运营勉强在每一场其充满挑衅意味的演出之时避开审查与镇压。

#### 15. 隼形船运清单公司（FALCON SHIPPING MANIFEST COMPANY）

隼形船运清单公司十年前便已经歇业。其彩绘的招牌已被磨蚀至几近无迹只剩隼鸟的爪子。偶尔仍会有订单出现在其装货码头之外它们通常逗留约一周然后消失。

11. THE MUSEUM OF HISTORY
Corentyn's Museum of History catalogs the city's history,
starting with its founding in 1520 ar as a Taldan army
fortification, through the rise of Cheliax as a nation, and
into its role as a pivotal port city in the present day. Its
curator, Zefiro Balinger (sneaky male human historian
7), directs the collection and preservation priorities,
constantly undermining pressure from the archheathen
to focus on the glories of the Infernal Empire. While no
one has been able to find (or fabricate) evidence that he
evades the censors, Zefiro remains on the archheathen's
personal watch list.

12. THE WICKED FORK
Owner and operator Neld Havasavu (talkative male
human tavern keeper 5) maintains the Wicked Fork's
security by reporting all obvious lawbreaking to the
dottari. He's much more concerned about keeping its
basement a secret, as he's been allowing the Bellflower
Network to use it for their own needs. Additional
bodies passing through the packed bar on the first floor
go unnoticed. If anyone ever takes note that some of the
halflings who enter don't leave, he points to the lodgings
on the second floor and winks—it isn't his business to
interrupt a chance meeting at his bar.

13. NAVAL DRY DOCK
Repairs to naval ships occur in the dry dock. Typically,
Corentyn citizens undergoing their mandatory
service would be trained as the primary
workers for these tasks. However, the war
effort demands more soldiers than dockworkers.
Anyone dressed in a dockworker's coat could slip
near the Chelaxian ships, provided they exuded
enough confidence.

14. WILL-O'-THE-WISP PLAYHOUSE
The Will-o'-the-Wisp Playhouse charges only 1 cp
per head for admission to their shows, with a focus
on comedic performances and popular music.
Their new proprietress, the famed Abrogail Croon
(outrageous genderfluid aiuvarin human comedian
7), was told that she wouldn't be able to break even
without offering a traditional opera, but she boasted
that her focus is on entertainment for commoners. These
endeavors include an excessive use of pyrotechnics,
which they import directly from Alkenstar.

However, even with a full house, it's impossible
to collect enough revenue to cover the cost of a
performance, leading to speculation that the playhouse
might be a front for an organized crime guild or that
Abrogail Croon was once a wealthy noble before being
exiled to Corentyn. Whatever the case, their operation
barely avoids censorship and crackdowns with each of
its provocative performances.

15. FALCON SHIPPING MANIFEST COMPANY
The Falcon Shipping Manifest Company went out of
business a decade ago. Its painted sign has chipped
away to mostly nothingness, leaving only the falcon's
talons. Occasional orders still appear outside its loading
dock, and they tend to stick around for about a week
before they disappear.
