## Page 253 — 厄登·砾掌（Urdun Gravelhands）· 背景

### 厄登·砾掌（Urdun Gravelhands）被囚的暮光之爪

问寇兰廷任何一名廉价酒馆的酒保他们是否认识厄登·砾掌他们会说认识。他是一名城里的醉鬼他的酒量很差他们会说。一名铁匠你若能从他夜里蹒跚回家的样子相信这一点。他好几个星期没露面但这并不意外；大多数人以为他最终会遭遇某种醉酒悲剧。

厄登作为醉鬼的恶名是一项精心演练的骗术经他的表演天赋与一名食人魔的明显的酒精耐受磨砺。一旦看似醉酒他便能在光天化日之下隐藏为一名蠢笨的傻瓜城内官员以为他不可能窃听他们的秘密谈话。事实上他是寇兰廷——「**鹰骑士团**」——的最高级别卧底其——「**暮光之爪**」（Twilight Talons）分支的一员。当他在奇怪的时辰东倒西歪之时厄登便把秘密讯息放置到情报投递点监视切利亚斯海军动向、把逃犯指引到城界之外、收集把柄并伺机把偶尔的一名恶棍推入港湾。

不幸的是他近来指引出寇兰廷的一名被囚者背叛了他使多塔里可以轻易地逮捕他。他们抓住这位长久以来的劲敌并把他扔给——「**镣铐骑士团**」。在他们的堡垒——「**格拉戴斯卡堡垒**」——之中厄登腐朽。无数的囚禁之岁月等待着他他所帮助的数百名逃犯的刑期累加。**执棒官 乌罗·阿登**——懒得做审判更愿取悦女王并证明他的忠诚。

一名暮光之爪的生活意味着理解风险而厄登把他的一条命换几百条命视为一笔公平的交易。**琳奈塔**——则不同意因为她被留下来完成——「**破钥行动**」。这场起义一直意在与——「**泰莎·费尔温**」（Tessa Fairwind）——的海军行动同时进行而他的工作已经快要超时。

#### 派系关系（Faction Relationships）

在他被捕之前厄登与琳奈塔正在为寇兰廷的解放以及其姊妹城市——「**卡利**」——做准备。由于他与——「**铃花会**」——的长期关系厄登把——「**暮光之爪**」——的计划托付给了——「**无声剪影**」。她正在把他与他们在卡利的舵手联系起来以便他们能为可能的冲突做准备。是——「**铃花会**」——的一员目睹了他的被捕以及随后未经审判便被拖往格拉戴斯卡堡垒。无声剪影知道他在城内有一名同伴但不知道是谁。一旦琳奈塔就历史博物馆的突袭与她联络铃花会便终于可以分享厄登的下落。

然而厄登与其他派系的联系更为紧张。「**碎冠者**」——并不信任他因为——「**凯旋**」（Triumph）——与——「**艾里尔**」（Eryr）——注意到他溜过寇兰廷的后巷太多次并看穿了他的醉酒伪装。他们以为他是多塔里或者地狱骑士的眼线尽管他最初试图让他们相信并非如此。他们尤其对他最初承诺的以暴力推翻切利亚斯掌控心存疑虑因为这正是一名卧底多塔里特工会说的话。当然他们的拒绝可能是出于一种保持独立无政府主义者的渴望以及一名真正的——「**暮光之爪**」——可能会证明——「**凯旋**」——多年来一直在撒谎说自己是其成员。

「**细缕者**」（Thin Wisps）——的阿波罗盖·克鲁恩在她不合时宜地被捕之前便开始与他交谈。这举动似乎与他们的利益相违因为城市战争会伤害平民并使更大规模的抗议运动效率降低。厄登提出与整个派系会面解释寇兰廷自由的必要性并给他们提供一个未来的愿景但他的被捕切断了这种可能性。

#### 战役角色（Campaign Role）

在本次冒险的开端之时厄登·砾掌被囚禁在格拉戴斯卡堡垒。他的被捕开启了——「**琳奈塔·海凿**」——重新团结这破碎的叛乱的努力。要救他她必须在多塔里甚至意识到离开寇兰廷的逃跑路径已被堵死之前开始——「**破钥行动**」。他在冒险开端的失踪制造了——PC——所介入的权力真空即便他们并不察觉。

作为最初被指派执行——「**破钥行动**」——的特工厄登为这次任务带来了琳奈塔所缺乏的经验。他多年的卧底生涯也为他提供了对寇兰廷的深入知识尽管这随着格拉戴斯卡堡垒的渗透的余波迫使叛乱逃离这座城市而证明较少有用。不过——「**暮光之爪**」——的间谍技艺意味着他可能会作为一名同伴在整场战役之中证明有用而他也是一名尤为忠诚的同伴因为他把他的命欠给了——PC。他最有可能在埃戈里安的渗透之中再次出现他对地狱蕴卫的寇兰廷要塞的了解可以协助——PC——闯入——「**帝国宫殿**」。

Urdun Gravelhands
Ask any swillhouse bartender in Corentyn if they
know Urdun Gravelhands, and they'll say yes. He's
a city drunk who can't hold his drink, they'll say. A
blacksmith, if you could believe it from the way he
spent his nights stumbling home. He hasn't been seen
for a few weeks, yet that's no surprise; most assumed he
would suffer some intoxicated tragedy eventually.

Urdun's infamy as a drunkard is a practiced ruse,
honed by his acting flair and the apparent alcohol
tolerance of an ogre. Once seemingly inebriated, he's
able to hide in plain sight as a dull-witted oaf whom city
officials assume couldn't be listening in on their secretive
conversations. In truth, he's the ranking undercover Eagle
Knight in Corentyn—a member of its Twilight Talons
branch. As he totters about at odd hours, Urdun deposits
secret messages at dead drops, spies on the Chelaxian
naval movements, shepherds fugitives beyond city limits,
gathers blackmail, and opportunistically pushes the
occasional villain into the harbor.

Unfortunately, one of the recent captives he ushered
out of Corentyn double-crossed him, making it easy for
the dottari to arrest him. They gripped their longtime
rival and threw him to the Order of the Manacle. In
their stronghold, Citadel Gheradesca, Urdun rots.
Lifetimes of imprisonment await him, the accumulated
sentences for the hundreds he helped escape. Lictor Uro
Adom didn't bother with a trial, preferring to please the
queen and prove his loyalty.

The life of a Twilight Talon means understanding risk,
and Urdun considers his one life for hundreds to be a
fair deal. Linetta disagrees, as she's been left to complete
Operation Broken Key. The uprising was always meant
to coincide with Tessa Fairwind's naval operation, and
his work was already running out of time.

Faction Relationships
Before his arrest, Urdun and Linetta were preparing
Corentyn for liberation, as well as its sister city Khari.
Due to his long-term relationship with the Bellflower
Network, Urdun entrusted Silent Silhouette with
the Twilight Talons' plans. She was in the process of
connecting him with their tiller in Khari so they could
prepare for potential conflict. It was one of the Bellflower
Network who witnessed his arrest and subsequent
hauling off to Citadel Gheradesca without a trial. Silent
Silhouette knew he had an ally in the city, but not who
it was. Once Linetta contacted her about the raid on
the Museum of History, the Bellflower Network could
finally share Urdun's whereabouts.

However, Urdun's connection with the other factions
are more strained. The Crowncrushers don't trust him,
as Triumph and Eryr noticed him sneaking through the
back alleys of Corentyn too many times and saw through
his drunken ruse. They assume that he's an informer for
the dottari or the Hellknights, despite his initial attempts
to convince them otherwise. They're particularly hesitant
about his initial promise for a violent overthrow of
Chelaxian control, as that's exactly what an undercover
dottari agent would say. Of course, their rejection might
be out of a desire to remain independent anarchists—and
the likelihood that a real Twilight Talon would prove that
Triumph had been lying about being a member for years.

Abrogail Croon of the Thin Wisps began talking with
him before her untimely arrest. The action seemed against
their interests, as urban warfare would harm civilians
and make a greater protest movement less effective.
Urdun offered to meet with the entire faction, explain the
necessity of Corentyn's freedom, and offer them a vision
of the future, but his arrest cut short that possibility.

Campaign Role
At the start of this adventure, Urdun Gravelhands is
imprisoned in Citadel Gheradesca. His arrest starts
Linetta Seacarver's push to unite the broken rebellion.
To rescue him, she must begin Operation Broken Key
before the dottari are even aware that the escape route
from Corentyn is blocked. His disappearance at the
start of the adventure creates the absence of power that
the PCs step into, even if they aren't aware of it.

As the original agent assigned to Operation Broken
Key, Urdun brings experience to the mission that
Linetta lacks. His years spent undercover also provided
him with a deep knowledge of Corentyn, though that
proves less useful as the fallout from the infiltration
of Citadel Gheradesca forces the rebellion to flee the
city. Still, the Twilight Talons' spycraft techniques mean
that he might prove useful as an ally throughout the
campaign—and a particularly loyal one since he owes
the PCs his life. He's most likely to reappear during the
infiltration of Egorian, where his knowledge of infernalwarded Corentyn fortresses can assist the PCs with
breaking into the Imperial Palace.
