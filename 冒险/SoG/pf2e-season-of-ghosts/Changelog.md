## 13.0.0 Initial Release

- **Completely remade, highly detailed, and immersive adventure maps** with support for Foundry Virtual Tabletop's Animated Doors, Overhead Tiles and Foreground Layer features.
- **Journal Entries** for the entire contents of the book with customized styling for the adventure.
- **Scenes pre-configured** with walls, animated doors, lights, sound, tokens, and hazards already placed to provide GMs the most convenient experience running the adventure.
- **Soundscapes** by [Michael Ghelfi Studios](https://www.michaelghelfistudios.com/) provided as ambient playlists and local sound sources to immerse your players.
- **High-resolution character artwork** and tokens for all NPCs and foes in the Adventure.
- **Full support for FoundryVTT's Dynamic Token Rings**, including a unique dynamic ring.
- **A Custom Turn Marker** specifically made for the Adventure's unique ring.
- **Macros and Effects** created to help run your adventure.
- **Custom made 3D dice** usable with the free add-on FoundryVTT module Dice so Nice.
- **Music** in the form of an entire album by Michael Ghelfi Studios, composed for the Season of Ghosts Adventure Path.
