[18] normal | A More Natural Opening. Horror in ttrpgs is about a build up of tension. There is value in starting players straight into the scary stuff, really getting them in the mood, but there's also as much value in starting them in the calm before the storm. This is also a better place to let players get their footing RPing the characters. Normally, your players are thrown head first into this culture during a crisis. All the while they are figuring out their character and everyone else's. The reenactment festival is far less pressure, and its focus on rp over setting up the mystery is a better ground work for players to settle into things. This way when it comes time to deal with the crisis they will already be invested in the npcs, knowledgeable of the culture, and confident in rping their characters.
